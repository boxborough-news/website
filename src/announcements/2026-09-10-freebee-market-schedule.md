The **FreeBee Community Market** is volunteer-powered food rescue: all welcome, all free.

- **Food Rescue Saturdays:** every Saturday from July 11 to October 24, with rescued fresh fruit, vegetables and baked goods.
- **Monthly themed Community Markets**, adding craft and art supplies, books, community booths and kids' activities: July 11 (first Community Market), August 15 (school supplies), September 12 (harvest/agriculture theme) and **October 24 (costume exchange)**.

Both are always 10:30 a.m. to noon (or until the food is gone) at 30 Middle Road, Boxborough, across from Town Hall. More information: <http://bit.ly/FreeBeeMarket>, @FreeBeeCommunity.
