You're invited! **Boxborough Village for a Day** is **Saturday, October 3, 11:30 a.m. to 4 p.m.** (rain date Saturday, October 17) along Mass Ave (Route 111) at Middlesex Bank Plaza and the UCC Church lawn. Eat, shop, discover and support local: live music, kids' activities, food vendors, local businesses and artisans, and new this year, mini golf. Sponsored by the Boxborough Economic Development Committee.
