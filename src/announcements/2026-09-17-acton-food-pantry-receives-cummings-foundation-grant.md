The Acton Food Pantry (AFP) is proud to announce that it received an additional $40,000 grant from the Cummings Foundation to support food purchasing costs and new facility enhancements. The gift was made following a site visit by Cummings representatives.

The Cummings Foundation Make a Difference Dollars (MAD$) program invites Cummings Site Visit Committee members to nominate organizations they have visited for additional funds. The committee discusses the nominees and determines which nonprofits will receive supplemental donations. The selection of the Acton Food Pantry indicates that the site visitors found the organization as contributing valuable service to its community.

Following his visit, one of the Cummings visitors wrote an email to Lisa Gordon, AFP’s Executive Director explaining how impressed he was “…not only by the efficiency and excellence of the Pantry's operations, but…by its heart. What stays with me is the extraordinary respect with which every person is welcomed, listened to, and treated. In a world where those struggling with food insecurity can so easily feel invisible or diminished, your Pantry has created a place where people encounter compassion before they select and receive groceries. That is a remarkable achievement.” 

The site visitor extended his congratulations to the “nimble staff” and board, but especially to the Pantry’s volunteers, saying “They are the quiet heroes that keep this social safety net together.  Their generosity, kindness, and faithful service are written into every shelf that is stocked, every bag that is packed, and every family that leaves knowing they have been treated with dignity and compassion…”

While the Cummings site visitors were inspired to nominate AFP to support food purchasing costs, which have risen dramatically in recent months, and new facility enhancements that have yet to be completed following the renovation of our new home, the funds are not restricted so the organization maintains the discretion to apply these funds wherever they are most needed.

AFP is grateful to the Cummings Foundation for these additional funds and recognizes that this gift is owed largely to the exceptional support of our community members who invest their funds and their time into making our pantry a vital and effective organization.
