Domestic Violence Services Network, Inc. (DVSN) **Volunteer Advocate Training, September 28 through October 16, 2026.**

DVSN is a community-based organization that works in collaboration with the police departments of its twelve member towns (Acton, Bedford, Boxborough, Carlisle, Concord, Lexington, Lincoln, Maynard, Stow, Sudbury, Wayland, and Weston), Hanscom AFB, Concord District Court, and Emerson Hospital to combat domestic violence. DVSN advocates provide direct service to domestic violence survivors.

The 3-week, 40-hour classroom training will be held at Lexington PD, 1575 Massachusetts Ave., Lexington: Monday and Wednesday evenings, 4 to 7:15 p.m., and Fridays, 9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. Participants include potential volunteer advocates, community service providers, and local law enforcement personnel. Free to all participants. Volunteer advocates also complete 10 to 12 hours of supervised field training afterward.

Call (978) 318-3421 or email <training@dvsn.org> for more information or to request an application. Applications were due by Wednesday, September 16, 2026.
