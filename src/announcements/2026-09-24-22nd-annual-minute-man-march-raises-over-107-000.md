Inclusive Walk and Celebration Supports Critical Programming for
Individuals with Disabilities in Middlesex County

CONCORD, Mass. (Sept. 22, 2026) – [Minute Man Arc](https://www.minutemanarc.org/), a social services organization for children and adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD) in Middlesex County, raised over $107,000 at its annual “Minute Man March” on September 19 to support comprehensive services for individuals with IDD. 

 Supporters joined individuals of all ages and abilities served by Minute Man Arc, and their families, for an accessible, one-mile lap around Concord center.  Clad in the event’s signature tie-dye shirts, over 400 participants raised funds and awareness to help sustain the agency’s residential, employment, and day habilitation services.  After the walk’s conclusion, attendees gathered at the Alcott School in Concord for refreshments, music, lawn games, and prizes.

“I am so grateful for all the amazing work that Minute Man Arc does, whether it’s early intervention, daycare, group homes, or helping parents in transition,” said Massachusetts state Senator and Minute Man Arc board member Jamie Eldridge to kick off the event.  “It serves such an important purpose to make sure that all people live a life with dignity.”

 “For more than two decades, the Minute Man March has been a powerful reminder of community, and today demonstrates how far the disability community and the agency have come since our founding,” said Peter Kilbridge, board president of Minute Man Arc.  “Each step and every pledge directly fuels the supports necessary for our neighbors with disabilities to live full, empowered lives.  We are beyond grateful to everyone who makes the March so special.”

In its 22nd year, the awareness walk and fundraiser continues to support over 1,400 individuals in more than 45 Massachusetts communities, including Acton, Bedford, Boxborough, Carlisle, Concord, Lincoln, Littleton, Maynard, Sudbury, and Stow. 

 Minute Man Arc applauds the generous support from its many event sponsors.

 About Minute Man Arc

 Established in 1958, Minute Man Arc changes the lives of children and adults with disabilities in Middlesex County by increasing independence, personal choice and self-advocacy.  The organization, since its inception, has grown to offer a wide range of programs and services including Early Intervention, Recreation & Family Services, Employment Services, Day Habilitation and Residential Services.  For more information, please visit [www.minutemanarc.org](https://minutemanarc.org/) or call (978) 287-7900.
