Andrew Gruskay is again captaining Team AFP for the 25th annual Three Squares New England Ride & Stride for Food which is held on September 27th, 2026.

One hundred percent of the donations to any of the participants will end up at the Acton Food Pantry; the riders themselves are fiscally supporting the ride via the entrance fee.

As team captain again this year for the Acton Food Pantry Ride, I'm looking to add teammates to the ride / walk; we had 10x last year. If you would prefer to ride / walk with your local town team, I can get you contact with them {if they exist}.

Food insecurity doesn’t always look the way people expect — especially in communities like Acton. On a weekly basis the Acton Food Pantry supports over 400+ households from the towns of Acton, Boxborough, Carlisle, Concord, Littleton, Maynard, Westford, and Stow.

In 2025, our ten members raised over $17K. This year it would be great to get more riders, and break the $20K mark. Every dollar raised by our team will go directly toward providing fresh and healthy foods to individuals and families in greater Acton who need it most.
