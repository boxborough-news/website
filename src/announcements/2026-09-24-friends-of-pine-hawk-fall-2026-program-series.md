The Friends of Pine Hawk 2026 Fall Program Series is presented with support from Acton Memorial Library and Freedom's Way National Heritage Area. **Events are free but require registration** at <https://tinyurl.com/2026-Pinehawk>.

- **Stone Prayers: An Update to the Inventory**: Thursday, October 15, 7 p.m., Acton Memorial Library
- **Exploring Ceremonial Stone Landscapes**: Saturday, October 24, 10 a.m.–noon, Upton (walk, in person only; limited to 15)
- **The Big World of New England's Native Americans**: Tuesday, October 27, 7 p.m., Acton Memorial Library (in person only)
- **Ceremonial Landscapes and the Algonquian Calendar**: Thursday, November 5, 7 p.m., Acton Memorial Library (in person only)
- **Community Service Day**: Sunday, November 8, 1–3 p.m., Nashoba Brook Conservation Land (limited to 25, ages 13+)
- **The Algonquin Atlantic**: Tuesday, November 10, 7 p.m., Acton Memorial Library
- **Fish Weir Creation Event**: Sunday, November 15, 1–3 p.m., NARA Park, North Acton (limited to 35)
- **Boxborough's Beaver Brook Valley Preserve (Esker) Presentations**: Saturday, November 21, 10–11 a.m., Sargent Memorial Library, Boxborough
- **George Krusen Memorial Beaver Brook Valley Preserve (Esker) Walk**: Saturday, November 21, 12:45–2:30 p.m., Boxborough (limited to 40, ages 13+)

Full descriptions of each program are in the flyer.
