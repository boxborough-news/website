Phones, social media, gaming, screen time, AI, online safety, mental health: how do we help our kids navigate it all, and where do we even begin?

Join local parents, teens, and experts for an honest, practical, judgment-free conversation about kids and technology on **Tuesday, October 20, at 7 p.m.** at Blanchard Memorial School, 493 Massachusetts Ave., Boxborough. Bring your questions, concerns, and experiences, and leave with ideas, resources, and strategies you can actually use at home.

Guest speaker: Adrienne Principe, founder of Turning Life On, a grassroots movement empowering parents, schools, and communities to rethink tech norms and support healthier screen use. Presented by the Blanchard PTF.
