The Nashua Valley Railroad Association's **Railfair 2026** model railroad train show is **Saturday and Sunday, October 3 and 4, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.** at the Boxboro Regency Hotel, 242 Adams Place, Boxborough (I-495 Exit 75, Route 111).

Two-day admission is $10 per person; children under 12 and Scouts in uniform are free. The show features dozens of vendors, operating layouts brought by a number of organizations, and a Kid's Caboose Corner with fun activities and an operating layout for kids of all ages.

Vendor inquiries: <showchair@nvrra.com> or [NVRRA.com](https://nvrra.com).
