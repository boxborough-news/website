The NABH has asked for assistance in completion of the following Massachusetts Community Health Equity survey (CHES). The survey can be accessed here: [https://www.mass.gov/community-health-equity-survey](https://www.mass.gov/community-health-equity-survey)

The Community Health Equity Survey uplifts groups that have often been underrepresented in important ways, like in data about their health and well-being. By taking the survey, you are adding your experience and expertise, and ensuring that your community is represented. 

Massachusetts is working to ensure that communities previously underrepresented or invisible in data and decision-making have a voice – particularly in the current climate. 

Participation in the survey is voluntary and completely anonymous. The survey does not ask for any personal identifying information (like a name, phone number, or email). Any question can be skipped. All survey responses and data are anonymous, and data that is shared is combined with other responses (aggregated). 

Please take a few moments to click on the link above to complete the survey.
