Boxborough's fifth annual **Diwalifest** is **Sunday, October 18, from noon to 4:30 p.m.** at the Sargent Memorial Library (rain date October 25).

The festival includes food from 17 local vendors, music, dancing, the Diya lighting, the Rangoli contest and performances. New this year: a games corner for teens on the library patio, folk Indian music, and a story reading at The Silver Unicorn.

Parking is at Blanchard Memorial School, Middlesex Savings Bank, Town Hall and the UCC church, with shuttle service; look for the Diwalifest parking signs. Bring a lawn chair if you like.
