Join us for our annual Halloween Spooktacular event including a magician (11am) and other fun activities provided by local preschools and businesses. Entertainment will be from 11:00-11:45. The first 30 minutes of the party (10-10:30am) will be geared for families that desire a more sensory-friendly experience.

Come dressed in your best family costume for a chance to win prizes donated by local businesses!

Attendance is free for ABFN families. Non-Member Families: $15 per family.

Pre-registration REQUIRED for Non-Member Families. Pre-registration for Non-Member Families closes the morning of 10/24. Register [Here](https://abfamily.org/content.aspx?page_id=4091&club_id=802795&item_id=3040806)

We will be collecting items for the Acton Boxborough Food Pantry. Please consider bringing a donation with you! Most needed items are: cereal, healthy snack items, baby food, and larger sized diapers (5, 6, or 4T-5T pullups). We will have a bin/donation area for drop off.

Saturday, October 24, 2026, 10:00 AM until 12:30 PM Eastern Time
