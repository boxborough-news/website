**Bazaar Russe** is Saturday, November 7, 11 a.m. to 5 p.m., at Holy Annunciation Orthodox Church, 15 Prospect Street, Maynard.

Eat in and take out, by reservation. Delicious meat and vegetarian choices include fresh home-baked goods, borscht, pierogi, stuffed cabbage, potato pancakes, peroshki, blini, kielbasa and more. Russian-style crafts and collectibles will be available for sale.

Advance orders are accepted starting Monday, November 2. Details: <http://tinyurl.com/BazaarRusse>
