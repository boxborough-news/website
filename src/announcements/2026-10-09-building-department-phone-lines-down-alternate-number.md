Town Planner Alec Wade is alerting residents that Boxborough’s Building Department and its inspectors have lost access to the Inspectional Services phone line.

“Over the last week, we've experienced a fault in our phone system…I don't know how long this has been occurring, but I have been told that technical support has not been able to resolve this issue. I also anticipate there are multiple permit holders who have not received inspections or call backs as a result…We are asking contractors and permit holders to call (978) 264-1726.”

“I and the Building team apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused, and we are working persistently to restore access to the voice mailbox. All permit holders requiring support or inspections from the Electric or Plumbing and Gas inspectors should contact Stephanie in the Building Department. Stephanie is available Monday through Thursday, and will coordinate with the inspectors promptly to schedule an inspection. If you have left a voice message in the typical inspectors line, please follow up with Stephanie to confirm if your inquiry was received.”
