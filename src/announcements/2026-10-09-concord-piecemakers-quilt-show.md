The **Concord Piecemakers Quilt Show** is Friday, October 16, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., and Saturday, October 17, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., at St. Matthew's United Methodist Church, 435 Central Street, Acton.

Admission is $10; children under 12 are free. The show features quilts, a boutique, vendors, a raffle quilt, fabric sale, sewing machine repair, scissor and knife sharpening, raffle baskets and a silent auction.

More information: [concordpiecemakers.org](http://www.concordpiecemakers.org)
