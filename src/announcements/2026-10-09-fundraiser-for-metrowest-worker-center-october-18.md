Joint Immigration Rights Event at Congregation Beth Elohim on October 18th @ 7pm

Many of us are asking, “How can I help my neighbors during this unprecedented attack on immigrant families?” UCC Boxborough members met with Congregation Beth Elohim, our partners in refugee support, to find answers to that question.

One way to help is to ensure that families and individuals are getting the legal representation they need and deserve, along with basic support when one of their family members is detained.

We invite you to join us on October 18th @ 7pm for a panel discussion and fundraiser at Congregation Beth Elohim.

All proceeds from the event will go to Metrowest Worker Center (MWC) in Framingham. They are an immigrant worker-led organization that provides accompaniment and support for legal services and basic needs. They are working with families through these terrible times. We hope to help them in their important work. Come join our friends at Congregation Beth Elohim for an important community effort.
