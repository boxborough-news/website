The Boxborough Conservation Trust presents **the George Krusen Memorial Talk and Walk** at the Beaver Brook Valley Preserve (Boxborough's esker).

**Presentation: Saturday, November 21, 10–11 a.m.**, Sargent Memorial Library, 427 Massachusetts Ave., Boxborough. Geologist Britt Argow will discuss not only how the local landscape developed over millions of years but also how Boxborough's esker was sculpted much more recently in the last Ice Age. Local historian and author Brent Ranalli will provide an overview of the local Native American cultural history, especially the critical contact period when Europeans arrived three centuries ago. Rita Grossman, the Boxborough Conservation Trust's president, will note how George Krusen's efforts were key to protecting this significant geological feature from being fully excavated for gravel to build I-495. The presentations will also provide helpful context for those who sign up for the walk later that day.

**Walk: Saturday, November 21, 12:45–2:30 p.m.**, Beaver Brook Valley Preserve, Whitcomb Road, Boxborough. Geologist Britt Argow will lead the walk on the trail on Boxborough's esker. George deeply appreciated both the natural beauty and geology as well as the Native American imprints still present on the land in our region. Britt will note significant geological features throughout the walk and Brian McClain of the Friends of Pine Hawk will point out possible Indigenous stone structures, including some described in the landmark book *Manitou*.

**Please note:** Key details, including preparation and where to meet for the walk, will only be emailed to registrants a few days in advance. This trail walk involves climbing steep terrain. The walk is for ages 13 and up and is limited to 20 participants. Rain date: Sunday, November 28, at 1 p.m.

Please RSVP for both events at <https://tinyurl.com/Nov21-BCT>.
