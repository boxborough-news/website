NorthEast ComicCon Brings Celebrity Guests, Comics, Toys and Holiday Shopping to Boxborough November 27-29

Thanksgiving weekend event combines pop culture shopping, artists, cosplay and evening entertainment at the Boxboro Regency Hotel

BOXBOROUGH, Mass., September 30, 2026 - Give the holiday shopping weekend a different kind of spark. NorthEast ComicCon & Collectibles Extravaganza returns to the Boxboro Regency Hotel November 27-29, 2026, bringing celebrity guests, comic creators, toys, memorabilia and fan activities together for three days of shopping and entertainment.

Held at 242 Adams Place in Boxborough, Massachusetts, the Holiday Shopping Show invites visitors to browse comics, original artwork, books, crafts and pop culture collectibles while meeting the people behind familiar characters and creative work. Free parking is available at the venue.

The show offers a holiday outing for collectors, families and fans whose gift lists reach beyond the usual department-store shelves. Comics and toys share the spotlight with art and memorabilia, giving shoppers a chance to discover something that connects with a recipient’s favorite character, creative interest or childhood memory.

General admission hours are:

Friday, November 27: 4 to 8 p.m.; VIP entry at 3 p.m.

Saturday, November 28: 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.; VIP entry at 9 a.m.

Sunday, November 29: 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.; VIP entry at 9 a.m.

Tickets are available online through Eventbrite and at the door. Current ticket options, pricing and availability can be checked through the official ticket listing.
