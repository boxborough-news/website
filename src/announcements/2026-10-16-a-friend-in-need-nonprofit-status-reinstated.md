**A Friend in Need** (AFIN) announces that after a 10-month hiatus, its tax-exempt nonprofit status has been retroactively reinstated, so donations made during that period are now fully tax-deductible.

AFIN provides immediate assistance, generally up to $600 at a time, until a person in need can receive more substantial aid from other agencies. Rising utility bills have kept it busy: several people it helped recently would otherwise have had their power cut off or been unable to fill their heating oil tanks.

AFIN will consider helping anyone recommended by clergy or social professionals affiliated with a local agency. Anyone in urgent need can ask a member of the clergy or a social service representative to contact AFIN on their behalf, or write to [afriendinneedacton@gmail.com](mailto:afriendinneedacton@gmail.com).

AFIN has a new mailing address: **15 Charter Road, Acton, MA 01720**. You can also donate at [abccompass.org/a-friend-in-need](https://abccompass.org/a-friend-in-need).
