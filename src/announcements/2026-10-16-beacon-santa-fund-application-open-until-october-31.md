Families who need help this holiday season can apply to the **Beacon Santa Fund** through **Saturday, October 31, 2026**. Approved applicants receive a store gift card, mailed in early December.

Applications must be co-signed by a licensed social worker, clergy member, school nurse, principal or other designated professional, and must include a letter explaining how the Beacon Santa could brighten the family's holiday. Signatures must be handwritten. Mail completed applications to Beacon Santa Fund, PO Box 643, Maynard, MA 01754.

For information or help applying, contact Boxborough Community Services Coordinator Wendy Trinks at 978-264-1735 or [wtrinks@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:wtrinks@boxborough-ma.gov), or any of the professionals listed above. Questions about the form can go to [beaconsanta1965@gmail.com](mailto:beaconsanta1965@gmail.com).
