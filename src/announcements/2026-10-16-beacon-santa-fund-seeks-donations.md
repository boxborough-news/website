Since 1965, the **Beacon Santa Fund** has helped families in Acton, Bedford, Billerica, Boxborough, Chelmsford, Concord, Lexington, Lincoln, Littleton, Maynard, Stow and Westford put a little something under the tree at Christmas.

The fund is run entirely by volunteers, so donations go directly to families in need. This past year it received more than 360 applications serving 837 children, including 50 children in Boxborough. Even a small donation can help provide a gift card for a family.

**How to donate:**

- By mail: Beacon Santa Fund, c/o Middlesex Savings Bank, 7 Digital Way, Maynard, MA 01754, or Beacon Santa Fund, PO Box 643, Maynard, MA 01754
- Online through [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/fundraiser/charity/1541809)
- Venmo: @beaconsanta1965
