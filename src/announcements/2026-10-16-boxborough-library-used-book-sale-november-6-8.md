Due to popular demand, the **Friends of the Boxborough Library used book sale** will again be a three-day event, November 6–8, in the meeting room of Sargent Memorial Library, 427 Massachusetts Avenue.

The weekend begins with a **Preview Sale for members on Friday, November 6, from 5 to 7 p.m.** Memberships will be available at the door. The main public sale is **Saturday, November 7, from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m.**, and the "Everything Must Go" sale is **Sunday, November 8, from 2 to 4 p.m.**, when you can fill a standard-size bag (provided) for $5.

Proceeds support library programs and museum passes. Cash and checks only.

Please support the sale by donating gently used hardcovers, paperbacks, music and audiovisual items. Puzzles and board games must be complete and in like-new condition. Donations will be accepted Monday, November 2, through Thursday, November 5, during regular library hours. Please no VCR tapes, magazines or materials in very poor condition.
