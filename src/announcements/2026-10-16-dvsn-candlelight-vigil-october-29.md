Domestic Violence Services Network (DVSN) will hold its annual **Light in the Darkness Candlelight Vigil** on **Thursday, October 29, at 6 p.m.** at the Fairbank Community Center, 40 Fairbank Road, Sudbury.

Held during Domestic Violence Awareness Month, the vigil commemorates the lives lost to domestic violence in Massachusetts in the past year. Community members will read each victim's story, represented by the reader, a rose and a candle lit in their memory. This year's keynote speaker is Middlesex District Attorney Marian Ryan.

"While the Vigil is a solemn occasion, it is also a night of hope," says DVSN Executive Director Jacquelin Apsler. "By publicly standing together to witness and commemorate, we build awareness and show the survivors in our communities that we believe their stories and support their journeys."

The event is free and open to the public, and a light supper follows the ceremony. More information: [DVSN.org/Vigil](https://www.dvsn.org/vigil) or 978-318-3421. DVSN's confidential, toll-free help line is 888-399-6111.
