Students will work together to build, test and drive their own go-kart at the **MABLE SWENext Club's Build a Go-Kart workshop on Saturday, October 17**, and have some fun along the way.

Space is limited, so register early at [conta.cc/4ziR24R](https://conta.cc/4ziR24R). The club, which serves Maynard, Acton, Boxborough and Littleton, has a new website at [mableswenextclub.com](https://mableswenextclub.com).
