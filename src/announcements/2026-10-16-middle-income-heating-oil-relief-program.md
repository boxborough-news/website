Massachusetts is offering a new **Middle-Income Heating Oil Relief** program: a one-time emergency benefit of up to **$680** for households that heat with #2 home heating oil. Household income limits have been expanded.

Applications open in **December 2026** for the 2026–2027 heating season. The program is expected to run from December through April 30, 2027, or until funding runs out.

Learn more at [Mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov) by searching "Middle Income Heating Oil Relief," or contact Wendy Trinks, Boxborough's community services coordinator, at Town Hall: [wtrinks@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:wtrinks@boxborough-ma.gov), 978-264-1735.
