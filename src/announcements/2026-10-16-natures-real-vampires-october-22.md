Learn about leeches, bats and other spooky creatures at **Nature's Real Vampires**, a free program for kids and their families on **Thursday, October 22, at 6:30 p.m.** at Sargent Memorial Library.

The evening includes a 45-minute talk followed by a Halloween craft. It is presented by the Blanchard Memorial PTF and the Boxborough Conservation Trust, together with Hands On Nature.
