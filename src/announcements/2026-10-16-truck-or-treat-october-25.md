Kids in costume (no toy weapons, please) can trick-or-treat at fire and police vehicles at the Boxborough Fire Station, 502 Massachusetts Avenue, on **Sunday, October 25, from 9 to 11 a.m.** This year's event starts an hour earlier than in past years.

Children can sit in the vehicles and learn about public safety, and Police K9 Koda is expected to give a short demonstration. Parking is available across the street at Blanchard Memorial School.
