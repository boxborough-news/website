Once again, the Boxborough Historical Society is proud to present Ted Reinstein speaking this year on “Greetings from Breed’s Hill: Fact & Folklore on America’s 250th”.  The presentation will take place in the Grange Room of the Boxborough Town Hall (29 Middle Road) on Sunday November 15 starting at 2:00 PM.   Admission is free, all are welcome, and registration is not required. 

Ted has been a reporter for the WCVB TV (Channel 5) Chronicle team for over 30 years during which he has garnered many national awards including an Emmy in 2018. From every corner of New England, he’s found the offbeat, the unique, the moving, and the just plain memorable, all while telling the enduringly colorful stories of the region’s people and places. “Fun & Folklore” is a fast-paced, fun, and informative look at some of the unique stories he has done and researched relative to the Revolutionary War period, as well as some later history.
