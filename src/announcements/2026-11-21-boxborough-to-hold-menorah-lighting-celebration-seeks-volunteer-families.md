The Boxborough Public Celebrations and Ceremonies Committee invites all to celebrate the first night of Hanukkah on Friday December 4 at 6 p.m. at the town menorah in front the Boxborough Fire Station, 502 Massachusetts Avenue. 

  This year, the town will host a larger first night celebration to light the menorah with complimentary refreshments, and a craft table for kids.

  The menorah lighting will continue at 5 p.m. on Saturday December 5 in advance of the annual Holiday Tree Lighting and at 6 p.m. each subsequent night, December 6-11. The town invites Boxborough families to volunteer to lead the lighting. Interested in participating? Sign up at [https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeR0Qa1w9VPcFuZpTlhoyGxMWIC9-jdjikSOgtiBXsiArGVzg/viewform](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeR0Qa1w9VPcFuZpTlhoyGxMWIC9-jdjikSOgtiBXsiArGVzg/viewform) and then invite your friends and neighbors to join you! 

  It is the town’s third year hosting a menorah lighting in addition to the annual tree lighting. The town’s menorah was designed, built, and donated to the town in 2024 by the Kollmann-Klein family. 

  Parking for the menorah lighting will be available across Massachusetts Avenue at the Blanchard Memorial School. 

  Don’t forget to bring your food pantry donation Saturday night.
