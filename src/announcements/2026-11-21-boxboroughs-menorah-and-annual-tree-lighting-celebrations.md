The Boxborough Public Celebrations and Ceremonies Committee invites all to celebrate the season with menorah and tree lightings on Saturday December 5.

The second night of Hanukkah will be commemorated at the town menorah in front the Boxborough Fire Station, 502 Massachusetts Avenue, starting at 5 p.m. Boxborough’s annual Tree Lighting celebration, also at the Boxborough Fire Station, will follow the lighting of the menorah. 

Enjoy a performance of holiday favorites by the Blanchard BrassWind Ensemble and the Blanchard Chorus, along with complimentary cookies, contributed by the Boxboro Regency, and hot chocolate, contributed by the Boxborough Dunkin'. There will be a craft table with wooden ornaments for children to decorate. Once the tree has been lit and the songs have been sung, Santa Claus will be escorted down Massachusetts Avenue by the Boxborough Fire Department to greet children at the Fire Station. This year, the town has invited representatives from Boxborough Dunkin' to flip the switch and light the tree. Other recent honorees included the Boxboro Regency, Two Friends Chocolates and the FreeBee Market team. 

Parking will be available across Massachusetts Avenue at the Blanchard Memorial School.

Don’t forget to bring your food pantry donations!

Come kick off the holiday season with friends and neighbors at these festive Boxborough traditions!
