BOXBOROUGH, Massachusetts, November 18, 2026, Give your Thanksgiving weekend a pop culture upgrade! NorthEast ComicCon & Collectibles Extravaganza brings celebrity guests, comics, toys, art, books, crafts, memorabilia and holiday shopping to the Boxboro Regency Hotel & Conference Center, [++242 Adams Place, Boxborough, Massachusetts++](https://www.google.com/maps/search/242+Adams+Place,+Boxborough,+Massachusetts?entry=gmail&source=g), November 27-29, 2026. Bring your friends, bring your family, and bring the gift list for the fans and collectors in your life. Free parking makes getting together that much easier.

Meet Sam J. Jones of Flash Gordon and Ted, Vincent Martella and David Errigo Jr. of Phineas and Ferb, Michael Fishman of Roseanne and The Conners, Peter Dante of Grandma's Boy and Little Nicky, and Richie Ramone of The Ramones. The guest lineup also features Pamelyn Ferdin, whose credits include Lucy in Peanuts, Charlotte's Web and Star Trek, Kristine DeBell of Meatballs, voice actor Dick Terhune of Transformers and World of Warcraft, and voice actor Christina Costello.

Comic art, fantasy illustration and storytelling get their own spotlight with Michael Golden, Steve Geiger, Joe St. Pierre, Bob Eggleton, Marianne Plumridge, Keith Champagne, Rick Keene, Bob Tkacik, Rich Maurizio, Brian Hall and Mach Bell. Meet the creators, discover their work, and find a gift with a connection to the comics, characters and stories you love. Magician and actor Steve Charette joins the weekend lineup with magic shows on Friday at 5:30 p.m. and Saturday at 10:15 a.m.

Saturday's courtyard interviews, hosted by Craig Semon, feature Richie Ramone at 11:15 a.m., Pamelyn Ferdin at 12:45 p.m., Peter Dante at 1:45 p.m., Vincent Martella and David Errigo Jr. at 3 p.m., and Michael Fishman at 4 p.m. On Sunday, Dick Terhune and Christina Costello take the interview stage at 11:15 a.m., followed by Sam J. Jones at 12:45 p.m.

There's more to this weekend than the shopping bags! Roger Kabler brings his Musical Comical Holiday Spectacle to the seminar room Saturday at noon. We're Not OK Improv Comedy Troupe presents an interactive game show Friday at 6:45 p.m. and Saturday at 5 p.m. Cowboy Mach Bell hosts Cosplay Karaoke with the Stars Friday and Saturday from 8 to 11 p.m.

Sunday begins with Mr. Toad's Nature Stories & Songs from Behind The Mask Studio & Theatre, scheduled for 10:15 a.m. on the detailed show schedule. Original masks and puppets bring a New England forest to life in a program for all ages. NorthEast Idol Project brings anime singing and dancing to the courtyard Sunday from 2 to 4 p.m.

Show off your imagination at the Create A Character Cosplay Contest, Saturday at 12:15 p.m. and Sunday at noon. Kids Cosplay Parades follow at 12:30 p.m. both days. Throughout the weekend, the boardroom hosts guided Pokemon TCG learn-to-play sessions and playtesting for a new format built around themed mini-decks and quick battles. New players can learn the basics, while returning players can pull up a chair and join the fun.

Public shopping hours are Friday, November 27, 4 to 8 p.m., Saturday, November 28, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., and Sunday, November 29, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. VIP admission begins one hour earlier each day. Celebrity schedules differ from shopping hours, with most guests appearing Friday from 4 to 8 p.m., Saturday from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., and Sunday from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. The detailed official schedule has been posted online. Programming and appearances are subject to change.

Paid admission is required. Tickets are available online and at the door. Visit the official ticket page for current admission options and prices. Main stage entertainment is included in admission.

Find the gifts, meet the guests, enjoy the shows, and make a weekend of it. NorthEast ComicCon, guaranteed not boring!
