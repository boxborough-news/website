Are you interested in bringing cultural programming to the local community? The Acton-Boxborough Cultural Council (ABCC) is accepting grant applications until October 15. Grants support a range of programs in the arts and humanities throughout the year. Any person or entity residing in Massachusetts can apply for a grant from ABCC.

Boxborough News asked Boxborough resident and ABCC Chair Ben Davies about the grant program and process. Davies told Boxborough News that the ABCC funds programs "that fall very broadly under the umbrella of arts and culture. This includes things like dance, music, and theater, but also things like art classes, lectures, installations, public art, and more….The most recent list of successful grantees (is) here: https://massculturalcouncil.org/local-council/acton-boxborough/."

"After the application window closes (October 15), the (ABCC) meets regularly to review the grants in terms of multiple criteria such as benefit to the community, artistic excellence, and originality. We aim to fund successful grant requests in full wherever possible. Successful grantees are notified in early January," Davies stated.

A grantee reception is typically held in early June at the Sargent Memorial Library. Davies told Boxborough News, "during the reception, we recognize the grantees who enrich our communities through their talents and efforts. Usually, we invite a few artists to perform previews during the reception… It's a lot of fun, and a great way to catch up with folks bringing arts to AB. All are welcome to attend!"

ABCC is one of 329 all-volunteer local cultural councils serving every municipality in Massachusetts. The state legislature appropriates funds annually to the Massachusetts Cultural Council (MCC), which then allocates funds to local councils. Local councils use funding guidelines and priorities established by the MCC and may add local guidelines and priorities as well. "ABCC then distributes these funds to support performances and cultural activities in and around our towns," Davies wrote.

Davies joined the ABCC in 2022, shortly after he moved to Boxborough, and he has chaired the committee since 2025. "I joined the council because the local arts scene had a profound impact on me as a younger person growing up in New England, and I wanted to do something meaningful to support that scene here in AB. I would encourage anyone with any interest in putting on a show, giving a class, or using their creativity to make our towns even more beautiful and interesting places to live to consider applying for a grant. And we're always happy to answer any questions folks have!"

The ABCC currently consists of three members from Boxborough and four from Acton, but additional members are welcome; Boxborough places no limits on the number of council members. Davies told us that the committee is "always looking for more folks from either town…If you're interested in helping support arts programming in our communities, please submit an application at your local town hall, come to one of our meetings, or reach out to us at abccinformation@gmail.com."

The ABCC meets via Zoom on the first Tuesday of every month. Meetings are posted on the Town's website and open to all.
