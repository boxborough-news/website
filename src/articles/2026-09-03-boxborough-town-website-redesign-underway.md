The Town of Boxborough is working with website developer CivicPlus to redesign the Town's website. The new site is anticipated to launch in November 2026.

The project is intended to be more than a visual makeover. According to the town administrator's office, the redesign process has incorporated resident feedback, website usage data, professional guidance, staff knowledge, and input from members of the community to make information easier to find and the website easier to navigate.

A website redesign working group began meeting in early April 2026. According to Town Administrator and Select Board Administrative Assistant Kelley Price, resident input has been an important part of the process. Price told Boxborough News that the Town conducted a survey through social media asking residents "how they use the current website, what information they typically look for, and what information they would like to be able to find more easily."

Residents were also asked to submit photos for the website "that capture the character of Boxborough…and we received beautiful submissions," Price said. "The community really showed up for us when we asked for their help."

The Town also asked CivicPlus to review website statistics and search data to determine which information and pages residents access most frequently.

The website working group has used both the survey responses and actual website usage data to help determine which information should be most prominent and easiest to access from the new homepage. The group has also spent time reviewing colors and the overall visual design of the site with a goal of developing a color palette that complements the Town seal while also reflecting the character of the community.

Dustin Younse, a member of the working group, told Boxborough News, "As a new Boxborough resident, when I heard the town was looking for volunteers I jumped at the opportunity to pitch in. While I love helping make things look nice, I try to focus more on the plumbing of a website - usability, findability, accessibility, and making sure you can get the content fast on any device or internet connection."

One new feature being developed is a dedicated "Business Resources" section to serve both existing Boxborough businesses and those interested in starting or locating a business in Boxborough. To help shape the new section, the Town is seeking input from existing local business owners to learn which information and resources would have been helpful to them when they were establishing their businesses.

A "New Residents" section is also planned. According to Price, the goal is to make it easier to locate important town information for people who have recently moved to Boxborough or are considering moving to the community.

Recognizing the importance of the school system to residents and potential residents, Price told Boxborough News that the redesigned site will provide easy-to-locate information about the Acton-Boxborough Regional School District, as well as links to relevant school district resources.

Town Administrator Mike Johns told Boxborough News, "We asked for input and the energy and dedication that staff and residents have volunteered have had a great impact on the high quality of the new user friendliness of the site. I am so appreciative that Boxborough showed up to make this project great."
