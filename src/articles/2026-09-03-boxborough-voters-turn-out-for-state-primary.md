On Tuesday, September 1, twenty-five percent (1,021) of Boxborough's 4,068 registered voters cast their ballots in the state primary, surpassing Boxborough's 2024 turnout by almost eight points. According to Town Clerk Rebecca (Becky) Harris, 483 voters cast their ballots during the early voting period or by mail.

Results in Boxborough mimicked statewide and district results. On the Democratic ballot for "Senator in Congress," incumbent Senator Ed Markey received 524 votes over his challenger Representative Seth Moulton who received 348 votes. Markey won statewide and will face Republican John Deaton in the November 3 general election.

Incumbent Democratic Governor's Councillor for District 3 Mara Dolan received 629 votes over her challenger Margie Palladino, who received 145 votes. Dolan was declared the winner in the district and does not have a Republican opponent in the general election.

Incumbent Middlesex Attorney General Marian Ryan received 637 votes, and her challenger, David Solet received 161 votes. Ryan was declared the winner in the district and will face Republican candidate Michael Walsh in the general election.

On the Republican ballot, the only contested race was for the gubernatorial nomination between Mike Minogue and Brian Shortsleeve. Minogue received 112 votes and Shortsleeve received 29 votes. Minogue won the statewide contest and will challenge sitting Democratic Governor Maura Healey in the November 3 general election.

Complete election results are on the Town's website: <https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/6881>
