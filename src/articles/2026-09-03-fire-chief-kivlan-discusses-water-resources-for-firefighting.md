At the Boxborough Water Resources Committee (WRC) meeting on August 18, Fire Chief John Kivlan described how water is obtained for fighting fires in Boxborough, and he spoke about possible improvements to the water supply system. Chief Kivlan reported that Boxborough has recently purchased a new tanker truck and now owns two 30,000-gallon tanker trucks. The new tanker truck will provide increased capacity and better redundancy. Boxborough also owns three pumping trucks, which hold 1,000 gallons of water.

In terms of water sources, Boxborough has 30 fire hydrants connected to the new waterline west of Route 495. The town also contains 59 fixed water sources – both cisterns and fire ponds. Chief Kivlan explained that various hydraulic factors limit the useful length of the large-diameter hose used to convey water from a source. This includes pressure limitations, friction losses, and the comparative elevation of the source. Kivlan told the WRC that it is better for the water source to be as close as possible to the site of the fire, "but transporting water up to lengths of roughly 800 to 1,000 feet should be achievable."

The Fire Department is in the process of checking the fixed water sources around town. According to Kivlan, thus far, no defects have been found. The cisterns range in capacity from 5,000 to 30,000 gallons. The capacity of the fire ponds is largely unknown, because the depth of the fire ponds is not known. The Town's access to some fire ponds and cisterns is unclear because they are located on private property or conservation land.

Kivlan said that there are some regions in Boxborough that are in need of water sources. It is likely that cisterns will need to be added in those areas and to any other areas that are found by further analysis. The Town has recently acquired a software package that can produce a GIS map of the water sources. Chief Kivlan is working on resolving the uncertainties and will incorporate the details on the map. The next WRC meeting will be on September 15, 2026, at 7:30 pm, via Zoom.
