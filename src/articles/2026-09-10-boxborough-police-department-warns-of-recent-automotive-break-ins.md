On September 10, Boxborough Police Chief John Szewczyk issued an alert to the Boxborough community about two recent vehicle break-ins on Hill Road and Davidson Road.

In both break-ins, an unlocked vehicle was entered and items were stolen. Both break-ins occurred near the Littleton town line.

The alert stated, "The Boxborough Police Department, in coordination with area law enforcement, is currently investigating two separate car break-ins near the Littleton town line. … Investigating officers have successfully identified a suspect in one of these burglaries and the investigation is ongoing…Please remember to always lock your vehicle doors, especially during evening and overnight hours. Do not leave valuables, keys, or electronics inside your vehicle."

Boxborough News reached out to Chief Szewczyk for more details, but the department continues to investigate the two break-ins and could not disclose many specifics. The Chief was not able to confirm whether these are two unconnected events or two break-ins by the same suspect.

Chief Szewczyk noted that he dealt with automotive break-ins when he worked in a more densely populated community. In these areas, a rash of break-ins might all occur within one or two blocks. In a town like Boxborough, where residences are more spread out, officers must patrol a larger area and work with neighboring communities because suspects often cross town boundaries.

Chief Szewczyk emphasized that it is always a good policy to lock vehicles and not leave valuables inside vehicles, but with these two recent incidents residents should be especially careful.
