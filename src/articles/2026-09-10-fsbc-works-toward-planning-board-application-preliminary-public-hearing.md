At the September 10 Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) meeting, town consultant Context Architecture ("Context") updated the committee on interactions with the Boxborough Planning Board as the fire station project moves into the design development phase.

Context has prepared "Pre-Design Site Plans" and "Pre-Design Narratives" in advance of the Planning Board's "Pre-Planning Board Hearing" scheduled for Wednesday, September 23.

The "Pre-Planning Board Hearing" is a preliminary public hearing that takes place before an official application is filed with the Planning Board. Context plans to file its application with the Planning Board on October 19.

The preliminary public hearing will be in a hybrid format, at Town Hall and on Zoom, beginning at 7:35 p.m. The pre-design materials prepared for the hearing are available on the town website at <https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/782/984-Mass-Ave---Boxborough-Firehouse>.

Context also presented a design update to the FSBC that led to discussions about a variety of design aspects, including parking lots, driveways, site grading and retaining walls, landscaping, building materials, and apparatus bay doors. FSBC members gave feedback and asked questions. No decisions were made.

The committee has several upcoming public outreach events. On Wednesday, September 30, Context will present a "design update" to the public at 7 p.m. at Town Hall. The FSBC will also have a booth at the "Village For a Day" event sponsored by the Boxborough Economic Development Committee on Saturday, October 3 and will have a booth at Diwalifest on Sunday, October 18.

The FSBC's next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, September 24, the day after the Planning Board's preliminary hearing.
