Do you enjoy reading Boxborough News each week? Have you considered joining the Boxborough News team? We're looking to expand our team and seeking volunteers who can help with the following roles: website developer/designer, social media manager, community calendar editor, and photo editor.

Over the past three years, Boxborough News has become a trusted resource for town news and information. Readers value our neutral, factual reporting on town issues, and our free weekly newsletter now reaches more than 800 subscribers.

To keep Boxborough News running, we rely on a small but dedicated team of volunteers. While we continue to need writers, we have also identified four specific non-writer volunteer roles:

- Website developer/designer: we need technical assistance to upgrade our website
- Social media manager: we need a point person for social media strategy
- Community calendar editor: we need someone to handle local announcements and events
- Photo editor: we need photography and photo organization

Think you're a good fit for any of these roles? Contact us at info@boxboroughnews.org. Consider donating your time and talents to Boxborough News!
