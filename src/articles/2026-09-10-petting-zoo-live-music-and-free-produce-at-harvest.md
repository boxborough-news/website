The Boxborough FreeBee community market is returning on September 12 from 10:30 a.m. to noon on the lawn of the UCC Boxborough at 30 Middle Road. Taking on a festive autumn "Harvest Fair" theme, this monthly full market event promises a wide variety of free, seasonal, community-centered activities for all.

Alongside the market's standard weekly offerings of fresh gleaned produce and bread, visitors can look forward to a lively atmosphere with fun for all ages. Highlights include a petting zoo, live music provided by the Nashoba Valley Concert band, a free bouncy house, and face painting sponsored by Middlesex Bank.

Attendees can also explore creative treasures at the craft table, take part in activities hosted by the Discovery Museum, enjoy free cookies from the Boxborough church, and connect with local organizations sharing valuable community information. Everything is completely free to help neighbors gather, connect, and celebrate the season together.

FreeBee was born during the pandemic and in the aftermath of the Swanson Road apartment fire, when Boxborough residents organized to support displaced families. Volunteers learned that most food pantries were closed on Saturday and excess food normally given to them ("gleaned") was going to waste. Volunteers began rescuing surplus produce and meat from local supermarkets and sharing it on the lawn of the UCC Boxborough. By 2021, the effort had become the FreeBee market, guided by its simple motto: "Give what you can, take what you need."

The "harvest" theme of Saturday's market is a nod to the Harvest Fair once sponsored by the Boxborough Grange, which included agricultural exhibits and judging.

In addition to the day's festivities, this event marks the kickoff for early donations of gently-used Halloween costumes. The final FreeBee community market on October 24 will feature a Halloween costume exchange.

For those who cannot make it to the September 12 event, the FreeBee Market continues to offer smaller, food-only "Food Rescue Saturday" tables at the UCC Boxborough lawn every week through October.
