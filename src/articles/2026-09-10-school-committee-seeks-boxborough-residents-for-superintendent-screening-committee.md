The Acton-Boxborough Regional School District (ABRSD) has launched its search for a new Superintendent of Schools and is looking for Boxborough residents to join the Superintendent Screening Committee.

Specifically, the School Committee is seeking (1) a Boxborough parent, and (2) a member of the Boxborough community who "may not be a parent/guardian of a current ABRSD student but who is actively engaged in the community through other roles, such as service on town boards, committees, or other community organizations."

These two residents will join School Committee members, district leadership, staff members, and other parents and community members on the 18-member Screening Committee to "review applications, identify candidates to interview, and potentially advance finalists to the School Committee for its consideration."

Boxborough residents interested in serving on the Screening Committee can submit an interest form at https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdwYO51oZjOlbPmT8ArdvoA5mp5BKCv_8JbImGjQGcperfksw/viewform.

Applications are due by 5 p.m. on Wednesday, September 16. According to a notice sent out by the district, applicants should "review the schedule carefully before expressing interest. Applicants should only express interest if they can commit to attending all scheduled meetings, including the tentative interview dates."

There are other opportunities to participate in the superintendent search process as well. A community survey is open now through Friday, September 18, inviting the public to provide "feedback about the priorities for our next superintendent and the qualities and characteristics we should seek in candidates." The survey is available at https://my.thoughtexchange.com/participate/656634640.

There are also six community forums scheduled for September 14-16, both virtual and in-person. The details for the forums are available on a new Superintendent Search website: https://www.abschools.org/superintendent-search.

The School Committee is working with the Collins Center for Public Management at UMass Boston to conduct the search. The deadline for superintendent candidates to submit an application is October 13. Interviews are expected to take place in late October and early November. Finalists are expected to be announced in November, with finalist site visits shortly thereafter. The schedule indicates that finalist interviews and a "possible vote" by the School Committee will take place in December.

Back in February 2026, current ABRSD Superintendent Peter Light announced that he had accepted a position as Superintendent at the American Community School in Abu Dhabi starting in July 2027. In June, the School Committee voted to choose the Collins Center as the district's superintendent search firm and voted to establish a Superintendent Search Subcommittee of three members: Boxborough member Adam Klein (Chair), Lisa Adil, and Jacob Fay.
