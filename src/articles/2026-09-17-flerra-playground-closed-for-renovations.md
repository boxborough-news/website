The playground at Flerra Meadows is currently closed to replace playground equipment and install rubber safety mats to bring the playground into compliance with the American Disabilities Act (ADA).

The closure began on September 1 and the renovated playground is expected to open in early October.

The playground will have a new main play structure, a new swing set, and permeable rubber safety mats that will lead from the playground entry to each piece of equipment. The remainder of the ground surface will be wood chips. Boxborough Recreation Commission Chair Hilary Greven noted that "this meets ADA requirements while saving on overall cost."

According to Greven, the installation, equipment, and surfacing costs are currently still below the expected total cost (including contingency) of $300,000. The funding for this project was approved at the May 2026 Annual Town Meeting.

After the playground closed on September 1, the Boxborough Department of Public Works (DPW) removed the old swing set, the large playground structure, and the asphalt ramp that led up to the structure.

On Thursday, September 17, Greven told Boxborough News that the new equipment has been delivered to Flerra. The installation will be completed by a contractor for the manufacturer, Kompan.

Flerra fields remain open while the playground is closed.

"Over the years, the current large structure has seen a lot of wear and tear," Greven told Boxborough News last year. "So we are looking to install a new large structure to complete the improvements to the playground, providing a safe and exciting playground for residents and the hundreds of children who attend Flerra Summer Playground."
