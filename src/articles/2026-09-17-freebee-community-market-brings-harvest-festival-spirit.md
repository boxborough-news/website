The FreeBee Community Market held on September 12 on the lawn of the UCC Boxborough was reminiscent of past Boxborough harvest festivals. There was, of course, the regular FreeBee Food Rescue. Gleaned food comes from various sources such as The Boston Gleaners in Acton, local farms, supermarkets, and food pantries that have leftover food which will not last until the following week.

Mary Pavlik, one of the FreeBee coordinators said "What a beautiful day to celebrate the harvest season together! I especially appreciate the creativity of the organizations, from music to face painting to kites and even activities made by a young FreeBee volunteer about food waste!"

In addition to the food rescue, "Harvest" Community Market goers were treated to music by The Nashoba Valley Concert Band. Children (and adults) enjoyed the petting zoo that featured a miniature donkey, some geese, chickens, and other farm animals. The UCC provided cookies for everyone and a bouncy house for the little ones. Judging from the long line, face painting, sponsored by Middlesex Bank, was extremely popular. Adding to the "FreeBee" spirit, the Boxborough Transfer Station Swap Shed volunteers brought an assortment of items that needed new homes.

Several local organizations also had tables and were handing out information, including the Boxborough Minutemen, the Boxborough Emergency Response Committee (BERC), and the Sargent Memorial Library. Everyone's favorite "Old Coot," Boxborough resident Jim Moss, was there, offering free advice to anyone on any topic.

The FreeBee Program is a Boxborough volunteer organization which had its beginning when a group of individuals gathered materials such as dishes, clothing, pictures, paper goods (toilet paper, tissues, paper towels), flatware, and other useful items to help the victims of the 2021 fire at one of the Harvard Ridge Condominiums buildings. It burgeoned to the mission to keep food out of the waste stream. The program is in its sixth year now.

At the FreeBee events, all is free. No money is charged for the food or other items. It is an opportunity for people, not only from Boxborough, but several other local towns to participate, meet neighbors and have a chance to socialize.

The FreeBee Market continues to offer smaller, food-only "Food Rescue Saturday" tables at the UCC Boxborough lawn every week through October. The final FreeBee Community Market will take place on October 24 and will feature a Halloween costume exchange.
