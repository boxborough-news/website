On Thursday, September 17, Boxborough Fire Engine 62 was involved in a motor vehicle accident. The accident occurred at approximately 4 p.m. when the Boxborough Fire Department was responding to a medical emergency on I-495 North.

When asked for details about the crash, Boxborough Fire Chief John Kivlan explained that "the fire engine was traveling northbound on I-495 near the Route 2 interchange with its emergency lights activated when a vehicle merging onto I-495 North from Route 2 collided with the side of the engine."

"Fortunately, there were no injuries to our personnel or the other driver. The fire engine did sustain damage and remains out of service due to safety concerns while we await an examination by the insurance adjuster. The other vehicle also sustained damage, but I do not know the extent of it," Kivlan wrote.

"While I cannot comment on the cause of the accident, I would remind motorists to remain alert and avoid distractions when driving, especially when emergency vehicles are operating nearby.

When you see an emergency vehicle with its lights activated, please slow down, be aware of its movement, and yield appropriately. Giving emergency vehicles the space they need helps keep our responders, other motorists, and the people we are responding to safe."
