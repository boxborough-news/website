For a second year in a row, the Boxborough Economic Development Committee (EDC) is planning a "Village for a Day" event intended to promote local businesses and help the community imagine what a walkable town center in Boxborough could look like.

The event will return on Saturday, October 3rd (rain date October 17) from 11:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. and will take place in multiple locations along Mass Ave, including Middlesex Bank Plaza, the UCC Church lawn, and Town Hall.

The EDC has invited local businesses, artisans, and organizations to set up a booth at one of these three locations, and the public will be encouraged to walk along Mass Ave from one location to another. Food will be available for purchase from Oscar's Burritos, Craft Food Halls, and Craft Indian Kitchen, and there will be free treats from local caterer C'est La Fête! and the Boxboro Regency. There will also be live music and activities for kids.

"'Village for a Day' is an event that aims to engage with Boxborough residents, local businesses, and organizations to help them envision and experience a walkable village center along Mass Ave at the intersections of Stow and Middle Roads," commented EDC Chair Abby Reip. "Sidewalks and crosswalks will be drawn to encourage foot traffic to participating businesses."

The idea of developing a village center is something that the town has explored for a number of years. Most recently, the town hired consulting firm Tighe & Bond to conduct a Village Center Feasibility Assessment in Spring of 2024, funded by a community planning grant from the state.

Reip hopes that the event will move the "town center" project forward by "help[ing] build public awareness and support for a village center." Reip added that "Town Planner Alec Wade is applying for a grant that will help fund a consultant who can develop a village center overlay/zoning district. The EDC and Planning Board will be collaborating on this effort."

This year, the event will also include a 9-hole mini-golf course behind Town Hall, with each hole sponsored by a local business. The activity is free and open to the public, with prizes for kids. The mini-golf course will be open throughout the event, from 11:30 a.m. to 4 p.m.

There is still an opportunity to sponsor one of the holes on the mini-golf course. Sponsorships are $105 and sponsors' names and logos will be prominently displayed at each tee. To sponsor, contact Town Planner Alec Wade at <awade@boxborough-ma.gov> (or fill out the Village for a Day Application Form at <https://bit.ly/4xNazJJ>).

"I am very excited to be a part of the EDC's Village for a Day," said Megan Connor, EDC Clerk. "It's a great opportunity for residents to come together to support our local businesses and organizations and experience our community in a whole new way. On behalf of the Economic Development Committee, I hope that people will stop by for the food, the music, the mini golf and other fun activities, but stay for the chance to connect with their neighbors and imagine what a vibrant village center could mean for Boxborough's future."

Local businesses or organizations interested in learning more about "Village for a Day" or getting involved in the event can contact Associate Town Planner Ian Gilson at <igilson@boxborough-ma.gov> or 978-264-1723.
