At its meeting on September 16, the Boxborough Conservation Commission (ConsCom) met with Flamingo Market manager Keyur Patel regarding an Enforcement Order (EO) that was issued to Keymi Realty, LLC (owner of Flamingo Market) on September 2, 2026.

The property referenced in the enforcement order, located at 104 Mass Ave, is within a 100-foot buffer to the adjacent wetlands.

The EO was issued due to "significant tree clearing and removal within the buffer zone across the entire southern half of the property" and "conducted in an area subject to protection" under the Wetland Protection Act and the Boxborough Wetlands By-Law without approval" from the ConsCom.

Patel told ConsCom members that there was a willow tree on the east side of the building that was dropping branches close to the building. He was concerned for the safety of his building and second-floor tenants and considered the removal as "maintenance."

ConsCom Chair Liz Markiewicz told Boxborough News that a "property owner is allowed under our maintenance policy to remove such a tree without coming before the ConsCom, even if the tree is in the buffer zone. The problem was that the tree was so large and close to the house, that they had to remove other trees in the buffer zone in order to safely remove it. The owner should have filed with us before removing the other trees. They are not contesting the Enforcement Order and are coming before us at our next meeting (October 7) with a restoration plan."
