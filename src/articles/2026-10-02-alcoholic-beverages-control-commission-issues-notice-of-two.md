On September 1, 2026, the Massachusetts Alcoholic Beverages Control Commission (ABCC) issued a Notice of Suspension (“Notice”) to Keymi Corp., doing business as Flamingo Market, at 104 Massachusetts Avenue, Boxborough.

The Notice states that the ABCC suspends Flamingo Market’s alcoholic beverages license for a period of two days, from Monday, October 19 to Tuesday, October 20.

The ABCC ordered the suspension after a hearing in March 2026 in which Flamingo Market was found to have “violated 204 CMR 2.05 (2) Permitting an illegality on the license premises, to wit: M.G.L. Ch. 138 sec. 34 – Sale of delivery of an alcoholic beverage to a person under twenty-one (21) years of age (2 Counts).”

According to the Notice, the violation occurred on October 24, 2025, when two underage individuals purchased alcohol at Flamingo Market using fraudulent licenses while the business was under surveillance by ABCC investigators.

The Notice states that at the March 2026 hearing, the ABCC “was informed as to changes made by the Licensee to prevent future violations. The Licensee has invested in an identification scanner and intends to upgrade to a more sophisticated model.”

Boxborough Town Administrator Michael Johns announced the violation and suspension of Flamingo Market’s license at the September 14 meeting of the Boxborough Select Board. Johns stated, “The administration and the Select Board, as the oversight authority for the liquor licenses in town, take this very seriously….”

When contacted by Boxborough News, Flamingo Market owner Keyur Patel confirmed that he had received the Notice of Suspension and that Flamingo Market will be closed on October 19 and October 20. Patel declined to make any further comment.
