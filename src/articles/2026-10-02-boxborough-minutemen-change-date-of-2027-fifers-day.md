At their meeting on Sunday September 27, the Boxborough Minuteman Company voted unanimously to change the date of the 2027 Boxborough Fifer’s Day to June 12, the second Saturday of June.

For many years, Fifer’s Day has taken place on the third Saturday of June. In 2027, the third Saturday will be June 19, a federal holiday.

“There are a few reasons for this change”, said Boxborough Minutemen captain Rich Grady. “For one thing, Juneteenth is an official national holiday and we did not want to conflict with celebrations of this important anniversary. Also, by moving to June 12, we will avoid a conflict with the Acton Lion’s Club annual carnival. In addition, holding Fifer's Day on June 12 will fall within the Acton-Boxborough school year when more families are around town and able to come and enjoy a wonderful community event.”

The Minutemen discussed only the 2027 Fifer’s Day date; they did not discuss a permanent change going forward.
