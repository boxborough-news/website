Boxborough Human Services Director Kimberley Dee has announced her retirement; Dee’s last day working for the Town will be November 13, 2026.

Dee started as Boxborough’s Council on Aging Coordinator in November 2020 and was promoted to Human Services Director two years ago. Boxborough News reached out to Dee with questions about her career and her retirement plans. Dee’s responses have been edited for length and clarity.

**Q: Please tell us about your career path.**

A: I graduated from Salem State College with a BS degree in Geography and a specialization in Travel & Tourism. I worked at several travel agencies, most notably for Crimson Travel in Harvard Square, where I was a training specialist and later a travel instructor in Nashua, NH. I worked a variety of part-time jobs when my children were young. I sold home interiors and gifts through home parties in the evenings as an independent contractor. Later, I kept the books for a small company, and then I worked for my church as a religious education coordinator.

I began working with older adults when I was an administrative assistant for another Council on Aging about 20 years ago. Then I worked as an activity director at an assisted living for 12 years before I came here.

**Q: Looking back on your tenure in Boxborough, what are you most proud of?**

A: I am proud of so many things that I have done for the older adults in Boxborough. It is hard to pick what I am most proud of. However, I can probably sum it up as I am most proud of the influence I have made in contributing to a good and/or better quality of life for people who are over the age of 60. Over my tenure, we have increased the Buzz newsletter circulation and offered many enriching educational, physical fitness, health related clinics, mindfulness, educational, entertainment, and social programs.

Since becoming the Human Services Director, I hope I have extended that positive influence, through our Human Services team. We operate the Boxborough Connects van service, assist those in need through a variety of programs and referrals, offer some really fun recreational events and programs, and get Boxborough veterans assistance and compensation in recognition of their dedicated service to our country.

**Q: What are you going to miss the most about working in Boxborough?**

A: I am going to miss the people. I enjoy the work I do, but the people that participate in our programs and reach out to me for assistance are the most important part of my work life, and I will miss them. I will not miss waking up to an alarm at 5:30 am!

**Q: What are your retirement plans and what are you most looking forward to in retirement?**

A: I plan on going to Florida this winter, and to France sometime next year. So, traveling more is on my wish list; as is reading and attending live musical performances. But mostly, I am looking forward to spending more time with my family and friends.

[Dee has three grandchildren.] Being a grandmother brings me so much joy! Plus, I am fortunate that my 93-year-old Dad still lives in the house I grew up in, and my brother, sister-in-law and their children are a big part of my life as well. I am blessed that all of them, but one niece, live in Massachusetts.
