On Tuesday, September 29, the Boxborough Emergency Reserve Corps (BERC) met for the second time this year. The meeting was held at the Town Hall with twelve volunteers in attendance.

BERC serves as both the CERT (Community Emergency Response Team) and the CMMRC (Central Middlesex Medical Reserve Corps) for the Town of Boxborough.

The corps is entirely composed of volunteers and has three main missions: to partner with public safety officials to make the community safer and more able to handle natural or man-made disasters through volunteer training, education and planning; to maintain the highest level of proficiency through online and in-person training; and to recruit additional members, as the work is demanding and requires a multitude of volunteers.

At the meeting, BERC Chair Ron Sisco discussed the new Zoll AEDs (defibrillators) purchased by the town to be deployed in all the public buildings.

Sisco explained that having a consistent design in all locations will make it easier for volunteers to operate a device at any location. He noted that the design is the same one used by the emergency response team, so emergency responders can switch to the AED on the ambulance without removing the electrode pads.

Sisco also noted that the Boxborough Fire Department now has two certified instructors for the American College of Surgeons (ACS) Stop the Bleed program, so a “Stop the Bleed” (bleeding control) class will be offered to BERC volunteers in the near future. This complements the BERC volunteers’ first-aid, CPR, and AED training.

The corps discussed lessons learned from supporting Fifer’s Day, the FreeBee harvest market, and cooling center efforts at Sargent Memorial Library. BERC also works with neighboring towns and recently supported the Appleman Triathlon and the Littleton Road race. BERC plans to support the Public Safety Truck-or-Treat event in October and the Boxborough Tree Lighting in December.

BERC members also discussed long-term planning, including conducting a shelter drill at the direction of Boxborough Fire Chief John Kivlan and conducting an emergency response exercise for the BERC team and an inventory drill of the equipment trailer.

Two nights after the meeting, on Thursday, October 1, nine members of BERC met at the Sargent Memorial Library to complete hands-on training for CPR and AED with Captain Malinowski of the Boxborough Fire Department.

BERC is present at nearly every Boxborough event that needs crowd management or emergency services, and they are always looking for more members. For more information about joining the corps, contact BERC Chair Ron Sisco at [rsisco@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:rsisco@boxborough-ma.gov).
