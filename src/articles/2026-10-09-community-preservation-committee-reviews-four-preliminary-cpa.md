On Thursday October 3, the Boxborough Community Preservation Committee (CPC) met and discussed four preliminary requests for FY2028 Community Preservation Act (CPA) funds for the fiscal year starting July 1, 2027.

The CPC voted unanimously that all four requests were eligible to receive CPA funding and could move forward to the final application stage of the process.

The Agricultural Commission proposed the largest request, $300,000, for funds towards the acquisition of the Richardson property, including the historic house and barn, located at 276 Middle Road. At the time of the CPC meeting there was an article on the draft November 16, 2026 Fall Town Meeting warrant to purchase this property for $1.8 million; the requested CPC funds would partially fund this amount. This was the only new request for CPC funds.

Three ongoing projects were also submitted. The Housing Board submitted the Boxborough Rental Assistance Program (BRAP) in the amount of $69,000, which is the same as last year. The Cemetery Commission proposed $9,500, the same amount as in past years, for funding the continuing project of repairing, cleaning, restoring, and resetting of headstones, monuments, and other stone work in the town’s two cemeteries. The Conservation Commission requested $10,000 as the annual CPC contribution to the Conservation Trust Fund, which would be used for expenses related to acquiring, improving, monitoring, and otherwise maintaining new and existing parcels in town.

In order to allow the CPC to vote, and possibly approve, the Richardson property project before the November 16 Fall Town Meeting, the CPC voted unanimously to hold a public hearing on this project at its next meeting on Thursday, November 5.

The CPC will hold a second public hearing on the other CPA funding requests on Thursday, December 3.
