At its hybrid meeting on Tuesday, October 6, the Personnel Board heard a proposal from Alec Wade, Director of Land Use and Permitting, to make mid-cycle changes to the classification and compensation schedule at Fall Town Meeting scheduled for November 16. The proposal would change the way wiring, plumbing, and gas inspectors are paid. If approved at town meeting, the changes become effective immediately.

Currently, inspectors receive 90% of permit fees with the balance going to the town, generating annual revenues of approximately $12,000. According to the warrant article “The existing compensation model was common several decades ago. Since then, the majority of communities have moved toward an hourly or per inspection model of compensation.” Under the proposed plan, inspectors would receive a flat rate of $60 per visit which would generate annual revenues of approximately $30,000. According to Wade, the $60 per inspection rate is slightly above the average for communities who use this model.

Wade explained that in fiscal year 2027, there are two projects that could generate additional revenue for the town. The Campanelli project at Beaver Brook Road could bring in an estimated $45,000 in permit fees and a second project with electrical and plumbing permits could bring an estimated $70,000 in fees. Conservatively speaking, the combined revenue for these two projects is expected to yield over $100,000 for the town.

After hearing about the additional revenue generated by the proposed changes, the Personnel Board voted 4-0 to recommend the warrant article.

Previously, at their September 15 meeting, the Personnel Board had rejected the proposed change. See Boxborough News coverage of the prior meeting [here](https://www.boxboroughnews.org/news#h.itevttc1jymv).

The next Personnel Board meeting is scheduled for Tuesday October 27 at 5:30 pm at Town Hall.
