At its meeting on Monday, October 5, the Boxborough Planning Board reviewed and edited a draft letter of feedback to the Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) in response to the preliminary application public hearing held on September 23 for the town’s new fire station at 984 Mass Ave.

Before the board began their discussion, Town Planner Alec Wade spoke to the board about site plan rules and regulations and reinforced what is and is not in their purview. “Your job is to review this site for traffic flow, circulation, safety and health impacts. Those are the key elements that you are charged with…”

Planning Board Chair Rich Guzzardi characterized the meeting as a “working session” in which the board would finalize its memo to the FSBC, incorporating the initial public feedback heard at the public hearing as well as the board’s overall comments as they relate to site plan review.

The board’s memo is meant to assist the Fire Station Building Committee in assembling a complete site plan review application. According to Wade, the FSBC is expected to file the formal application on October 19, and the first public hearing will likely be scheduled for Wednesday, November 18.

The memo informs the FSBC that while “the application as submitted generally complies with the Boxborough Zoning Bylaw,”...additional requirements such as a “waiver for the size of parking stalls,” a determination from the Massachusetts Highway Division regarding the width of the curb cut, and a “variance from the [Boxborough] Zoning Board of Appeals for the Tower, where the structure exceeds the maximum building height of the B1 district” will be required.

The board discussed and detailed additional requirements for the full application in the memo, including the landscape plan, lighting plan, a storm water management report, and a traffic report. Additionally, the memo speaks to specific feedback from both the public and the board and suggests the FSBC consider providing a 3D model to demonstrate how the site may be viewed from neighboring elevations; scheduling a site walk for neighbors; using permeable surface layers wherever possible; detailing anticipated noise levels at the property line; providing written details on how vehicles are decontaminated; and several other details related to the building and its operation.

Signed by Guzzardi, the memo emphasizes that the list “should not be considered an exhaustive list of the proceedings and feedback” and encourages the FSBC to review the Planning Board’s recorded meetings from September 23 and October 5, which are available on BXB-TV via the town’s website ([https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/boxborough/](https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/boxborough/)).

The Planning Board’s next meeting is October 19 via Zoom. Agenda items include “goal setting” and voting their recommendations on the Fall Town Meeting warrant articles.
