On Sunday, October 25, the Boxborough Fire and Police Departments will host their sixth annual “Truck or Treat” at the fire station, 502 Massachusetts Ave., from 9 to 11 a.m.

The event will take place an hour earlier than past years, beginning at 9 a.m. instead of 10 a.m. Boxborough Fire Department Captain Jason Malinowski told Boxborough News that the time of the event was “moved up based on flow of traffic in the past and feedback from prior years, as well as staff availability.”

As in past years, kids are encouraged to wear their costumes (no toy weapons) and trick-or-treat at the fire and police vehicles parked in the fire station parking lot. Public safety personnel will give out candy and kids will have the opportunity to sit in the vehicles and learn more about various public safety initiatives. The event typically averages about 400 participants each year.

Captain Malinowski noted that the Fire Department has “new apparatus that was recently delivered that will be [at the event] even though not in service yet.” Boxborough Police K9 Koda and handler Officer Bressi are also expected to attend and give a short K9 demonstration.

Public safety vehicles from outside Boxborough, like Pro ALS and Medflight ground ambulance, may also participate subject to availability.

"We are always excited to partner with the Boxborough Fire Department at the annual Truck or Treat event as we share the day with community members,” said Boxborough Police Chief John Szewczyk. “The costumes are always terrific.”

Parking will be available across the street at Blanchard Memorial School.
