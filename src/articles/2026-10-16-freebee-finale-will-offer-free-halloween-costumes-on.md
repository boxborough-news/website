The side lawn of the United Church of Christ Boxborough (UCCB) at 30 Middle Road will transform into a vibrant hub of celebration on Saturday, October 24, from 10:30 a.m. to noon for the highly anticipated “finale” of this year’s FreeBee market season.

Wrapping up a successful season of produce-sharing and neighborly connection, this final community market promises a festive morning packed with local talent, family fun, and seasonal traditions.

Families looking to prepare for Halloween can stop by the Boxborough Community Center, which is converting into a one-day Halloween costume shop for the FreeBee finale. Neighbors can pick out festive outfits and holiday decorations to ensure everyone is ready for trick-or-treating. All Halloween items, like everything else at FreeBee, will be completely free.

For residents wanting to contribute to the costume shop, costume donation bins are already active across town at the Sargent Memorial Library, Blanchard School, the Discovery Museum, and the UCCB side porch.

Animal lovers are also in for a special treat at this final market of the season. Pastor Fran Graveson will host the traditional “Blessing of the Animals,” adding a creative photo booth twist this year. Community members are invited to bring along pet photos or beloved stuffed animals to join in on the celebration of animal companionship.

The FreeBee finale's main stage will stay lively with a diverse lineup of local entertainment. Attendees can look forward to vibrant routines from local dancers, interactive salsa lessons for all skill levels, and a thrilling aerobatic performance.

Alongside the live entertainment, children can enjoy a bouncy house and hands-on crafts, while residents can check out the local community groups’ booths.

All are welcome to gather on the lawn to celebrate the close of a bountiful, sharing-filled season and experience the very best of Boxborough's community spirit.
