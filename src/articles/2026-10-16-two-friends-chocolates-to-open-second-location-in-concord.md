This week, Two Friends Chocolates owner Lavanya “Lala” Selvaraj announced that she will be opening a second store at 1137 Main Street in Concord.

Selvaraj opened Two Friends Chocolates at 629 Massachusetts Ave. (Middlesex Bank Plaza) in Boxborough in 2020 after starting the business in her Boxborough home in 2016. The business has evolved over time, adding hot and cold beverages to the offerings of handcrafted chocolates with seasonal specialties.

“Expanding our retail with a second store was simply the next natural progression in our journey,” Selvaraj told Boxborough News. “We have always loved the unique vibe that Concord carries. When looking for a second location, we wanted a town with the same strong community values and wonderful support for small businesses that we’ve been lucky to experience in Boxborough. Operationally, Concord was a perfect fit because of its proximity—all of our chocolates will still be crafted at our Boxborough location, making it easy to manage both. On a personal note, with my eight-year-old at Blanchard, having the new shop close by makes balancing family life and business much easier. When we stumbled upon the space, everything just aligned perfectly.”

Selvaraj is hopeful that she can open the new store in Concord before the Christmas holiday. “We know it is a very ambitious timeline,” she said, “but at this moment that’s the goal we are actively working and hoping for.”

The new Concord store will be a “Chocolate Boutique” with a “Chocolate Room experience where you can sit down, relax, and enjoy a selection of specialty drinks, chocolates and other treats.”

She notes that the Boxborough store is “a strict certified facility where we handcraft chocolates for retailers across New England and beyond. Because it is a working studio, our guest offerings here have some limitations. Concord is going to be different… Think of Boxborough as the heart of our business, and Concord as our welcoming living room.”

Selvaraj is excited to take on the challenge of adding a second store. “Expanding to Concord feels like standing in front of a beautiful blank canvas again,” she said. “Building this place from the ground up and bringing to life the exact ambiance we’ve imagined for our guests is definitely the most exciting part.”

“But the hardest part of moving to a second location actually lies back at the first one,” she continues. “Ensuring completely uninterrupted day-to-day operations while you are entirely engrossed in building out a new space is a make-or-break moment for any small business. We have been incredibly lucky in that front. Our entire Boxborough team has their hearts set on making both locations a success, and they have stepped up immensely to ensure we get this right.”

When asked if she had anything else to share with the public, Selvaraj added, “we just want to take a moment to truly thank everyone who has walked through our doors over the past five years. Hearing that so many of you are planning to stop by both of our locations means the world to us. We are so incredibly grateful for the friends we’ve made here. This town has given us nothing but love and support, and as we take this next step to grow, we are just so thankful to have you all in our corner.”
