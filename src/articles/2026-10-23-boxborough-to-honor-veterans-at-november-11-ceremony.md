On Tuesday, November 11 at 11 a.m., Boxborough veterans and supporters will gather at the Veterans’ Tribute Memorial on the grounds of the Sargent Memorial Library, 427 Massachusetts Avenue, for the town’s annual Veterans Day Ceremony.

The ceremony will honor those who have served in the Armed Forces and celebrate and thank Boxborough veterans for their service. As in past years, the ceremony will recognize by name the Boxborough veterans who have passed away in the last year and Boxborough veterans who died in service.

Boxborough resident and veteran Ron Vogel has served as the master of ceremonies for the town’s Veterans Day ceremony since 2021, when the Veterans Tribute Memorial was completed and the town held its first Veterans Day observance there.

“Kevin Mahoney and I were the cochairs of the Veterans Monument Committee,” Vogel told Boxborough News last year, “and at the end of the project I took on the role of project manager to get it completed. Kevin organized the first Veterans Day observance and he asked me to be the master of ceremonies, and I willingly did that.”

Vogel served in the United States Navy on active duty from 1966-1968 and “served aboard the destroyer USS Forrest Sherman, destroyer number 931.”

Boxborough Town Administrator and veteran Michael Johns is also expected to participate in this year’s ceremony as he has in past years.

“I served just shy of 12 years in the Navy from 1986 through 1997,” Johns told Boxborough News last year. “I served at Naval Air Stations in Pensacola, FL, North Island (Coronado) CA, Patuxent River, MD, and Virginia Beach, VA. During that time I made two Middle East deployments on USS Enterprise and USS Abraham Lincoln from the West Coast, the second one during the first Gulf War in Iraq, and the third deployment from the East Coast on USS Enterprise (after major ship and nuclear reactor overhauls) to Bosnia Herzegovina, and then back to the Persian Gulf and Iraq again.”

The ceremony will also involve local scouts, the Boxborough Minutemen Company, and the Acton-Boxborough High School Madrigal Singers.

Information about Boxborough’s veterans’ services can be found at [https//wwwboxborough-ma.gov/281/Veterans-Services](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/281/Veterans-Services). Boxborough Veterans’ Services Officer James MacRae (978-929-6614) is available by appointment and has regular office hours at Boxborough Town Hall every Tuesday from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.
