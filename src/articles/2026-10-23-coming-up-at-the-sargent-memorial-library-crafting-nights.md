The Sargent Memorial Library will hold Family Craft Night next Wednesday, October 28 at 4 p.m., and the definition of “family” is very liberal.

“No children required, no spouse required, we accept families as small as one person,” says library staff member Laura Skorczeski.

“This month we’ll make do-it-yourself Spell Books. Get ready for spooky season with your very own Spell Book! We will work on the cover and spine and then the families can take it home to fill it with their own magic!”

Registration is required, by going to [https://boxlib.assabetinteractive.com/calendar/family-craft-night-4/](https://boxlib.assabetinteractive.com/calendar/family-craft-night-4/) and filling in the form at the bottom.

For adults who can’t make Family Craft Night, the library will host Adult Craft Time on Thursday, November 5 from 6 to 7 p.m., where participants will be making paper flowers for holiday decorations or even gifts. Register at the bottom of the page: [https://boxlib.assabetinteractive.com/calendar/adult-craft-time-paper-flowers-2/](https://boxlib.assabetinteractive.com/calendar/adult-craft-time-paper-flowers-2/)

That week, the library also will be preparing for the library book sale coming up on November 6, 7, and 8. Proceeds from the sale support library programs and museum passes.

Starting Monday, November 2, the library meeting room will begin to fill with donations of books, CDs, and DVDs. Anyone is welcome to donate gently used hardcovers, paperbacks, or audiovisual items during regular library hours between Monday, November 2 and Thursday, November 5. The library requests, “no VHS tapes, magazines, or any materials in really poor condition.” They also ask donors to bring materials into the meeting room and not leave any donations outside.

Friday, November 6, from 5 to 7 p.m., is the “Preview Sale” part of the book sale. This is open only to members of the “Friends of the Boxborough Library,” but one can join at the door if not currently a member. You might also see professional book resellers filling crates of books on this night. Since the prices are so low, and the sale only runs for two hours, the atmosphere is exciting.

Saturday, November 7, from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m., is the main book sale, which is open to anyone and has a more relaxed atmosphere. Come and peruse what is still available, and stock up for holiday gift giving. There is also a table to join the Friends of the Boxborough Library, with a drawing for a gift basket from local artisans. Membership fees also support library programs.

Sunday, November 8, from 2 to 4 p.m., is “$5 bag day.” Fill a standard paper shopping bag with anything that is left for just $5. The staff provide shopping bags. Sometimes people eagerly line up before the doors open, probably rehearsing for Black Friday.

Stay informed about more library events, including Dungeons and Dragons, Book Clubs, story times, and many types of crafting events at [https://boxlib.org/news-events/events](https://boxlib.org/news-events/events).
