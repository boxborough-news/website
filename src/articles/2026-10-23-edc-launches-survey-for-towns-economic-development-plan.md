Boxborough’s Economic Development Committee (EDC) has launched a survey to gather data for the town’s very first Economic Development Plan (the “Plan”).

The survey is open to residents and non-residents who visit Boxborough for any reason (to work, shop, use the library, walk conservation land, etc.) and is available at [mapc.ma/boxborough](http://mapc.ma/boxborough).

“The survey is quite detailed and will take 10 to 15 minutes to complete,” EDC Chair Abby Reip told Boxborough News, “and we are hoping for a robust response because the data will help us write a plan that reflects Boxborough's values while advancing our economic growth.”

"I'm excited to begin promoting this very important survey exercise,” wrote Town Planner Alec Wade when asked to comment. “A plan which is so data driven and can be so impactful to future development, requires deep thought by residents, employees, and business owners. I encourage anyone who lives, shops, owns a business or commutes to Boxborough to take the survey, and I anticipate rewards or discounts will be offered to those who complete the survey fully."

The Metropolitan Area Planning Council (MAPC), the regional planning agency providing technical assistance to the Town, developed the survey and will collect and synthesize the data. As detailed in the scope of work for the project, “data collection and equity analysis” is the first of four phases in writing the Plan.

The purpose of the Economic Development Plan, according to the project scope of work, is to “define actionable economic development goals” for Boxborough. The Plan is expected to identify “options and strategies for the town to consider,” explained Town Planner Alec Wade. These options may include potential bylaw changes, business outreach programs, and/or other steps the town could take to pursue opportunities for economic growth.
