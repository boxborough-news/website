The Superintendent Screening Committee established by the Acton-Boxborough School Committee is preparing to interview candidates for the role of ABRSD Superintendent in early November.

In February 2026, Superintendent Peter Light announced that he had accepted a position  
as Superintendent at the American Community School in Abu Dhabi starting in  
July 2027\. He also announced that he intends to remain Superintendent of  
Acton-Boxborough for the 2026-2027 school year.

In June, the School Committee voted to choose the Collins Center for Public Management as the district’s Superintendent search firm. Since June, a “superintendent search subcommittee” of three School Committee members – Vice Chair Adam Klein, Lisa Adil, and Jacob Fay – have been working to prepare for the superintendent search process.

In early October, the School Committee voted the membership and charge of a Superintendent Screening Committee (“Screening Committee”), composed of 18 school and community members. Membership includes School Committee members, district administration and staff, and parent and community representatives from each town.

Screening Committee members affiliated with Boxborough include School Committee Vice Chair Adam Klein, Blanchard Principal Mike Votto, parent representative and Blanchard PTF President Michelle Norris, and Boxborough community representative (and Boxborough Select Board member) Kristin Hilberg. In addition, Special Education Parent Advisory Council (SEPAC) representative, Amanda Bailey, is a former Blanchard parent, and one of the Elementary Certified Staff representatives, Danielle Fredericks, is a former Blanchard educator (currently at the Boardwalk campus).

The Screening Committee’s charge, as voted by the School Committee, is to “work collaboratively with the staff of the Collins Center for Public Management to screen applicants, create appropriate interview questions, conduct initial interviews and recommend candidates to be moved to the next phase in the process and potentially become finalists.”

As reported by Vice Chair Klein at the October 15 School Committee meeting, the Screening Committee is currently developing a set of nine interview questions to use at first round interviews. Soon, they will begin reviewing applications and deciding which candidates to interview.

According to the Screening Committee’s charge, “the names of people interviewed must remain confidential throughout and beyond the Screening Committee process and only the names of those to be interviewed by the School Committee will become public.”

Klein explained to Boxborough News that “applicants are kept in strict confidence until they are named publicly as finalists. This is to protect their information and privacy during the screening process. As many of them may be sitting principals and superintendents, we don't wish to put their current positions at risk should we not choose to move them forward as finalists. Once the screening committee completes its process and names the finalists, that's when they will be announced publicly.”

The Screening Committee is expected to conduct its first round interviews in early November and vote on an “unranked slate” of two to five finalists. The finalists are expected to visit the district the week of November 16 to meet with administrators, staff, and community members. The finalists are expected to be interviewed by the full School Committee on December 3\.

At the October 15 School Committee meeting, Vice Chair Klein emphasized the opportunities for the community to be involved in the superintendent search process. An initial round of community outreach, including a survey and live input sessions, was recently completed, and another survey is expected in late November. Klein also noted that community members are welcome to submit feedback via email to the School Committee at any time.

School Committee and Screening Committee member Jacob Faye of Acton acknowledged that the decision process is fast-paced, but explained that “we are balancing transparency and expediency here so that we can be one of the first to get to the end and make the decision among the candidates… we are trying to move quickly through the process so we have the choice of the candidates.”
