At their October 27 meeting, the Boxborough Recreation Commission (RecCom) discussed the status of the playground renovation at Flerra Meadows. The installation of the new playground equipment is complete, but the mulch surface is uneven and the installers must return to level the surface.

The playground remains closed to the public pending resolution of the uneven surface as well as completion of ADA accessibility requirements, specifically a compliant pathway from the parking lot to the playground. The Commission is looking to install an accessible gravel pathway before winter so the playground can re-open.

The town closed the playground at Flerra Meadows on September 1 to replace playground equipment and install rubber safety mats to bring the playground into compliance with the American Disabilities Act (ADA). The funding for this project was approved at the May 2026 Annual Town Meeting. The renovated playground was expected to open in October.

RecCom members discussed an official opening ceremony or ribbon-cutting in the Spring. Flerra fields remain open while the playground is closed.

In other meeting business, the RecCom discussed preparations for Boxborough’s annual Winterfest. The RecCom proposed a Winterfest date of Saturday, January 30th, with Sunday, January 31 as the backup weather date. The RecCom expects to ask local vendors and businesses to participate in the event, and more details will be confirmed at future meetings.

The Recreation Commission also discussed potential improvements to the Blanchard Running Club. The Running Club, held on Wednesday mornings, has approximately 70 registered participants – making it one of the largest and most popular recreation programs outside of Flerra Summer Playground. The program is expected to continue through most of November before ending for the season.

The RecCom also reviewed an updated draft of the town’s field use permit application and fee schedule and will continue discussing the fee schedule at their next meeting on Tuesday, November 17.
