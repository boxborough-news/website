Across the U.S., voters are casting ballots in federal, state, and local elections ahead of Election Day, which is Tuesday, November 3\.

Boxborough News reached out to Town Clerk Rebecca (Becky) Harris for the early voting statistics in Boxborough.

Harris reported, “...We've had a record turnout with 348 voters voting early in person, plus 9 absentee voters (357). We had a record-breaking day today \[Thursday, October 29\], with 65 early voters casting their ballots. I mailed out 1,002 Vote-By-Mail ballots, and of those, 617 were returned so far…So, as of today \[Thursday, October 29\], which is the last day for in-person early voting (in Boxborough), a total of 974 voters have voted so far.”

According to a report by WCVB-TV, Secretary of the Commonwealth William Galvin said he attributes a statewide early voting surge to the “interest in nine statewide ballot questions.” With Election Day only five days away, Secretary Galvin is encouraging voters not to return their mail-in ballots by mail and, instead, return them to their local election office (Boxborough Town Hall).

Last month, Secretary Galvin’s office mailed a booklet containing detailed information on each of the nine ballot questions to all registered voters. Hard copies of the information booklet are available at Town Hall and also online ([HERE](https://ma.omniballot.us/sites/25/vg/app/vig/voter-guide/info)).

At the Boxborough Select Board meeting on Monday, Chair Bob Stemple and member Wes Fowlks encouraged residents to research the ballot questions in advance and “bring a cheat sheet,” noting that the nine questions “took a while.”

Voters can cast ballots in person on Election Day–Tuesday, November 3; polls will be open from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. at Town Hall.

For additional information, including a [sample ballot](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/6942/BOXBOROUGH_ENG_439_ICP-SAMPLE-PDF?bidId=), visit the town’s website: ([https\://www\.boxborough-ma.gov/211/Elections-Voting](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/211/Elections-Voting)).
