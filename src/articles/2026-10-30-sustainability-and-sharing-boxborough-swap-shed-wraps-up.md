Hoping to find that last-minute Halloween costume, some reading material for the cold winter ahead, or wrapping paper for the upcoming holiday season? The Swap Shed at the Boxborough Transfer Station may have what you’re looking for.

But you better hurry–Saturday, October 31 (10 a.m. to 2 p.m.) is your last chance, as the Swap Shed’s doors will close for the winter.

Swap Shed volunteers told Boxborough News that on Wednesday, October 28, available finds included a glass-topped patio table, board games, framed artwork, kitchen chairs, and many other useful items.

The Swap Shed first opened in September 2025\. It is a collaboration between the Boxborough Sustainability Committee, Office of Land Use and Permitting, and Department of Public Works.

“A ‘swap shed’ is a community resource that encourages folks to share gently used, functional items in order to reduce waste, promote reuse, and save money on waste disposal,” Sustainability Committee Chair Kate Davies told Boxborough News when the Swap Shed opened last year.

The Swap Shed is operated entirely by dedicated community volunteers, and additional help is always needed. Anyone interested in helping when the Swap Shed reopens in April 2027 can [sign up to volunteer](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSf6KKVWI6Xe4aEGtyEE7c9QjcsiMGVwgjojw9eKi1vwIDiXjQ/viewform).

For more information about the Swap Shed, including volunteer opportunities and alternative locations and organizations to donate household items, see [https\://www\.boxborough-ma.gov/879/Swap-Shed](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/879/Swap-Shed)
