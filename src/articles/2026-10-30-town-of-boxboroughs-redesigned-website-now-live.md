On Wednesday, October 28, the Town of Boxborough launched its redesigned website after a months-long process to update the site.

Assistant Town Administrator Rajon Hudson began the website redesign process back in February 2026\. Hudson told Boxborough News at that time that the goal of the redesign was to make the website more user-friendly and to make sure website visitors could more easily find the information they were looking for.

A website redesign working group, including both residents and town employees, began meeting in April 2026\. They worked with the town’s website manager, CivicPlus, to implement the changes.

Town Administrator and Select Board Administrative Assistant Kelley Price told Boxborough News in September that resident input has been an important part of the redesign process. Price said that the Town conducted a survey through social media asking residents “how they use the current website, what information they typically look for, and what information they would like to be able to find more easily.”

The Town also asked CivicPlus to review website statistics and search data to determine which information and pages residents access most frequently. The website working group used both the survey responses and actual website usage data to help determine which information should be most prominent and easiest to access from the new homepage.

“We asked for input and the energy and dedication that staff and residents have volunteered have had a great impact on the high quality of the new user friendliness of the site,” Town Administrator Mike Johns told Boxborough News in September. “I am so appreciative that Boxborough showed up to make this project great.”
