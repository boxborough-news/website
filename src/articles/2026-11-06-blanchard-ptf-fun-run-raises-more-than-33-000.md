At Blanchard Memorial School, Friday, October 30 wasn’t just the day before Halloween. Anticipation of costumes and candy was temporarily forgotten as the students filed out to the field to the cheers of teachers, staff, friends, and family, and took their places under the giant balloon arch for the Blanchard Parents Teachers and Friends (PTF)’s annual “Fun Run.”

And then they were off…sprinting, skipping, walking hand in hand with a friend, dancing along to the playlist blasting out across the makeshift oval track, and waving to the crowd of parents juggling coffee mugs and pom-poms while cheering on their kid, their kid’s friends, and their friends’ kids.  

“We had an incredible turnout of parents and caregivers for this year’s Fun Run,” said PTF Vice President Ashley Jayne. “Parents and caregivers showed up in a big way, cheering on our students with pom-poms, signs, and encouraging voices. Their energy and support helped make the day extra special and reminded us of the amazing community we have at Blanchard.”  

The Fun Run was the culmination of a Blanchard PTF fundraising drive that raised a record-breaking $33,115. The total was “$7,000 more than last year,” said Jayne, “which was previously our highest year.”  

The Blanchard PTF has organized the Fun Run since 2019 and uses the funds to support teacher and school community needs and to fund activities and enrichment programs for students.  

As the fundraiser met certain dollar milestones along the way, the students earned school-wide prizes. “Students earned a pajama/comfy clothes day, staff inflatable race, extra recess for the whole school with new recess cart equipment, Busy Bee inflatables at recess and extra recess, [and] a Skydome Planetarium coming to Blanchard,” said Jayne. “[And] all students who registered for the Fun Run will also be entered into our raffle for Principal for a Day!” 

As he did last year, Blanchard Principal Mike Votto got in on the fun and added some extra incentives for the PTF’s “stretch” and “reach for the stars” goals of $25,000 and $30,000. 

“I wasn't sure how we would top [Mr. Votto sleeping on the roof last year](https://www.boxboroughnews.org/archives/july-dec-2025-features#h.iejixnxvfv28)!,” said Jayne. “Duct taping the principal is a fun trend that I've seen in other schools and Mr. Votto agreed to be duct taped if we hit our stretch goal of $25,000…  and graciously agreed to be ‘slimed’ if we hit $30,000. We named this our "Reach for the Stars" goal and this gained a lot of excitement and traction with our school community!” Jayne reports that the PTF is working with Principal Votto on a date for the students to enjoy their big reward.  

Added Jayne, “We couldn't have pulled off this event without the support of Kristin Hilberg  Love Where you Live Realty  who donated to cover our platform fees and a water bottle for every student at the run, and Boxborough Police and Fire for handing out popsicles all morning. We are so thankful for their continued support of this event year after year. Another special thank you to Busy Bee Jumpers for donating our recess inflatables and our parent volunteers who helped the day before and day of the event. Our Blanchard community’s efforts this month continue to remind me how special our community is, and that together we achieve great things!”
