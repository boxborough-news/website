On Thursday November 5, the Boxborough Community Preservation Committee (CPC) held its public hearing on the project request for $300,000 of fiscal year 2028 Community Preservation Act (CPA) funds towards the acquisition of 276 Middle Road, the Richardson Farm.

The presentation of the request was made by John Neyland, Vice Chair of the Boxborough Agricultural Commission. The CPA funding would be a portion of the total funding for the $1.8 million acquisition of the property, which is proposed in Article 4 of the Fall Town Meeting warrant.

Read prior Boxborough News coverage of Article 4 funding [here](https://boxborough-news.github.io/website/2026/10/in-advance-of-fall-town-meeting-town-looks-to-reduce-1-8/).

During the approximately one hour hybrid public hearing, the CPC also heard from a number of members of the public.

After questions and comments from members of the CPC and members of the public, the CPC voted unanimously to recommend appropriation of Community Preservation FY 2028 funds as specified in Article 4 of the November 16, 2026 Fall Town Meeting warrant: $22,000 from the Community Preservation Open Space Reserve Account, $58,000 from the FY 2028 Community Preservation Budget Reserve, and $220,000 from undesignated Community Preservation funds.

The next meeting of the CPC will be held December 3, 2026. The committee will have a public hearing and discuss and vote on three other FY 2028 CPA proposals.
