At its meeting on November 3, the Boxborough Finance Committee (FinCom) discussed a revised five-year budget forecast developed using historical performance of revenues and new growth over the same timeframes.

New growth refers to property values that increase due to new construction including new buildings, additions, or major renovations to existing properties; newly taxed parcels that were previously exempt; and parcels taxed as separate entities for the first time, like new subdivisions and condominium conversions.

FinCom Chair Tony Newton said that the preliminary forecast is “directionally correct, in that no override is expected to be needed for the next fiscal year.” However, Newton expects the forecast will shift and change over the course of the next several months as assumptions about revenues and expenses are “pressure tested” and locked down.

A member of the public spoke to FinCom about the need for fiscal restraint, citing the new fire station, school budgets, water quality and maintaining the rural character of the town. When reached for comment after the meeting, Newton said that many of the specifics proposed by the resident have in fact been discussed over the last several years, while others are “not realistic.”

With the exception of the meeting on November 3, all FinCom meetings are recorded by BXBTV and available on demand through the town website. Meeting minutes, which include summaries of deliberations, votes taken and budget forecasts, are also available on the town website after the minutes are voted by FinCom.

FinCom’s next meeting is scheduled immediately prior to Fall Town Meeting on Monday, November 16, at Blanchard at 6 p.m.
