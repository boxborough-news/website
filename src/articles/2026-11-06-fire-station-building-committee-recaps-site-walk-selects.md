At its meeting on Thursday, November 5, the Boxborough Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) recapped their site walk of the future fire station at 984 Mass Ave.

FSBC Chair Mary Brolin reported that the site visit “went well and was worth the effort.” She praised Town Planner Alec Wade and Associate Town Planner Ian Gilson for their work in setting up the site so that the committee and abutters could see the placement and height of the proposed tower as well as the location of the driveway that leads to the overflow lot.

Brolin said, “It was a great discussion, friendly, with good questions,” referring to the conversation with the abutters who attended the site walk. “It was so helpful to see it concretely, for them and for us.” Abutters told the committee that the tower was not visible from their homes, which had been one of their concerns, and were pleased to see that the access to the overflow parking lot was not as far up on Sara’s Way (close to the residences) as they thought it would be.

Abutters raised concerns about tree removal at the perimeter of the overflow lot. They asked that some of the existing trees be preserved and noted that the tall trees help minimize the tower. Town Administrator Mike Johns and committee members agreed that the design team should take another look at the existing trees.

Turning to design, Chris Logan and Jeff Shaw of Context Architecture (Context) walked the committee through a series of slides showing options for building exterior materials and colors. The apparatus section of the building will be composed of masonry items (brick, block, and precast concrete), and the administrative part of the building will be composed of clapboard and shingle siding, with a shingle roof.

Logan and Shaw placed design samples of the various materials on a table in the room and invited FSBC members to  “play with textures and colors.” The committee spent considerable time examining the materials before arriving at a color combination for each section of the building.

The committee then addressed the schedule for site plan approval and the bidding process. The Planning Board will conduct three public hearings for site plan review, starting on November 23 and continuing to December 7 and December 21\.

Owner’s Project Manager Steve Kirby of Vertex informed the committee that the cost estimation for the project is underway, and he expects to receive it before Thanksgiving and will present it to the committee at their meeting on December 3\. Another cost estimate will be complete by the end of January 2027, just before the bidding process begins. Kirby told the committee that the bidding process will take approximately two months. The bid set will be released on February 1, 2027 with bids due March 29, 2027\.

The FSBC’s next meeting is Thursday, November 19 at 7 p.m. in the Sargent Memorial Library Meeting Room.
