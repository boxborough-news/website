You may have seen them along Route 111 on Halloween morning. Donning safety vests and carrying bright yellow trash bags, members of the Boxborough Minutemen Company were not wearing costumes–they were performing their annual fall highway clean-up, removing litter along the roadway.

“This year we had 13 people, which made for light work,” newly elected Captain Tim Blankenship told Boxborough News. “While there were no interesting finds this year (such as cash and undergarments as in years past), we did find a Narragansett beer can with an actual pull top, which our resident beer can expert, Gerry Gaebel, suggests is about 50 years old. Don't know how we missed it for 50 years…”

The Minutemen filled more than a dozen bags of trash, which were picked up by the Massachusetts Department of Transportation for proper disposal. For more information about the Boxborough Minutemen Company, visit [facebook.com/BoxboroughMinutemenCompany](https://www.facebook.com/BoxboroughMinutemenCompany/) or [boxboroughminutemen.org](https://boxboroughminutemen.org/).
