Boxborough is about to become an even more welcoming destination for cyclists. CKBikes LLC, a full-service bicycle shop founded by Craig Kilmer in 2010, will open its doors at 233 Summer Road on December 1, bringing more than 16 years of cycling industry experience, specialized repair services and hands-on education to the Boxborough community.

For Kilmer, choosing Boxborough was more than finding a new location for his business. “We picked Boxborough because it sits at the absolute intersection of everything great about Massachusetts cycling,” Kilmer said.

Recognizing Boxborough’s popularity as a starting point and meeting place for cycling routes, Kilmer has designed the shop to serve as a convenient pit stop for riders passing through.

In addition to bicycles, parts and accessories, CKBikes will offer a dedicated refreshment area stocked with cyclist-friendly snacks, performance carbohydrates and electrolytes. The goal is to give riders a place to refuel, hydrate, pick up supplies or get help with their equipment while out on a ride.

A former manufacturing engineer, Kilmer brings a technical approach to bicycle maintenance and repair, combining his engineering background with a lifelong passion for cycling. His goal is to provide riders with a place where they can find expert service, quality equipment, practical education and a little support along the way.

CKBikes is an authorized dealer of Orbea and Ventum bicycles, offering road, gravel and mountain bikes, along with personalized fittings. Kilmer is also a Certified Fork and Shock Service Center for mountain bikes, providing advanced suspension tuning, maintenance and rebuilds. The business is also a Certified Bosch Motor Repair Center, offering diagnostic and repair services for Bosch-powered e-bikes.

Kilmer’s engineering experience informs his approach to repairs. “With my background in manufacturing engineering, I treat every tune-up, suspension rebuild and e-bike diagnostic with absolute precision,” he said.

Education is another important part of Kilmer’s vision. As an official Park Tool School, CKBikes will offer hands-on bicycle maintenance and repair classes for riders of all experience levels.

Courses will range from a four-hour introductory repair clinic for casual cyclists to an intensive 80-hour professional mechanics certification program for those interested in pursuing a career in the bicycle industry. The classes are designed to help riders become more confident by maintaining their own bicycles while also creating opportunities for those looking to develop professional mechanical skills.

“We aren’t just selling bikes; we are here to support riders at every stage,” Kilmer said, “whether that means fine-tuning a high-end trail system, grabbing a quick snack mid-ride, or teaching a hobbyist how to master their own repairs.”

CKBikes LLC will officially open to the public on December 1 at 233 Summer Road. Registration for winter maintenance and professional mechanics courses is open now. CK Bikes is currently located at 320 Great Road in Littleton, MA.
