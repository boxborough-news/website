At its meeting on Monday, November 2, the Boxborough Planning Board held a public hearing for a scenic road permit and stone wall alteration on Old Harvard Road.

Planning Board Chair Rich Guzzardi opened the hearing and invited the homeowners, Martin and Susan Green, to present their application. The Greens, who live on Steele Lane, explained that they were seeking to temporarily enlarge a stone wall opening on Old Harvard Road from 10 feet to 15 feet to replace their septic system. A portion of their property includes frontage along Old Harvard Road and is the only access to the septic system.

The Greens described the wall removal as “mostly soil” and said that the displaced stones would be restored after the project’s completion. They also noted that the town’s tree warden confirmed that no public shade trees would be removed or significantly impacted.

According to Associate Town Planner Ian Gilson’s report to the Planning Board, “Old Harvard Road is a designated scenic road and Massachusetts General Law (MGL) requires prior written consent from the Planning Board in a public hearing to disturb stone walls along designated scenic roads.”

Members of the Planning Board asked questions related to the schedule of the work, tree disturbance, and restoration after the work is completed. Abutters to the property also asked questions about tree removal and the grading along the roadway.

After discussing a list of conditions that included limiting the opening to a 15-foot maximum, keeping the roadway clear of dirt and construction debris, and full restoration using the original materials to the satisfaction of the Town Planner, the Planning Board voted to grant the scenic road permit (4-0-1).

In other business, the Planning Board discussed their strategic goals for the coming year, including future articles for consideration, master plan action items, and other objectives such as an overlay zoning district for village center.

The Planning Board’s next meeting is Monday, November 23 to conduct the first of three public hearings for the new fire station. Subsequent hearings will be held on December 7 and December 21\.
