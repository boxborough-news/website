On Tuesday, November 3, Boxborough voters went to the polls to cast their votes in state and federal elections and weigh in on the state ballot questions.   
  The Boxborough Town Clerk’s office released preliminary results just after 11 p.m. on Tuesday night. The following voting data comes from the preliminary results.   
  In the race for Senator in Congress, Edward Markey, the Democratic incumbent, received 1855 votes from Boxborough voters. John Deaton, the Republican candidate, received 588 votes from Boxborough voters.   
  In the race for Representative in Congress, Lori Trahan, the Democratic incumbent, received 1916 votes from Boxborough voters. Gary Grossi, the Republican candidate, received 542 votes from Boxborough voters.  
  In the race for Governor, Maura Healey, the Democratic incumbent, received 1858 votes from Boxborough voters. Mike Minogue, the Republican candidate, received 604 votes from Boxborough voters.   
  On the state ballot questions, Boxborough residents voted as follows:

| Question | Yes | No |

| Question 2, Collective Bargaining for CPCS Employees | 1640 | 846 |
| Question 3, State Primary Elections | 963 | 1560 |
| Question 4, Election Day Registration | 1570 | 964 |
| Question 5, State Revenue Limit and Rebate | 1033 | 1458  |
| Question 6, Natural Resource Conservation Fund | 1616 | 885 |
| Question 7, Single Family Homes | 1171 | 1331  |
| Question 8, Prohibit Retail Sale of Adult Recreational Use Marijuana | 698 | 1841 |
| Question 9, Firearms Regulation | 2004  | 549 |

Out of 4,106 registered voters in Boxborough, 2,566 voters (62.5%) cast ballots in this election.  
  “Turnout was on par with previous elections, but due to double the number of ballots and close to 2,400 ballot pages to process throughout the day while over 1,300 in-person voters were voting, the day felt a bit chaotic and busy,” Boxborough Town Clerk Rebecca Harris told Boxborough News. “I am extremely grateful for all of my election workers, many of whom stayed far longer than they had originally signed up for\!”
