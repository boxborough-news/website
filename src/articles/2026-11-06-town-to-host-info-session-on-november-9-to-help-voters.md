Boxborough’s Fall Town Meeting (FTM) will be held on Monday, November 16 at 7 p.m. at the Blanchard Memorial School.

There are 13 articles on the FTM warrant. These include the proposed purchase of 276 Middle Road; a proposed temporary moratorium on data centers; a proposal for a flow control system at the Hager Bridge to address beaver activity; and a proposed change to the MBTA Communities overlay district.

One week prior to FTM, on Monday, November 9, the town will host a “Fall Town Meeting Info Night” at the Sargent Memorial Library from 2 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. for residents who want to learn more about the warrant articles in advance of Town Meeting.  

The info session is intended to be a “drop-in” event; there will not be a timed presentation, and participants do not have to arrive at the 2 p.m. start time. Instead, visitors can move throughout the library meeting room and speak with town staff and volunteers about the issues on the FTM warrant.

Article 4, the proposed purchase of 276 Middle Road for $1.8 million, is expected to generate considerable discussion. The warrant reflects split votes on Article 4 from the Select Board (3-1-1), Finance Committee (4-1-0), and Capital Committee (1-3-1). The warrant also reflects that the Community Preservation Committee, Conservation Commission, and Water Resources Committee have deferred their vote and recommendation to Town Meeting floor.

The property at 276 Middle Road, including a house, barn, and 18.5 acres of land, belongs to the Richardson family. According to the warrant, the town would like to purchase the property “for the purposes of active agriculture;” additionally, “the land will join contiguously to 93 acres of existing conservation and agricultural land.” Article 4 is sponsored by the Boxborough Agricultural Commission. Their vision for the property, explained in the warrant, is to lease the farmhouse, barn, and at least part of the property to a farmer for agricultural use. Article 4 requires a two-thirds majority to pass.  

For more information about Fall Town Meeting, consult the warrant, which was mailed to every household in Boxborough and is [available on the town website](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/6950/FY27-Fall-Town-Meeting?bidId=). The warrant contains descriptions of each article, recommendations for and/or against the article by town boards and committees, and general information about what to expect at Town Meeting.
