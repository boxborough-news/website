Boxborough Police K9 Koda is recovering from tickborne anaplasmosis, after being diagnosed and treated earlier this month.

According to Public Information Officer and Canine Commander Lt. Steven Patriarca, K9 Koda became ill and was hospitalized at Westford Veterinary Emergency Center, but is recovering well.

“Koda is doing very well and seems more like herself now,” Lt. Patriarca told Boxborough News. “She should be back to full duty in a week or so and able to continue her training.”

K9 Sergeant Max Bressi added, “It’s amazing how the smallest critters can cause the biggest problems for our pups. The most important lesson I learned from this experience is that preventive tick medication is never a guarantee. Be sure to routinely check your pets for ticks throughout the year, even when they are on preventative medication.”

According to the Cornell University College of Veterinary Medicine, “Anaplasmosis is a bacterial tickborne disease. Some dogs with anaplasmosis may never show signs of illness or require treatment, but those that do commonly get a fever and respond quickly to antibiotics. … The best ways to avoid anaplasmosis and other tick-borne diseases in dogs include: year-round tick preventatives for all pets, backyard management (e.g. keep lawns cut short, remove leaf piles, etc.), routine tick checks, prompt removal of ticks, \[and\] avoid high-risk areas (wooded areas with a dense understory, tall grass and leaf litter).”

A November 7 social media post from Boxborough Police Officer’s Association explained that earlier that week, K9 Koda “began exhibiting symptoms that were completely out of character for her. She was extremely lethargic, refused to eat or drink, and would not even come out of her kennel, despite attempts to entice her with her favorite toy (her ChuckIt ball). Recognizing that something was seriously wrong, K9 Sgt. Bressi made the decision to rush Koda to an emergency veterinary hospital for evaluation.”

“Koda and I are incredibly grateful for the overwhelming support from our Boxborough PD family, the Boxborough Community, and our fellow Town Employees,” Sgt. Bressi told Boxborough News. “We send our sincere gratitude to the incredible staff at Westford Veterinary Emergency Center (Westford MA) for the outstanding emergency care they provided to Koda during such a scary time.”
