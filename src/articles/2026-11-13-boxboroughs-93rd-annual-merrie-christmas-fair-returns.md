The United Church of Christ Boxborough’s historic annual Merrie Christmas Fair will return on Saturday, November 21 from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.

A Boxborough tradition since 1933, the Fair will extend beyond the church at 723 Massachusetts Avenue, filling the Community Center next door and Town Hall across the street with holiday cheer, local talent, and neighborhood generosity.

This year, organizers have announced that 10% of all fair proceeds will directly benefit A Friend in Need, a local emergency fund supporting residents of Boxborough, Acton, and Maynard that provides immediate financial assistance to residents facing unexpected hardships. The remainder of the fair proceeds benefit the ongoing expenses of UCC Boxborough.

This year's Fair lineup blends longtime favorite exhibits with some new additions.

As in years past, the Greens Table will have fresh seasonal greenery, the Baked Goods table will offer homemade treats, and the Knits and Gifts Table offers handmade knitwear, crafted ornaments, and custom hand-sewn gifts. New this year, the Food Hall at the Community Center will feature lunch and breakfast items from Boxborough’s own Oscar’s Burritos.

The fair remains a showcase for regional craftspeople. Fair organizer Gloria Roe says, “I've been involved with the Merrie Christmas Fair for a very long time (50+ years) in various roles. For many years, my favorite has been the Ornament Table, where I have had the opportunity to be creative and offer a variety of handmade ornaments from talented people within and outside our church. It is always a surprise what crafters come up with each year.”

The fair takes pride in being family friendly; children can explore a Children's Table full of crafts to build and budget gifts to buy, enjoy a visit from Santa Claus, and then head outdoors to warm up by a s'mores fire pit and a free bouncy house.

Across the street, the unique “Attic Treasurers” portion of the Fair will once again fill the Grange Room at Town Hall with community-donated items for purchase. Donations are encouraged; community members interested in donating can drop items off at a “donation pod” at the church beginning on November 14.

The Auction portion of the Merrie Christmas Fair also continues this year – online, as it has been since 2020. Fair organizer Mary Anne Vogel says, “We have wonderful gifts and unique experiences for you and everyone on your Christmas list! We have Celtics tickets—not just *any* tickets, but for the Ford Sports Deck on the 5th floor of TD Garden. … Huge thanks to all the donors in our church and in our community for their wonderful gifts to the UCCB Online Auction 2026!”

The Online Auction is available at: [https://auctria.events/UCCBOnlineAuction2026](https://auctria.events/UCCBOnlineAuction2026).

“This is my second year as co-chair with Laurel Dickson Bull,” says Fair organizer Mary Pavlik. “Our role is to coordinate all the pieces and get the word out that this fair is fun, festive, and full of high-quality gifts! We have extraordinary talent in this group, comparable to the artisan crafts I saw on my European cruise through German and French markets last year. And this year, we'd love to have community folks join us in the fun behind the scenes!”

Organizers are actively welcoming community members to step behind the scenes as volunteers. For more information on how to become involved, contact the church office at [boxboroughchurch@gmail.com](mailto:boxboroughchurch@gmail.com) or call 978-263-7387.
