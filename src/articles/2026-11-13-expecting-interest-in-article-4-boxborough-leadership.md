On November 10, the Boxborough Leadership Forum (BLF), including Town Moderator Dennis Reip and representatives of the Select Board, Planning Board, Finance Committee, School Committee, and Library Trustees met at Town Hall to discuss the upcoming Fall Town Meeting and other updates from their respective groups.

Boxborough’s Fall Town Meeting (FTM) will be held on Monday, November 16 at 7 p.m. at the Blanchard Memorial School. There are 13 articles on the FTM warrant. These include the proposed purchase of 276 Middle Road; a proposed temporary moratorium on data centers; a proposal for a flow control system to address beaver activity at the Hager Bridge; and a proposed change to the MBTA Communities overlay district.

Moderator Reip began the discussion with Fall Town Meeting logistics and shared that he expects voter “interest” in Article 4, the proposed purchase of 276 Middle Road, which requires a two-thirds majority to pass. Reip noted that there is no provision for a second night of Fall Town Meeting.

The property at 276 Middle Road, including a house, barn, and 18.5 acres of land, belongs to the Richardson family. According to the warrant, the town would like to purchase the property “for the purposes of active agriculture”; additionally, “the land will join contiguously to 93 acres of existing conservation and agricultural land.” Article 4 is sponsored by the Boxborough Agricultural Commission. Their vision for the property, explained in the warrant, is to lease the farmhouse, barn, and at least part of the property to a farmer for agricultural use. The warrant reflects split votes on Article 4 from the Select Board (3-1-1), Finance Committee (4-1-0), and Capital Committee (1-3-1).

The BLF noted that other town committees have weighed in on Article 4 since the warrant was printed. On November 4, the Conservation Commission voted unanimously to recommend Article 4. On November 5, the Community Preservation Committee voted unanimously to recommend appropriation of Community Preservation FY 2028 funds as specified in Article 4. On November 6, the Water Resources Committee unanimously recommended the land purchase, provided that the Town “establishes adequate protection for the underlying aquifer recharge and protection area.”

Finance Committee Chair Tony Newton reported that he is aware of a group of residents concerned about the financial impact of Article 4 and the town’s financial outlook over the next five years. These residents shared their concerns with the Finance Committee at their November 3 meeting. Newton told Moderator Reip that the Finance Committee is not planning to give a presentation about town finances at FTM.

Moderator Reip also noted that free on-site childcare services will be available at FTM from 6:30 to 9 p.m. Caregivers are encouraged to sign up children in advance using the QR code in the FTM warrant (mailed to all households and available at [https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/6950/FY27-Fall-Town-Meeting?bidId=](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/6950/FY27-Fall-Town-Meeting?bidId=).

The Boxborough Leadership Forum’s next meeting is scheduled for February 3, 2027.
