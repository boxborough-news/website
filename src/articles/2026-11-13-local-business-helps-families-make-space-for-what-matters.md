For Boxborough resident Kristen Johnson and her business partner Alison Hardisty, an organized home is about much more than neatly arranged shelves and matching storage containers. The two women own and operate Clean Slate, a professional home organization business. Founded more than four years ago, their business helps individuals and families declutter, reorganize, and create functional spaces that better support their lifestyles.

Their partnership began unexpectedly. Alison, formerly a physical therapist, began exploring new opportunities when COVID disrupted her ability to work with patients in person. Kristen had recently moved to Massachusetts from Austin, Texas, where she had helped people organize their homes and create inviting spaces. When Alison mentioned a project involving shelving, power tools, and some creative problem-solving, Kristen volunteered to help. That first project sparked a friendship and eventually a business.

Today, the two combine their different strengths and shared experiences to help families throughout Massachusetts, with much of their work concentrated in the MetroWest Boston area. Although Clean Slate serves clients across Massachusetts, its “roots, relationships, and the heart of the business are in Boxborough and the surrounding communities,” they shared.

“We believe an organized home isn’t about perfection or looking like a magazine. It’s about creating a sense of calm, making room for what matters most, and helping people feel better in their everyday spaces,” they explained.

For the founders, supporting the community is an important part of running their business. They regularly donate clients’ unwanted but useful belongings to local organizations, including Acton Household Goods, the Acton-Boxborough Resource Center, and Welcome NST, which provides furnishings for immigrant and refugee families. They also work with local churches and organizations supporting veterans and women rebuilding their lives.

They also enjoy supporting fellow local entrepreneurs, particularly through their network of women business owners. Their own team reflects that commitment, with two team members, Jen and Lori, who also reside in Boxborough.

What Alison and Kristen find most rewarding about their work is the difference an organized home can make in someone’s life.

They recalled helping one family prepare for the arrival of a new baby. The home had become so overwhelmed with belongings that the parents struggled to imagine bringing their newborn into the space. Working room by room, Clean Slate helped reclaim living areas and transform a storage room into a nursery. When the mother saw the finished room, she cried. After years of uncertainty, she finally felt she could picture bringing her baby home. Two years later, the family was still maintaining the systems the team had established.

“That’s what we love most about this work. It's not about making a home look perfect for a day; it’s about helping people create spaces that support their lives, their families, and all the wonderful things yet to come,” they described. For the founders, these transformations are about creating lasting change rather than temporary results.

For anyone feeling overwhelmed by clutter, Alison and Kristen offer one simple recommendation: start small. Rather than attempting to reorganize an entire home, they suggest beginning with one drawer, cabinet, or countertop. They also encourage people to declutter before purchasing storage containers, give belongings designated places, and create simple systems that the entire family can maintain.

Their process begins with a complimentary 30-minute consultation, either virtually or in person, where they learn about a client’s needs, frustrations, and goals, before developing an individualized plan. Throughout the process, they emphasize a judgment-free approach, recognizing that every household has different circumstances.

“Your home should support the life you're living, not the life you think you’re supposed to live," they explained. “Peace should never require perfection\!”

Ultimately, Clean Slate’s philosophy is captured in its motto: “Making space for what matters most.” Whether helping a family prepare for a new arrival or simply reclaiming a kitchen countertop, Alison and Kristen hope to give people more opportunities to enjoy the spaces they call home.

As they put it, “The goal isn’t to spend your life organizing your home. It’s to spend more time enjoying it\!”
