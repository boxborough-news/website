At its meeting of November 6, 2026, the Water Resources Committee (WRC) discussed their recommendation for Article 4 of the upcoming Fall Town Meeting and unanimously recommended the purchase of 276 Middle Road, “provided that the Town establishes adequate protection for the underlying aquifer recharge and protection area.”

Article 4 seeks approval for the purchase of property at 276 Middle Road, including a house, a barn, and 18.5 acres of land. According to the Fall Town Meeting warrant, the town would like to purchase the property “for the purposes of active agriculture;” additionally, “the land will join contiguously to 93 acres of existing conservation and agricultural land.”

The Sudbury Valley Trustees (SVT), who have pledged up to $500,000 to offset the cost of the property, have indicated that at least part of their contribution is contingent on the negotiation of one of one or more “conservation restrictions” (CRs) on the three adjacent parcels already owned by the town.

At their last meeting on October 20, several WRC members expressed concerns that CRs on the four parcels could conflict with water-supply uses and that future access to water sources might be compromised.

At their November 6 meeting, Agricultural Commission Vice Chair John Neyland presented to the committee and emphasized that he will work with Town Planner Wade to add “water-friendly” provisions to the CRs if the Article passes and the project moves forward.

In response to questions from committee members about groundwater and soil quality at the property, Neyland explained that a 90-day due diligence process would begin when and if Article 4 passes at Fall Town Meeting. A “phase-one environmental site assessment” is expected to be completed during this diligence period.

In discussing the WRC’s recommendation, Chair Les Fox emphasized that the language of the recommendation is intended to suggest that “the town should take steps to make sure there is adequate protection for the underlying aquifer recharge area” and “keep our options open for the future.”

The next WRC meeting will be held via Zoom at 7:30 p.m. on December 15, 2026.
