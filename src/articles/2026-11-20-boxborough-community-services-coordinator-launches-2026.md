As the 2026 holiday season approaches, Boxborough Community Services Coordinator Wendy Trinks is once again organizing the town's annual holiday support program, distributing gifts and gift cards to local residents and families who need extra help during the season.

Thanks to Trinks' community outreach, the program has more than doubled in size since 2022. The effort relies heavily on the generosity of individual Boxborough residents alongside local non-profit and civic organizations, including the Boxborough Minutemen Company, Acton-Boxborough Community Compass, the St. Vincent de Paul Society, and the Boxborough Children's Center.

Community members looking to participate and help local neighbors this year can contribute in three ways:

-   Donate Gift Cards: Drop off gift cards (Market Basket, Target, or Walmart) to the Community Services Coordinator's office at Town Hall, 29 Middle Road.
-   Make a Monetary Donation: Write a check payable to the "Town of Boxborough" with "Community Services Gift Account" written in the memo field. Checks can be mailed or dropped off at Town Hall to the attention of Wendy Trinks.
-   Sponsor a Family: Residents or groups interested in sponsoring a local family should contact Trinks directly by Tuesday, December 1.

For Trinks, organizing the annual drive remains a highlight of her work in town. “It is the best part of my job to be able to spread holiday cheer to families in need during these difficult times,” she told Boxborough News. “The community is so generous, donating gifts cards and sponsoring families. This year the need continues to be great, as the cost of food and rents continue to increase.”

Trinks oversees the applications for the Boxborough Rental Assistance Program (BRAP) and the Boxborough Emergency Assistance Program (BEAP), and connects clients with other resources as well.

For questions, contact Wendy Trinks at wtrinks@boxborough-ma.gov or call 978-264-1735.
