At its meeting on November 19, the Boxborough Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) voted to authorize up to $26,400 for additional geotechnical testing at 984 Mass Ave. The testing will be used to help determine the best location for an underground fire protection tank and further investigate an existing embankment on the site.

Representatives from Context Architecture (Context) explained that the testing will not completely eliminate “unknowns” but will provide a “more accurate baseline.” The Committee noted that an existing contingency fund can cover the cost of this testing.

Context also updated the committee on their preparations for the first Planning Board hearing scheduled for Monday, November 23, as well as upcoming submissions to the Zoning Board of Appeals and the Conservation Commission. Additional Planning Board hearings are scheduled for December 7, December 14 (if needed), and December 21.

The Committee had a brief conversation about building materials but deferred any decisions, noting the importance of having cost data before comparing and choosing materials.

The FSBC expects to receive a highly anticipated project cost estimate at its next meeting on Thursday, December 3.

The FSBC will hold a public design update on Monday November 30 from 7 to 8:30 p.m. at Town Hall.

The FSBC’s November 19 meeting recording will be posted on the [BXB-TV website](https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/boxborough/?page=HOME) when available.
