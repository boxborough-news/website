At its meeting on Tuesday, November 17, the Boxborough Recreation Commission (RecCom) welcomed the Town’s new Human Services Director, Cassie Scott, and covered a variety of topics, including the ongoing search for a Recreation Coordinator, Winterfest plans, Liberty Field signage, possible uses for Erickson-Landry Field, and the status of Flerra Playground.

-   **Boxborough Winterfest**: Winterfest plans are underway. The event will be held at Steele Farm on Saturday, January 30 from 1 p.m. to 4 p.m., with January 31 as the “rain” or inclement weather date. Commission members agreed that snow remains the main Winterfest “unknown.” In the past, rain or excessive snow has led to Winterfest being canceled.

Dirigible Brewing and a DJ are confirmed, and a popcorn machine and hot chocolate stand are planned. The commission discussed potential new activities, including craft tables, a guided conservation trail walk, and s'mores kits using a smokeless fire pit.

-   **Liberty Field:** A welcome sign at Liberty Field was proposed as a freestanding, double-sided sign featuring funding acknowledgments and rules. The sign will be similar to the one at Erickson-Landry Field and will be installed by the DPW in the spring.
-   **Erikson-Landry Field:** Commission members discussed future plans for Erickson-Landry Field, the 7.5 acres of land at 144 Summer Road that was gifted to the town by Ruth Landry in 2022 for recreational use. The Commission’s original plan for the land was to build a softball field, but earlier this year they learned that use is limited by access, wetlands, and protected wildlife (turtles).

Given the land's restrictions, commission members discussed an approach that would keep the land in as natural a state as possible while developing it into another desirable recreational spot in town. One member suggested drawing on inspiration from the proposed “pocket park” in West Concord, but on a larger scale, and incorporating different types of vegetation and garden areas with seating and a walking path. Another member suggested creating a “secret garden,” like the Secret Garden in Shrewsbury, which is a free, family-friendly woodland walking trail and outdoor sculpture garden that blends nature with whimsical, eclectic art.

-   **Flerra Playground:** The Flerra Playground awaits a certified inspection on Friday, November 20 before opening. A new ADA-compliant gravel path has been added, and the DPW will clear leftover concrete footings and the dirt mound from the site. A grand opening celebration is planned for the spring.

The RecCom’s next meeting will be on Tuesday, December 15 in hybrid format.
