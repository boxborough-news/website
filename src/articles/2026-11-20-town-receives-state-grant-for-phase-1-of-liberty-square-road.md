On November 18, the Town of Boxborough was notified that it has received a $150,000 “design grant” from the Massachusetts Department of Transportation (MassDOT) towards Phase 1 of the Liberty Square Road Redesign project.

According to Boxborough’s Director of Land Use and Permitting Alec Wade, these grant funds will cover design and engineering for Liberty Square Road from its intersection with Route 111 (Mass Ave) to its intersection with Sargent Road.

“Phase 1 refers to the first length of design and engineering. Future phases will include design and engineering of the second length and construction,” Wade explained to Boxborough News.

“$150,000 is the maximum award for design projects,” said Wade, “and this award is a testament to the community-driven approach and support for this project.”

Wade told Boxborough News that the Phase 1 design process is expected to include “a roadway survey (if digital files are insufficient), delineation of any resources (wetlands, historic, etc...), at least one community meeting if not two, ample engineering services, and a public presentation.” At the end of the Phase 1 design, Wade expects that the town will “either apply for construction funding for the first length or apply for design funding for the second length.”

The Boxborough Select Board authorized Wade to apply for Liberty Square Road redesign funding back in July. A few months earlier, in April 2026, a group of residents on Liberty Square Road and adjacent neighborhoods had approached Wade to explore “increased safety and connectivity” along Liberty Square Road, including the possibility of sidewalks and bike lanes. A survey conducted by the neighborhood group received more than 100 responses, the vast majority in favor of the project.

“Over the past several years, residents noticed a steady increase in both the speed and volume of traffic [on Liberty Square Road],” resident Geoff Osmun told Boxborough News in June. “The corridor used to be a comfortable road for walking, biking etc. but has increasingly felt unsafe. Dangerous near-misses have unfortunately become a frequent occurrence. Further, there is a huge missed opportunity for connectivity to the town center including the school, library, shops, Town Hall etc., as well as Liberty Fields.”

When asked for comment on the grant receipt, Osmun told Boxborough News, “we are looking forward to hosting more community engagement events in the spring to further develop a plan that suits a wide variety of needs.”

The town’s Phase 1 application was submitted through MassDOT’s “fully integrated application,” which evaluates eligibility for multiple funding programs, including the “Complete Streets” program. Wade explained that “MassDOT's decision to fund this award out of the Shared Streets and Spaces program…will preserve our eligibility for Complete Streets funding when it comes time to construct roadway improvements.”

Wade added, “I look forward to working with Geoff and the neighbors to make Liberty Square Road safer for all users. Residents interested in working with the Land Use team on future transportation projects should contact our office to learn more.”
