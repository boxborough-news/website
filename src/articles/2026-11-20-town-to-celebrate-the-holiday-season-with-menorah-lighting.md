On Friday, December 4, the Boxborough Public Celebrations and Ceremonies Committee (PCCC) will kick off its seasonal celebrations by lighting the town menorah at 6 p.m. in front of the Boxborough Fire Station at 502 Mass Ave. The next night, Saturday, December 5, the PCCC will continue the festivities with a double celebration beginning at 5 p.m. to light both the town menorah and the town evergreen tree – both at the Fire Station.

“The PCCC is very excited to host both the Menorah Lighting and Tree Lighting on the same night in December,” PCCC Chair Jennette Kollmann told Boxborough News. “This is a great opportunity for our community to come together and celebrate some of the diverse cultures in Boxborough.”

For the first night of the menorah lighting on Friday, December 4, the PCCC welcomes the entire community to join and enjoy cookies and cider, as well as a craft table for kids. This is the town’s third year hosting a town menorah lighting. The town’s menorah was designed, built, and donated to the town by the Kollmann-Klein family in 2024.

The festivities on Saturday, December 5 will begin with the lighting of the menorah and then proceed to the tree lighting. After the tree is lit, the community is invited to enjoy holiday tunes performed by the Blanchard BrassWind Ensemble and the Blanchard Chorus. The PCCC will host a craft table with ornaments and coloring pages and provide cookies and hot chocolate.

This year, the PCCC has invited representatives from Dunkin’ in the Mobil at 1425 Mass Ave to light the tree. Other recent tree lighting honorees include the Boxboro Regency, Two Friends Chocolates, and FreeBee Market.

“This year we celebrate the Boxborough Dunkin’ as our tree lighters,” said Kollmann. “They have been a staple in our small community, welcoming their regular customers by name, and they have been generously supporting our community events.”

Once the students’ music concludes, Santa Claus will arrive by fire truck to greet children and give out candy canes.

The PCCC also encourages community members joining the holiday lightings to bring a donation for the Maple Street Food Pantry (formerly known as the Acton Food Pantry); a collection bin will be at the fire station during the event.

On subsequent nights, from December 7-11, the PCCC will light the menorah at 6 p.m. each night. The PCCC invites local families to help lead the menorah lighting. To sign up, use this form: [https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeR0Qa1w9VPcFuZpTlhoyGxMWIC9-jdjikSOgtiBXsiArGVzg/viewform](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeR0Qa1w9VPcFuZpTlhoyGxMWIC9-jdjikSOgtiBXsiArGVzg/viewform).

Parking for the menorah lighting and tree lighting celebrations will be available across Mass Ave at the Blanchard Elementary School.
