Email us at [info@boxboroughnews.org](mailto:info@boxboroughnews.org) or [boxboroughnews@gmail.com](mailto:boxboroughnews@gmail.com). News tips, story ideas, corrections and announcements are always welcome.

## Get involved

We welcome:

- **Volunteer writers** interested in reporting on local events and issues
- **Columnists** with expertise or passions to share regularly with the community
- **Community groups** with announcements about projects and events

## Submission guidelines

**Send.** Email your article, or attach it as a Microsoft Word document, to [boxboroughnews@gmail.com](mailto:boxboroughnews@gmail.com) no later than one week before the Friday you want it published.

**Include.** A title identifying the subject and/or a possible headline, which edition(s) you want the item published in, and the contributor's name, address, phone, and email.

**Straight news.** At this time, we can only accept factual reporting, calendar items, and announcements. We cannot print editorials, letters to the editor, or opinion-only content.

**Features.** Stories of human interest, Boxborough profiles, and colorful experiences may depart from the straight news format.

**Photos (with captions) and art.** Encouraged, and should be in JPEG format.

**Editing.** Double-check the spelling of names and the accuracy of dates, days, and times. Submissions are subject to copyediting and rewriting for clarity and conciseness, following local and AP guidelines.

**Links.** Links to your website or event page are welcome. We suggest shortening them (using Bitly or TinyURL) or including a QR code for our print-only readers. If you need assistance, Boxborough News staff can help.
