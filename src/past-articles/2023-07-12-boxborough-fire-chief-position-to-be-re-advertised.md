The position of Boxborough fire chief, vacant since Chief Paul Fillebrown’s June 29 retirement, remains unfilled as the town administrator and Select Board plan a second round of recruitment efforts.

The position was initially advertised some weeks ago and three candidates were identified. However, one individual has since withdrawn his candidacy. Rather than decide between the other two, Town Administrator Michael Johns says he’s recommended to the Select Board that the job search be reopened with the goal of attracting a larger number of applicants.

“From the outset, I’ve been concerned, as has the Select Board, that our candidate pool was quite small,” Johns said. “The intention is to reevaluate our recruitment strategy and come back with a larger pool of qualified candidates.”

Johns said he is considering retaining a consultant specializing in fire chief recruitment “to help cast a wider net.”

Town officials now expect that October will be the soonest a new chief could be on board. In the meantime, Captain Shawn Gray will serve as interim chief. Johns said, “I’m confident that the captains, lieutenants, and the fine men and women that serve as Boxborough‘s EMTs and firefighters are tactically sound, and fully prepared to respond to all fire and medical emergencies.” Johns added, “We’ll rely heavily on Captain Gray, Captain Malinowski, Lieutenant Williams, and Lieutenant Nichols, who are all excellent leaders in the field, tactically sound, and fully capable of commanding the scene of a fire or medical emergency.”
