At their Zoom meeting July 10, the Planning Board saw revised plans for construction of French Brothers Boxborough’s three-unit trade shop at 100 Codman Hill Road, located in an aquifer protection district. The board also decided to seek opinions about what the town might like to see as amenities on Campanelli property, the former Cisco campus.

After explaining their latest plans for the Codman Hill Road project, representatives said they are seeking approval from the Conservation Commission on July 19 to discuss details such as the setback from a nearby stream and the site of a turtle nesting area. The PB asked for the commission’s response and for details regarding fencing, a holding tank, heating systems, drainage, and vehicle repair and dumpster locations.

Town Planner Alec Wade will draft conditions which might accompany possible board approval of the trade-shop project at a meeting August 21. Owner Michael French and Paul Kirchner of Stamski and McNary also agreed to respond to comments from the town’s engineering consultant Sue Carter.

Board member Cindy Markowitz expressed concern about the most recent plans for development of the Campanelli land, hoping the developers might consider further amenities for the town. The board asked Wade to seek suggestions about possible amenities from town boards and committees. Wade said the board’s next meeting with Campanelli representatives will likely take place in September or shortly thereafter.

Wade reported that 14 people attended the first Town Forum, a discussion of how a new layout of Sargent Memorial Library might best serve the town. He said library Director Peishan Bartley is asking everyone to respond to a survey, available here: [https://boxlib.org/about/library-redesign-project](https://boxlib.org/about/library-redesign-project). The next forum, about town streets, sidewalks, and signage, will take place July 26.

On July 31, the board will meet to discuss the proposed plan for 242-244 Adams Place. On August 21, the board will continue its meeting about 100 Codman Hill Road and will consider the 1414 Mass Ave definitive plan.
