After almost 11 years, Rev. Cindy Worthington-Berry will be leaving the United Church of Christ Boxborough on September 24 to begin a new ministry as senior pastor at First Congregational Church of Natick, UCC.

Rev. Cindy’s message to her congregation expressed her gratitude for her time here, writing “I make this decision knowing UCC Boxborough is in a place of strength. We have accomplished so much together, including authentic worship, renovated buildings, and interfaith partnerships. We’ve embraced a ministry of Open Doors, and become a key resource in our community. I am confident a new pastor will help UCC Boxborough continue to strengthen its mission and its ministry, for parishioners and the world alike.”

The church and community celebrated Rev. Cindy’s tenth anniversary in Boxborough last fall, with recognition of her community contributions coming from Sen. Jamie Eldridge, Rep. Dan Sena, and other community leaders. In 2021, Rev. Cindy was the first recipient of the Acton-Boxborough United Way’s Mike Tobia Award, which recognizes “an outstanding community leader dedicated to serving residents' needs with compassion and dignity, while fostering a sense of belonging for all.”

Next steps for the church involve setting up an interim leader for a year or more who will help oversee a search process for a settled pastor. Cindy will leave behind an inspiring legacy of commitment to the community at large as well to her congregation. Farewell opportunities are expected to be held in August and early September.

Her email signature quotation reminds us all, “Never doubt that a small group of thoughtful, committed people can change the world. Indeed, it is the only thing that ever has. - Margaret Mead.”
