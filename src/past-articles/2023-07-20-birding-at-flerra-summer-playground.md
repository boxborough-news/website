Becky Harris led an enthusiastic group of birders at Flerra Summer Playground on Tuesday, July 11. Sponsored by the Boxborough Recreation Commission, Flerra Summer Playground runs four week-long sessions through the month of July for kids in grades K-7. The program has been a community fixture since the 1970s. If you’re driving near Flerra Meadows this week, watch out for kids walking and biking to and from Flerra Summer Playground.
