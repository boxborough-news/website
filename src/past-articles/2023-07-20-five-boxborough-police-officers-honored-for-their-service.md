At a luncheon event held at Town Hall on July 11, five members of the Boxborough Police Department were recognized and honored for their service to the town:

Chief of Police John Szewczyk was appointed on April 10, 2023, after serving as interim police chief since November 2022.

Szewczyk is a graduate of Trinity College and holds an MBA in Management and International Business from the University of Connecticut. He is also a graduate of the Southern Police Institute’s Command Officers Development Course.

Szewczyk is recognized as a progressive law enforcement leader and a strong advocate of community policing, diversity programs, restorative justice, and improving the interactions between law enforcement and communities that have been historically disenfranchised. Chief Szewczyk is extremely proud to lead the dedicated members of the Boxborough Police Department.

Lt. Steven Patriarca has been a police officer for more than 20 years. He has been with the Boxborough Police Department since 2019. He has a Master’s Degree from Lasell University in Criminal Justice/Emergency and Crisis Management. Patriarca had held the position of detective for the last two years, developed a drone program, and played a large role during the pandemic as the Emergency Operations Center Coordinator.

Sgt. Philip Gath has been with Boxborough Police Department for 13 years. He holds a Master’s Degree in Criminal Justice from the University of Massachusetts Lowell. He has served as the local school resource officer for the past seven years. Gath has completed numerous training programs and is a recipient of the FBI's LEEDA Trilogy leadership training award.

Det. Robert Arakelian has been with Boxborough for more than seven years. He is a graduate of Syracuse University with a Bachelor’s Degree in Public Policy. In addition to his patrol duties, Det. Arakelian has served as liaison to social services agencies: working with residents suffering from mental health, substance abuse, and domestic violence related issues. Detective Arakelian has attended a multitude of specialized training classes that have prepared him for his new role as detective.

Officer Safaraz Mushtaque was sworn in as a Boxborough Police Officer on June 13, 2023, after working with the Worcester State University Police Department, and on Cape Cod with both the Falmouth and Truro police departments. He is currently working towards completing a degree at the University of Massachusetts Boston. Officer Mushtaque says he is proud to become a member of the Boxborough Police Department and is looking forward to engaging with the community.

Honored at the event, which was hosted by Town Administrator Michael Johns (far right), were (from left): Lieut. Steven Patriarca, Sgt. Philip Gath, Det. Robert Arakelian, Officer Safaraz Mushtaque, and Chief John Szewczyk.
