The Recreation Commission reports that the “soft” reopening of Liberty Field this fall will include the playground, the adult fitness area, and basketball, tennis, and pickleball courts. These new areas will all open to the public on the same date, likely in September. The rest of Liberty Field will remain fenced off until construction is complete next year.

Boxborough News previously reported that two areas of Liberty Field, the playground and the adult fitness area, were expected to open by September 1. This is no longer accurate. Instead, the playground and fitness area will open at the same time as the basketball, tennis, and pickleball courts, at a later date.
