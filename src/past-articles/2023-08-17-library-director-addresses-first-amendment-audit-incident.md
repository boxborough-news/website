Dear Readers:

I am writing to inform you of an incident that happened last Wednesday.

At a little before 2 p.m. last Wednesday, August 9, two people came into the library to conduct a "First Amendment audit." These "audits" are conducted by members of the general public with the intention to “test” the government’s response to someone filming in public spaces, and to see if government employees will intervene and thus violate the individual’s First Amendment rights. The encounter is usually then uploaded to social media channels to solicit response and to be monetized.

At the "visit" that we received, the "auditors" were greeted by the Youth Services Librarian, Heather. She politely asked them to abide by the library's photography policy, and not include children in their recording. The request became a point of contention. Eventually, the situation was resolved and the "auditors" went on to finish filming and left.

The following day, they uploaded their recordings with a very misleading tagline. Since then, we have received harassing phone calls and emails. You might also have seen disparaging comments on our social media channels.

None of this, I'm happy to report, are from members of our own community who know us best. And none of it would stop us from continuing to provide library services to you.

We are firm believers in the fundamental rights that are protected by the Constitution. We are also firm believers of kindness, compassion, common decency, and respecting each other's boundaries and privacy. The library should be a safe and welcoming place for everyone and anyone. It is our goal to make it so.

If you have any questions about this, my door is always open. Thank you for reading.

Peishan T. Bartley

Library Director
