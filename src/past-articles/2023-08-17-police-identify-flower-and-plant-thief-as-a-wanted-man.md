On Saturday, August 5, Boxborough police confronted a man identified as having stolen flowering plants from Summer Road, Flagg Hill Road, and Mass. Ave. homes. Chief John Szewczyk reported that after learning the man was wanted for more serious crimes in two other states, officers arrested him and later that day sent him to out-of-state authorities.

That morning, an early victim of the thefts, Fran Spayne, President of Summerfield Condo Association, had given the police a motion-activated camera video of a man carrying a stolen plant from the Summer Road condos at 6 a.m. Spayne recognized him, and police identified him as a resident of Liberty Square Road where they confronted him and discovered stolen plants on his back porch.

A month earlier, on July 4, Spayne reported that her Mother’s Day gift of a large planter was taken from her porch. During the next three weeks, she learned that plants had been stolen from five or six neighboring homes, but the victims were reluctant to report the thefts. She said, “I was extremely uncomfortable knowing someone was roaming around here at night, and that led me to install the camera.”

In late July, Boxborough News told Lt. Steven Patriarca about Spayne’s concerns about thefts that victims had not reported to the police. Patriarca also found that no plant-theft reports had reached Boxborough’s four bordering towns.

Searching for specific information, Patriarca, Sgt. Philip Gath, and Officers Amanda Flower and Maxwell Bressi visited local victims to learn more. When the thief was identified, Dispatcher Fiona Barry uncovered his out-of-state warrants.

Patriarca said he thanked Spayne profusely for her initiative. He also wanted citizens to know, “If you see suspicious activity, say something. You are not bothering us. We really want to know how to protect you and our community.”

Susan Lemere, the department’s co-response clinician, reported that after Bressi talked with one victim who was particularly frightened by the repeated loss of large plants, Bressi gave her a flowering plant, a gift from him and the Boxborough Police.
