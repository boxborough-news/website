At the Boxborough fire station, firefighter/ EMT Stephen Wright rings a bell, a tradition signifying that a firefighter has died in the line of duty. The occasion was a solemn ceremony for firefighters, police officers, and townspeople to observe the 22nd anniversary of the tragic events that occurred at the World Trade Center, the Pentagon, and rural Pennsylvania on September 11, 2001.
