Adam Klein, Chair of the A-B School Committee, has released a memo detailing the School Committee’s anticipated agenda items for meetings through January 2024.

The School Committee typically meets on the first and third Thursdays each month “to manage the general business of the school district as well as hear presentations on district goals, major initiatives, and make decisions related to the next year’s budget,” writes Klein. “Some of these topics are items that may be of interest to Acton and Boxborough community members, both those with children in the district, and those without.”

- October 5 Presentations: multilingual education update, budget development schedule.
- October 19 Presentations: Health Insurance Trust update, High School course leveling update.
- November 2 Presentations: enrollment and class size update, current trends in open enrollment, budget guidelines. Planned vote on budget timeline.
- November 16 Presentations: District Goal #1 - social emotional learning, High School course leveling recommendations, finance update.
- December 7 Presentations: District Goal #3 - culturally responsive practices, School Resource Officer subcommittee recommendation.
- January 4 Presentations: District Goal #2 - equitable outcomes for students, 2024-25 school calendar. Planned vote on High School course leveling.
- January 18 Presentations: FY25 Budget Presentation #1 – Superintendent’s Preliminary Budget. Planned vote on School Resource Officer program.

Dates and topics are subject to change. School Committee meeting agendas are posted the Friday prior to each Thursday meeting and can be accessed at abschools.org.

School Committee meetings are held in the ABRSD Administration Building auditorium at 15 Charter Road in Acton. Members of the public are invited to attend the meetings in person, watch live at https://www.youtube.com/actontv1, or watch previously recorded meetings on-demand on ActonTV at https://actontv.org/on-demand/government.
