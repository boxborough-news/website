Did you see the bright yellow trash bags along Route 111 last weekend? On Saturday October 28, members and friends of the Boxborough Minutemen Company fanned out along Route 111 for their bi-annual highway clean-up. The Minutemen filled over two dozen bags of trash, which were picked up by the Massachusetts Department of Transportation for proper disposal. For more information about the Boxborough Minutemen Company, visit [facebook.com/BoxboroughMinutemenCompany](https://www.facebook.com/BoxboroughMinutemenCompany/) or [boxboroughminutemen.org](https://boxboroughminutemen.org/).
