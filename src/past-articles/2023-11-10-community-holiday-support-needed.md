Boxborough Community Services is collecting gift cards for Boxborough families who need help with groceries and gifts for the holidays. Gift card donations for Market Basket, Roche Brothers, Target, Walmart, and other stores can be dropped off at Town Hall in an envelope addressed to Wendy Trinks, Community Services Coordinator by December 8, 2023. Questions? Contact Trinks at [wtrinks@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:wtrinks@boxborough-ma.gov) or (978) 264-1735.
