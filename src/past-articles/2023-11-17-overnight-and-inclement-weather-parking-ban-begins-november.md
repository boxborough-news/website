The Boxborough Police Department reminds residents and visitors that the town’s annual overnight and inclement weather street parking ban is in effect starting November 15 and ending March 31. During that time, on-street parking is prohibited from 1 a.m. until 6 a.m. and at all hours when inclement weather is forecast. Unattended vehicles can obstruct snow removal operations and may be towed. Additionally, no overnight or weekend parking is allowed at any town-owned facilities without permission.
