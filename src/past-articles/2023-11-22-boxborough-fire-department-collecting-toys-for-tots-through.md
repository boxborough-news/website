The Boxborough Fire Department will once again collect new, unwrapped toys for the Toys for Tots program. Donations are already being accepted. The last day for donations is December 9. There is no flexibility with this date. Toys may be dropped off at the Fire Department.

Toys for Tots is a program run by the United States Marine Corps Reserve, which distributes toys to children whose parents cannot afford to buy them Christmas gifts.

The Fire Department appreciates the continued generosity of the Boxborough community. Questions may be directed to Captain Jason Malinowski at [jmalinowski@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:jmalinowski@boxborough-ma.gov).
