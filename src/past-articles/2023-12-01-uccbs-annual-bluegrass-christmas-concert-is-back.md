Many people consider the annual Christmas In New England (CINE) concert in the UCCB Sanctuary to be an important part of their holiday season. We’re thrilled to have the CINE performers back! Join us in the Sanctuary on Saturday, December 9 at 7:30 p.m. for a fabulous Christmas tradition at the UCCB! Doors open at 7:00 pm. To learn more, visit [www.christmasinnewengland.net](http://www.christmasinnewengland.net/). There will be baked goods and other snacks during intermission. Concert held at United Church of Christ, Congregational in Boxborough at 723 Mass. Ave. (Route 111).
