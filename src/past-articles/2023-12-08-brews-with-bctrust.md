Join the Boxborough Conservation Trust on Thursday, December 14 from 7-9 p.m.for an informal gathering at Dirigible Brewing, 24 Porter Rd., Littleton. Snacks will be provided; craft beer and sodas and coffee are available for purchase. All are welcome to join and share in some winter cheer!
