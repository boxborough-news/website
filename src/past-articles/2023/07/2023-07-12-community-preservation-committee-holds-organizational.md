The Boxborough Community Preservation Committee held its 2023-2024 organizational meeting on Thursday July 6.

The first key item on the agenda was discussion and approval of the timeline for projects that will be seeking FY 2025 Community Preservation Act funding at the May 2024 Annual Town Meeting. The approved timeline has preliminary applications due October 5, 2023, final applications due November 2, and the public hearing on projects to take place on December 7, 2023.

The second key item was discussion and approval of the application procedures for applying for funding and the preliminary and final application forms for such projects. These procedures and application forms will be available on the town website shortly.

The next meeting of the committee and the informational meeting for potential applicants will take place on Thursday September 7 at Boxborough Town Hall at 7:30 p.m.
