On the afternoon of June 29, retiring Boxborough Fire Chief Paul Fillebrown Jr. poses with his firefighters for a farewell photo before being honored with a last day on the job ride home in Engine 62, the town’s new fire engine. Fillebrown, who began his firefighting career in Ayer 38 years ago, had been Boxborough Fire Chief since 2019.
