Brand new playground equipment has been installed at Liberty Field. Boxborough’s Recreation Commission reports that the playground and the adult fitness area at Liberty Field are expected to be open for public use by September 1. Construction on the remainder of the property is expected to be complete by late spring of 2024. Stay tuned for details on a ribbon-cutting celebration.
