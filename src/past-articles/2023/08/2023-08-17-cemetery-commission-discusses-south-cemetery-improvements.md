The Cemetery Commission met on August 10 to discuss the continued efforts of the Boxborough Department of Public Works to improve the grounds at the South Cemetery. The DPW has re-set low and flat cemetery markers and taken down dead trees to be replaced sometime in the fall. Commission member Jim Moss commented that small changes like this add up over time.

The commission is also working with the co-superintendent to convert written documents to an electronic system which tracks information about who is buried where, date of burial, and whether the burial was a traditional burial or a cremation. The commission has also been asked to consider naming the roadways within the cemetery to make it easier to find a specific plot.

The commission continued to discuss and research the requirements for green burials.

The next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, September 14, at Town Hall.
