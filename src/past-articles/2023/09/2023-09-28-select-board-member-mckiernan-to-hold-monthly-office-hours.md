Select Board member Dave McKiernan has announced that he will hold monthly office hours at Town Hall. His office hours will be held the first Wednesday of each month from 10 a.m. to 11 a.m. in the Morse-Hilberg room at Town Hall, starting Wednesday, October 4. During his office hours, the public is welcome to speak with McKiernan in person or by phone at (978) 264-1714.

McKiernan decided to hold office hours “to follow through on a commitment made during the League of Women Voters candidate forum. Office hours may be increased based on resident interest.” McKiernan was elected to the Select Board in May 2023.
