The Acton-Boxborough community is invited to join a Walk Against Hate on October 15, from 2 p.m. to 3:30 p.m**.** sponsored by Congregation Beth Elohim and the Anti-Defamation League. The one-mile route will begin at the high school's Regan Gym and will end at Gardner Field in West Acton. All are welcome to participate. To register for the walk, sign up at [https://www.bethelohim.org/event/walk-against-hate.](https://www.bethelohim.org/event/walk-against-hate)
