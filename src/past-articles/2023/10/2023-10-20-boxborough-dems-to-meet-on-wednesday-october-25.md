The Boxborough Democratic Town Committee will conduct its monthly meeting on Wednesday, October 25 at 7 p.m. via Zoom. All registered Democrats are welcome. Agenda includes nominations for membership on the committee. Nominated candidates’ names will appear on the presidential primary ballot on March 5, 2024. For more information about DTC membership or to obtain the Zoom link for the meeting, email [boxborough.dtc@gmail.com,](mailto:boxborough.dtc@gmail.com) or visit [boxboroughdems.org](http://boxboroughdems.org/) or [facebook.com/BoxboroughDTC](http://facebook.com/BoxboroughDTC).
