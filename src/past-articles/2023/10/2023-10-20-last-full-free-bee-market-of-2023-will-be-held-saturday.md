The last Free Bee event of 2023 will be held Saturday, October 21 from 10:30 a.m. to noon. One of the Free Bee organizers, Heather Fleming wrote, “It's truly been a great season of sharing, connecting, collaborating, and reducing food waste and insecurity in our communities. We can't wait to welcome you back for many of the full market features you've come to enjoy.”

Free Bee is held at 30 Middle Road, Boxborough, on the lawn of the church. Visitors to this week’s market can expect the usual “rescued” food as well as miscellaneous pantry items and baked goods, a Halloween costume table, crafts, used books, and so much more!

Come enjoy performances by the Acton-Boxborough High School's Proscenium Circus program, singing excerpts from their upcoming musical, The Music Man. The Nashoba Valley Concert Band will also be performing live from 10:45 a.m.-11:45 a.m.

A free special gardening seminar honoring late well-known Boxborough resident Jeanne Kangas will be held after the Free Bee full community market from 11:45 a.m. to 1 p.m. in the Grange room at Boxborough Town Hall. The speaker will be Kate Despres of Daisy Hill Farm. This event is sponsored by Jeanne’s family. RSVP here: [https://bit.ly/3RMtByj.](https://bit.ly/3RMtByj)
