On Monday October 23, the Boxborough Select Board will hold a public hearing at 7 p.m. in the Grange Meeting Room at Town Hall on the adoption of a Residential Factor, thereby determining the percentage of the tax burden borne by each class of real and personal property for FY2024. After a presentation by the Town Assessor, the Select Board will vote on whether to establish different rates for different classes of property or tax all classes of property at the same rate.
