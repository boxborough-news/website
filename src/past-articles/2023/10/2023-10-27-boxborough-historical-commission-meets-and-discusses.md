The main focus of the Boxborough Historical Commission meeting on October 19 was a review of the various historically related projects that are underway in the town. The first area discussed was ongoing maintenance and future capital work at the Boxborough Museum, and planned restoration work on the 1784 Levi Wetherbee Farmhouse at Steele Farm and the Steele Farm ice house. The commission was pleased that some progress is being made in all areas.

In addition to physical projects, the commission reviewed four efforts that are underway to recognize, by way of signage, two historic houses and two locations in the town. Finally the commission confirmed a planned winter presentation on one of Boxborough’s outstanding 19th century citizens.

The next meeting of the commission will take place on Thursday, December 14 at the Boxborough Museum, 575 Middle Road, starting at 7 p.m.
