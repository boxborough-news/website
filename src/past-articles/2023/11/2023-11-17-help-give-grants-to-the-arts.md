Join the Acton Boxborough Cultural Council. The council was established to promote the arts in the community, and one of its big tasks is to administer the Massachusetts Cultural Council grant funds. Each year, arts and music organizations apply to the council asking for money, and the council has to decide how best to distribute the funds it was allocated.

The committee is made up of members from both Boxborough and Acton, and they are currently looking for new members. For more information, check their website at [www.actonboxboroughculturalcouncil.org](http://www.actonboxboroughculturalcouncil.org/) or email [abccinformation@gmail.com](mailto:abccinformation@gmail.com).
