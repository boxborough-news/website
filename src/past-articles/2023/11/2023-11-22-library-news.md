The next five Movie Mondays at the library will be the last. There was great attendance in the past, but since streaming services have become more popular, attendance has dwindled. And, it’s no secret that the chairs in the meeting room are not the coziest or most comfortable. Thus, the library will be drawing the curtain on Movie Mondays at the end of 2023, and if there is interest in the future, they will happily restart it. For movie descriptions visit [boxlib.org](https://boxlib.org/news-events/news/movie-mondays). Free and open to the public, no registration is required.
