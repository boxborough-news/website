The first night of Chanukah is Thursday, December 7 and we have a fun week of holiday programming for the entire family.

- Friday 12/8: Kabbalat Shabbat with Chanukah music. Join us for some holiday singing with our Friday night services.
- Sunday 12/10: Chanukah party 11:30-1. We’ll have dreidels to play with and latkes (and pizza) to eat, plus crafts and activities for the whole family. Sponsored by the CBE Community Engagement and Programming committees.
- Thursday 12/14: Celebrate the 8th night at the downtown Maynard Menorah lighting. Friends from all towns are invited for our outdoor Menorah lighting, singing, hot cocoa, and sufganiot. *Sufganiot* is a round [jelly doughnut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jelly_doughnut) eaten around the world on the [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judaism) festival of Chanukah. The doughnut is deep-fried, injected with [jam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruit_preserves) or [custard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Custard), and then topped with [powdered sugar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powdered_sugar). Sponsored by the Congregation Beth Elohim school committee.
