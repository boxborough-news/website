On Sunday, December 17, a parade of Boxborough Fire and Police Department vehicles will escort Santa Claus through the streets of Boxborough. The parade will kick off at 9 a.m. at the Kendall Road cul-de-sac, and will proceed as detailed below. It will stop at the Liberty Fields Parking Lot at approximately 9:50 a.m. and will end at Flerra Field at approximately 11 a.m.

Along the route, Fire and Police personnel will be collecting donations for the Acton Food Pantry. Please direct any questions in advance of the event to Captain Jason Malinowski at [jmalinowski@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:jmalinowski@boxborough-ma.gov). Please note that all arrival times are approximate.
