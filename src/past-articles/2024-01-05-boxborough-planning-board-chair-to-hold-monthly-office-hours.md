Planning Board Chair Mark White will begin holding office hours the first Wednesday of each month from 10 to 11 a.m. in the Morse-Hilberg room at Town Hall, beginning Wednesday, Jan. 3. White’s office hours and location are the same as those of Select Board member Dave McKiernan.

“I applaud Dave for taking the initiative to make himself available to the public in such a way,” said White. “Hopefully, we can make it just a bit easier for folks to communicate with us by doing it at the same time and place.” White indicated that he will add dates or extend the hours if there is interest from the public.
