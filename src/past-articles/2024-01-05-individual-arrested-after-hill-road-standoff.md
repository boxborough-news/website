On Wednesday, January 3, the Boxborough Police Department responded to a call in the area of 1195 Hill Road. Police Chief John Szewczyk issued the following statement:

“After a four-hour standoff with a barricaded individual, a successful joint operation between the Boxborough Police, Boxborough Fire, Stow Fire/Rescue, Acton Fire and the Massachusetts State Police Special Tactics and Operations Team led to the arrest of an individual for Domestic Assault and Battery. The scene has been cleared and Hill Road has been reopened. All residents have been allowed to return to their homes. No injuries during the operation were reported. The call originally came into 911 at 9:41 AM on 1/03/2024.”
