On Thursday, January 4 the Community Preservation Committee continued its December 7 public hearing to consider applications for Community Preservation Act funds and decide which CPA-funded projects should move forward as warrant articles at the 2024 Town Meeting.

After discussion, the committee decided not to recommend the Housing Board’s “Homeowner Opportunity for Preservation Extension” program (HOPE) for homeowners whose deed-restricted homes need repairs. While the committee supported the concept, it felt that details of how the program would work needed more definition, especially the type and amount of staff support needed from town hall. The public hearing was then closed.

In addition to the usual administrative warrant article, the committee will be bringing five warrant articles to the May 2024 town meeting: the Housing Board’s Rental Assistance Program ($44k), Conservation Trust Fund ($10K), Blanchard School Playground ($171K), community garden fence at Flerra ($6K), and the restoration of headstones and stonework at Boxborough’s cemeteries ($9.5K).

There was a brief discussion of the impact on the CPA financial worksheet when the FY2024 tax surcharge amount is finalized. The clerk was authorized to change the numbers in the administrative warrant article as needed.

The next meeting of the CPC is February 1, 2024.
