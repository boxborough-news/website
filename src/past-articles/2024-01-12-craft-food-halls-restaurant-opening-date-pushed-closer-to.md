Local restaurant chain Craft Food Halls has pushed the opening date for its Boxborough location at 500 Beaver Brook Road “closer to mid-2024.” In August 2023, a representative of Craft Food Halls had indicated to the Boxborough Select Board that they were aiming to open the new restaurant by December 2023.

Responding to an inquiry from Boxborough News about the status of the restaurant, a representative from Craft Food Halls stated, “We are still waiting on renovations and maintenance for our Boxborough location. Unfortunately there is no estimated time of completion yet, but it will be closer [to] mid 2024.”

Boxborough Town Planner Alec Wade commented, “On December 5th, I had the opportunity to walk the restaurant with the construction team and our building commissioner. The space is well under construction and shaping up to be a beautiful venue, rich with space for community use and leisure. It's unfortunate, but not uncommon that their opening date has been delayed. Their business model relies in part on daytime transactions generated by the businesses who cohabitate The Park; and as businesses in the park continue to grow their operation and outfit their space, I'm confident we are rapidly approaching an opening date for Craft Food Hall.”

According to a representative who appeared before the Boxborough Select Board in August 2023 at a hearing on the restaurant’s All Alcoholic Beverages License, Craft Food Halls will serve breakfast, lunch, and dinner to both office park employees and the general public. The food will be prepared using the “sous vide” method (low-temperature, long-time cooking), and alcoholic beverage offerings will include a “beer wall” with 30 taps. The 7,000 square foot restaurant will feature work areas, games, and outdoor space with fire pits.

Craft Food Halls currently operates restaurants in Boston, Lexington, Marlborough, Waltham, and Wellesley.
