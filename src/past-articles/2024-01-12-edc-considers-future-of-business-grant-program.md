At its meeting on January 4, the Boxborough Economic Development Committee considered the future of the Business Grant Program, a program funded by Boxborough’s American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) appropriation that provides up to $20,000 in matching funds as a grant to small businesses that meet the requirements of the program.

The application period for “Round 1” of the grant program closed on January 3, 2024. There are potentially $40,000 in remaining funds, depending on the final award amounts made in Round 1. Committee members agreed that the program should continue for a “Round 2” and discussed whether and how the dollar amount of the grant awards might change for Round 2. The Committee expects to discuss further at its next meeting on January 18.

Town Planner Alec Wade reported that prior to the application period closing, his office received two additional applications. The EDC will hold public hearings to consider those two new applications, as well as the application from Oscar’s Burritos received November 2023, for late January or early February.

The Committee also discussed next steps on the Town Center Revitalization Project, funded by a $46,950 community planning grant from the state to create a conceptual plan and alternatives to a town center-style development. The consulting company hired by the town, Tighe & Bond, expects to conduct a day-long community “charette,” a tool for gathering feedback from the public, and work with a stakeholder group to include members of invited town boards and committees. The Committee discussed the importance of publicizing the “charette” in order to get input from a diverse group of Boxborough residents with different backgrounds and perspectives.

Chair Rich Guzzardi also introduced the town’s newly hired intern, JP LoGiudice, who is primarily tasked with compiling a “business database” of all businesses located in Boxborough. A recent college graduate, LoGiudice will also assist Town Planner Alec Wade on a number of projects intended to strengthen the relationship between the town and Boxborough’s business community.
