At its meeting on January 4, Chair Christoper Hydak of the Steele Farm Advisory Committee reported positive progress on the committee’s capital projects. A contract has been awarded for the repair of the farmhouse roof and chimney as the first part of the Phase II effort to preserve the envelope of the 1784 Levi Wetherbee farmhouse at Steele Farm. Work is expected to start soon but will be staged not to conflict with Winterfest on January 27. A request for proposal for the restoration of the ice house has been issued and bids are expected shortly.

After review, the committee decided not to submit an article for painting the exterior of the barn for the May 2024 town meeting. This will stay on the capital plan for a future year.

The committee also discussed work that is underway to create an updated charter for the committee as well as farm maintenance being done by the Boxborough DPW.

One item of new business was a proposal brought to the committee by two Boxborough residents related to installing a disc golf course at Steele Farm. The first step will be to determine if this is allowed under the conservation restriction. If so, more information will be gathered and a second discussion will take place.

The committee’s next meeting will take place on Thursday February 8 at the Boxborough Museum, 575 Middle Road, starting at 7 p.m.
