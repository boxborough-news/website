At its meeting on January 2, Boxborough’s Water Resources Committee continued to wrestle with the scope and deliverables of a Request for Proposal to assist with a town-wide water resources plan. Although the Committee indicated an intent to finalize the RFP during the meeting, it was ultimately decided that a revised draft would be prepared for review and further discussion at the next meeting. Members discussed the “phases” of the project, cost breakdowns, the framing of the RFP, and the qualifications of the consultant.

The budget for the project is $50,000. WRC Chair Les Fox briefly discussed how the funding for the RFP has recently changed. The WRC originally sought and received approval to use certain funds from prior town meetings for the project, but recently discovered that $15,000 of that funding was no longer available. The WRC worked with the Town Administrator to identify other funds to cover the $15,000 shortfall, so the WRC will not need to go back to town meeting in order to undertake this project.

The committee also discussed the potential future Massachusetts Water Resources Authority (MWRA) water system expansion to the MetroWest area. Fox characterized the massive coordination required for such a project as a “challenge” and clarified that the timeframe for this type of project is “decades.” Boxborough and surrounding towns (e.g., Acton, Littleton, Maynard, Ayer, Groton) are staying involved in ongoing conversations, but specific next steps are “unclear.”
