At its meeting on January 10, the Boxborough Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Committee established five goals for 2024: (1) Lend a diverse lens to town activities; (2) Develop a process for addressing anti-bias complaints; (3) Support employees with DEI needs; (4) Conduct feasibility study to determine ways to provide DEI services to the town, including exploring partnerships with other towns; and (5) Amplify and acknowledge underrepresented voices.

The DEIC has openings for new members. Boxborough residents interested in serving on this (or any other) committee are encouraged to fill out the “Resident Interest Form” on the town website: [https://bit.ly/3tXE4O3](https://bit.ly/3tXE4O3).

The DEIC was formed by the Boxborough Select Board in October 2020. The DEIC Charge states, “Diversity, Equity and Inclusion (DEI) have been brought to the forefront of public awareness and Boxborough has made a commitment to create and foster a welcoming, inclusive and equitable town for all residents and guests regardless of their age, gender, orientation, race, ethnicity, mental health, physical ability, religion or socio-economic status. In this commitment, the Select Board is forming the Diversity, Equity and Inclusion Committee to help research and propose changes to the respective Boards and Committees who oversee the respective areas where changes are proposed.”
