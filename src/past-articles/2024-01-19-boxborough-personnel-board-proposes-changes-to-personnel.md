At its meetings on December 12 and January 9, the Personnel Board discussed proposed revisions to the Personnel Plan (the “Plan”), a cost-of-living adjustment for non-union employees for FY2025, and ratings for several Library positions.

Proposed revisions to the “Plan” for non-union reserve officers and the police lieutenant working private details explain how a private detail is paid by differentiating work performed Monday through Friday from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. compared to work performed at other times. Current language provides payment in four-hour increments. The proposed language provides a minimum of four-hour increments for work performed more than eight hours. The change aligns the compensation for per diem officers with that of full time officers.

The board also considered changes to the sick leave portion of the “Plan.” Sick leave is currently accrued monthly and calculated based on hours worked. Rather than requiring employees to earn sick time benefits throughout the year, the board discussed front loading sick time based on hours worked at the beginning of each year or the anniversary date of an employee’s hiring. This change in procedure would make it easier to administer the sick leave benefit; the change is still under consideration and has not been finalized.

Vacation time is also accrued and prorated based on hours worked. The board does not plan to change the accrual process but may clarify the language as to how vacation time is calculated for regular reduced-hour and regular part-time employees. The clarification is still being considered.

All proposed revisions to the “Plan” will be presented for voter approval at Annual Town Meeting in May 2024.
