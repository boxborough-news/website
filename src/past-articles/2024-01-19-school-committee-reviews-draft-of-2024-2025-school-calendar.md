At its meeting January 4, the School Committee reviewed a draft academic calendar for the 2024-2025 school year. As drafted, the school year would start on August 28 (grades K-7, 9) and August 29 (grades 8, 10-12), and the last day of school would be June 18. School Committee members discussed concerns about the number of school holidays on the calendar and whether or not to revisit the current approach to holidays. The Committee will vote on the calendar at its next meeting. The draft calendar can be found here: [https://bit.ly/3O2eONm](https://bit.ly/3O2eONm).

The Committee voted unanimously to approve the High School “course leveling” plan to be implemented by the administration as the budget permits. The course leveling plan will “reduce course levels” to give students “more equitable access to advanced-level coursework” and “a broader range of learning and social environments.” ABRSD Superintendent Peter Light noted that the proposed course leveling plan may need to be paused or delayed because of the impact of potential budget cuts.

The Committee also voted unanimously to approve the policy on Student Use of Electronic Communication Devices, which is intended to provide district-level guidance for the schools as they each develop policies and procedures on electronic communication devices.

Superintendent Light also gave an update on how the district has been working through the budget challenges, including engagement with teachers and parents. He also mentioned that the district decided to delay the kindergarten information session and enrollment process (that typically begins mid-January) because of budget uncertainty. The kindergarten information session is expected to be held in late February instead. School Committee Chair Adam Klein announced that the Committee will hold a “budget Saturday” workshop on February 10.
