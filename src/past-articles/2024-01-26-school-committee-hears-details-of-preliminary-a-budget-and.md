At its meeting on January 18, the ABRSD School Committee heard a presentation from Superintendent Peter Light on the preliminary FY2025 budget.

The district is facing a $9.5M funding deficit, driven primarily by personnel, special education, and health insurance costs. The Town of Acton is preparing for a “Proposition 2 ½” override vote (to increase taxes beyond the state levy limit). Superintendent Light presented two preliminary budgets: the “A” budget, to use if the override passes, and the “B” budget, to use if the override does not pass.

The “A” budget (if override passes) is a $2 million reduction from a level-services budget. While this “A” budget requires cuts, it would allow the district to “maintain strategic direction for most initiatives.” This budget would eliminate 25 staff positions. It would also raise tuition for kindergarten and Pre-K and increase athletic and activity fees. Overall, the “A” budget would require $7.5 million in additional funding for schools. Boxborough’s total assessment would be $16 million, a 15.59% increase from last year.

The “B” budget (if override fails) is a $9.5 million reduction from a level-services budget. Superintendent Light emphasized that the impact of this “B” budget would be “devastating” to the district. It would eliminate 90 staff positions and increase class sizes beyond the maximums set by school committee guidelines, as well as increase tuition and fees. This budget would require no additional funding for schools. Boxborough’s total assessment would be $14.8M, a 6.7% increase from last year.

School Committee members expressed concerns about the impact of the “B” budget on students and urged Acton voters to pass an override so that the district can move forward with the “A” budget. Committee members asked Superintendent Light to explore further cuts to the “A” budget to help make it “more palatable.” This could include potential “structural changes” to further reduce costs, like eliminating transportation for students living within a certain radius of each school or eliminating open enrollment for the elementary schools.

The School Committee will hear another presentation on the budget at its next meeting on February 1. The School Committee will hold a budget workshop on February 10, and expects to vote on the budget at its March 21 meeting. The Town of Acton’s override vote will take place in April and the resulting budget will go to Acton and Boxborough Town Meetings for approval. For more information about the budget, visit [https://bit.ly/47ICKfR](https://bit.ly/47ICKfR).

At the end of the meeting, the School Committee voted (7 in favor, 2 opposed) to continue the School Resource Officer Program, authorize the Superintendent to develop a new Memorandum of Understanding with the Acton Police Department, and reconsider the SRO program in FY2025. The Committee also voted to accept the 2024-2025 school calendar as proposed.
