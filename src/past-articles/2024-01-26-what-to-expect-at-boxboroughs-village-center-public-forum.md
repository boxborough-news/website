On Saturday, February 10, Boxborough will hold a forum at Town Hall to gather public input for the town’s Village Center Revitalization project. The project is funded by a Community Planning Grant secured by Boxborough’s Economic Development Committee. As the project develops, more details will become available at [https://bit.ly/Village-Center.](https://bit.ly/Village-Center)

Boxborough News reached out to EDC Chair Rich Guzzardi with questions about what to expect at the forum and how the day of public engagement fits into the larger Village Center project.

**Q**: Why should Boxborough residents spend their Saturday at this event?

**A**: The town was fortunate to receive a grant from the state to explore how we can bring a village center to life in Boxborough. This is the major opportunity for people in town to weigh in on this project and what their expectations are for a village center. Questions planned to be addressed are: What does a village center look like? Where would the village center be located? What businesses and community spaces are planned? What would be different from today? While people are invited to spend the day participating in the event, there will be a posted agenda with different sessions scheduled. So, people can pick and choose what they want to attend.

**Q**: What can folks expect to do while they are there?

**A**: Our experienced planning consultant Tighe & Bond will lead the event. They are experts in the business and have done multiple community sessions of this type. Overall, I would expect an overview of the project, review of past work, sharing of preliminary ideas, and group discussions of what residents and businesses need and want. There may even be a site walk.

**Q**: What are some examples of topics and questions on which folks will be able to give their opinions?

**A**: One of the first things we need to do is identify the land that should be a part of a village center. Today we have two zoned districts near what we consider Town Center - our Town Center and Business Districts. Is this the land that should be included, or are there other parcels? How might we integrate all of this together into a uniform village center? Additional topics such as density, design guidelines, roadways, walkability, commercial businesses vs. community services, and constraints may also be discussed.

**Q**: How will residents’ feedback and opinions be used by the consultants?

**A**: Tighe & Bond is taking a practical approach to this project based on the realities on the ground. We are trying to get from big ideas to practical opportunities. The consultants want to understand what it is that the community wants, likes, and dislikes, so that the plan they identify fits both physical realities and the desires of the community.

**Q**: What is the ultimate goal of the Village Center project?

**A**: Beyond the community event on February 10, there will be as many as four meetings led by our Town Planner Alec Wade with the consultants and members of a number of boards and committees in town. This group will help distill all the information collected and develop a couple of scenarios for the town to pursue. This would be in the form of a report delivered to the town sometime in May. This report will come with an action plan and set of next steps to launch the preferred scenario into existence.
