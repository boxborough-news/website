At the Boxborough Well-Being Committee meeting on January 18, Boxborough’s Community Services Coordinator (CSC) Wendy Trinks reported on services that she provided to Boxborough residents throughout 2023.

In 2023, Trinks assisted more than 50 families in need with emergency assistance grants, referrals to food pantries, applications for SNAP benefits, rental assistance, clothing referrals, gift cards, Common Housing Application for Massachusetts Programs (CHAMP), and Residential Assistance for Family in Transitions (RAFT). She also ran a holiday support drive that provided gift cards, toys, and gifts to Boxborough families identified as needing support.

The CSC also oversees three different programs that use American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) funds. The first ARPA program provides $12,000 for two additional slots for the Boxborough Rental Assistance Program (BRAP). BRAP provides rental assistance to income-eligible households who are renting qualifying units in Boxborough. The BRAP is funded by the Community Preservation Act and managed by the Boxborough Housing Board and the CSC. During 2023, Trinks enrolled a total of 11 new families into BRAP, which filled the program to capacity.

The second ARPA program overseen by the CSC is $15,000 for the Acton Food Pantry. These funds will be fully expended by the end of January 2024.

The third ARPA program adds $105,000 to the Boxborough Emergency Assistance Program (BEAP). This program provides qualifying Boxborough households with $1,000 grants for short-term financial assistance for emergency needs such as rent, utilities, medical bills, etc. As of the end of December 2023, forty-two grants have been written for residents.

During 2023, Trinks collaborated with many other local entities in order to serve Boxborough residents. These include the Boxborough Police Department, the Massachusetts Department of Children and Families, United Church of Christ Boxborough, Saint Vincent De Paul Society, the Domestic Violence Services Network, and A-B United Way.

Mary Pavlik, Chair of the Boxborough Well-Being Committee applauds the work done by Trinks. “In her first full year as CSC and working part time, Wendy has gone above and beyond in reaching out to connect those in need with every possible accessible resource. She's continually looking for ways to reach out to those who need help and tireless in her effort to find additional resources.”

The CSC “monitors the social service needs of residents of all ages in the areas of housing, emergency assistance and mental health. Work includes outreach and communication of locally available resources to Boxborough residents as well as assessing specific client needs and connecting those clients to resources for assistance with follow up as needed.”

Trinks has served as Boxborough’s CSC since August 2022. Trinks can be reached at [wtrinks@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:wtrinks@boxborough-ma.gov).
