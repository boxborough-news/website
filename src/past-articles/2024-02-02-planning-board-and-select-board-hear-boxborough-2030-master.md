On January 29, the Planning Board and Select Board held a joint meeting to hear Town Planner Alec Wade present an annual update on the “Boxborough 2030” Master Plan.

Boxborough’s Master Plan serves as a “comprehensive road map” that “sets tangible goals for the community.” It is a 15-year plan that was adopted in 2016 after a significant community engagement effort. The plan outlines 125 action items for the town and community to undertake by the year 2030. Included in the Master Plan is a mechanism for annual updates and progress reports.

Wade reported to the Planning Board and Select Board that based on recent data collection and analysis, 18 percent of the 125 action items have been completed, 42 percent are in progress, and 31 percent are “delayed” or “no update.” Due to gaps in the data collection process, there was no data collected with respect to the remaining 9 percent of action items.

Wade identified room for improvement in the data collection process and suggested that more boards and committees might benefit from town staff support. He also flagged the Town’s reliance on volunteer-run boards and committees to carry out the vast majority of the action items in the Master Plan and suggested that the Town consider connecting boards with more technical support. He presented a series of recommendations on where to focus resources in order to efficiently accomplish incomplete action items.

Following his presentation, Planning Board and Select Board members discussed the need for prioritization in light of budget challenges, the role of Town staff, and availability of resources. Members of both boards expressed their appreciation for a comprehensive update, but also expressed concern about overburdening Town staff, particularly the Town Planner.

Following the joint meeting, the Planning Board continued with its own agenda. The Board discussed adjustments to its May 2024 Town Meeting warrant article to fund a traffic study. The Board also heard an update on the 984 Massachusetts Avenue enforcement action. The respondent has agreed to submit for a Site Plan Review and the Town Planner expects to receive their “existing conditions plan” by February 1. The Board discussed potential next steps but decided to wait for additional data from PLACES Associates before taking any further action.
