On Saturday, February 10, Boxborough will hold a forum at Town Hall to gather public input for the town’s Village Center Revitalization project. All Boxborough residents are invited to join for public engagement and discussion about what Boxborough’s “village center” should look like.

Registration will begin at 9:45 a.m. and late arrivals are encouraged to join until 11 a.m. The program will conclude at 1 p.m. The event will have multiple sessions, including meetings, workshops, and presentations. Zoom credentials will also be available for those who prefer virtual participation.

The event will be run by the town’s recently-hired consultant Tighe & Bond, a landscape, architecture, engineering, and environmental firm, joined by Innes Associates. The town has hired T&B and Innes to review “existing zoning infrastructure, and environmental constraints” and research “best practices” in order to recommend changes to promote town center “revitalization.” The consultants will give the town a final report in mid-May.

The Village Center Revitalization project is funded by a Community Planning Grant secured by Boxborough’s Economic Development Committee.

More information will be provided at [https://bit.ly/Village-Center](https://bit.ly/Village-Center) as details become available.
