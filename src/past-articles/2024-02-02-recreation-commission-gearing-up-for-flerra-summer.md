Can you believe the Flerra Summer Playground Directors have already met to plan for next summer? They are so excited about what they have scheduled! The themes for this coming summer will be:

- Week 1 (July 1-3): Holidays
- Week 2 (July 8-12): Celebration
- Week 3 (July 15-19): Decades
- Week 4 (July 22-26): Olympics

They will also be doing “Tie-dye Tuesdays” and “Water Wednesdays” again this year.

CIT, Counselor, Lead Counselor and Volunteer applications will be open March 1 - March 31. Registration for campers will be open from April 1 - May 31. Questions can always be sent to [FlerraSP@gmail.com](mailto:FlerraSP@gmail.com).
