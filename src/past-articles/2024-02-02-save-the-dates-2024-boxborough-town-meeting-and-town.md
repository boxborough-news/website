Boxborough’s 2024 Annual Town Meeting will start on Monday, May 13 at 7 p.m. at Blanchard Memorial School. It will proceed on Tuesday May 14, Wednesday May 15, and Thursday May 16 as needed.

The 2024 Town Election will take place on Tuesday, May 21. The last day to register to vote at Town Meeting and the Town Election is Friday, May 3.

Nomination papers for elected town offices are currently available from the Town Clerk’s office. The last day to obtain nomination papers is Friday, March 29. Offices to be on the 2024 town ballot are as follows:

- Select Board, three-year term: 2 seats
- School Committee, three-year term: 1 seat
- Planning Board, three-year term: 1 seat
- Library Trustees, three-year term: 2 seats
- Board of Health, three-year term: 1 seat
- Town Moderator, one-year term: 1 seat
- Constable, three-year term: 1 seat

Tuesday, April 2, is the last day to file nomination papers with the Board of Registrars/Town Clerk in order to be on the ballot for the 2024 Town Election. Papers are due in the Town Clerk’s office by 5 p.m. and a minimum of 25 signatures is required. Thursday, April 18 is the last day to withdraw (or object to) a name from nomination for town office.
