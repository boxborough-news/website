The Acton Exchange is Acton’s new not-for-profit, volunteer-led news source. They publish some content in the Acton/Maynard edition of the Action Unlimited and launched their own website, [actonexchange.org](http://actonexchange.org/), in November.

The project began last year after an Acton-Area League of Women Voters event on local journalism brought a group of Acton residents together with the goal of creating a resource for local news in Acton.

Check out [actonexchange.org](http://actonexchange.org/) for more information and to read news about our neighboring town.
