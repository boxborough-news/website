After the storms in 2023, many homeowners face the challenge of cleaning up their yards. Burning of brush, cane, driftwood, and forestry debris is allowed in Boxborough from Jan. 1 to May 1.

The Boxborough Fire Department’s web page outlines the rules and regulations for burning and provides a link to register for a daily burn permit when conditions are safe: [boxborough-ma.gov/251/Burn-Permits](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/251/Burn-Permits).

Burning is permitted only between the hours of 10 a.m. and 4 p.m. No fires may be started after 1 p.m.
