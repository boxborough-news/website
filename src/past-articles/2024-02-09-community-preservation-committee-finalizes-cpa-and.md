On Thursday, February 1, Chair Ron Vogel led the Community Preservation Committee through a review of Community Preservation Act (CPA) financial reports.

The committee reviewed the CPA report submitted for the 2023 Town Report and reviewed and voted to approve the CPA report for the May Town Meeting warrant. It also reviewed the final CPA Financial worksheet for the May 2024 Town Meeting warrant, which projects FY 2025 CPA revenue of roughly $290,000. As a result of the finalizing of the worksheet, the numbers in the committee’s administrative warrant article have been revised upward.

The next meeting of the CPC is May 2, 2024.
