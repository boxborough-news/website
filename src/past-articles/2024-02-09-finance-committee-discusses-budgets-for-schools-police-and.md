At its meeting on January 30, the Finance Committee (Fin Com) continued to discuss budgets for the Acton-Boxborough Regional School District (ABRSD) and the Police and Dispatch Departments.

Fin Com Chair Maria Neyland reported that the Acton Leadership Group has proposed a $6.6M override for Acton. No override is planned for Boxborough.

If the override passes, the ABRSD budget increases by $7.5M for a total budget of $115M. If Boxborough votes to support this budget, Boxborough’s share of the increase would be $2.2M for a total assessment of $16.1M, which would bring the ABRSD budget to 58% of Boxborough’s total budget.

If the Acton override fails, the ABRSD budget increases by $1.8M for a total budget of $108M. Boxborough’s share of the increase would be $944K for a total assessment of $14.8M or 56% of Boxborough’s total budget.

Neyland said that ongoing increases in the ABRSD budget are not sustainable and puts Boxborough in a difficult financial position. Currently, Boxborough has a strong financial position with an AAA bond rating, healthy reserves for debt and OPEB (Other Post Employment Benefits), and an excess tax levy of $2.3M.

Neyland explained that over the years, Boxborough has budgeted conservatively by looking for ways to reduce costs. Recently, the town voted to regionalize dispatch which will result in a savings of $2M over five years ($400K in FY2025).

Several Fin Com members said that they needed to see a plan from ABRSD with some real structural changes to better control operational expenses before they could commit to supporting any proposed increases in the assessment. Adam Klein, ABRSD School Committee Chair, said that the School Committee will be discussing possible structural changes at their Budget Saturday meeting on February 10.

Fin Com also discussed the Police and Dispatch budgets. Salaries represent 91% of the Police budget including a 2.8% COLA (Cost of Living Adjustment), Steps, and “Quinn” bill education incentives. Under the “Quinn” bill, eligible officers are entitled to a higher rate of pay based on their level of education. The salary line increased by 7.36%.

Police Chief John Szewczyk also presented the Dispatch budget, which is down 85% from last year due to the regionalization of dispatch.

Fin Com has put a hold on voting department budgets until they have a better idea as to how the ABRSD budget will affect Boxborough’s financial position with respect to the levy limit. Fin Com may have to ask for cuts to departmental budgets to accommodate additional expenses in the ABRSD budget.

Fin Com meets every Tuesday at 7 p.m. during the budget season. The meetings are hybrid.
