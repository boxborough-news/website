The Select Board and Finance Committee (Fin Com) held a joint meeting on Saturday, February 3 to review and discuss warrant articles currently under consideration for Annual Town Meeting scheduled for May 13, 14, 15 and possibly 16.

The 6-hour marathon meeting included presentations by the Department of Public Works, the Fire Department, the Police Department, and Town Hall, followed by a discussion about funding sources for warrant articles.

Some of the larger warrant articles include $1.2M for the purchase of land for a fire/public safety building, $500K for professional services for the design of the fire station, $650K for the redesign of the interior of the library, $475K for an ambulance, $424K for road and culvert maintenance, $300K for Other Post Employment Benefits (OPEB), and $200K for a new chlorine system at Hager Well (the cost is shared on a pro rata basis with the Acton Boxborough Regional School District).

The total for all warrant articles currently stands at $5M, but not all articles are funded through taxation. Other funding sources include: free cash, funds from the Community Preservation Act (CPA), bonding, stabilization fund, and revolving accounts. After allocating those funding sources to all warrant articles, funding through taxation is currently projected at $1.7M. At least one warrant article may be pulled before the warrant is finalized, which would further reduce funding through taxation.

Over the years, the town has used a number of mechanisms to plan for the future including capital planning and revolving accounts. A revolving account was used to set aside 50% of ambulance revenues to help fund the purchase of the next ambulance. Those funds currently stand at $335K. It is expected that additional revenues will fund the entire cost of $475K before the actual purchase of the ambulance.

Boxborough leadership also discussed the use of $171K in CPA funds for the Blanchard playground. Several Fin Com and Select Board members believe that the cost of improving the Blanchard playground rests with ABRSD, who owns the school and the land.

Boxborough leadership also discussed the use of ARPA (American Rescue Plan Act) funds to help offset the cost of repairs to the Guggins Brook culvert running under Liberty Square Road. Repairs to the culvert are currently not included in the warrant.

Fin Com Chair Maria Neyland provided an overview of the ABRSD budget. The Acton Leadership Group has recommended a $6.6M override plus the allowable increase in taxation under Proposition 2 ½. Based on the current ABRSD proposed budget, that would mean an increase of 15.59% ($2.2M) in Boxborough’s assessment. This brings Boxborough’s total budget at or very near the levy limit. Boxborough leadership is not planning an override this year but may consider an override next year.

Fin Com expressed concern about making any recommendation on the ABRSD budget without knowing what the district is going to do about structural changes to improve efficiencies. Both Fin Com and the Select Board will meet with ABRSD Superintendent Peter Light and members of the School Committee on February 6 at 7 p.m. at Town Hall to discuss and review the school budget. The meeting is hybrid.
