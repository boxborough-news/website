How many hours could you leave an LED light bulb switched on to produce the same greenhouse gas as a single load in a clothes dryer? According to The NewYork Times: 300 hours, or 13 days.

To learn more about carbon reduction, sign up for a free, four-session course called “Where Climate Meets Community.” Choose from three meeting options via Zoom:

- March Saturdays: 2,9,16 and 23 from 10:30 to 11:50 a.m.
- March Tuesdays: 5,12,19 and 26 from 10:30 to 11:50 a.m.
- March Tuesdays: 5,12,19 and 26 from 6:30 to 7:50 p.m.

To sign up, email Boxborough resident Marjorie Kamp at [marjoriek@comcast.net](mailto:marjoriek@comcast.net), placing “2040” in the subject line and indicating choice of sessions. Kamp says the course will help people reduce their carbon footprint by as much as 30 percent.

The Boxborough Sustainability Committee is sponsoring the course, a discussion of Damon Gameau’s book “2040: a Handbook for the Regeneration Based on the Documentary 2040.”
