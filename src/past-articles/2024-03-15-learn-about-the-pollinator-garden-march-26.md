The public is invited to learn about garden pollinators at a virtual event at 7 p.m., Tuesday, March 26, sponsored by Boxborough’s Sargent Memorial Library. Kate Donoval, Owner-Consultant of Blackstone Valley Veggie Gardens, will explain how bees, butterflies, hummingbirds, and other pollinators are “the lifeblood for good production.”

Those planning to attend should register on Zoom by going to [https://bit.ly/42XUN0C](https://bit.ly/42XUN0C). The mission of the Blackstone Valley Veggie Gardens is to inspire others to grow their own fresh produce. They deliver residential and community based training, consulting, and assistance in vegetable garden development.

They are dedicated to the belief that most people should have the knowledge and opportunity to grow wholesome fruits and veggies in containers, raised beds, or in-ground gardens. This program is sponsored by Whitcomb House Trust.
