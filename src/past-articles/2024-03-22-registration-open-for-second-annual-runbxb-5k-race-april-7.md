The Boxborough Recreation Commission has opened registration for the second annual RunBXB road race, a 3.2-mile course for all ages, starting and ending at Steele Farm, 484 Middle Road in Boxborough, on Sunday, April 7. Check-in starts at 10:15 a.m., and the race will begin at 11 a.m.

The registration fee is $25. Participants must register and pay prior to the event. The registration form is available at [https://bit.ly/3IEaBwq](https://bit.ly/3IEaBwq).

Vendors Dirigible Brewing Co., Fat Rooster Food Truck, and Two Friends Chocolates will sell food and drink to runners and their fans.
