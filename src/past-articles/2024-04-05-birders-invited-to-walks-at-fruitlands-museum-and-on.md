Boxborough Birders invite experienced and inexperienced birders of all ages to join groups visiting Fruitland Museum in Harvard on Saturday, April 6, and George and Lucy Yapp Conservation Land in Littleton on Sunday, April 14.

Sandy Oxley will lead the Fruitlands walk which begins at 7 a.m. and ends at 10 am. Birders should meet at the top parking lot at Fruitlands Museum, 102 Prospect Hill Road, Harvard.

Birders going on the Littleton walk should meet at 7 a.m. in the Yapp Conservation Land parking area on Newtown Road, just south of the Shaker Lane intersection.

The walk’s leader, Greg Clark, says, “We will walk a two-mile loop trail, with a visit to Cobbs Pond. We will be looking for spring migrants as well as year-round, resident birds.” The walk will end at 10 a.m. For further information, including directions and maps, go to the calendar at [boxboroughbirders.org](http://boxboroughbirders.org/).
