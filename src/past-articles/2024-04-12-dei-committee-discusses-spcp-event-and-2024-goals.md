On Tuesday, March 26, the town’s Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Committee (DEIC) met via Zoom with a robust agenda. The committee first spent time recapping the Strengthening Police and Community Partnerships (SPCP) event that took place at Blanchard School on March 16.

Multiple committee members were present at the event and participated in the morning and afternoon discussions facilitated by conciliation specialists from the U.S. Department of Justice. At least two DEIC members - Wes Fowlks and Amy Osenar - volunteered to join the proposed special advisory council that would report directly to Police Chief John Szewczyk and would oversee the implementation of any recommendations from the final DOJ report, which the town should receive in about a month.

There was a general feeling of success following the event even amidst some to-be-expected tension. “There were definitely some sparks,” one committee member reflected, “but it was productive, too.” The event reportedly was well attended by a diversity of folks, including a strong representation of Boxborough police officers, members of the school community, the Acton Police Chief and Deputy Chief (also a Boxborough resident) and some 50 residents.

Also discussed were actions to take on the DEIC’s goals for 2024, of which they have set five.

- Goal (1) “to lend a diverse lens to town activities”: Chair Campbell suggested that the DEIC could develop a statement “asking for DEI consideration in [the town’s] policies, programs, and procedures,” inspired by a similar statement written by the Sustainability Committee.
- Goal (2) “to develop a process for addressing bias reports”: Town Administrator Mike Johns is reportedly looking into securing an ombudsperson for the town who could receive anonymous reports of bias incidents and other DEI-related concerns. The committee agreed that an ombudsperson was a good solution.
- Goal (3) “to support employees with DEI needs”: The Personnel Committee has an Employer of Choice Initiative that will likely overlap with this goal so committee member Fowlks agreed to follow up with the Personnel Committee.
- Goal (4) “to conduct a feasibility study to determine ways to provide DEI services to the town including partnerships with other towns”: The DEIC would need to secure funds from the town to employ a neutral, external consultant to carry out such a study, so a timeline for drafting and proposing an article to said purpose was discussed.
- Goal (5) “to amplify and acknowledge underrepresented voices”: The committee discussed translation services for all town meeting notes and the town website, as there are multiple languages spoken town-wide.

The DEIC meets on an as-needed basis and voted to meet again after Annual Town Meeting.
