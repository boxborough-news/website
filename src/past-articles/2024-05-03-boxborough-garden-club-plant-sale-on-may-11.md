The Boxborough Garden Club Plant Sale will take place on Saturday, May 11 from 9 a.m. until noon in front of the UCC Church, 723 Massachusetts. Avenue, rain or shine.

This is a great opportunity to get annual and perennial plants, dahlia starts, seedings, and houseplants. It’s also perfect timing for Mother’s Day gifts. In addition to sales, Garden Club members will be happy to offer free gardening advice. Cash or check only.

The proceeds from the plant sale pay for a scholarship for a graduating AB student, town beautification, and maintaining the Colonial Herb Garden, located at the top of Middle Road. For more information, phone Flo Hanover at 978-263-3250, or email her at [fhanover10@gmail.com](mailto:fhanover10@gmail.com).
