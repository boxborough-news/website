Boxborough’s new dedicated pickleball courts at the Liberty Fields are open for the season, and organized community pickleball play sessions have been scheduled.

To participate in an organized session, download the TeamReach application and enter group code 01719PB to sign up for upcoming sessions.

Boxborough’s Recreation Commission has also partnered with the Marcus Lewis Tennis Center and is offering Spring clinics for both beginners and advanced players. To sign up, go to [https://tinyurl.com/BoxboroughPickleBall](https://tinyurl.com/BoxboroughPickleBall).
