At their meeting on May 23, at Craft Food Hall, the new restaurant in Boxborough, the Boxborough Recreation Commission discussed scheduling and rules for the pickleball courts at Liberty Fields and decided to extend the Marcus Lewis tennis and pickleball lessons at Liberty Field into the summer, since the lessons this spring are going well.

During public discussion, Colleen Whitcomb assisted RecCom regarding the “Team Reach” system for the pickleball court reservations.

The commission noted that a shed for equipment storage will be built at Liberty Field in June. They also have plans to start building a pavilion at Liberty Field this summer. For the pavilion project, the town expects to receive earmark funds from the state.

RecCom members said that Flerra Summer Playground plans are going well. The commission will have an orientation for all counselors and counselors-in-training on Wednesday, June 12, at 6 p.m. at Town Hall.

The commission is also making plans for “local celebrities” to speak at Flerra this year. One will be Boxborough author and illustrator Jarrett Lerner, whose illustrations are on the wall outside of Blanchard Memorial School.

Lerner is the award-winning creator of more than a dozen books for kids, including the EngiNerds series of middle grade novels, the Geeger the Robot series of early chapter books, the Hunger Heroes series of graphic novel chapter books, two activity books, the illustrated novel in verse, “A Work in Progress,” and the Nat the Cat series of early readers.
