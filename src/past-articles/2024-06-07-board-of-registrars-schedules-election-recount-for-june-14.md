On Monday, June 3, Town Clerk and Board of Registrars Chair Rebecca Harris called to order a meeting of the Boxborough Board of Registrars regarding the recount petition submitted by Nathaniel “Than” Stuntz for a recount of votes in the Planning Board race.

All Registrars present voted in favor of setting the recount date at Friday, June 14, at 3 p.m. in the Grange Room at Town Hall, 29 Middle Road.

Town Clerk Harris also gave an overview of the petition and explained the process for the recount. The recount will be conducted by election workers (likely seven) hired by the Town Clerk. The election workers will conduct a hand count of all 679 ballots.

Board of Registrar members Nancy Brown and Heather Fleming, in addition to Chair Harris, were present for the meeting.

On election day, Town Clerk Harris reported that Stuntz lost the election to incumbent Rebecca Verner by only three votes. According to the Town Clerk’s election results, available at [bit.ly/4brBJLK](https://bit.ly/4brBJLK), incumbent Verner received 336 votes and challenger Stuntz received 333 votes. Stuntz submitted his petition for a recount on May 30.
