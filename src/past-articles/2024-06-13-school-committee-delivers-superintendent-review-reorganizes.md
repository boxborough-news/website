At its meeting on June 6, the ABRSD School Committee delivered its annual review to Superintendent Peter Light and approved the Superintendent’s contract and salary.

School Committee Chair Adam Klein read aloud the “annual summative review” for Superintendent Light, acknowledging the “gargantuan task” of this year’s budget process and the many challenges that arose over the course of the 2023-2024 school year, Light’s sixth year with the district.

For his overall performance rating, a majority of the committee rated Superintendent Light as “proficient.” “Peter’s unwavering dedication, empathy, and forward-thinking approach have solidified his role as an invaluable asset to the district, setting a high standard for educational leadership and community calibration,” said Klein.

Chair Klein described changes to the Superintendent’s contract and announced a 3.5% increase in the Superintendent’s salary, which the Committee then approved.

The School Committee also conducted its reorganization procedure. Chair Klein was re-elected as Chair of the committee. Rebeccah Wilson was re-elected as Vice Chair from Acton. Liz Fowlks was re-elected as Vice Chair from Boxborough.

The School Committee also heard presentations from the Special Education Parent Advisory Council and from the ABRSD Energy Manager Kate Crosby.

Energy Manager Crosby outlined two actions that the School Committee must take in order for ABRSD to support the Town of Acton’s application to the Massachusetts Department of Energy Resources “Climate Leader Communities” program. The first is to adopt a “Zero Emission Vehicle First” policy, and the second is to adopt a “decarbonization roadmap.”

After Crosby’s presentation, the School Committee did a “first read” of a draft “Zero Emission Vehicle First” policy, the purpose of which is to “set standards and guidelines for the purchase, operation, and maintenance of [ABRSD] fleet vehicles that will advance the economic, energy, and climate sustainability of municipal operations by achieving long-term reductions in energy costs, energy consumption, and greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions.”

The Committee will vote on the “Zero Emission Vehicle First” policy at their June 24 meeting, which will primarily be a workshop, not a full business meeting.
