At a hybrid meeting on June 13, the Boxborough Sustainability Committee applauded the completion of all the requirements needed for Boxborough to become a Green Community. The BSC also approved the appointment of Kate Davies as BSC Chair.

By the end of the summer, the State is expected to approve the town’s application to become a Green Community. Boxborough is then expected to receive an initial grant of about $130,000 which the town plans to use to begin inserting energy-saving LED lights in Town Hall, the DPW, Sargent Memorial Library, and the Police Station.

The BSC expects LED lighting and other energy-saving measures will be part of the design of a new fire station, and Davies will talk with Kate Crosby, Acton-Boxborough Regional Schools’ Energy Manager, about LED lighting in Blanchard School. As a Green Community, the town will also be authorized to seek future grants to apply to sustainability projects.

The BSC voted to approve Davies’ recommendation to submit, for Select Board approval, a revised BSC Charter establishing a current membership of ten, a quorum of six, and a chair and clerk as BSC officers.

After over 20 years as chair of the town’s Energy Committee which soon became the Sustainability Committee, Francie Nolde served as co-chair with Davies for the last three months. Nolde proposed that Davies become the committee chair, and Davies and the committee approved the change.

The BSC discussed the possibility of working with Town Planner Alec Wade to discuss waste management issues and information sessions with the public regarding the adoption of the Specialized Opt-In Building Code for new construction and large additions. The committee will discuss these topics at its next hybrid meeting at 7 p.m. on July 11. At the July meeting, the BSC will also discuss Littleton Electric Light Department’s solar policies as well as the committee’s recommendations regarding large commercial construction.
