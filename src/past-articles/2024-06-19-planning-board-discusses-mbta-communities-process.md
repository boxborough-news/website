At its meeting on June 17, the Planning Board discussed its role in the MBTA Communities process.

The 2021 MBTA Communities Act requires that certain cities and towns designated as MBTA Communities “have a zoning ordinance or by-law that provides for at least one district of reasonable size in which multi-family housing is permitted as of right.” MBTA Communities are those that have an MBTA station within them and those that are adjacent to a community with an MBTA station. As an “Adjacent Small Town,” Boxborough faces a December 2025 deadline to adopt the required zoning.

Town Planner Alec Wade shared with the Board his plan to convene an MBTA Communities working group, consisting of two Planning Board members and three other residents, to focus on public engagement and report back to the full Board.

The Planning Board pushed back on Wade’s vision for the working group, with members expressing a desire for the Planning Board to be more involved in driving the town’s MBTA Communities process. Wade and the Board agreed to schedule a meeting focused exclusively on MBTA Communities planning. This meeting is scheduled for July 8.

The Board reorganized for the upcoming year and held a working session on its rules and regulations. The Board elected Mark White as Chair and Rebecca Verner as Clerk. The Board also reappointed Janet Keating-Connelly as associate Planning Board member.

The Board also revisited a proposal by Mark Lombardo of Lombardo Loam and Gravel Co. Inc at 1160 Liberty Square Road for a fence as required by a condition of a 2018 Site Plan Approval Decision. At its last meeting, the Board approved a proposal for the fence and adjacent plantings. At this meeting, the property owner presented a revised proposal with an adjusted fence location and a change to the type of plantings. The Board approved the revised proposal as amended, subject to revised drawings reflecting the agreed-upon changes.
