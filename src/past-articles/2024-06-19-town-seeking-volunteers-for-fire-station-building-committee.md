The Select Board is encouraging interested residents to apply for membership on the town’s new Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC).

According to a town press release available at [https://bit.ly/3VLc8Z1](https://bit.ly/3VLc8Z1), the purpose of the FSBC is to “oversee the site selection, budget, design, and construction of a new fire station, including approval of all invoices and change orders.”

At their June 10 meeting, the Select Board announced their intention to dissolve the current Building Committee and create a new Fire Station Building Committee.

The Select Board will appoint five to seven residents to “represent the community” and be voting members on this new committee.

According to Town Administrator Michael Johns, the Select Board is looking for “open-minded residents committed to attending in-person evening meetings.”

Volunteers should be prepared to attend two meetings per month of approximately three hours each and spend “another two to four hours a month reading materials and preparing for meetings.”

The FSBC “will be meeting with project management, design, and construction professionals … as well as Town staff,” says Johns. Members will be “reviewing OPM applications, ranking them, and making a hiring recommendation to the Select Board, and will be providing oversight to the OPM.”

Anyone interested in serving on the FSBC may email the Select Board at [selectboard@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:selectboard@boxborough-ma.gov) indicating their interest and are welcome to include a resume (or brief background info), but this is not required. Applications are due July 1.

The Select Board will choose whom to appoint by discussion and vote in open session at their July 8 meeting.
