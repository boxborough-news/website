A Silver Alert has been issued by the Plymouth, MA Police Department for Rodney Riviello, the father of Boxborough resident Michelle Norris.

The silver alert, issued on Tuesday, June 18, stated the following: “We are looking to locate Rodney Riviello. He is a 69 year old male with a history of dementia. He was last seen at the Beth Israel Deaconess in Plymouth at 11:15 a.m. [Tuesday]. He is a bald heavy set white male wearing a collared striped shirt, blue shorts, and possibly a Yankees baseball hat. If you believe you have seen the individual please call us immediately.”

On Wednesday, June 19, Plymouth police issued the following update on their facebook page: “This silver Nissan Armada picked up a passenger while exiting the BID Plymouth driveway yesterday at approximately 12:30PM. The vehicle was headed towards the rotary at Plymouth North High school. Could the operator or anybody that knows this vehicle please call Plymouth Police detectives at 508-746-1212. This may or may not be related to a missing person, Rodney Riviello.”

Anyone with information about Riviello’s location is asked to contact Plymouth Police detectives at 508-746-1212.
