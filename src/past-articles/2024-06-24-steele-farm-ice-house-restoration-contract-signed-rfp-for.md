At the Steele Farm Committee meeting on June 20, Chair Christoper Hydak reported that the contract to restore the exterior of the Steele Farm ice house has been signed. This work is anticipated to begin in early July and finish during the summer. All of the cost is being funded by contributions, including a very generous contribution from an anonymous donor, those made to the Steele Farm Committee in memory of Sharon Burke, contributions to the Boxborough Historical Society in memory of Jeanne Steele Kangas, and general funds from the Historical Society.

The first draft of the request for proposals for Phase III of the effort to preserve the envelope of the 1784 Levi Wetherbee farmhouse at Steele Farm has been completed. Phase III includes new windows, new siding, and exterior painting. Hydak reported on the generous bequest by Jeanne Steele Kangas of $50,000 for future expenses on the farmhouse after the Phase III project is completed.

The committee discussed ongoing maintenance of the landscape, including mowing, tree removal, eradication of invasive species, and haying of the fields.

The first phase of the inventory and organization of the contents of the barn has been completed. Owners have been contacted and have taken responsibility for all items in the barn; some items have been removed, other items have been organized, and decisions have been made on what should stay at the barn.

The town purchased the Steele Farm property in the fall of 1994, making this year the 30th anniversary of town ownership and thus the preservation of the buildings and property. The committee is still planning to have a 30th anniversary celebration in mid October; details will be arranged over the next few months.

The next meeting of the committee will take place on Thursday July 11 at the Steele Farm Barn, 484 Middle Road, starting at 7 p.m.
