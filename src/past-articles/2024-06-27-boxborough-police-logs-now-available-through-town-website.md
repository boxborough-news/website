The Boxborough Police Department has informed Boxborough News that a link to the Nashoba Valley Regional Dispatch Police Logs is now on the police department’s page on the town website, here: [https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/724/Nashoba-Valley-Regional-Dispatch-Police-](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/724/Nashoba-Valley-Regional-Dispatch-Police-)

The link provided on the town website goes to the Nashoba Valley Regional Dispatch “Press Logs” page, which displays a list of weekly logs.

Each log contains police activity in all jurisdictions served by Nashoba Valley Regional Dispatch. Jurisdictions are listed alphabetically; scroll past Berlin and Bolton to see the Boxborough log.
