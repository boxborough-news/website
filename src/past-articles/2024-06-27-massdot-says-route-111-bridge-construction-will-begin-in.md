The MassDOT Communications Office has confirmed that the replacement of the Route 111/Massachusetts Ave bridge over I-495 in Boxborough is currently in the “design phase” and that MassDOT is “eagerly anticipating the start of construction this fall.” The expected completion date for the project is summer 2027.

Once the construction begins, one lane is expected to be open in each direction until the project is complete. However, said John Goggin of the MassDOT Communications Office, “there may be instances when additional lane closures are required, including the total closure of the bridge. Any lane closures will be scheduled at night to minimize traffic impacts.”

According to information presented at the Design Public Hearing for this project, held virtually on April 4, 2023, the bridge replacement was initiated after a bridge inspection in 2017 noted “advanced deterioration and deficiencies for structure.”

MassDOT intends to replace the bridge with improved vertical and horizontal clearances as well as accommodations for pedestrians and cyclists.
