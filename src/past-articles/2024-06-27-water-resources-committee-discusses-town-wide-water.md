At their meeting on June 25, the Water Resources Committee (WRC) reviewed the timeline for the Request for Proposals (RFP) issued by the Town of Boxborough on June 5 for the development of a town-wide water resource plan.

Four interested parties attended the town’s pre-submission conference to discuss the RFP on June 20, and a fifth contacted the town by phone after the conference. Proposals are due July 19, and the WRC hopes to hear presentations on at least two proposals.

The WRC also discussed private wells. Dave McKiernan, the Select Board’s appointed member of the WRC, suggested that the WRC webpage include resources for Boxborough residents who want to learn more about private well testing.

Committee members discussed how existing resources from the state about private well testing are sometimes difficult to find, difficult to use, and/or confusing. The WRC webpage currently has a list of educational resources, like “Well Care 101” and information from the state about water quality.

However, these don’t explicitly answer common questions from Boxborough residents like, “how and when should I test my well?” and “how do I find out whether my water has PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances) in it”? The Committee agreed to coordinate with the Board of Health and potentially publish resources related to private well testing on the WRC webpage.

For more information about Boxborough’s water supply, visit the interactive “Boxborough’s Water Story” presentation, prepared by the WRC in partnership with the Metropolitan Area Planning Council, at [https://bit.ly/3RMOY1P](https://bit.ly/3RMOY1P).
