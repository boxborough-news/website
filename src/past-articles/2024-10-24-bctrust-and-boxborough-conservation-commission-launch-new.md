The Boxborough Conservation Trust and Boxborough Conservation Commission are collaborating on a new program for local kids and their caregivers: a Family Walk series.

The first family walk will be held at Steele Farm, 484 Middle Road, on Sunday, November 17 starting at 9:30 a.m. Participants should meet at the parking lot next to the big white farmhouse. Interested families are encouraged to RSVP at [https://bit.ly/3UmgLr3](https://bit.ly/3UmgLr3).

From the organizers: “Do you love to get outside but sometimes need a little extra motivation to take the first step? Gather up the family, pack some snacks, maybe some binoculars, and join us at the designated trailhead. We will select a kid-friendly walking loop and head out together to explore Boxborough’s natural beauty. All paces are welcome, and we hope the children will help us to stop and explore whatever we may observe along the way. We envision some families will complete the full loop, and others may only make it part way. The main goal is to create some community and get excited about exploring nature together! Some walks may feature a shared activity, such as a scavenger hunt, bird watching, or trail bingo.”

Questions? Contact Liz Caldwell at [LCaldwell@BCTrust.org](mailto:LCaldwell@BCTrust.org) or Sam Anderson at [SAnderson@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:SAnderson@boxborough-ma.gov).

As a reminder, long pants, high socks, and bug repellent are recommended for tick prevention. For a link to Boxborough’s Trail Use and Regulations document and trail etiquette guidelines, visit [https://bit.ly/3C8dHZl](https://bit.ly/3C8dHZl).

The Boxborough Conservation Trust (BCTrust) is a private, nonprofit 501(c)(3) organization founded in 1997 to preserve and protect Boxborough’s rural character and natural resources.

The Boxborough Conservation Commission is a seven-member board that is appointed by the Boxborough Select Board. The Conservation Commission spends the majority of its time fulfilling its legal obligations under the Massachusetts Wetlands Protection Act and the Boxborough Wetland Bylaw. It also plans, manages, and maintains the comprehensive conservation trail system in Boxborough.
