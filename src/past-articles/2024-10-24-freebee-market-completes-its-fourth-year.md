On Saturday, October 19, the FreeBee Market completed its fourth season with a final community market featuring free Halloween costumes.

The FreeBee project began after a massive fire at a condo complex on Swanson Road in February 2021. Originally it was organized to support the residents displaced from the fire. From there, it evolved into an ongoing market run by a group of volunteers where everything is free.

This year, FreeBee held “food rescue Saturdays” every week from May until October with larger FreeBee “community markets” - including entertainment and information booths - about once a month.

Each week, the FreeBee volunteer team collects gleaned (older or excess, but edible) food from farms, stores, restaurants, and backyard gardens, and gives it away.

On Fridays, volunteers collect food from local farms and food banks. More food is delivered on Saturday mornings. Generally on a Saturday morning, there may be 20-30 volunteers checking and sorting the food before it is put out for the public. At 10:30 a.m. the bell rings, and the food is shared with the community.

In its four years, the FreeBee has kept an estimated 24 tons of food out of local landfills and given away untold crafts, books, plants and clothes. The FreeBee team thanks all of its volunteers for their hours of work.

Volunteers of all ages are always welcome. For more information, visit [https://sites.google.com/abuw.org/freebeemarket/home](https://sites.google.com/abuw.org/freebeemarket/home).
