The United Church of Christ Boxborough’s annual Merrie Christmas Fair will be held on November 23, the Saturday before Thanksgiving, from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. at 723 Massachusetts Avenue.

From the UCC Boxborough: “Save the date for the upcoming UCC Boxborough fair! This fair has something for everyone, regardless of the season. Think home crafted, homemade gifts for birthday, holiday and Thanksgiving hosts. Think delicious Sugar Plum café with jazz singers, Santa dancing, and an outside fire pit for s’mores. Think the ultimate thrift shop in Attic Treasures with floor to ceiling goods. Think fresh greens and arrangements ready to place on the Thanksgiving table to stun your guests or host. See you at the fair, your ultimate local shopping event!”

The online auction component of the fair will open on November 13 with more than 200 items to bid on.

Proceeds support the ongoing expenses of UCC Boxborough (a FreeBee Market partner) with 10% of proceeds going directly to North Central Massachusetts Habitat for Humanity.

Those interested in volunteering behind the scenes can contact [mary.j.pavlik@gmail.com](mailto:mary.j.pavlik@gmail.com) to find out more. Volunteers do not need to be church members.
