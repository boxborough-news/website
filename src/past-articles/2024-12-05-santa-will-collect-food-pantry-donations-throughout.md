Santa Claus is returning to Boxborough on Sunday, December 15 to collect food items for the Acton Food Pantry. He will be escorted around town by the Boxborough Fire and Police departments.

Santa will spend two hours in Boxborough starting at 9 a.m. before heading back to the North Pole to make final preparations for the big day. His exact route is still in the planning stages, but will be posted on the Town website and the Boxborough Fire Department Facebook page closer to the event.

The Acton Food Pantry has an urgent need for baby formula, food, cereals, oatmeal, and snacks. Other items on the wish list include: individual fruit cups, canned fruit, walnuts, almonds, trail mix, canned vegetables, canned pasta, vegetable oil, gluten free snacks, corned beef hash, pancake mix, and broth. Any and all donations are greatly appreciated.

The Boxborough Fire Department is also collecting new, unwrapped toys for distribution through the Marine Toys for Tots Foundation. Donations are currently being accepted and can be dropped off at the fire station at 502 Massachusetts Avenue through Saturday, December 7. Toys for Tots does not accept realistic-looking weapons or gifts with food. If donated, such items will not be distributed.

The Fire Department appreciates the continued generosity of the Boxborough community. Contact Captain Malinowski at [jmalinowski@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:jmalinowski@boxborough-ma.gov) for questions.
