Five former Blanchard Memorial School Band students have been selected for the Massachusetts Music Educators Association (MMEA) Eastern District Senior Festival. Auditions were held on November 2 and those auditioning had to prepare a solo piece, perform scales, and perform a sight-reading of a piece given to them that day.

The students who earned their way into the festival, along with their instruments and assigned ensemble, are:

Ev Bartley - Horn - Orchestra

Lauren Bartley - Choir

Cooper Bentley - Euphonium – Concert Band

Alex Hua - Trumpet - Orchestra

Madeline Sjoblom - Trumpet - Orchestra

Players are scored on a variety of skills, and those with the highest scores are selected for one of the festival ensembles, either Orchestra, Concert Band, Jazz Ensemble, or Choir. Those who score in the top 50th percentile also receive a recommendation to audition in January for the All-State Festival, which will be held in March. All of these students also received the All-State recommendation.

The Senior Festival will be held on January 10 and 11 at Milton High School. It is one and a half days of rehearsing, culminating with performances on Saturday, with the Concert Band and Orchestra starting at 1:30 p.m. and the Choir and Jazz Band at 3 p.m. Tickets are $10 and are sold to the students first, but any remaining tickets will be sold on a first come first served basis at the door on Saturday at noon.

The Eastern District Junior and Senior Festivals are extensions of school music programs and are designed to offer students in public, private, and parochial schools additional opportunities to rehearse and perform challenging music with guest conductors. Senior Festival Auditions are open to students in grades 9, 10, 11 and 12. For more information, visit [https://www.mmeaeasterndistrict.org](https://www.mmeaeasterndistrict.org/).
