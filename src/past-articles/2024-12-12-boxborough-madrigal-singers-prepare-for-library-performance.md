On Thursday, December 19, the Madrigal Singers from the Acton-Boxborough Regional High School will perform their annual holiday concert at the Sargent Memorial Library beginning at 6 p.m. Three members of the Madrigal Singers are from Boxborough: sophomore Theo Greene, sophomore Omar Marrero, and junior Sahithi Setty. Boxborough News reached out to the Boxborough Madrigals members to learn a little more about them and what it’s like to sing with the Madrigals.

**Boxborough News:** How long have you participated in Madrigals?

- **Greene:** I just joined Madrigals this year, after auditioning in the summer.
- **Marrero:** This is my first year being part of the Madrigals.
- **Setty:** I have been in Madrigals for 2 years.

**Boxborough News:** What do you enjoy most about participating in Madrigals?

- **Greene:** The thing I’ve enjoyed most about Mads so far has been the caroling.
- **Marrero:** I really enjoy the community of Mads. It really feels like you're one big family together. The group's traditions are also super fun, especially because this is my first year, and I don't know what's coming!
- **Setty:** Some of my favorite memories from Madrigals come from our rehearsals. We have a lot of fun while learning and perfecting our songs which gives us a large sense of community! I also love performing because it really shows how our music impacts people and helps create relationships with people outside our madrigal community. You can really see that through caroling as it helps create a joyful atmosphere leading up to the holidays.

**Boxborough News:** What are you most looking forward to about your upcoming holiday performances?

- **Greene:** I'm looking forward to letting everybody see what we've been working on for half a year.
- **Marrero:** I'm looking forward to sharing the music we've been working on with everyone!
- **Setty:** During the next few weeks we will be singing at a few senior centers. It's really fun seeing how much they enjoy us singing, and it makes the work we put into learning our songs worth it.

**Boxborough News:** What are your future plans, career goals, or interests?

- **Greene:** I hope to keep doing Mads for the rest of my time at AB, and I wish I had joined in freshman year.
- **Marrero:** I'm planning on pursuing computer science (specifically game development), but I've also been thinking about potentially pursuing music as well. Being a part of the Madrigals and other aspects of performing arts at AB have sparked my brain's interest in music and how it sounds the way it does.
- **Setty:** I am really interested in the environment, specifically renewable energy, so in the future I want to do something involving that. I really hope I continue singing in my future as well.
