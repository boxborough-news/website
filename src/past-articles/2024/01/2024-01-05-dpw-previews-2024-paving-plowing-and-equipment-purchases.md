Boxborough’s Department of Public Works (DPW) is charged with the upkeep of the town’s roads and public spaces. This includes everything from roadway paving and plowing, to field and building maintenance, to waste disposal and recycling. Boxborough News reached out to DPW Director Ed Kukkula for a 2024 preview.

For months, Kukkula has been working on fiscal year 2025 (mid-2024 to mid-2025) capital budget items, identifying the equipment that he and his team propose to acquire in order to carry out their mandate around town.

This includes a field mower with an 11-foot cutting width for all of the big playing fields; a sit-down mower with a 52-inch cutting width for buildings and grounds; a new trash compacting unit for the transfer station; and a permanent chlorine system for the Hager Well. It is likely that these items will appear as articles on the Annual Town Meeting Warrant in May 2024; they total approximately $500,000.

The DPW is responsible for the repair and maintenance of Boxborough’s 30 miles of roadways. When asked which roads would be paved in 2024, Kukkula indicated that the west side of Burroughs Road and Priest Lane were on the list. The DPW website has a five-year list of scheduled road paving projects.

Visit [tinyurl.com/DPW2024](http://tinyurl.com/DPW2024) to see the plan through FY28. Kukkula noted that the list may change since the conditions of roads vary each year.

As of this writing, Boxborough has yet to experience a winter snowstorm, but Boxborough News asked how the DPW prepares for a typical winter season. Kukkula responded, “very little changes in preparation of the winter season from year to year.

All of the trucks that treat the roads are converted over in late October; then, every truck is checked to make sure all of the plows work when attached and that all of the trucks are ready to go mechanically.

Salt is the only thing used to treat the roads and we purchase around 900 tons throughout the year. My staff is always ready and are on call when there is word of an upcoming storm and will remain at work until the roads are completely cleared of snow once the storm stops.”

The town no longer uses sand to treat the roads, but residents can obtain sand at the DPW garage (577 Massachusetts Avenue). It is located in the shed to the left behind the main building. The sand is free to pick up at any time. DPW contact information is available at [boxborough-ma.gov/169/Public-Works](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/169/Public-Works).
