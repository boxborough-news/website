On January 7, Town Planner Alec Wade introduced a new effort to study Boxborough’s town center at a gathering of over 25 citizens, including a “stakeholder group,” representatives from town boards and committees.

Wade will serve as project manager for the work of recently hired consultants: Tighe & Bond, a landscape, architecture, engineering, and environmental firm, joined by Innes Associates for zoning, design guidelines, and strategic planning.

In late January or early February, the consultants will invite the Boxborough community to express their opinions in a series of meetings, workshops, and design presentations regarding the town center which extends east and west along Route 111 from the crossroads near Town Hall.

Wade explained that the “stakeholder group” will talk often with the consultants in virtual meetings open to the public.

During March, April, and May of 2024, the consulting firms will refine conceptual plans and ask for public opinion before giving the town a final report in mid-May.

T&B and Innes intend to review “existing zoning infrastructure, and environmental constraints” and research “best practices” in order to recommend changes to promote town center “revitalization.” EDC Chair Rich Guzzardi said he hopes further study will support established companies and bring new firms to Boxborough.

At the January 7 meeting, citizens recommended the need to study other towns, use outreach to involve citizens, offer Zoom connections for public meetings, and require sustainability practices in all future development.

In emphasizing the importance of public and town staff input, Sharon Rooney of T&B reviewed the town’s prior work which began in 2016 with the “Boxborough 2030 Master Plan,” followed by the formation of the Economic Development Committee. The EDC initiated the UMass market analysis study in 2019-2020, and in late 2023, the town received a grant to support the new study.
