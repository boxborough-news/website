At its meeting on January 8, the Select Board discussed with the Town Administrator a draft of “Select Board and Town Administrator Strategic Goals” for the coming year. TA Michael Johns provided updates on the status of last year’s strategic goals and walked through his ideas for new strategic goals. Once the goals are finalized and voted at a future meeting, they will be published on the town’s website.

The new draft strategic goals included “fire station,” “town sewer project,” development of legal counsel and risk mitigation strategies, fee structure analyses, and a “governance document” to describe the leadership structures in town government.

Last year’s goals included an ARPA spending plan, “water project and IMA,” succession planning, professional development, employee wellbeing, development of IT and communication strategies, and creation of a capital committee. TA Johns indicated that some of last year’s goals had been completed and many were in progress.

TA Johns updated the Select Board on town employees’ health insurance. Minuteman Nashoba Health Group is dissolving and TA Johns has formed an Insurance Advisory Committee with representatives of each type of town employee group (e.g., union, non-union, retirees) to work with an insurance broker and learn about their options. TA Johns emphasized that he wants the employees to be making the decisions about their future health insurance.

TA Johns also walked the Select Board through the draft warrant for the May 2024 Annual Town Meeting as it currently stands. Board members asked questions about some of the articles but took no action.

Finally, the Select Board agreed that the town should light a menorah, in addition to a Christmas tree, during the next holiday season. TA Johns reported that a resident had recently made this suggestion and Select Board members expressed support for the idea.

Chair Kristin Hilberg also suggested that the town move the annual tree lighting from the large evergreen tree at the corner of Hill and Middle roads to an evergreen tree along Route 111 between the police and fire stations. Hilberg mentioned that both the DPW Director and the Police Chief had expressed safety-related concerns about the current location of the tree lighting. The Board agreed that the town should look into having both a tree lighting and a menorah lighting at the proposed Route 111 location.
