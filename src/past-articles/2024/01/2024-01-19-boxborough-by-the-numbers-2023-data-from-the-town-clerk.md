Curious about population data, voter numbers, and licenses issued by the town? Boxborough Town Clerk Rebecca Harris provided Boxborough News with annual data that her office keeps in the normal course of business.

With respect to population, the town counted 5,314 total residents on the 2023 annual street listing. In 2022, the town counted 5,301 residents. For further comparison, the population ten years prior, in 2013, was 5,172.

Voter data shows Boxborough had 4,036 registered voters as of the May 2023 town election. The town also processed 302 voter registrations or other “changes in status” over the course of 2023. In 2022, there were 4,029 registered voters. Ten years prior, in 2013, there were 3,401 registered voters.

In 2023, there were 38 births, defined as “children born this year to parents living in Boxborough,” and 24 deaths, defined as “people living in Boxborough who died this year or died in Boxborough, living elsewhere.” In 2022, the town recorded 49 births and 26 deaths.

Harris also provided the following tallies on other paperwork processed by the Town Clerk’s office in 2023: 12 marriage licenses, 633 dog licenses, and 7 kennel licenses. In 2022, there were 13 marriage licenses, 620 dog licenses, and 5 kennel licenses.
