At a virtual meeting of the Boxborough Sustainability Committee January 12, Littleton Electric Light and Water Department encouraged homeowners to apply for Green Rewards rebates to save “natural resources and money.”

Connor Reardon, LELWD’s Energy and Sustainability Manager, said that the company has received grants to help customers reduce a “home’s environmental impact” and lower electric bills. To learn more, go to [lelwd.com/greenrewards](http://lelwd.com/greenrewards).

Homeowners can receive Green Rewards for installing a solar system, purchasing energy-efficient appliances, choosing renewable energy electricity, receiving a free energy assessment, installing an electric vehicle charger, and purchasing LED bulbs and low-flow water products.

Reardon reported that the program allows the largest installation of solar panels to receive $8,000 in assistance. He says six Boxborough homes have installed solar arrays during the last three years, and two Boxborough homeowners have applied for 2024 solar grants; $50,000 is available for 2024 solar installation incentives.

BSC Chair Francie Nolde said she will encourage Campanelli, the developers of proposed buildings near Route 495, to talk with Reardon about solar installations which might benefit both Campanelli and LELWD.

The remainder of the BSC meeting was spent revising the town’s sustainability policy which will go to the Boxborough Select Board for approval at its February 26 meeting. BSC member Kate Davies led the discussion of the latest draft she and member Richard Garrison presented to the committee, emphasizing that these are “guiding principles” which will drive the sustainability plans of the town.

The committee added a sense of urgency regarding climate change damage to the town from flooding, drought, and severe storms. Revisions also emphasized the importance of protecting the town’s established open space and conservation land. The BSC also added the need for a manager to oversee the town’s sustainability plans and projects.

The committee unanimously approved the final draft. The BSC will next meet on Thursday, February 8, at 7 p.m.
