At its meeting on January 9, the Boxborough Finance Committee discussed a number of topics, including a Proposition 2 ½ override, the Acton-Boxborough Regional School District (ABRSD) budget, and several warrant articles.

FinCom discussed whether an override for Boxborough was necessary given the town’s strong financial position and excess levy capacity. There was strong agreement among FinCom members that Boxborough did not need an override at this time.

Several FinCom members expressed concern about the ABRSD budget. Maria Neyland, FinCom Chair, said an $8M increase was simply not sustainable. Member Becky Neville said the ABRSD annual year-end audit shows that district administration and instructional leadership costs have increased by 29% between the 2018 and 2023 school years.

At their last meeting, the Acton Leadership Group (ALG) discussed a $10M override, but did not finalize that number nor did they decide how the funds would be allocated between the school budget and the Acton town budget. The ALG plans to meet again on January 29.

If the override in Acton fails, Boxborough would be assessed a 6.7% increase over the fiscal year 2024 budget of $14M for an additional $960K. If the override passes, Boxborough could be assessed somewhere between 6.7% and 16.8% over last year’s budget. Once Acton has finalized its plans for the override, FinCom will calculate the Boxborough tax rate for the school budget versus the tax rate for everything else.

FinCom also discussed several warrant articles, including the re-design of the interior of the library at a cost of $650K, an ambulance at a cost of $475K (less funds set aside from ambulance receipts), $300K for Other Post Employment Benefits, $80K for a traffic study, and $80K for a hybrid police cruiser. No votes were taken on any of these items.

FinCom meets every Tuesday at 7 p.m. at Town Hall. All FinCom meetings are hybrid.
