[The following was written by Sargent Memorial Library Director Peishan Bartley.]*

Back in August, the Library was visited by two "First Amendment auditors". Their visit and subsequently posted video caused a lot of disruption to our operation, but time went by and we soldiered on. On Wednesday 1/10/2024, one of them came back with a different partner with the specific intent to elicit a response from a staff member. While they were here, they kept their voices low, but their intention and the comments they made were threatening. They eventually left after about an hour.

I have been told that the recordings have been posted online. It will possibly lead to phone calls and social media comments from people outside of our community who do not know us. If your call is not picked up immediately, please go ahead and leave a voice message or contact us by email. We are here to help you, but we may be fielding the calls in order to protect ourselves. Same goes with the comment section of our social media accounts.

We chose this profession because we are community-service-minded book lovers. We love being able to connect with our patrons, talk about books and movies and databases, and run interesting programs. We believe in providing a safe and welcoming space for the public, and offering you resources that would entertain, inspire, and maybe even challenge you to think differently. Unfortunately, even though our own community has been supportive and wonderful to work with, the greater world is making it harder and harder for us to carry out our mission. These incidents have left us feeling targeted and vulnerable. As I've written to you before, we are firm believers in the fundamental rights that are protected by the Constitution, and also in kindness, compassion, common decency, and respecting each other's boundaries. The library should be a safe and welcoming place for everyone and anyone, including the staff. We will continue our work to keep it so. If you have any questions about this, my door is always open. Thank you.

Sincerely,

Peishan Bartley

Sargent Memorial Library Director
