On Friday, January 12, the Town of Boxborough relaunched its website, [https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/) . The changes are intended to “improve the website, not only visually, but make it more accessible to the community,” says Assistant Town Administrator Rajon Hudson. The goal is to “enhance the viewer experience and present a better professional representation for our current web footprint.”

The town has been working on the redesign since the summer of 2023, after voters approved $14,000 in funding “for the purpose of redesigning and making content upgrades to the Town website” at Annual Town Meeting in May 2023. The team involved in the project included Town Clerk Rebecca Harris, administrative support staff from the offices of Town Administrator, Police, Fire, DPW, and Assessor, as well as Assistant TA Hudson, in collaboration with representatives from website vendor CivicPlus.

At this time, no further changes to the website are expected. “We plan to reassess any further needs or updates within the next year or two,” says Hudson.
