Boxborough’s Capital Committee met on January 17 to review the town’s capital plan for fiscal year 2025.

The FY2025 capital plan is projected at somewhere between $3.9M and $4.5M. Some of the larger items include: $1.5M for the purchase of land for a public safety building, $650K for the interior redesign/reconfiguration of the library, $475K for an ambulance, $300K for culvert and road repairs, $200K for a chlorine system at Hager Well (this cost is shared with the Acton-Boxborough Regional School District based on usage), $171K for the Blanchard Elementary School playground, $115K for a wing lawn mower, and $100K for painting the exterior of Town Hall.

The plan also calls for the replacement of three vehicles including a police vehicle, the assessor’s vehicle, and the animal control vehicle. The cost of the animal control vehicle is shared with the other two towns (Stow and Littleton) serviced by the animal control officer.

The Select Board and FinCom will meet on Saturday, February 3 at 8:30 a.m. at Town Hall to discuss the proposed capital plan and funding sources for each item. Funding sources include the Community Preservation Act, bonding, taxation, and free cash.

The Capital Committee, created last year, consists of two members of the Finance Committee, two members of the Select Board, and the Town Administrator. The committee reviews capital needs over $10,000 with a useful life of more than 5 years. Gary Kushner, a member of both the CapCom and the Finance Committee, developed the first capital plan 17 years ago as a way to track the town’s capital needs and spread them out over a period of time. The Capital Committee continues this process.
