At its January 16 meeting, the Finance Committee (FinCom) heard from Library Director Peishan Bartley and Library Trustee Mary Brolin about the library’s proposed budget for fiscal year 2025.

The budget presented by Bartley included new software for booking rooms at the library, a website upgrade for content management, and 150 hours of pay for substitute assistant librarians to cover vacation time for part time employees. A final budget is pending the addition of a COLA (Cost of Living Adjustment) for employees and a salary increase for the director position.

Bartley also presented a possible warrant article for the renovation/reconfiguration of the library. Bartley explained that while the library is the one of the newest buildings in town, there are some areas that are underutilized and other areas where the space could be made more user friendly. The reconfiguration would include more areas for quiet studies and video calls, and spaces for small groups. While the architect’s plans call for $650,000 in renovations, Bartley said that some of those costs could be scaled back.

Brolin indicated that she planned to request a $100,000 grant from the Boxborough Library foundation to offset the costs.

Some FinCom members expressed concern over the construction costs. Other members felt that given the projected increase in the Acton-Boxborough Regional School District budget and the need for a new fire station, this was not the year to make this request.

Additional warrant articles included a supplemental article at $50,000 to replace the roof on the library building, over and above the $175,000 that was approved at last year’s town meeting; a $25,000 article to replace a booster water pump and $15,000 for an HVAC pump.

FinCom’s next meeting is scheduled for January 23 at 7 p.m. at Town Hall and will be accessible via Zoom. Town Planner Alec Wade and the Recreation Commission are scheduled to present budgets.
