At its meeting on January 23, the Finance Committee (Fin Com) reviewed proposed budgets for fiscal year 2025 for all Land Use departments as well the Recreation Commission.

Town Planner Alec Wade presented the Land Use and Permitting Department budget. There was a modest increase of $2K for consulting services. The salary line for the inspector’s office was increased to reflect “step 1” on the town’s Classification and Compensation Plan. Wade explained that this was done as a precaution in case the current part time building inspector could not continue in this capacity. Committee members indicated that they would not support this increase because they did not want to tax residents for something that may not happen. No vote was taken.

Wade presented level funded and level service budgets for the Conservation Commission, the Planning Board, the Zoning Board of Appeals, and the Economic Development Committee. Expenses for meeting secretaries for the Planning Board and ZBA will be moved to the Town Planner’s budget.

Wade reported that the budget for the Board of Health reflects modest increases for the cost of services provided by the Nashoba Associated Boards of Health for nursing, landfill monitoring, and environmental services. The expense line for the mosquito control budget is pending.

Recreation Commission (Rec Com) Chair Megan Conner and member Hilary Greven presented the Rec Com budget. Salaries for programs such as Flerra Summer Playground were increased by 6%, which includes a 3% COLA (Cost of Living Adjustment). Expenses remained flat.

Most of the discussion centered on whether Rec Com can be a self-sustaining department where revenues from programs pay for salaries and expenses. Currently, it’s difficult to track that information. Rec Com plans to purchase a software package in the current fiscal year called “MyRec.com” to manage and schedule programs, track incoming applications, as well as track revenues and expenses.

In other matters, the Fin Com discussed the Dispatch budget which included $198K to cover future expenses for the Regional Dispatch program. Committee members said they do not want to tax residents for something that doesn’t happen until FY2028. They suggested that this expense be removed from the FY2025 budget. They also asked that costs associated with Annual Town Meeting and Fall Town Meeting be clearly delineated in the budget.

The next Fin Com meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, January 30 at Town Hall. The Committee plans to discuss the police budget.
