At its meeting on Wednesday, January 31, the Building Committee voted 9 to 3 in favor of locating a new fire station on the property at 70-72 Stow Road. The property is currently owned by the Boxborough Housing Board and was purchased with the intent of building affordable housing.

On January 18, the Housing Board passed a motion to inform the Building Committee that the Housing Board will accept payment for the property at 70-72 Stow Road in the amount of $1,200,000, according to the terms of a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) that was prepared in 2011.

The procedure for changing the land use from housing to municipal use (fire station) will require a two-thirds vote in a town meeting.

Although the Stow Road property is large enough to accommodate a public safety facility that would house both fire and police, the consensus of the committee is to build only a fire station on the property. The police station will remain on Mass Ave and will be improved at a later time.

The Building Committee will meet on Feb. 21 to discuss process, procedure, and timing of warrant articles for the land purchase and reuse at upcoming town meetings.
