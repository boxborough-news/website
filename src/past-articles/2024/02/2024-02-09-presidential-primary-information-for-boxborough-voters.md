Tuesday, March 5 is “Super Tuesday,” the day when 15 states and one U.S. territory vote in the Presidential Primary. In Massachusetts, the ballot also includes seats for the Democratic and Republican State Committees as well as Democratic and Republican Town Committees.

The last day to register to be eligible to vote in the primary election is Saturday, Feb. 24. Boxborough residents can register at Town Hall, 29 Middle Road, until 5 p.m. or online until midnight by going to [sec.state.ma.us/OVR/.](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/OVR/)

Early voting begins on Feb. 24 at Town Hall from 1 p.m. to 5 p.m and continues on Monday, Feb. 26 through Thursday, Feb. 29 between the hours of 11 a.m. and 2 p.m.

The last day to apply for a vote-by-mail ballot for the Presidential Primary is Tuesday, Feb. 27; applications must be received by the Town Clerk by 5 p.m.

In-person voting on March 5 is at Boxborough Town Hall. Polls are open from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m.
