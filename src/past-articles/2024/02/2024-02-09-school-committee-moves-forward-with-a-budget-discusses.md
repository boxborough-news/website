At its February 1 meeting, the School Committee heard a second presentation by Superintendent Peter Light on the FY2025 budget, which included details on Facilities & Capital, Transportation, EdTech, and Proposed Fees. The proposed fees include a tuition increase for all-day kindergarten, increased athletic fees, increased parking fees at the high school, and instituting a global student activities fee.

The district is facing a $9.5M funding deficit, driven primarily by personnel, special education, and health insurance costs. The Town of Acton is preparing for a “Proposition 2½” override vote (to increase taxes beyond the state levy limit).

At the School Committee’s last meeting on January 18, Superintendent Light presented two preliminary budgets: the “A” budget, to use if the override passes, and the “B” budget, to use if the override does not pass. The Committee is moving forward with the “A” budget and is urging residents of Acton to pass the override.

The “A” budget (if override passes) is a $2 million reduction from a level-services budget. Overall, the “A” budget would require $7.5 million in additional funding for schools. Boxborough’s total assessment would be $16 million, a 15.59 percent increase from last year.

During the public comment period, Boxborough Finance Committee member Gary Kushner expressed concern that the School Committee is not giving adequate consideration to Boxborough’s perspective. He told the School Committee, “we’ve supported the schools historically…but the problem we have is that we have a high population of citizens with fixed incomes. We are concerned the population within Boxborough cannot support a total increase of 15.59 percent.”

Later in the meeting, School Committee Chair Adam Klein commented, “I think we need to equally be responsive to a town that doesn’t have to go to an override but still gets to vote our budget and we need to consider, what if we get a very strong feedback from the Boxborough community, the Fin Com and the Select Board, that says ‘15.59 percent is untenable for our community.’ I say that as a resident of Boxborough speaking for my constituents, that’s a serious thing. …[N]ot only do we need to consider where our additional savings are to save some of those student-facing things, but what are the additional cuts we may need to make - to actually get an agreement from both communities on a budget - that don’t affect student-facing services…. [W]e have to be responsive to our communities because we need them in the end to vote the budget.”

Klein also announced that School Committee members will hold office hours at Sargent Memorial Library in Boxborough from 2:30-3:30 pm on February 1, 6, 8, 13, 15, 22, and 26.

The School Committee will hold a budget workshop on February 10 and expects to vote on the budget at its March 21 meeting. The Town of Acton’s override vote will take place in April, and the resulting budget will go to Acton and Boxborough Town Meetings for approval.
