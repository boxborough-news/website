On Sunday, March 10, the Music Sunday Concert will take place at 4 p.m. at the United Church of Christ in Boxborough, Congregational on the corner of Middle Rd. & Rte. 111 (723 Mass. Ave).

A variety of local musical talent will be featured along with the church’s Senior Choir and Sound of Peace Group. Come and bring your family and friends for an enjoyable musical afternoon.

Light refreshments being offered following this free program. For more information contact Gloria Roe (978) 502-9625 or [boxborochurch@verizon.net](mailto:boxborochurch@verizon.net).
