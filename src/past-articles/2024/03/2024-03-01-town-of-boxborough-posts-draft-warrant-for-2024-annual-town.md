On Monday, Feb. 26, the Town of Boxborough posted a draft warrant for 2024 Annual Town Meeting. The draft warrant currently contains 47 articles, three of which will be considered in a Special Town Meeting to occur within the Annual Town Meeting.

The Town Meeting warrant will be finalized in early April and mailed to every residence in Boxborough at the end of April.

Town Meeting will begin on Monday, May 13 at 7 p.m. and is scheduled to continue as needed on May 14, 15, 16, and 20.

The draft warrant is available at [https://bit.ly/48xXjvK](https://bit.ly/48xXjvK).
