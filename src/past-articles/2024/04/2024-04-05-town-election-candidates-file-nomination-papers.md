April 2 was the last day to file nomination papers with the Town Clerk for the May 2024 town election.

Offices on the 2024 town ballot are Select Board (two seats), School Committee (one seat), Planning Board (one seat), Library Trustees (two seats), Board of Health (one seat), Town Moderator (one seat), and Constable (one seat).

The following individuals have filed nomination papers:

- Select Board: Dave McKiernan, Bob Stemple
- School Committee: Adam Klein
- Planning Board: Rebecca Verner, Than Stuntz
- Library Trustees: Mary Brolin, Teresina Tremblay
- Board of Health: Lori Lotterman
- Town Moderator: Dennis Reip
- Constable: Owen Neville

Candidates have until April 18 to withdraw from nomination. On May 2, the League of Women Voters will hold a Candidates Forum at 7 p.m. at Town Hall. The town election will be held on Tuesday, May 21.

For more information on town government and running for office, contact Town Clerk Rebecca Harris at 978-264-1727 or [rharris@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:rharris@boxborough-ma.gov).
