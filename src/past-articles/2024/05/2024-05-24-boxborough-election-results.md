On Tuesday, May 21, Boxborough voters went to the polls to fill nine town positions. The only contested race was for one Planning Board seat. According to the Town Clerk's results, incumbent Rebecca Verner received 336 votes and challenger Nathaniel Stuntz received 333 votes.

Results for uncontested races are as follows:

- Select Board: David McKiernan (535 votes) and Bob Stemple (468 votes)
- School Committee: Adam Klein (549 votes)
- Library Trustees: Mary Brolin (558 votes) and Teresina Tremblay (491 votes)
- Board of Health: Lori Lotterman (575 votes)
- Town Moderator: Dennis Reip (584 votes)
- Constable: Owen Neville (580 votes)

Of 4,081 total registered voters, 679 voters cast ballots. The Town Clerk’s “Town Election Results” can be found at [bit.ly/4brBJLK](https://bit.ly/4brBJLK).
