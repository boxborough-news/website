The Boxborough Housing Board (BHB) met at Town Hall on Thursday, May 23, to discuss the next steps for the Homeownership Opportunity for Preservation and Equity (HOPE) program to assist the owners of affordable homes with the maintenance and preservation of their units.

Recognizing that the affordable homes monitored by the BHB are aging, the BHB welcomed Town Administrator Mike Johns to outline the legal guidance he had received. He explained that the BHB must prepare Requests for Proposals (RPFs), with attention given to quality, schedule, and cost (which must be based upon prevailing wages), as detailed in respondent bids.

With the Town Administrator’s help, a draft RFP will be prepared by BHB members Korinne Stephens and Ron Vogel. After costs are estimated, a pilot HOPE program will be proposed to the Boxborough Affordable Housing Trust for support. If the pilot program succeeds, funding will be sought from broader sources.

The BHB also discussed how to ensure that the BHB can provide proper guidance to the town in matters related to affordable housing, such as in the resale of affordable homes and in ways to improve town support.

The next meeting of the BHB will be a public, in-person meeting at Town Hall at 7 p.m. on Thursday, June 13.
