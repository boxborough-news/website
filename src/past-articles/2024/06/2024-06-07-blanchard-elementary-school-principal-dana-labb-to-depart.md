At the June 6 ABRSD School Committee meeting, Superintendent Peter Light announced that Blanchard Elementary School Principal Dana Labb has accepted a position as Assistant Superintendent for Harvard Public Schools and will leave the district at the end of the school year.

Superintendent Light explained that “at this point in the school year, we’re well outside the window where we would be able to hire a permanent principal… so we are likely looking at an interim principal.” Superintendent Light is working with Blanchard staff on next steps and expects to reach out to the Blanchard community with an update next week.

Principal Labb has served as principal of Blanchard for ten years. Superintendent Light wished Labb well, calling him “an absolutely incredible educator, an incredible leader, and an incredible person.”

In a letter to the Blanchard community earlier this week about his departure, Principal Labb stated, “the past ten years have been the highlight of my career, and being part of this incredible community has been one of the best experiences of my life. … I want to express my deep gratitude for the support you have shown throughout my time here. Together, we have achieved so much, and I am proud of the positive impact we have made on our students' lives.”
