The Boxborough Community Preservation Committee had its first meeting of the 2024-2025 CPC cycle on June 6. Town Planner Alec Wade introduced Julia Collyer, a CPC intern who is a Boxborough resident and student at Clemson University. Collyer presented a draft version of the 2025 Community Preservation Plan, including a description of the contents, background, and methodology. The plan was well received by the committee, which felt that it would be a big improvement over what exists today.

Currently, the CPC has no formal award mechanism. Wade briefly reviewed the concept of having the Boxborough CPC issue award letters to committees and groups who receive CPC funds. The committee was in favor of this concept.

Chair Ron Vogel led the committee through review and approval of the FY 2026 CPC project documents including the timeline for FY 2026 CPA project applications, application procedures, the preliminary application form, and final application cover sheet. Preliminary applications are due September 26, and final applications are due October 31.

The next meeting of the CPC is Wednesday July 10 at 7:30 p.m. in the Grange Room of the Town Hall.
