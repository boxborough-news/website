At its meeting on June 10, the Select Board voted unanimously to appoint John Kivlan as Boxborough’s next fire chief. Kivlan currently serves as Fire Captain for the Chelmsford Fire Department. He is also Team Coordinator for the Massachusetts Hazardous Materials Division, member of the Joint Hazard Incident Response Team, instructor at the Massachusetts Firefighting Academy, and adjunct professor at Middlesex Community College.

Before choosing Kivlan, the Select Board also interviewed Arthur Cheeks, Deputy Fire Chief for the Town of Groton, and Robert Wollner, Captain of the Belmont Fire Department. Town Administrator Michael Johns noted that all three were very strong candidates and that there were a total of sixteen applicants for the position.

After a presentation by a representative from the Metropolitan Area Planning Council, the Board voted to approve two policies required for Boxborough to apply for “Green Community” designation: a Fuel Efficient Vehicle Policy and a non-binding Energy Reduction Plan that aims to achieve 20% reduction in energy use in five years. These two policies represent the final two of five requirements for Green Community designation.

With the adoption of both policies, Boxborough can now be designated as a Green Community. This allows the town to receive designation funds and apply for further grant funding. The Green Community program is funded by the Massachusetts Department of Energy Resources to help municipalities reduce energy usage and invest in energy efficiency.

The Select Board also discussed their intent to dissolve the current Building Committee and create a new Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) to oversee the design and construction of the new fire station.

Board members debated the structure of the FSBC, including the number of members, whether the Select Board and/or Finance Committee should have a voting member, and the process to solicit and choose volunteers to serve as members. The Board’s only vote regarding the FSBC was to decide that a member of the Finance Committee will be a voting member of the FSBC. The Board expects to decide the FSBC structure and membership at its meeting on July 8.

The Board also heard from Boxborough Police Chief John Szewczyk, who introduced the new volunteer police department chaplain, Father Theodore Barbas. Barbas, a Boxborough resident who has been a Greek orthodox priest for more than thirty years, will be available as a resource for police officers as well as for town staff and residents. The Select Board welcomed Barbas and thanked him for his service to the town.

Finally, the Select Board conducted its annual reorganization. Kristin Hilberg will continue as Chair and Priya Sundaram will continue as Clerk.
