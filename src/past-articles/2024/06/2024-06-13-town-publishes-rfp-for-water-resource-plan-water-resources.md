On June 5, the Town of Boxborough published a Request for Proposals (RFP) for town-wide water resource planning services.

According to the RFP description on the town website, the town is “soliciting proposals from licensed professionals for the development of a town-wide water resource plan… that will address long-term water and wastewater needs.”

On June 4, the night before the RFP was published, the Water Resources Committee (WRC) met to prepare for the RFP process, which will include answering questions, participating in a pre-submission conference on June 20, and reviewing bids after the July 19 deadline.

The RFP asks potential consultants for both qualifications and pricing, and the WRC discussed how they will negotiate with potential contractors on both the scope of work and the pricing.

The WRC went on to discuss how some area towns, including Acton and Littleton, are evaluating whether to participate in the Massachusetts Water Resources Authority (MWRA) municipal water system, and whether Boxborough needs to consider potential participation in MWRA as well.

The essential question is “whether [Boxborough] is going to need a municipal water supply or whether we can keep doing what we’re doing indefinitely.” This fundamental question is within the scope of the RFP, and the WRC expects that it will be a central component of the water resources plan to be developed by the consultant that the town hires.
