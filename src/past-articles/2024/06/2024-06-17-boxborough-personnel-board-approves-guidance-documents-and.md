At its meeting on June 11, the Personnel Board voted unanimously to adopt “guidance documents” explaining the Domestic Violence Leave statute and the Family Medical Leave Act; both of which describe allowable time-off under those laws. The guidance documents will make it easier for employees to access information about those laws.

The board also voted unanimously to approve the “Open Door and Supervisor Communication Guidelines.” The intent of those guidelines is to affirm the Town’s commitment to open communication by and among the Town Administrator, Department Heads, and all employees. Personnel Board member Allan MacLean said that “frequent, candid, and clear communication helps to build a culture of trust within the workplace that supports employees so they can do their best work.”

Town Administrator Michael Johns said these guidelines “put into written practice what for me has always been the way to do business.” Johns plans to implement the guidelines right away.

The board also discussed what it means to be an “Employer of Choice.” The process involves a systematic evaluation of every aspect of the “employee life cycle,” including recruiting, compensation, development, performance management, rewards, end of employment, and the “institutionalization of processes and metrics to make it sustainable for the long term.” Chair Lee Slade is in the process of finalizing the board’s proposal for approval by the Select Board.

In other matters, Rajon Hudson, Assistant Town Administrator, updated the board on the employee survey conducted earlier this year and plans for townwide training including performance management for both individual contributors and department heads, as well as “transformative leadership” training also known as situational leadership.

The next meeting of the Personnel Board is scheduled for Wednesday, July 10, at 5:30 p.m. at Town Hall.
