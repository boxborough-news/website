On Friday, June 14, the Town of Boxborough held a recount of votes for the position of Planning Board in the May 21, 2024 election.

The results of the recount were the same as the results on election day. Incumbent Rebecca Verner won the Planning Board seat with 336 votes and challenger Nathaniel Stuntz received 333 votes.

After the election materials were retrieved from the vault and town counsel Devan Braun from KP Law gave an overview of the recount process, election workers hired by the Town Clerk set to work hand-counting all 679 ballots.

Election workers acting as “readers” were instructed to examine each ballot, determine “who the voter intended to vote for,” and then state the name of that candidate to their election worker partner acting as the “recorder.” The recorder marked down the votes, which were then tallied once the counting process was finished.

Both Verner and Stuntz were present for the recount along with their attorney or representative as well as a team of “observers” who were stationed by each of three tables where the election workers were counting.

The Board of Registrars, led by Chair and Town Clerk Rebecca Harris, conducted votes on contested ballots and also held a hearing on rejected ballots.

The entire process lasted approximately 90 minutes.

Said Town Clerk Harris, “It was gratifying to get confirmation that the election process works, and I so appreciate everyone putting in the work to get this done."
