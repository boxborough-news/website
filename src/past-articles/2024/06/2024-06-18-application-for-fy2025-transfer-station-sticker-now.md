Applications for Boxborough Transfer Station Stickers for fiscal year 2025 are now available.

The fiscal year 2025 stickers are valid from July 1, 2024 until June 30, 2025. Stickers may be purchased online or in person at Town Hall.

Stickers cost $150 for 2 vehicles in a household. There is no charge for senior citizens and those with Affordable Housing Trust Waivers.

More information is available at : [https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/266/Transfer-Station.](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/266/Transfer-Station.)
