The Boxborough Housing Board (BHB) met at Town Hall on Thursday, June 13. It reviewed progress in the Homeowner Opportunity for Preservation Extension program (HOPE), a program designed to assist the owners of affordable homes with the preservation of their aging units.

A Request for Proposals (RFP) is being prepared for the replacement of the affordable-home roofs at Boxborough Meadows. The roof replacements will comprise the main activity of a pilot HOPE program.

Housing Board member Ron Vogel reported that he had adapted an RFP that was developed for work at Steele Farm. He has submitted the revised RFP to Assistant Town Administrator Rajon Hudson for further refinement and hopes to issue the RFP within several weeks.

The BHB is also preparing a proposal to the Boxborough Affordable Housing Trust (BAHT) for funding to replace the roofs. The BHB intends to submit the proposal to the BAHT after a satisfactory approximation of the project’s cost is established by the responses to the RFP. A draft proposal was reviewed and edited at the meeting.

The BHB also discussed whether it should update the Town’s Housing Production Plan (HPP). The board decided against this effort at this time because Boxborough’s Affordability Ratio will likely remain above 10% through 2040 and because a housing-needs assessment for Boxborough has recently been provided by Metro West Collaborative Development.

The next meeting of the BHB will be a public, in-person meeting at Town Hall at 7 p.m. on Thursday, July 11.
