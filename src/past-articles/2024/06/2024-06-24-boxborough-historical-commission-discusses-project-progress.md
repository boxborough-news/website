At its meeting on June 20, the Boxborough Historical Commission reviewed several historically related projects that are underway in town. The ongoing maintenance and repair work at the Museum, undertaken by the Boxborough DPW, was discussed as was the storage space in the basement.

Chair Alan Rohwer announced that signs marking the location of the Boxborough railroad depot and the Luther Blanchard homesite had been received and would be installed by the DPW in the near future.

The Commission reviewed the presentation on Lucie Hager, Boxborough’s outstanding 19th century historian and poet, that was delivered on May 23. The group was quite pleased with the interest and attendance. This presentation is now available at [boxboroughhistoricalsociety.org](http://boxboroughhistoricalsociety.org/). It was noted, with pleasure, that the tradition of having a Boxborough student portray Lucie at Fifer’s Day was continued in 2024.

The next meeting of the Commission is Thursday, August 22 at the Boxborough Museum, 575 Middle Road at 7 p.m.
