On Sunday, October 6, six Boxborough residents participated in the Boston Marathon Jimmy Fund Walk presented by Hyundai as part of “Team Addie.”

Keith Baum, Eric Baum, Kenneth Dow, Erika Baum, Laura Baum, and Gail Dow of Boxborough, along with thousands of other walkers, participated in the iconic annual event that raised funds to support all forms of adult and pediatric care and research at the Dana-Farber Cancer Institute. The Jimmy Fund Walk has raised more than $176 million for Dana-Farber in its 35-year history, raising a record-breaking $9.4 million in 2023.

Team Addie consists primarily of family and friends that started walking in 2014. While on the subway in Boston coming home from work 14 years ago, Ken Dow saw a poster promoting the Jimmy Fund Walk, and it struck a chord. He says, “I’ll always remember the first two years we walked because we started at Dana-Farber, and we would turn around and wave to the patients on the overhead glass bridge.

Those two years, my granddaughter, Addie, was on that bridge, in the hospital receiving treatment. Today, she is a cancer survivor walking with us, and we are so lucky. Our family has walked every year since Addison’s diagnosis, so that more patients can become cancer survivors like Addie and will have a chance to grow up and follow their dreams.”

The Jimmy Fund Walk is the only organized walk permitted to use the famed Boston Marathon course, and participants have the flexibility to choose from four distance options: 5K walk (from Dana-Farber’s Longwood Medical Campus); 10K walk (from Newton); Half Marathon walk (from Wellesley); and Marathon walk (from Hopkinton).

Team Addie participants are primarily from Acton and Boxborough. Because this is a family group of many ages, they have always walked the shorter 5K distance that starts at Boston Children’s Hospital and ends at Copley Square. Even though they are not walking the longer distance, they run into hundreds of people who are on their final leg of their longer walks. They are always amazed at the number of groups, families, and friends that are all walking to secure a cure for cancer.

While this year’s Jimmy Fund Walk is over, it’s not tolate to donate to Team Addie. Those interested in supporting Team Addie can visit [https://bit.ly/4f7YAy1](https://bit.ly/4f7YAy1).
