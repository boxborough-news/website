On Sunday morning October 27, families enjoyed a surprise visitor at the annual “Truck or Treat” hosted by the Boxborough Fire Department and the Boxborough Police Department.

About halfway through the event, excited kids and their caregivers crossed the street (with the help of Boxborough Emergency Response Corps members) from the fire station to the Blanchard parking lot to watch the Massachusetts State Police Air Wing Unit land a helicopter behind the school.

Throughout the morning, kids enjoyed exploring fire trucks, police cruisers, and other emergency response vehicles, as well as the town’s “MART” shuttle bus and a “book truck” from the Sargent Memorial Library, all while collecting candy from the friendly faces of Boxborough’s firefighters and police officers.
