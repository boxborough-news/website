At its meeting on March 13 at the Boxborough Museum, the Boxborough Historical Commission unanimously voted in favor of recommending three articles relating to the museum building that have been placed on the warrant for the May 2025 Boxborough Annual Town Meeting.

The articles are designed to correct issues in the building by replacing the aging roof, which has leaked, replacing the two leaking back doors, and caulking and painting the exterior windows. The goal is to maintain the integrity of the envelope of the town building.

Chair Alan Rohwer noted that, while the first floor of the building is used as the Museum, the basement is used as the locked hub for Boxborough’s public safety communication network and is also used as a storage area by the Town Clerk, Town Planner, Recreation Commission, and Public Celebrations and Ceremonies Committee.

The Commission then briefly reviewed other items of historical interest, including progress on the renovation of the Steele Farm icehouse and farmhouse and the plans in surrounding communities to commemorate this year’s 250th anniversary of April 19, 1775.

The next meeting of the Commission will take place on Thursday May 8 at 7 p.m. at the Boxborough Museum.
