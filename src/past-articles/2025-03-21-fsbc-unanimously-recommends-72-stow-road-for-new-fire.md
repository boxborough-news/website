At their meeting on March 20, the Boxborough Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) voted unanimously to recommend to the Select Board that the town’s new fire station be built at 72 Stow Road.

Accordingly, the FSBC also voted unanimously to recommend Special Town Meeting Warrant Article 2, “New Firehouse Design Funding,” as revised to reflect the chosen site location and the updated dollar amount ($2,622,445) for the next phase of the fire station project.

Before conducting the site selection vote, FSBC members acknowledged that there is opposition to the Stow Road site from abutters and other members of the public.

However, FSBC members expressed they could not vote for the Mass Ave site because a fire station at 502 Mass Ave is estimated to cost roughly $6 million more (an estimated $39.8 million compared to $33.7 million for 72 Stow Road) and is expected to be a less functional facility than a fire station at 72 Stow Road.

Members also noted other concerns with building at 502 Mass Ave, including the cost of a temporary swing space for the fire department and potential disruption to fire department operations; the potential limitations to future expansion of the neighboring police station; and assorted site challenges related to wetlands.

Before making their decision, the Committee also reviewed Owners Project Manager Vertex’s site selection matrix. After some adjustments to Vertex’s recommended scores, the final scoring was 57 points (436 weighted points) for 72 Stow Road and 50 points (353 weighted points) for 502 Mass Ave. FSBC members noted that the matrix scoring alone did not dictate the committee’s site selection choice.

The FSBC also discussed the related municipal zoning issue that the town is currently working to address. While there may have been a path to building a fire station at 502 Mass Ave without an amendment to zoning bylaws, the town’s current understanding is that a zoning bylaw amendment is required in order to build a fire station at 72 Stow Road.

In January, Building Inspector Ed Cataldo informed the FSBC of his opinion that Boxborough’s zoning bylaws do not allow a fire station to be built anywhere in town and that a zoning bylaw amendment would be required to correct this apparent error. The Select Board has brought forward a proposed zoning bylaw amendment for Annual Town Meeting that would allow municipal buildings in any district in town by right. The Planning Board began a public hearing on the Select Board’s proposal at their meeting on March 17 and the hearing is continued to March 31.

The FSBC’s next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, April 10, at 7 p.m.
