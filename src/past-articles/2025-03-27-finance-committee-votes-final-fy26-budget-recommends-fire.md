At its meeting on March 25, the Boxborough Finance Committee (FinCom) voted the remaining department budgets and the Acton-Boxborough Regional School District assessment of $16,824,370, for a total final budget of $29,295,131. This is a 4.32% increase over the current fiscal year.

The operating budget does not include several other warrant articles representing an additional $96,000 in taxation, nor does it include the collective bargaining agreements (CBAs) for police, fire, and the Department of Public Works, which have not been finalized by the Select Board. The CBAs will be funded through taxation. FinCom also voted 6-0 to recommend and fund through bonding Special Town Meeting Warrant Article 2 for the design and development of construction drawings for the proposed fire station at 72 Stow Road.

If Article 2 passes at the Special Town Meeting (within the Annual Town Meeting), it will be funded through a short-term, interest-only Bond Anticipatory Note (BAN). The BAN will be refinanced into a long-term construction bond and voted at Annual Town Meeting in May 2026. There will be no payment on the BAN until Fiscal Year 2027. The next FinCom meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, April 1 at 7 p.m. at Town Hall. FinCom meetings are hybrid.
