At their meeting on March 24, the Boxborough Select Board reviewed the warrant for Annual Town Meeting (ATM), including the Special Town Meeting (STM) within the Annual Town Meeting, which will begin on Monday, May 12 at Blanchard Memorial School.

Before voting unanimously to approve the warrant and authorize its mailing to every household, the Board reviewed each article in the warrant, making edits to some of their recommendations and voting a handful of remaining articles.

The articles generating the most discussion among Board members were STM Article 1, Municipal Governmental Zoning Allowed Across All Zoning Districts, which they recommended unanimously; STM Article 2, 72 Stow Road Firehouse Design and Bid Funding, which they recommended 4-0-1; and STM Article 6, Taylor Farm Road Parcel Acceptance, which they recommended 4-1-0.

The Select Board also heard a presentation from Personnel Board Chair Allan MacLean on ATM Article 4. Article 4 includes seven amendments to the town’s Personnel Plan and provides a 2.7% “across-the-board wage adjustment” for FY26 for non-union and non-contract employees. After discussion and questions from Board members, the Board recommended the article 4-0-1.

In other business, the Board accepted the resignation of Megan Connor from the Boxborough Recreation Commission, which she has served on for the past eleven years. The Board also heard a report from Town Administrator Michael Johns that he has begun discussions with Acton Water District Manager Matthew Mostoller about potentially bringing in water from Acton for fire suppression and other purposes.
