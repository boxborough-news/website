In order to be listed on the ballot, potential candidates for the May 2025 Boxborough town election must file nomination papers with the town clerk by Tuesday, April 1 at 5 p.m. A minimum of 25 signatures is required in order to file.

The following individuals have “pulled” and “filed” nomination papers as of Thursday, March 27. The deadline to obtain nomination papers is Friday, March 28 at 5 p.m.

Voters who wish to vote in the town election on May 20 and at the May 12 Annual Town Meeting must register to vote by 5 p.m. on Friday, May 2.

A “Candidate’s Check List” with information about getting on the ballot and campaign finance law requirements is available at [https://bit.ly/3TfHKDA](https://bit.ly/3TfHKDA).
