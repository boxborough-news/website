On Monday, March 31, Town Clerk Becky Harris confirmed that all individuals who pulled nomination papers for the May 2025 town election have filed them.

These individuals have until Thursday, April 17 to withdraw their names from the ballot.

Currently, there are contested races for the three-year Select Board seat, the Planning Board, and the Board of Library Trustees.

Voters who wish to vote in the town election on May 20 and at the May 12 Annual Town Meeting must register to vote by 5 p.m. on Friday, May 2.
