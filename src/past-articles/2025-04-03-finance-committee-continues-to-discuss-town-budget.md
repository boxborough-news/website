At its meeting on April 1, the Finance Committee (FinCom) discussed the overall budget and their presentation for Annual Town Meeting (ATM).

FinCom Chair Antony Newton said that Proposition 2 ½, which limits the amount of revenue a town may raise from local property taxes each year to fund municipal operations, coupled with the lack of projected growth (new construction), continues to make the budgeting process challenging.

The proposed fiscal year 2026 town operating budget of **$**29,313,132 is an increase of 4.57% over fiscal year 2025.

Budget drivers include increases in employee benefits, retirement assessment, the cost of property/casualty insurance, the addition of staff in the recreation department, increased enrollment in vocational education, increased Acton-Boxborough Regional School District assessment and the centralization of software services.

At ATM, voters will be asked to authorize additional debt for several capital items as well as the design fees for the new Fire Station.

FinCom also discussed the ClearGov software, which is supposed to make it easy to view and report financial data for budgeting. The data is uploaded by the town accountant and then made available in ClearGov for reporting. Despite having reached out to the ClearGov representative several times, the software presently does not provide the kind of reports FinCom needs to analyze trends and present a budget at ATM. Chair Newton was “disappointed” in the product. FinCom members want the data to be downloadable into an Excel-like format that can easily be reviewed. Currently, that is not possible.

The next FinCom meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, April 15 at 7 p.m. at Town Hall. FinCom meetings are hybrid.
