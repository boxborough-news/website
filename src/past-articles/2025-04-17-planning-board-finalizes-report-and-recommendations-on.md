On Monday, April 14, the Boxborough Planning Board met for the exclusive purpose of finalizing a report on Article 1 for Special Town Meeting, which begins on Monday, May 12.

Article 1 is a proposed zoning bylaw amendment that would include “Municipal Governmental” uses across all zoning districts in Boxborough. The Select Board brought the proposed zoning article forward to address what they believe is a “scrivener’s error” or “oversight” in the zoning bylaw. The discovery of the “error” was the Fire Station Building Committee’s realization that the zoning bylaw, as currently interpreted, would not allow the building of a fire station anywhere in town.

Although the Select Board initiated the article, the Planning Board has the ministerial duty to conduct a public hearing and issue a report and recommendation. During the meeting on April 14, Planning Board members reviewed a draft report documenting the public hearing proceedings and the recommendations of both the majority and minority positions. A copy of the report will be available at Special Town Meeting.

On March 17, the Planning Board opened the public hearing on the proposed zoning bylaw amendment. Select Board Chair Kristin Hilberg gave a history of the bylaw and emphasized, “without passing this (proposed zoning bylaw amendment) article, we literally have no zoning mechanism to build any municipal facility anywhere in Boxborough.”

The public hearing continued on March 31 and closed that evening. The Planning Board voted to recommend the article by a split vote of 3-2, and members collaborated on their respective written opinions for inclusion in the STM Warrant and final report.
