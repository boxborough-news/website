At its hybrid meeting on April 10, the Boxborough Sustainability Committee (BSC) reviewed data on Boxborough waste disposal and progress with waste reduction projects.

BSC member Richard Garrison presented annualized data from the Department of Public Works on waste disposal and recycling rates at the Boxborough transfer station. In 2023, a total of 1205 tons of waste flowed through the transfer station, of which 55% was solid waste and 45% was recycling or compost. In 2023, Boxborough disposed of a total of 1813 pounds of trash per household according to the annual solid waste survey from the Massachusetts Department of Environmental Protection (MassDEP). In contrast, the average household in Massachusetts disposed of 1383 pounds. “We’re high. The transfer station is doing some importing of waste from surrounding towns,” commented BSC chair Kate Davies. The committee expressed interest in revisiting this data as a benchmark to measure progress.

In other progress, Chair Davies shared that the swap shed article is up for a vote at the May 12 Annual Town Meeting. If approved, work on implementing the swap shed at the transfer station can begin immediately.

Chair Davies shared that the Waste Reduction Working Group held its first meeting in early April. The Working Group is composed of members of the BSC, the Select Board, FinCom, and Julia Greene, the MassDEP municipal assistance coordinator. Greene provided an overview of various Pay-As-You-Throw programs, and the Working Group aligned on high-level priorities. The Working Group’s objective is to identify a single recycling or waste reduction project most appropriate for Boxborough. The Working Group will next meet in May.

The next Sustainability Committee meeting is scheduled for May 8 at 7 p.m.
