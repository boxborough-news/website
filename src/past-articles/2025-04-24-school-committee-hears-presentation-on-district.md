At its meeting on April 17, the Acton-Boxborough School Committee heard a presentation from Joe Costello of District Management Group (DMG), the consultant hired by the district for its “strategic planning and district reorganization” project.

The purpose of the project, which was initiated in response to ongoing budget challenges, is to consider how the schools in the district can potentially be reorganized and/or consolidated to better serve the community.

In his presentation, Costello spoke about DMG’s experience, the timeline for the project, the importance of public engagement, and the two final deliverables: a written strategic plan and two to three reorganizational options.

Turning to new business, the Committee voted, with seven members abstaining, to abandon the district’s 2024 updates to Title IX-related policies and reinstate the 2020 versions of the policies.

Deputy Superintendent Andrew Shen explained that the district is reverting to the 2020 language on the advice of legal counsel in order to comply with a recent federal court decision that vacated the expanded Title IX protections implemented by the Biden administration and the resulting decision by the Department of Education to enforce the 2020 Title IX regulations. The district had implemented the expanded Title IX protections in 2024.

The Committee also did a “second read” of the district’s Student Discipline Policy. After a first read in a prior meeting, some Committee members expressed concern about the withholding of recess as a form of discipline. The policy was then revised to acknowledge the value of recess and the circumstances under which a students’ recess time might be limited. Committee members continued to express concerns with the revised policy; the policy will be discussed further at a future meeting.

The Committee also heard a presentation from RJ Grey Junior High Principal Jim Marcotte, who shared updates on student learning, chronic absenteeism, and “interrupting hate and bias,” and recognized the two School Committee members who are retiring from their positions: Ginny Kremer and Rebeccah Wilson, both of Acton.
