The Grange Room inside Town Hall was the site of the 3rd Annual Recognition Luncheon on Tuesday, April 29. Town Hall was closed to the public from 11:30 a.m. until 1 p.m. so that Town employees and volunteers could attend the event. Over 60 people from various boards, committees, and commissions as well as Town Hall staff and town employees from other departments were in attendance.

Assistant Town Administrator Rajon Hudson kicked off the luncheon by welcoming everyone. After lunch, Town Administrator Mike Johns joined Hudson at the podium to give his remarks and announce the awards. Prior to announcing the awards, Johns mentioned that the luncheon buffet was made possible because of the Select Board’s willingness to forgo their stipends.

Johns emphasized that these awards were the result of peer nominations for recognition of exceptional service to the community and the workplace. In his solicitation for awards, Johns wrote, “In my experience, I have found that nominations from peer committee members and co-workers carry a lot of weight, and remove any perception of favoritism.”

Categories of nominations were not specified, though the call for nominations gave some suggestions such as team player, unsung hero, and behind-the-scenes work-horse. Some of the nominations were unique, such as the award given to Council on Aging volunteer Barbara Birt who was recognized as “Boxborough's Energizer Bunny.”

Johns and Hudson recognized 26 individuals, reading each nomination aloud and posing for a photo with the recipient to loud applause.

Just before Johns started his closing remarks, former Town Moderator and town volunteer John Fallon rose to say that there was still one more award to be granted. Fallon came to the front of the room and recognized TA Johns with the “Steady Hands and Cool Head” award.

When asked for a statement about the luncheon and being a recipient himself, Johns wrote: ”I truly appreciated and enjoyed the opportunity to recognize and celebrate all that we as a Boxborough Team have accomplished in the past 12 months. In addition, I find it rewarding to gather the Team, share time together and break. For most of the rest of the year, we are all busy doing our important little sub-sections of Boxborough's larger work; so I find it satisfying to all get together socially.”

Johns continued, “I am really grateful of the Select Board, the individuals who did the nominating, and the award recipients who had all the amazing individual accomplishments, making today at all possible…As far as being recognized, it's the last thing I wanted, but I was really humbled, and appreciated the thought and consideration that John Fallon and the others he collaborated with put in, when they decided to secretly sneak that in.”
