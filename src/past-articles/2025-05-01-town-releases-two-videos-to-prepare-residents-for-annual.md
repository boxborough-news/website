The Town of Boxborough has posted two videos on its website to help residents prepare for Annual Town Meeting (ATM), which will begin Monday, May 12, at 7 p.m. at the Blanchard Memorial School gymnasium.

The first video is the “2025 Boxborough Annual Town Meeting Preview,” featuring Town Administrator Michael Johns and Town Moderator Dennis Reip. The video gives an overview of what to expect at town meeting, and walks through this year’s warrant articles. Johns and Reip also explain what “special town meeting” is and what “consent agenda” means. The video is available at [https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/boxborough/video/68028eca119aac0008ba9453?page=HOME](https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/boxborough/video/68028eca119aac0008ba9453?page=HOME).

The second video is the Finance Committee’s “Pre-ATM Budget Presentation, featuring Finance Committee Chair Tony Newton and Select Board member Bob Stemple. Newton and Stemple discuss the budget process, the Town’s operating budget, and sources of revenue. Newton also explains the tax levy limit, the use of bonding and free cash, and the potential for an override next year. The video is available at [https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/boxborough/video/680967d63935f70008199f45?page=HOME](https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/boxborough/video/680967d63935f70008199f45?page=HOME).
