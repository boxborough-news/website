The Boxborough Leadership Forum (BLF) held its second meeting of the year on April 30 in the Grange Room at Town Hall. Town Moderator Dennis Reip led the pre-town meeting discussion of logistics, process, and issues that may come up at Special and Annual Town Meeting. Representative participants included the Select Board, Finance Committee, School Committee, Fire Station Building Committee, and Library Trustees.

There was considerable conversation surrounding Special Town Meeting Articles 1 and 2.

Article 1 proposes amending the zoning bylaw to allow municipal governmental buildings across all zoning districts. Following the Select Board presentation in support of the article and recommendations from other boards and committees, voters will be allowed to voice their opinions at one of the microphones labeled “yes” and “no.” Reip said the debate must fall within the four corners of the warrant article; however, he plans to allow some latitude for “what if” questions. Town Planner Alec Wade and KP Law Esquire Jonathan Eichman will be available as subject matter experts on all zoning related questions.

BLF members anticipate there may be amendments from the floor to disallow zoning of municipal buildings in the Agricultural/Residential district.

Article 2 proposes bonding of $2.6 million for the design and construction drawings and all related incidental costs for a new firehouse at 72 Stow Road. Reip said Article 2 is about “funding for the design of the fire station, not about site selection,” as such debate will be limited to a discussion about funding.

BLF also discussed logistics and a backup plan in the event that the number of attendees exceeds the capacity of the Blanchard gym. At Annual Town Meeting in 2024, the town used the Blanchard Library as an overflow room, but glitches in technology created problems in communicating between the two rooms. This year, BLF members suggested that all non-registered Boxborough voters be assigned to the library, leaving more seating for registered voters. Reip said that he will continue to explore all options available.

The next BLF meeting is scheduled for June 10 at 7 p.m. in the Grange Room at Town Hall. BLF meetings are in-person.
