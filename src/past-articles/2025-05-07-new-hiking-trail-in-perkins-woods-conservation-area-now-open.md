There is a new hiking trail in the Perkins Woods conservation area. This new trail leads from Perkins Woods into the Rolling Meadows conservation area and is marked with yellow blazes. It is approximately a half mile in length and can be accessed from the existing yellow trail in Perkins Woods.

In January 2025, a small handmade bridge was constructed off site, carried into the woods, and laid over a stream crossing. During April, the trail was cut, cleared, and marked. The bridge construction and trail clearing work was done by members of the Boxborough Land Stewards, led by Dave Barnett.

When reached for comment, Barnett said the new path “provides a wonderful trail through the woods near adjacent wetlands connecting the new Perkins Woods trail to the older existing Rolling Meadows trail, adding to all the great trails we are fortunate to have here in Boxborough.”

The Perkins Woods trail system already provided connections to the Helen Volkmann Trail at Half Moon Meadow and the Fort Pond Brook conservation area, which is on Littlefield Road. It now also connects to the Rolling Meadows area, forming a large continuous trail system.

Perkins Woods can be accessed from two entrances on Sargent Road between Littlefield Road and the railroad tracks; the one closest to the tracks is marked with a red blaze; the other entrance has a parking area and a sign. There is also an entrance on Littlefield Road across from the Fort Pond Brook area.

In April 2024, the original Perkins Woods trails were cut and opened to the public. A ribbon cutting ceremony was held in September 2024.

For more information about Boxborough's trails, see [https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/351/Conservation-Trail-Maps](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/351/Conservation-Trail-Maps).
