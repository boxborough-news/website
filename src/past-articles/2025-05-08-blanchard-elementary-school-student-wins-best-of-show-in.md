Annabelle Xu, an 11-year-old 6th-grade student at Blanchard Elementary School, has won Best of Show in the 2025 Massachusetts Junior Duck Stamp (JDS) Contest and placed in the Top 25 in the 2025 National Junior Duck Stamp Contest, becoming the youngest winner among the National JDS Top 25.

Annabelle’s award-winning artwork, which depicts a swimming duck in colored pencil and pastel, was selected as “Best of Show” at the state level after several rounds of judging. She shares that her “Duck Stamp Picture Message [is] ‘Nature is like a crystal. It is beautiful, fragile, and everything we do is reflected in it.’”

![Annabelle Xu's award-winning painting of an American wigeon duck on the water](/media/archive/2025/05-blanchard-elementary-school-student-wins-best-of-show-in-2.jpg)

At the state level, first, second, and third places, along with honorable mentions, are awarded in each of the four JDS age categories. Only one student’s design is chosen as “Best of Show” and represents Massachusetts at the national level.

The Massachusetts JDS Program is sponsored by MassWildlife and the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, with support from the Massachusetts Sportsmen’s Council. Students from kindergarten through grade 12 across Massachusetts submit “original works of art depicting waterfowl in their natural habitats, demonstrating artistic talent and a knowledge of the value of wetlands for wildlife and suitability for reproduction on a 1.5 x 2-inch stamp.”

Boxborough News reached out to Annabelle to congratulate her and ask a few questions:

**How did you learn about the Duck Stamp contest, and what made you want to enter?**

*I found out from my art studio classes, and it seemed to fit my interests.*

**What medium was the duck stamp done in? (it looks like pastel or colored pencils).**

*You guessed right! It is colored pencil AND pastel.*

**How long did it take you to draw the Duck Stamp?**

*It was one of the art studio projects. I started around Thanksgiving and submitted it in February. Besides drawing, I spent a lot of the time conceiving and designing the plan.*

**How old were you when you started drawing?**

*Drawing has always been a passion of mine. I started drawing classes in September 2024.*

**What is your favorite medium?**

*Colored pencil and watercolor. Recently, I have been exploring art mediums and have been working on watercolor. I would like to explore oil painting sometime.*

**Do you take art classes outside of school?**

*Yes, I take them at Apple-Leaf Studio (in Boxborough).*

**What is your favorite subject in school?**

*All of them, especially art and ELA.*

**What are your other hobbies?**

*Singing, dancing, piano, hand crafts, cooking, rubik’s cube.*

Annabelle also shared her “recipe for success: 1 mind of good spirit, 1 heart of love, 1 pinch of laughter, 2 cups of stamina/drive. Boil on low in a pot of hard work.” Junior Duck Stamps featuring national winners from previous years can be purchased to support the JDS program and wetland conservation. Stamps can be bought at [duckstamp.com](http://duckstamp.com/).
