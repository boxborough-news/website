The third and last night of the 2025 Boxborough Annual Town Meeting was called to order by Town Moderator Dennis Reip at 7 p.m. on Wednesday May 14 at the Blanchard Memorial School gymnasium with roughly 100 voters in attendance. Twenty- four articles were passed by show of hand votes; no action was taken on one article at the request of its sponsor.

The first item on the agenda was Article 15, funds for the assessor cyclical revaluation, which was tabled on Tuesday night. This article was discussed with new information and passed.

The Meeting then considered and approved ten capital articles to be funded by free cash. The largest items were $80,000 for a Fire Department emergency response vehicle, $80,000 for a Fire Department utility terrain vehicle, $70,000 for a DPW heavy duty pickup truck, and $50,000 for alteration of the Sargent Memorial Library’s interior space.

Also approved were three larger capital items funded by debt: $1.2 million for a new Fire Department pumper truck, $300,858 for road maintenance, and $160,000 for refurbishing of a DPW dump truck frame and replacement of its existing dump/spreader body.

Four Community Preservation articles were approved with no debate and the Meeting adopted the section of Massachusetts General Law authorizing the town to bill users for the costs associated with waterline extension on the west side of route 495.

The Meeting ended with consideration of six zoning by-law amendments proposed by the Planning Board. Five of these passed, including, among others, changes to the Floodplain bylaw to bring it in compliance with current federal flood insurance standards and changes to the Accessory Dwelling Unit bylaw to bring it in line with current State law and Attorney General guidance.

An amendment relating to the Aquifer Protection District bylaw was passed over at the request of the Planning Board; it will come back to a future Town Meeting after additional discussion with the Board of Health and Water Resource Committee.

The Meeting ended at 10:28 p.m.
