On Sunday, May 18, the Steele Farm Committee and the Boxborough Historical Society welcomes the public to visit Steele Farm from 2 to 4 p.m. Volunteers from the Steele Farm Committee and the Boxborough Historical Society will be on-hand to answer questions about Steele Farm. Steele Farm is a thirty-six-acre historic New England farmscape located at 484 Middle Road.

There are three buildings on the property: The 1784 Levi Wetherbee farmhouse; the circa 1904 Richardson ice house, and the early 1940’s Steele barn. The barn and ice house will be open for self-tours, and visitors will be able to see inside the farmhouse, but won’t be able to go in.

During its meeting on May 8, Steele Farm Committee Chair Christopher Hydak reported that Phase III of the effort to preserve the envelope of the 1784 Levi Wetherbee farmhouse has made significant progress–all of the new farmhouse windows and exterior clapboard have been installed. Both the farmhouse and the newly restored ice house will be painted when the weather allows.

In mid-April, Acton-Boxborough Regional High School students participating in a day of service assisted Steele Farm Committee members with their seasonal clean up of the property.

The next meeting of the Committee will take place in the Steele Farm Barn on Thursday, June 5 at 7 p.m.
