At its meeting on May 20, 2025, the Boxborough Water Resources Committee (WRC) reviewed the latest section of the Weston & Sampson (W&S) water resources report, which concerned future population growth. WRC Chair Les Fox also provided an update on the Littleton waterline project, and the committee discussed future projects, focusing on the need to reduce water contamination by “forever chemicals.”

Emily DiFranco of W&S presented a draft of the latest section of the water resources report, a buildout analysis, which will be used to identify areas in which water resources may be stressed. In the analysis, W&S first considered practical constraints that limit areas in which building can occur, such as wetlands, and then added existing zoning constraints. The analysis indicates that 262 dwelling units could be added in Boxborough by 2050, with a corresponding 28% growth in population to 6,996 residents. DiFranco said that potential upcoming zoning changes, such as those related to the MBTA Communities Act, can easily be folded into the analysis.

Chair Fox updated the WRC that the Littleton waterline project is rapidly progressing. Work is proceeding on Swanson Road, and the waterline is being extended at a rate of roughly 300 feet per day. Proposed hydrant locations have been approved by Boxborough Fire Chief John Kivlan. The WRC also discussed concerns about the future, including with respect to water contamination. Contamination by chemicals such as perfluoroalkyl and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) presents unique challenges.

The WRC discussed how to help homeowners access guidance on water filtration systems from the Massachusetts Department of Environmental Protection (DEP). The WRC concluded that the matter requires careful thought and much attention in the future. The next WRC meeting will be on June 17, 2025, at 7:30 pm, via Zoom. It is expected that the next section of the W&S water resources report, on water and septic needs, will be reviewed at this meeting.
