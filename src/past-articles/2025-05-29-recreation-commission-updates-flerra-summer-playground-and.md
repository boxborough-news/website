At their meeting on May 22, the Boxborough Recreation Commission (Rec Com) discussed planning for Flerra Summer Playground, the half-day summer program run by the Rec Com throughout the month of July.

Directors Brian Picca and Anastasia McVey provided an update to the Commission on their plans for the program. This year, the program will run Monday through Friday for five weeks, from Monday, June 30 through Friday, August 1.

Activities in the works include the “ABCs of Flerra” and a visit from Koda (Boxborough’s police dog). Every week will also have Tie-dye Tuesdays and Water Wednesdays.

Currently, 140 campers are registered and 70 counselors have been hired. Orientation for counselors is scheduled for June 4 at 6:30 PM in the Grange room at Town Hall.

Camper registration is still open at [https://tinyurl.com/FlerraRegistration](https://tinyurl.com/FlerraRegistration).

The Rec Com also discussed damage to the pickleball nets at Liberty Field caused by players trying to tighten the nets. The nets may need to be replaced.

Pickleball players are asked not to adjust the nets themselves and, instead, call Recreation Services Coordinator Ami Scheen at 978-264-1730 so that the Boxborough Department of Public Works can handle net adjustments.

The Rec Com will be installing a sign with the pickleball court rules and a notification will be sent through the “Team Reach” system regarding court care.

Rec Com also discussed signage for the Erikson Landry Field on Summer Road. The 7.5 acres of land at 144 Summer Road was gifted to the town by Ruth Landry in 2022 for recreational use.

Les Fox spoke at the meeting about the history of the land, noting that Ruth Landry collaborated with Jeanne Kangas to donate the land to the town for recreational use. Although the exact purpose of the field has yet to be determined, Fox stated that he believes a sign should be installed displaying the name of the field, the date of purchase, and the acreage.
