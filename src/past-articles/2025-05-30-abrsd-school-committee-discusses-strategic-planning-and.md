At its meeting on May 22, the Acton-Boxborough Regional School Committee voted to establish a steering committee for the district’s Strategic Planning and Reorganization project, initiated in response to ongoing budget challenges.

The steering committee of 19 administrators, school committee members, educators, and parents is expected to meet monthly to support and guide the district’s work with consultant District Management Group “to create an updated strategic plan as well as a set of three to five actionable options for district reorganization.”

The steering committee meetings will be open to the public. Superintendent Light noted that the steering committee is not as representative of the community as he had hoped, but that there are plans to get a more diverse group of community members involved. Light also stressed the importance of communication throughout the project and outlined his plan to keep educators and families updated.

Community members are encouraged to visit a new website dedicated to this initiative, at [https://www.abschools.org/district/strategic_planning___reorganization_-_2025](https://www.abschools.org/district/strategic_planning___reorganization_-_2025). The site includes news and updates about the project, a calendar of the steering committee meetings, and a mechanism to submit public comments.

The School Committee also heard from ABRSD High School Principal Joanie Dean about proposed changes to the 2025-26 ABRHS Student Handbook, including a new cell phone storage policy.

The proposal includes updated graduation requirements to reflect the new graduation requirements recently adopted by the School Committee; a new “open gradebook” system that will give students access to their grades throughout the year; and a new cell phone storage policy, in which students will leave cell phones in cubbies while in the classroom.

Some School Committee members voiced concerns about the cell phone policy, while others voiced support. The Committee will vote on the Student Handbook at its next meeting on June 5. Superintendent Light shared that he will bring a budget amendment proposal to the Committee’s next meeting on June 5, as the district is anticipating additional revenue from the State.

The numbers are not expected to be final until July, but the district is currently confident enough about increased state aid to amend the budget accordingly. Light told the Committee that the proposal will reduce the assessment to both towns and add back staff positions that were previously cut.

In their reorganization for the upcoming school year, the Committee elected member Tori Campbell of Acton as the next Committee Chairperson, replacing current Chairperson Adam Klein. Klein will serve as the next Vice Chairperson from Boxborough and member Leela Ramachandran will serve as the next Vice Chairperson from Acton.
