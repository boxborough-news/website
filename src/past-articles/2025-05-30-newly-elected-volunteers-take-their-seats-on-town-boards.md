Now that the election is over, town boards are welcoming newly elected members. On Wednesday, Sam Anderson took his two-year seat on the Select Board alongside re-elected incumbent Wes Fowlks. Anderson replaces Dave McKiernan, who announced in March that he intended to resign in May due to an upcoming move to Cape Cod. The other current Select Board members are Kristin Hilberg (Chair), Bob Stemple, and Priya Sundaram.

“I'm honored to have been elected to Boxborough's Select Board at a time when our community has such top-of-mind issues to address such as building a new fire station and ensuring that our schools have the resources they need,” said Anderson. “As we set off on the process of bringing these questions to the voters, the challenge will be bringing people together and engaging all corners of the community.”

He added, “Separately, I am sad to have left the Conservation Commission, and I feel that I walked away from work to be done. While the Select Board needs to keep its focus on building a new fire station and securing sustainable funding for our schools, I'd like to lift up climate resiliency and sustainable development as two issue areas where I'll continue to work, and hope to have an impact.”

At the Planning Board’s next meeting on June 2, Chris Dowdy will take his seat alongside re-elected incumbent Mark White. Dowdy takes the place of former member Kathy Vorce, who did not run for reelection. The other current Planning Board members are Rebecca Verner, Rich Guzzardi, and Cindy Markowitz.

“I want to take a moment to thank everyone for their support, and I will serve the Planning Board to the best of my abilities,” says Dowdy. “There are several important projects likely to come to the Planning Board soon, and this year's town meeting has highlighted a flaw in our current zoning bylaw. I look forward to taking in public input and helping incorporate that feedback into a bylaw amendment that reflects what the town wants. Thank you!”

Says White, “First and foremost, I want to thank everyone who supported my candidacy—with your help, your vote, and your trust. To those who did not vote for me, I hope to earn your trust over the next three years. I truly believe that even when we disagree, we are not all that far apart.

We share common values, just in varying degrees. Life, politics, and planning all require the ability to compromise without abandoning core principles. When full agreement isn’t possible, I hope we can at least meet in the middle. Over the next three years, I intend to bring empathy, fairness, and transparency to every decision we make.

I will support thoughtful growth that fits Boxborough’s character and protects what we all value: our open space, natural resources, and rural charm. I’ll continue to advocate for housing and development that allow both young families and seniors to stay in Boxborough—and that help grow our tax base in a sustainable way.

To achieve this, I plan to work closely with our fellow Town leaders, landowners, and businesses to find mutually beneficial solutions. After six years on the Planning Board, I know the work isn’t always easy, but it is meaningful. Thank you again for your support. And please attend our meetings when you can. An informed community is our best partner in planning.”

At the next Library Board of Trustees meeting, Mikki Macdonald will take her seat alongside re-elected incumbent Rob McNeece. They join other current Trustees Mary Brolin (Chair), Jessica Levesque, Nancy Settle-Murphy, and Teresina Tremblay.

Said McNeece, “I want to thank everyone who voted for me. I look forward to bringing the new interior design of the library to life and to another three years of supporting our Director and staff as they keep our library current and focused on the future.”
