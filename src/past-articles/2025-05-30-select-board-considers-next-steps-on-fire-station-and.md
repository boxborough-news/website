At its meeting on May 28, the Boxborough Select Board debriefed on the defeat of Article 1 (to allow municipal governmental zoning across all zoning districts) and Article 2 (to borrow $2.6 million for the design and bidding of a firehouse at 72 Stow Road) at the Special Town Meeting within Annual Town Meeting earlier this month.

The Board heard from Priya Sundaram, Select Board member and Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) member, who relayed the FSBC’s request for the Select Board to call a joint meeting between the Select Board, Planning Board, and FSBC to determine a path forward on the fire station project, particularly with respect to the municipal zoning changes needed to build a fire station.

Board members agreed that such a meeting would be a good next step and tentatively scheduled it for June 17 or 18. The Board also indicated that it was in favor of the FSBC reexamining four potential fire station sites – 72 Stow Road, 502 Mass Ave, 700 Mass Ave, and 1300 Mass Ave – and bringing back to the Select Board a ranking of these four sites, instead of a single recommended site.

The Board briefly considered increasing the size of the FSBC but declined to do so. The Board also discussed the dates and locations of this year’s Special Town Meetings (STMs). Expected STM topics include a proposal for Boxborough’s compliance with the state’s MBTA Communities Act requirements, another attempt at a zoning bylaw change to address municipal government zoning, and another proposal regarding the fire station project.

The Board decided, in consultation with Town Planner Alec Wade, that mid-October is the best time for an MBTA Communities vote, with a contingency plan for a second STM in December, if needed. The deadline for compliance with the MBTA Communities Act is December 30, 2025.

The December STM may also be used for the other two topics – municipal zoning and the fire station – if those proposals are not ready in time for the October STM. The tentative dates for the two STMs are October 21 and December 8.

Moderator Dennis Reip recommended that the Board consider changing the location of the STMs from the Blanchard Memorial School to the Boxborough Regency Hotel and Conference Center (Regency) in order to better accommodate the large number of residents expected to attend and vote on these issues. The Board agreed and instructed town staff to plan the meetings at the Regency, if possible.

The Board also discussed the date and location of Boxborough’s 2026 Annual Town Meeting, tentatively deciding on the week of May 18 at the Regency. This meeting was the first Select Board meeting attended by newly elected member Sam Anderson, who ran unopposed for the two-year seat – the remainder of Dave McKiernan’s term.

In their reorganization vote, the majority of the Board voted for Kristin Hilberg to continue to serve as Chair and Bob Stemple was unanimously voted as Clerk. In accepting the role of Chair, Hilberg noted that she is not planning to run for re-election next year.
