At its May 22 meeting, the Boxborough Economic Development Committee (EDC) reviewed the status of the grants given out through the Boxborough Business Grant Program. The program, funded by Boxborough’s American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) appropriation, awarded $102,000 in forgivable loans to five Boxborough small businesses throughout 2023 and 2024. Award recipients were Two Friends Chocolates, Oscar’s Burritos, Swymfit, AlphaCars, and the not-yet-launched Thinking Flower.

Town Planner Alec Wade reported that Two Friends Chocolates, which received two grants, has closed out its first grant and is in the process of closing out its second grant. He also gave updates about permitting for the grant-funded improvements at Oscar’s Burritos and noted that Thinking Flower is poised to receive its first round of reimbursements after hitting required milestones.

Thinking Flower, a coffee shop, bakery, and wine bar, is expected to open in September next to Middlesex Bank at #629-C Massachusetts Ave.

The EDC also discussed how to move forward with the Village Center revitalization initiative, including using “tactical urbanism” to engage with the community. “Tactical urbanism,” a concept introduced to the committee by Town Planner Wade, generally refers to low-cost, temporary, community-driven projects that are considered a first step towards more permanent improvements.

Members brainstormed different types of community events and projects intended to drum up community awareness and support for Village Center efforts, including crosswalk art and local business “pop-up” shops. The committee expects to discuss these ideas further at its next meeting.

The Town Center Revitalization Project was funded by a $46,950 community planning grant from the state. The town contracted with Tighe & Bond to conduct public outreach and create a conceptual plan for a town center-style development. The Village Center project materials are available online at [https://boxborough-ma.gov/698/Village-Center-Revitalization-Project](https://boxborough-ma.gov/698/Village-Center-Revitalization-Project).
