At its June 5 meeting, the ABRSD School Committee heard a presentation on the district’s “Vision of a Graduate” initiative, in which a steering committee of educators, administrators, students, and community members tackled the question, “What are the most important skills and experiences our students need to prepare them for success in learning and life?”

The result of this process, which began in Spring 2024, is a list of five “competencies”: Adaptability, Communication, Collaboration, Intellectual Curiosity, and Integrity.

The School Committee is expected to vote to adopt the Vision of a Graduate at its next meeting on June 12. The Steering Committee will then work to incorporate the Vision of a Graduate into teaching throughout the district and develop ways to measure student growth toward the five competencies.

The Committee then heard from Acton and Boxborough police officers about the School Resource Officer (SRO) program. Acton Detectives AJ Rotella and Ana Dapkas and Boxborough Officer Diosmar Reynoso spoke about their goals, training, how they interact with students and the community, and year-to-date statistics.

School Committee members asked many questions about the SRO’s role in the schools and asked for more information and communication going forward. The Committee approved new Memorandums of Understanding for the SRO program with the Acton and Boxborough Police Departments in fall of 2024.

In other business, the Committee voted to approve the 2025-26 ABRHS Student Handbook, which now requires that students leave their cell phones in cubbies while in the classroom. The Committee did a “first read” of the Handbook at its last meeting on May 22, with some committee members expressing concerns about the new cell phone requirement.

The Committee approved the Handbook with two abstaining and one “nay” vote. The Committee also heard a report from the Special Education Parent Advisory Council and recognized 23 retiring teachers and staff members for their contributions to the district. Individuals retiring from Blanchard Elementary are: Kristen Kilcommins, Connie Long, Sheila Owen, Laura Russell, Dawn Charlton, Linda Vierkant, and Kathy Bower.
