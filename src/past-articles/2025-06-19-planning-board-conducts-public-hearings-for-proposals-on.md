At its meeting on Monday, June 16, the Planning Board reopened the public hearing for the renewal of a Special Permit Application received from Campanelli-Trigate LLC for the buildout of The Park at Beaver Brook (former Cisco campus). Permitting Counsel for Campanelli, Johanna Schneider, gave an abbreviated history of the property and a summary of the application.

Cisco had originally permitted 1,400,000 square feet of commercial development under a previously granted special permit, but that permit has lapsed, and the property was not fully developed.

Campanelli is seeking to reinstate the lapsed Open Space Commercial Development (OSCD) Special Permit and is requesting that the Commercial Development Period be extended twenty years, until January 1, 2045, to allow for the full build-out of the project as shown on Campanelli’s 2025 Master Plan. The original permit was for fifteen years.

The application proposes to put an additional 94 acres into conservation/open space restriction in return for 667,500 square feet of new development, which translates to the construction of five new buildings.

Russell Dion, partner at Campanelli, discussed the types of uses in the proposed buildings, including research & development, light manufacturing, and office uses. The property is in the Office Park (OP) zone, and all of the proposed uses are permitted by right. If the OSCD Special Permit is approved, the applicant will need to seek site plan approval for each of the buildings they plan to construct.

After the presentation, Planning Board Chair Rebecca Verner asked members of the public who were in attendance to weigh in. Property abutters and environmental advocates expressed concerns about the long duration of the permit, noise, and traffic. Representatives from the Harvard and the Boxborough Conservation Trusts, as well as the Sudbury Valley Trustees, urged the applicant to prepare a long term management plan for the parcels slated for protection/conservation, noting that the campus is a popular birding spot that features species only seen in this area.

The hearing will be continued on Monday, July 21 at 7:05 p.m.

Prior to the Campanelli hearing, the Board opened a public hearing to review the site plan approval and stormwater permit application from Brett Gutheil of BHG109 LLC for the construction of an indoor, 8-unit contractor storage facility at 975 Mass Ave.

Gutheil developed and owns the adjacent property at 881 Mass Ave, which serves as building trade rental units. As proposed, the building at 975 Mass Ave will have the same aesthetics of the building at 881 Mass Ave, which is a corrugated steel exterior with a single garage bay for each unit.

During the hearing, the applicant’s engineer, Limhuot Tiv of Goldsmith, Prest & Ringwall, Inc., presented the application and answered questions from the Board that focused on the driveway location, lot coverage, total area of disturbance, visibility from the road, and proximity and visibility to the conservation land trails that abut the property.

Two members of the public commented on the appearance of the proposed building. Although the Boxborough Zoning Bylaw does not regulate the exterior materials and aesthetics in the Business (B) zoning district in which this building is proposed, Planning Board members asked the applicant to consider revising the exterior design. The hearing was continued until July 21 at 7:35 p.m.
