On Saturday October 18, approximately twenty small businesses from Boxborough and surrounding towns will gather in the “village center” of Boxborough for a day of community celebration to promote and support local business.

Organized by the Boxborough Economic Development Committee and the Boxborough Office of Land Use and Permitting, Boxborough’s first “Village for a Day” will run on Saturday, October 18 from 11:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. and will take place at and between the Middlesex Bank Plaza, the UCC Church lawn, and 25 Stow Road.

Some businesses in the vicinity of these locations will conduct their own “open house,” while other businesses will set up a “pop-up” booth at one of the three main locations. A map of all participants will be provided, food will be available for purchase, and there will be live music and kids’ activities.

Participating businesses include the following, all based in Boxborough: Ludwig Agency Insurance, AlphaCars & Motorcycles, Fine Line Woodworking/Architectural Elements, Inc., Gina’s Studio for Dance and Fitness, Thoreau Foods, Two Friends Chocolates, Middlesex Savings Bank, Boxboro Regency, The Art Studio, Own Your Game, and Thinking Flower.

“Boxborough’s first Village for a Day is an exciting opportunity for residents to experience what a walkable, connected village center could one day look and feel like — and to get to know the many local businesses that make our community unique,” said Associate Town Planner Ian Gilson.

“It’s been inspiring to see the enthusiasm and support from the local business community,” he continued. “We hope residents come out to explore, connect, and reflect on how both walkability and business vitality can help create a vibrant, welcoming village center. Our goal is for Village for a Day to be both a celebration of what we already have and inspire the community for what can be.”

Gilson and Town Planner Alec Wade are working with the Boxborough Department of Public Works Director Ed Kukkula to create additional crosswalks and a temporary sidewalk on the west side of Stow Road. The idea of developing a “village center” in Boxborough is something that the town has explored for a number of years.

Most recently, the town hired consulting firm Tighe & Bond to conduct a Village Center Feasibility Assessment in Spring of 2024, funded by a community planning grant from the state, resulting in a [Feasibility Assessment Report](https://boxborough-ma.gov/698/Village-Center-Revitalization-Project).
