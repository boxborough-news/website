As pedestrians perused vendor booths and explored open houses at the Boxborough Economic Development Committee’s “Village for a Day” event on Saturday, October 18, “I didn’t even know this was here” was a frequent refrain. More than twenty local businesses from Boxborough and surrounding towns, as well as other local organizations, set up booths or opened their doors to create a temporary walkable “downtown” to promote and support the Boxborough business community.

The event was based at three main locations: the UCC lawn at 723 Mass Ave, the small green at 25 Stow Road (across Mass Ave from the church) and the Middlesex Bank Plaza. Pedestrians walked between the three locations, seeing Boxborough on foot instead of by car, with the support of Boxborough police officers stationed at the Mass Ave crosswalks.

At 25 Stow Road, Ludwig Agency Insurance and The Art Studio held open houses in the historic brick building on the corner, while other local businesses showed their wares at tables outside. “I feel very blessed to have been able to participate in [Village for a Day],” said The Art Studio owner and artist Barbara Shapokas.

“Many of The Art Studio visitors felt comfortable enough to share appreciation for my art work. This gave me an opportunity to know more about their tastes and interests in art, in general. My love of the town of Boxborough was validated once again that day. Participating in this very well organized event was a joyful experience.”

At the Middlesex Bank Plaza, tenants Gina’s Studio for Dance and Fitness, Two Friends Chocolates, and Middlesex Bank all enjoyed the increased pedestrian traffic. Divya Suryaprasad of Thinking Flower, a new bakery expected to open in the plaza in the coming weeks, shared information about her new venture with curious and excited residents.

“Overall the day was a success in achieving our primary objectives of demonstrating a walkable village district, supporting our local businesses, and bringing the community together to experience our business village district,” said Rich Guzzardi, a member of Boxborough’s Economic Development Committee.

During the event, Guzzardi was stationed at the Middlesex Bank Plaza, sharing information about the Town’s goals for a “village center” and collecting feedback from residents. “During the day we engaged participants in understanding what was most important to them when they think of a Village Center,” continued Guzzardi.

“Walkability was the number one response, followed by a desire for specific business types (including restaurants and taverns) and community gathering. Overall people were happy to have the opportunity to get out, walk around our village and visit the local businesses that participated.” Guzzardi noted that the EDC has set up a post-event survey and welcomes feedback from anyone who attended.
