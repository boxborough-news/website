On Tuesday, November 11 at 11 a.m., Boxborough veterans and supporters will gather at the Veterans’ Tribute Memorial on the grounds of the Sargent Memorial Library, 427 Massachusetts Avenue for the town’s annual Veterans Day Ceremony.

The ceremony will honor those who have served in the Armed Forces and recognize Boxborough veterans for their service.

Boxborough resident and veteran Ron Vogel has served as the master of ceremonies for the town’s Veterans Day ceremony since 2021, when the Veterans Tribute Memorial was completed and the town held its first Veterans Day observance there.

“Kevin Mahoney and I were the cochairs of the Veterans Monument Committee,” said Vogel, “and at the end of the project I took on the role of project manager to get it completed. Kevin organized the first Veterans Day observance and he asked me to be the master of ceremonies, and I willingly did that.”

Vogel served in the United States Navy on active duty from 1966-1968 and “served aboard the destroyer USS Forrest Sherman, destroyer number 931.”

“I hope that when people come out for the Veterans Day ceremony they will reflect upon the sacrifices of veterans and their families throughout the years and appreciate what they’ve accomplished and given us,” said Vogel.

Boxborough’s Town Administrator and veteran Michael Johns will also participate in the ceremony.

“I served just shy of 12 years in the Navy from 1986 through 1997,” said Johns. “I served at Naval Air Station’s Pensacola, FL, North Island (Coronado) CA, Patuxent River, MD, and Virginia Beach, VA. During that time I made two Middle East deployments on USS Enterprise and USS Abraham Lincoln from the West Coast, the second one during the first Gulf War in Iraq, and the third deployment from the East Coast on USS Enterprise (after major ship and nuclear reactor overhauls) to Bosnia Herzegovina, and then back to the Persian Gulf and Iraq again.”

“I am confident that everyone who comes [to the ceremony] will once again recognize and enjoy what a special place Boxborough is and how fortunate we are to live in the United States of America, with freedom that is earned for all by the many generations who serve and have served our nation,” said Johns. “My hope is that everyone who attends will learn that whether or not they served in the military, there is something that every single one of us can do to ensure freedom and democracy will thrive in this nation forever.”

Information about Boxborough’s veterans’ services can be found at [https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/281/Veterans-Services](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/281/Veterans-Services). Boxborough Veterans’ Services Officer James MacRae (978-929-6614) is available by appointment and has regular office hours at Boxborough Town Hall every Tuesday from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.
