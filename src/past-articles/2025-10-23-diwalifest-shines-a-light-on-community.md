After a one-week postponement due to inclement weather, abundant sunshine greeted Diwalifest attendees on Sunday October 19 on the lawn of the Sargent Memorial Library in Boxborough. Almost 2,000 people from Boxborough and surrounding communities gathered to celebrate Diwali, the Indian festival of light. Savory and sweet Indian foods, crafts, clothing, music and dance performances delighted the crowd.

When reached for comment, Diwalifest founder and organizer Deepika Prakash expressed her satisfaction with the event. “Creating a platform where the entire community comes together to celebrate Diwali — while also giving small businesses and student entrepreneurs and volunteers a place to shine — is truly an honor and the brightest feather in my cap. I’m happy to keep doing the work, as long as people keep showing up.”

Sargent Memorial Library Director Peishan Bartley also reflected on the event. “The festival wouldn't have happened so smoothly without the help of DPW, the Police, and the MART van. I am also grateful to the Blanchard Memorial School and the UCC for sharing their parking lot with us…and for the wonderful high school volunteers for helping out with the trash and clean up and to everyone who showed up. The event has become another reminder of why I love my community.”
