Liberty Fields:

- The Pickleball rebounder has been delivered and is being assembled by the DPW.
- YourCourts will be the new scheduling system for court reservations. It will replace the current SignUpGenius. It will be available when courts open in the spring.
- All courts (tennis, pickleball, and basketball) will be closed for the winter, starting November 30 or when a significant weather event occurs. Courts will reopen on April 1.
- Quotes for a tennis backboard are being sought and will be discussed at the next RecCom meeting.

Landry Field:

The Commission voted to approve a mock-up of the Landry Field sign. Prior to installation, it must be approved by the Zoning Board of Appeals.

Winterfest:

Winterfest will be held on Saturday, January 31, with a “weather date” of February 1. The Commission discussed ideas for food and entertainment.
