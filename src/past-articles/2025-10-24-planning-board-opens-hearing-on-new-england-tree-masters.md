At its meeting on Monday October 20, the Boxborough Planning Board opened a public hearing to review the application from applicant “984 Massachusetts Avenue, LLC” for site plan review and a stormwater management permit for a proposed landscaper contractor yard at 984 and 986 Massachusetts Avenue and 38 Sara’s Way.

According to the application, the applicant “intends for New England Tree Masters to continue to use the site to store and process logs as part of its landscape and tree removal business.” The applicant’s project narrative states that the applicant has occupied the site since August 2023 and that “differences of opinion have formed concerning the Applicant’s compliance with zoning laws. As a means of achieving resolution, the Town has requested that the Applicant submit a site plan to address specified regulations…”

During the public hearing, the applicants’ representatives gave a brief opening statement about the property history and usage, which was described as the “storage and processing of logs that have been removed as part of the New England Tree’s tree removal business, and the storage of vehicles and equipment necessary to those operations.”

Planning Board members asked questions about the chipping operations, impervious surface area covering the site, and environmental concerns, including dust, noise, and runoff. A residential abutter spoke about the negative impacts of living near the site, including “unreasonable hours of operation, hazardous road conditions…and noise and air pollution.”

Members of the Planning Board will conduct a site walk on November 4. The public hearing continues on November 17 at 7:05 p.m.

Public records indicate that on August 20, 2024, Plaintiffs 984 Massachusetts Avenue LLC, New England Tree Masters, Inc., and owner Luis Gutierres filed a civil action against the Boxborough Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA) and a former ZBA Chair individually, seeking to annul an August 1, 2024 ZBA decision regarding a March 2024 Enforcement Action issued by the Town’s Zoning Enforcement Officer concerning the 984 Massachusetts Avenue property. A recent court filing by Plaintiffs states, “The Parties' efforts to reach an out-of-court resolution are active and ongoing…Hearings before the relevant Town Boards are anticipated.”

*Correction: A prior version of this article stated that "the ZBA Chair individually" is a defendant in the lawsuit referred to in the final paragraph. This has been corrected to clarify that a former ZBA Chair is a defendant in that lawsuit.*
