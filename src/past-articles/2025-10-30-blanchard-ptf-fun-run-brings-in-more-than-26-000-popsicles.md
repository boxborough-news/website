The sky was blue, the leaves were orange, and pop songs and bubbles filled the morning air as the Blanchard Memorial School kindergarteners took their places under a giant inflatable rainbow arch to kick off the Blanchard Parents Teachers and Friends (PTF)’s annual “Fun Run” on October 24.

As the kids circled the field sprinting, strolling, and everything in between, they were cheered on by teachers and staff, parents, Blanchard PTF volunteers, the Blanchard bulldog mascot, Principal Mike Votto in an inflatable bulldog costume, and Assistant Principal Julie Babson in an inflatable unicorn costume.

The runners’ reward at the end? Popsicles handed out by the Boxborough Police and Fire Departments, and a chance to cheer on the next group, the first graders. The rest of the grades followed suit – a grand entrance to parent cheers, followed by enthusiastic laps, plenty of high-fives, and popsicle prizes. Later in the day, the students were even treated to a visit from the WooSox “Smiley Ball” mascot.

“The Fun Run was so much fun,” said first-grader Cora. “I loved running with my friends. At the end we all got popsicles – that was the best part!” “It was tiring!” said kindergartener Evelyn. “But we got blue, red, and yellow popsicles after – the primary colors!”

The Fun Run was the culmination of a Blanchard PTF fundraising drive that raised $26,160 for the school – surpassing their goal of $20,000. The Blanchard PTF has organized the Fun Run since 2019 and uses the funds to support free school-wide events and enrichment programs.

“I am absolutely blown away by the support of the Blanchard staff, parents, students and Boxborough community,” said Blanchard PTF Vice President Michelle Norris. “Every person I reached out to gave me a resounding yes – from Kristin Hilberg with water bottles for every runner to Fire Chief Kivlan who wore our bulldog mascot costume for the entire three-hour event."

As the fundraiser met certain dollar milestones along the way, the students earned school-wide prizes including a pj/comfy clothes day, inflatable games at recess donated by Busy Bee Jumpers, and an all-school assembly in December with Engineering Wonder, a STEM-based magic show. And as a reward for surpassing the “stretch goal” of $25,000, Principal Votto pledged to sleep on the roof of the school – certainly a thrill worth every penny.

“Our students showed incredible dedication – running laps, raising dollars, and doing everything they said they would to support our school,” said Votto. “So now it’s my turn to live up to my word. Spending the night on the roof isn’t just a fun stunt – it’s a lesson in commitment. If we expect our kids to follow through, we have to model that ourselves.”

Next week (weather-permitting), Principal Votto will head up to the roof for his overnight adventure. The Blanchard PTF is a 501c3 organization.
