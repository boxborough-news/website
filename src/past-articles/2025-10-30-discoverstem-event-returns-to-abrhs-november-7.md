Local non-profit AB STEAM, in collaboration with the A-B Regional High School Science Department, is hosting DiscoverSTEM, a free career exploration event for middle and high schoolers on Friday, November 7 from 6 to 8:30 p.m. at Acton-Boxborough Regional High School lower level.

Last hosted in 2019, DiscoverSTEM is a reverse science fair set up like a trade show where local STEM companies exhibit state-of-the-art technologies. Students and community members will have the opportunity to interact with exhibitors, who are representatives from local STEM firms and organizations, and learn about products, services, inventions, and careers in STEM fields.

The event will feature more than 40 exhibitors from companies like Qualcomm, Boston Dynamics, SolidWorks, Haartz Corporation, and National Grid and from organizations and institutions, including Tufts University Veterinary Science, the Acton Lions, Acton Water District, Energize Acton, Acton Fire, Acton Police, and many others.

In 2024, AB PIP STEM became AB STEAM, a non-profit organization with an expanded mission to “provide and promote free K-12 educational events in science, technology, engineering, art and math.”

When asked to share her thoughts on the event, DiscoverSTEM 2025 Lead Ruchi Jain stated, “For me STEM is more than a subject — it’s a mindset and a lifestyle. As we bring back this event after six years, our hope is to share the excitement for exploration and critical thinking with students. Inspiring young learners through education outreach is something I deeply value.”

To volunteer or learn more about the event, visit [absteam.org/discoverstem](http://absteam.org/discoverstem).
