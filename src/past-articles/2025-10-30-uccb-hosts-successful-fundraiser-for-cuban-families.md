Boxborough’s United Church of Christ (UCCB) came alive on October 25 as more than 120 people from near and far gathered for a moving and memorable fundraiser concert to support the people of Cuba, who are enduring their most severe economic hardship since the 1950s revolution. The event raised almost $4,000, all of which will go directly toward relief efforts for Cuban families.

The evening featured a dynamic performance by Sol y Canto, the award-winning pan-Latin ensemble known for their uplifting blend of music, rhythm, and heart. Their songs of hope and resilience beautifully reflected the spirit of the night.

The event was the vision of Maricel Lucero, whose personal story and dedication to helping people in Cuba inspired many to contribute and volunteer. A highlight of the evening came when Sol y Canto invited Maricel to join them on stage for a lively rendition of *Quizás, Quizás, Quizás* (“Perhaps, Perhaps, Perhaps”). Guests also enjoyed a raffle featuring a basket of Cuban-inspired items.

Concert attendee and UCCB member Mary Pavlik reflected on the event, "It was an evening of spirit, compassion, and community—proof once again that when people come together in love and music, they can make a meaningful difference."
