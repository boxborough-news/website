On Thursday, October 30, with nationwide uncertainty surrounding access to Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) benefits, Town Administrator Michael Johns and Assistant Town Administration Rajon Hudson brought together town officials and volunteers to launch a “Neighbors Feeding Neighbors” Boxborough Food Security Program.

The program came together on Thursday morning at Town Hall when Johns and Hudson met with Council on Aging Director Kim Dee, Community Services Coordinator Wendy Trinks, Town Planner Alec Wade, and members of the Boxborough Well-Being Committee to discuss the uncertainty of SNAP funding. Trinks shared with the group that she had been attending state and local meetings to gather updates on the status of SNAP and had reached out to clients with current information and available resources. According to town administration, Boxborough has more than 150 households (representing more than 250 individuals) who rely on SNAP benefits.

The immediate goal of the Boxborough Food Security Program is to strengthen systems already in place. Boxborough residents are encouraged to donate nonperishable food items and contribute to the Acton Food Pantry. New food drop-off bins will soon be available at Town Hall and the UCC Boxborough (near the Little Free Food Pantry), joining the existing collection bin at the Sargent Memorial Library.

The town is also collecting Market Basket gift cards and encouraging donations to the Boxborough Community Services Gift Account, both of which will be managed and distributed by Trinks to residents in need. Donations to the Gift Account can be made payable to the “Town of Boxborough” with “CS Gift Account” in the memo.

Individuals who would like more information about receiving assistance from the Food Security Program can contact Wendy Trinks at wtrinks@boxborough-ma.gov.

The Boxborough Well-Being Committee will meet at Town Hall on Monday, November 3 at 10 a.m. to continue these efforts and discuss next steps. To get involved and help coordinate ongoing efforts, contact Mary Pavlik mary.j.pavlik@gmail.com or Liz Markiewicz lizmarkiewicz78@gmail.com.

For more information and local resources, visit the AB Community Compass website at [abccompass.org](https://abccompass.org/).
