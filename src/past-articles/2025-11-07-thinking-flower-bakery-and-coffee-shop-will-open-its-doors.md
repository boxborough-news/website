After much anticipation, Boxborough resident Divya Suryaprasad will open Thinking Flower bakery and coffee shop on Monday, November 10. Thinking Flower is located at 629 Massachusetts Avenue in the Middlesex Bank plaza.

“Thinking Flower is a forward thinking small-batch artisan bakery specializing in handcrafted viennoiserie (croissants) and cakes baked fresh daily, paired with specialty coffees sourced from a local roastery,” Suryaprasad [previously told Boxborough News](https://www.boxboroughnews.org/features#h.g63es3dulz36). The shop has a seating capacity of 25, with several small tables, one larger communal table, and a counter along the front window.

On Monday, Suryaprasad is planning to hold a ribbon-cutting ceremony at 8 a.m. with town and state officials expected to attend.

This week, in preparation for opening to the public on Monday, Thinking Flower opened for a soft launch by invitation only. Roughly 50-60 friends and supporters – as well as a few curious customers who simply saw the lights were on – stopped by the shop on November 6 and 7. Patrons enjoyed a variety of pastries, including croissants, pain au chocolat, chocolate chunk cookies, marble cake, and gluten-free buckwheat cake, as well as a coffee menu including espresso, cortado, cappuccino, americano, and hot or iced lattes.

“The soft launch went quite smoothly,” said Suryaprasad on Thursday afternoon, after the first day of the soft launch. “We’re currently working on making the team more efficient at answering questions about our pastries and improving our overall workflow. That was the whole purpose of doing a soft launch—to prepare both the team and myself, and to gain a better understanding of how we can serve our guests more effectively moving forward.”

For its first week open (a holiday week with schools closed on Monday and Tuesday), Thinking Flower will be operating on adjusted hours: Monday and Tuesday 8 a.m. to 3 p.m., closed Wednesday, and Thursday through Saturday 8 a.m. to 3 p.m.

Starting the week of November 17, Thinking Flower will begin its standard operating hours: Wednesday through Friday 7:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. and Saturday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

“I’ve accomplished my first goal,” said Suryaprasad, “and now I’m on to the next chapter - running this shop with the same passion and vision I’ve always had. I look forward to welcoming each and every one of you to Thinking Flower.”
