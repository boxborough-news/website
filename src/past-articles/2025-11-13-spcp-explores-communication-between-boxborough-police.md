The Boxborough Strengthening Police Community Partnerships (SPCP) Council continues to meet monthly to look for opportunities to strengthen the partnership between the Boxborough Police Department (BPD) and the community it serves. The Council’s October and November meetings were held in person at the Town Hall.

At the October meeting, the Council reviewed a draft School Resource Officer (SRO) survey to collect feedback on how the community views the Acton-Boxborough Regional School District’s SRO program. However, due to the existing AB Forward survey that targets the same audience, the Council decided to set this SRO survey aside for the moment and consider returning to it in the Spring.

At the November meeting, the Council revisited the Department of Justice report from the original March 2024 SPCP session to determine the next step for the group. From this exercise, the Council determined a need for better communication between the community and the BPD.

Inspired by this need for communication channels, the Council brainstormed methods. Ideas included walking door to door to canvass the residents, speaking to residents at the transfer station, advertising the SPCP email address on the electronic sign at the fire station, and soliciting input via sandwich board signs at major intersections. But the Council concluded that the most promising solution identified was word of mouth.

The Council believes there are members of each part of the Boxborough community who know, from word of mouth, what that part of the community needs from the BPD. The challenge is connecting those informal community leaders to the BPD.

Ultimately, the Council decided to arrange a potluck gathering to stimulate a discussion. The event would be a chance to sample some varied cuisines in a friendly environment, get diverse community members to meet each other, and hopefully get a conversation going about the needs of the various parts of the community and the ways the community can best provide input to the BPD.

The Council agreed to approach Library Director Peishan Bartley and determine if this event could be arranged at the Sargent Memorial Library soon after the holiday season. At the next meeting, in December, the Council will discuss feedback from Bartley and begin to compose a flyer announcing the event.

The public is encouraged to reach out to the Council at [spcp.bxb@gmail.com](mailto:spcp.bxb@gmail.com) with any inquiries about the Boxborough Police Department, ideas to strengthen the partnership between the community and the police, or to get involved with the Council.
