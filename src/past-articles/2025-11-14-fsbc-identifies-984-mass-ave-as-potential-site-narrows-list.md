At their meeting on November 13, the Boxborough Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) voted unanimously to move forward on only three potential fire station sites: 984 Mass Avenue, 1300 Mass Avenue, and 72 Stow Road.

Other sites that the FSBC has studied and now “dropped” are 502 Mass Ave (current fire station) and 700-832 Mass Ave. The committee also briefly discussed 296 Middle Road and 276 Middle Road as potential sites but agreed that neither are worth exploring this late in the process.

The FSBC decided to narrow their focus due to the committee’s dwindling time and resources. The committee has roughly $65,000 left in their budget and is working towards a March 2026 Special Town Meeting.

With not enough money and time to continue studying all sites, the committee asked Boxborough Fire Chief John Kivlan his opinion on how he would rank the sites. Chief Kivlan responded that his first choice would be 984 Mass Ave, followed by 1300 Mass Ave, followed by 72 Stow Road. Chief Kivlan explained that he has concerns about sightlines at the 700-832 Mass Ave site and believes that the “program” (operational needs) will not fit at the 502 Mass Ave site.

Committee members agreed that Chief Kivlan’s three preferred properties are the most promising based on all the information gathered to date and voted unanimously to proceed with only those three sites.

The committee then voted unanimously to authorize $26,400 for consultant Context Architecture (Context) to further study 984 Mass Ave and 1300 Mass Ave. The committee expects that this amount of further study for those two sites will give them enough information to determine which site(s) to bring to the Special Town Meeting.

At the Special Town Meeting, originally scheduled for December 2025 before being pushed to March 2026, the FSBC is expected to ask for land purchase approval(s), as well as funding for the design and bid phase of the project.

This is the first time the town has publicly stated the address of the 984 Mass Ave site. The site was first brought to the FSBC’s attention by Town Administrator Michael Johns about a month ago; Johns reported to the committee on October 16 that he had been approached by a landowner with an offer to sell the town a certain piece of land for the fire station, but he did not publicly disclose the address. At that meeting, the committee asked Context to begin investigating the site.

Town Administrator Johns also shared updates regarding property negotiations, stating that the “asking price” of 984 Mass Ave is $2.4 million and the “asking price” of 1300 Mass Ave is $4.5 million.

The FSBC’s next meeting is Thursday, November 20. The meeting will take place at the Sargent Memorial Library and is expected to begin at 7:30 p.m. (with a possible executive session at 7 p.m.).
