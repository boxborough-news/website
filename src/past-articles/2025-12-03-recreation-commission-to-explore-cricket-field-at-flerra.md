At its meeting on November 24, the Recreation Commission (RecCom) heard a presentation from Arun Koul, President of the Merrimack Cricket League (MCL) and Mukesh Nirmal, cricket team captain, player, and youth coach, requesting support to build a cricket field in Boxborough.

Koul and Nirmal indicated that they have already inspected all of the athletic fields in Boxborough and identified an area at Flerra they believe could be suitable for a cricket field. They assured RecCom members that a cricket field would not damage sprinkler systems or interfere with soccer schedules.

The Recreation Commission agreed to visit the field with Town Planner Alec Wade and members of the Boxborough Conservation Commission and discuss it further.

**RecCom Briefs:**

- Basketball registration will begin in mid-December. Those interested should check the Recreation Commission website: [https://boxboroughma.myrec.com/info/default.aspx](https://boxboroughma.myrec.com/info/default.aspx)

- The Pickleball Rebounder will be installed in the spring.

- Tennis and pickleball courts will be closed from December 1 through April 1.There will be no exceptions.

- The Landry Field sign measurements have been submitted; a sign permit has not yet been approved. The sign will be installed in the spring.

- Winterfest is scheduled for Sunday, January 25 from 1 p.m. to 4 p.m. at Steele Farm. There will be food, games, and musical entertainment.

- The Commission agreed to allow croquet to be played at various fields in Boxborough, provided it does not conflict with other scheduled sports activities.

RecCom’s next meeting will be on December 18 via Zoom.
