On Thursday December 4, the Boxborough Community Preservation Committee (CPC) held its public hearing on the six final project requests for FY2027 Community Preservation Act (CPA) funds.

During the 90-minute public hearing, the CPC heard from the proponents, four of whom made presentations on their projects.

The Recreation Commission proposed the largest request, $300,000, for replacement of the main structure and installation of an ADA-compliant surface at the children’s playground at Flerra Field. According to the application, this project would rectify all of the issues raised in a 2016 inspection of the site.

The next largest project is a $144,000 proposal from the Select Board to replace the entire roof at Town Hall, the newest section of which is 38 years old. The third new request is for $20,093 to replace the windows at the 1857 School House #2 at the corner of Picnic Street and Hill Road; the windows appear to be original to the structure. This request is from the owners of the School House, Nancy Morrison and Jane Sawisch, who are the third generation of the Robinson family to own and maintain the school house as a museum.

Three ongoing projects were also submitted:

- The Housing Board submitted the Boxborough Rental Assistance Program (BRAP) in the amount of $69,000 (same as last year). This program provides rental assistance, up to $500 per month, for 14 families.
- The Cemetery Commission proposed $9,500 (same as last year) for funding the continuing project of repairing, cleaning, restoring, and resetting of headstones, monuments, and other stone work in the town’s two cemeteries.
- The Conservation Commission requested $25,000 as the CPC contribution to the Conservation Trust Fund, which is used for expenses related to acquiring, improving, monitoring, and otherwise maintaining new and existing parcels in town. This one-time increase, from previous years’ $10,000, is primarily for funding the update of the town’s Open Space and Recreation Plan, which is due in 2027.

The next meeting of the Committee will be held January 8, 2026, when they will discuss and vote on project approvals and potential Annual Town Meeting warrant articles.
