At their meeting on December 4, the ABRSD School Committee received and began to discuss the four district reorganization options recommended by the AB Forward Steering Committee.

The four options are:

- Option 3v2: “Shift to 5 K–6 schools by closing the Merriam school (based on lowest enrollment preference) and relocating the Conant community into the available space.”
- Option 4: “Shift to 5 K–6 schools by merging Merriam and McCarthy-Towne, and relocating the Conant community into the available space.”
- Option 5v2: “Hybrid Grade-Band Model: Blanchard remains K–6, while Boardwalk and Parker Damon campuses are split into Lower Schools (K–3) and Upper Schools (4–6).”
- Option 6: “Shift to 3 larger K–6 schools (The Blanchard School, The Boardwalk School, The Parker Damon School).”

These four options were chosen from a list of thirteen possible district reorganization options developed by the district’s consultant DM Group in connection with the “AB Forward” strategic planning and reorganization initiative. The AB Forward Steering Committee includes administrators, school committee members, educators, and parents and is charged with guiding the district’s work with the consultant.

After DM Group presented the four options, School Committee members asked questions about student disruption, student outcomes, and operational logistics. The Committee then heard from members of the public, many of whom spoke passionately about the reorganization’s impact on the Conant and/or Merriam school communities.

School Committee Vice Chair and Boxborough member Adam Klein made a motion to remove Option 3v2 and Option 4 from consideration, stating “I really do not like that we’re pitting each one of these school communities against each other,” but withdrew the motion after Superintendent Peter Light spoke in favor of keeping all four options on the table as the Committee moves through the planned timeline for committee deliberation and public input.

The discussion about the reorganization options also included conversation about how to distribute students across the potential new school structures and about how to conduct enrollment going forward. The district provided to the School Committee a “draft revised Enrollment Procedure” and a “preliminary draft Student Placement Process,” both with the caveat that these are early iterations intended only for discussion purposes.

The draft revised Enrollment Procedure removes the current “hometown guarantee” language – which gives Acton and Boxborough students the right to attend an elementary school located in their town of residence – and replaces it with a “hometown priority.”

The draft language states, “[u]nder hometown priority, students will receive preferential consideration for placement in an elementary school located in their hometown (Acton or Boxborough) provided that space is available at the student’s grade level at the time the placement is made.”

The School Committee is expected to continue their discussion of the reorganization options at their next meeting on December 18. The School Committee’s January 8 meeting will be reserved for public comment on AB Forward, and the School Committee is expected to vote on a final reorganization plan at their meeting on January 22. AB Forward materials are available at [https://www.abschools.org/read-the-plan](https://www.abschools.org/read-the-plan).
