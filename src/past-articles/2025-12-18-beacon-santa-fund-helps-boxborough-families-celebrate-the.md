Since 1965, the Beacon Santa Fund has helped “put a little something under the tree at Christmas” for families in need.

Each year, families from the 12 towns served by Beacon Santa submit applications asking for financial assistance for Christmas. Boxborough Community Services Coordinator Wendy Trinks coordinates the distribution of applications to local families in early October, and the Beacon Santa volunteers process them.

Trinks told Boxborough News, “I work with Beacon Santa every year, and every year the ‘ask’ gets bigger and bigger. This year was the biggest year ever for Boxborough families…This year (Beacon Santa) helped 21 families and about 49 children with Target gift cards worth $2,700…It is amazing how a small group of volunteers can make such a difference in families lives especially around the holidays."

In 2025, Beacon Santa received over 360 applications in total (serving over 850 children).

Long run by GateHouse Media, the fund is now managed by three women from Maynard. Since all board members and helpers volunteer their time, the donations go directly to help families in need.

“It’s not too late to make a donation to the fund,” said Trinks. Although 2025 gifts have been distributed, Beacon Santa collects funds throughout the year so that they can be ready to serve families in need next Christmas.

Donations can be made by mail to Beacon Santa Fund, c/o Middlesex Savings Bank, 7 Digital Way. Maynard, MA 01754 or to Beacon Santa Fund, PO Box 643. Maynard, MA 01754. Donations can also be made online through PayPal: [paypal.com/fundraiser/charity/1541809](http://paypal.com/fundraiser/charity/1541809) or through Venmo: @beaconsanta1965.
