With a blanket of snow on the ground, approximately 30 Boxborough residents gathered Sunday evening at the Boxborough Fire Station to light the town menorah to mark the start of Hanukkah.

The menorah was built and donated in 2024 by Public Celebrations and Ceremonies Committee (PCCC) Chair Jennette Kollman and her husband, Adam Klein. Kollman said the project was intended to represent and support the Jewish community in Boxborough.

"Boxborough celebrates many events, and we wanted to have one that celebrates Hanukkah," Kollman said to the group. "This is about celebrating light and bringing people together. Boxborough is a diverse community, and events like this help strengthen the sense of community by being the reason to gather."

Inside the fire station, tables were set up with refreshments and crafts for children attending the event.

Massachusetts State Senator Jamie Eldridge was also in attendance and spoke briefly before lighting the Menorah. "I'm so proud to represent such a significant Jewish community," Eldridge said. He also addressed a deadly shooting that occurred earlier that day in Sydney, Australia, which targeted members of the Jewish community, noting that gatherings like menorah lightings underscore the importance of community during times of tragedy.

The menorah lighting will continue nightly at 6 p.m. through December 22.
