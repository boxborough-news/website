At its December 11 meeting, the Boxborough Economic Development Committee (EDC) discussed the upcoming departure of SynQor, a power converter manufacturer, from Boxborough. The business is currently located at 155 Swanson Road.

In early December, SynQor announced that it will move its global headquarters and manufacturing facility from Boxborough to Salem, NH in early 2026. SynQor is a “supplier of power conversion solutions to the military, industrial, rail transportation, commercial avionics, medical and telecom/datacom markets.”

EDC Chair Abby Reip learned of the departure from local news outlets, alerted town administration, and requested that a representative from the town reach out to SynQor to conduct an “exit interview” to learn about their experience in Boxborough and why they decided to leave. She also suggested outreach to Jumbo Capital Management, LLC, the landlord of the Swanson Road building currently occupied by SynQor, to learn about their plans for the space. Town Planner Alec Wade has reached out to representatives from both companies to schedule a meeting.

When reached for comment, Reip stated that “SynQor’s departure came as a surprise to everyone and is a significant economic loss for the town. Boxborough will lose 250 jobs, and if the landlord does not have a tenant to lease the building, they could, conceivably, request an abatement. Given the town’s current financial demands, we need to be doing everything we can to learn how we retain and, more importantly, grow our business base.”

EDC members discussed how the town can better engage with the large companies that have a presence in town and the role of the EDC in helping to recruit and retain these businesses. Members agreed that identifying the businesses and establishing a communication channel is the first step.

EDC member Rich Guzzardi reported that the town has set up a call with MassEcon, a nonprofit whose mission is to “promote and champion Massachusetts as the best place to start, grow and locate a business.” The discussion will include how MassEcon may be able to help Boxborough create a “large business engagement plan” to retain and grow business in Boxborough.

“We need to… hav[e] that open dialogue with these companies… on a proactive basis…” said Guzzardi. “We have to have a real pitch, if you will, about what we are as Boxborough.”

The EDC’s next meeting is scheduled for January 15 at 8 p.m.
