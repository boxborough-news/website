The Boxborough Strengthening Police Community Partnerships Council (SPCP) met on December 3 and discussed ways to solicit input from the public and promote Council activities.

Council members discussed various ways to gather input from the public and promote their activities. After a lengthy discussion about organizing their own event, the Council decided that handing out flyers at an established event, such as Winterfest and Fifer’s Day, was the best solution for reaching their audience. The flyers will explain how to send input to the Council.

SPCP plans to develop a webpage portal for community members to provide their input. For those who prefer written forms or do not have access to a computer or smart phone, a drop box will be established in a public building. Town-owned sandwich boards will also be used to advertise the portal and Council activities.

In other business, the Council heard from Police Officer Max Bressi, who provided information about the monthly statistics that the department now publishes on the town website as well as on social media. When asked why the statistics seem so consistent from month to month, Officer Bressi explained that these are high level statistics, but more variation would be seen if they were broken down into more specific sub-categories. The Council expressed interest in exploring this and will address the sub-categories at a future meeting.

The public is encouraged to reach out to the Council at [spcp.bxb@gmail.com](mailto:spcp.bxb@gmail.com) with any inquiries about the Boxborough Police Department, ideas to strengthen the partnership between the community and the police, or to get involved with the Council.

The Council’s next meeting is January 7, 2026.
