The deadline to renew dog licenses is April 1. Residents can come in person to the Town Clerk’s Office, apply online via the Online Payment Portal at [https://bit.ly/3P5Ahpj](https://bit.ly/3P5Ahpj), or download and mail in a Dog License Application PDF available at [https://bit.ly/3Tn7ajW](https://bit.ly/3Tn7ajW).

After April 1, a $25 per dog late fee will be imposed. After May 1, the late fee rises to $50 per dog. Anyone who has not re-licensed their dog(s) by June 1 will be subject to a $100 late fee per dog and may have their names turned over to Ayer District Court by the Animal Control Officer.
