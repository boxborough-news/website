At its hybrid meeting on March 13, the Boxborough Sustainability Committee (BSC) reviewed the Specialized Stretch Code and updates for waste reduction projects.

BSC member Jay Krishnasamy presented an overview of the Specialized Stretch Code and led a discussion on proposed implementation for Boxborough.

Buildings in Massachusetts may adhere to three different levels of building energy code. The “Base Code” is the baseline energy code, which provides guidelines for building energy use such as heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems, lighting systems, and insulation. The opt-in “Stretch Code,” which Boxborough adopted in July 2020, is a more stringent set of energy efficiency requirements. The opt-in “Specialized Stretch Code” is an alternative version of the Stretch Code with even more rigorous standards. The goal of the Specialized Stretch Code is to encourage net-zero energy buildings through reducing use of fossil fuels, encouraging electrification, and promoting renewable energy.

Committee member Barbara Salzman noted that at the time Boxborough adopted the Stretch Code, “we were late to the party.” As of November 2024, 253 of 351 municipalities in Massachusetts have adopted the Stretch Code, and 48 municipalities have adopted the more stringent Specialized Stretch Code. The committee anticipates submitting a proposal for Boxborough to adopt the Specialized Stretch Code for the 2025 Fall Town Meeting.

A map of building codes for each Massachusetts municipality is available on the state website: [https://www.mass.gov/doc/building-energy-code-adoption-by-municipality/download](https://www.mass.gov/doc/building-energy-code-adoption-by-municipality/download).

BSC Chair Kate Davies informed the committee that the swap shed warrant article for Annual Town Meeting in May was unanimously recommended by the Select Board on March 10.

A working group comprised of members from the BSC, the Select Board, the Finance Committee, and Julia Greene, the MassDEP municipal assistance coordinator, will convene in early April to begin discussions on waste reduction projects.

The next Sustainability Committee meeting is scheduled for April 10, 2025, at 7 p.m.
