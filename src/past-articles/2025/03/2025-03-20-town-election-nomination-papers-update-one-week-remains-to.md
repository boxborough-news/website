One week remains to pull nomination papers for the May 20, 2025 Boxborough town election. The following individuals have “pulled” and “filed” nomination papers as of Thursday, March 20.

The deadline to obtain nomination papers is next Friday, March 28 at 5 p.m. In order to be listed on the ballot, nomination papers must be returned to the clerk, or “filed,” by Tuesday, April 1 at 5 p.m.

Those who have pulled papers may choose not to file them. A minimum of 25 signatures is required in order to file.

Voters who wish to vote in the town election on May 20 and at the May 12 Annual Town Meeting must register to vote by 5 p.m. on Friday, May 2.

A “Candidate’s Check List” with information about getting on the ballot and campaign finance law requirements is available at [https://bit.ly/3TfHKDA](https://bit.ly/3TfHKDA).
