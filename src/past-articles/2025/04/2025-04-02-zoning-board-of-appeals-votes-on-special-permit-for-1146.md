At its meeting on Tuesday, March 18, the Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA) continued the public hearing from March 4 to review the application submitted by Robert Lunnin of Element, a Testing, Inspection, and Certification business located at 1146 Mass Ave. Mr. Lunnin is seeking a special permit to replace existing signage.

Element acquired National Technical Systems (NTS) in 2022. In 1997, NTS obtained a special permit for one freestanding sign on Mass Ave and several smaller directional signs on the property. In 2023, Lunnin applied for and was granted a sign permit by the building department for a new freestanding sign and several directional signs.

In 2024, it was discovered that the 2023 sign permit was granted in error and that a special permit was required. Mr. Lunnin was notified that he needed to apply with the ZBA for a special permit for the freestanding (roadside) sign as well as the directional signage on the property, and he complied.

During the hearing, ZBA members reviewed the sizes of all of the signs and determined that the newly branded, freestanding sign was correctly sized and met the special permit requirements under the bylaw. Since this sign was a direct replacement for the NTS sign, which had been allowed under the previous special permit, the board agreed that it could remain in place.

However, the Board determined that the directional (accessory) signs were too large to be approved under the special permit and must be removed and replaced with signs that meet the requirements of the zoning bylaw. The Board voted unanimously to grant the special permit for the previously installed freestanding sign and for new directional signs that meet the zoning bylaw size requirement.
