At its meeting on April 3, the Boxborough Recreation Commission (Rec Com) tackled a variety of projects, including final plans for the upcoming RunBXB 5K race, summer programming updates, and a potential pump track.

The third annual RunBXB 5K race, sponsored by the Rec Com, will take place on Sunday, April 27th, from 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. at Craft Food Hall, 500 Beaver Brook Road. All safety and logistical services are set: police, fire, and EMT teams will be present. The Girl Scouts will once again run the water stations. T-shirts will be available during registration. Registration is $25 and is open here: [https://bit.ly/43Fg7uo](https://bit.ly/43Fg7uo).

Registration for Flerra Summer Playground, the half-day summer program run by the Rec Com throughout the month of July, is now open for campers. Registration for Flerra Summer Playground is available here: [https://tinyurl.com/FlerraRegistration](https://tinyurl.com/FlerraRegistration) .

The Commission is eyeing a family-friendly trip to Fenway Park during one of the weeks that Flerra Playground is active in. They are exploring the possibility of bus transportation; fundraising efforts are already in motion to support the excursion.

Rec Com is also considering building a pump track, a looping bike trail that mountain bike riders navigate using momentum instead of pedaling, on a local conservation site. Inspired by similar installations in Chelmsford and Harvard, the estimated $4,000 - $5,000 project could be constructed with help from volunteers. The New England Mountain Bike Association (NEMBA) has already offered its expertise, and the Commission is actively looking into whether the idea is viable in Boxborough.
