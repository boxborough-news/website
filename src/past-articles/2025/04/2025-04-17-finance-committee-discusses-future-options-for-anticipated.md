In the course of reviewing their presentation for 2025 Annual Town Meeting (ATM), the Finance Committee (FinCom) discussed future options for structuring the anticipated debt service for the proposed Fire Station, which is expected to be an article at next year’s (2026) ATM.

Debt service is the total amount of money required to repay both the principal and interest of a loan or debt obligation over a specific period of time. The Fire Station Bond is expected to be paid over 30 years. Debt service is similar to a mortgage or a student loan.

Historically, the town used a **level principal payment**, where the principal is paid down sooner. When the principal goes down, interest on the debt also goes down. The advantage to structuring the debt this way is that the town saves money over time.

The other way to structure the debt is through a **fixed payment approach**. With this approach, the interest is paid first and whatever is left over, goes to pay the principal. If the debt is structured using a fixed payment approach, the cash flow is better for the town, and at least initially, less expensive for taxpayers.

The decision as to how to structure the debt will be made by the Town Treasurer/Collector in consultation with the Town Administrator and our bonding agency prior to the 2026 ATM, where the Town will be asked to authorize funding for the proposed Fire Station.
