Boxborough’s Annual Town Meeting (ATM) will take place at the Blanchard Memorial School, 493 Massachusetts Ave. The 2025 ATM begins on Monday, May 12, with subsequent nights on May 13, 14, and 15 if necessary. Voter check-in for each session of the ATM begins at 6:30 p.m. and the meeting starts at 7 p.m.

A copy of the ATM Warrant was mailed to the household of every registered voter in Boxborough on April 18 and is available on the Town’s website. There are 54 articles on the Warrant, which includes a Special Town Meeting of six articles at the start within the ATM. Thirty consent agenda articles may be voted on with no debate.

In order to vote at Town Meeting and the Town Election, you must be registered to vote in Boxborough by Friday, May 2. You can register at Boxborough Town Hall or the Massachusetts Secretary of State website ([https://www.sec.state.ma.us/ovr/](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/ovr/)).

Boxborough’s Annual Town Election will take place Tuesday, May 20 at Town Hall, 29 Middle Rd. Polls open at 7 a.m. and close promptly at 8 p.m.

There are three contested races in this election: a three-year seat for the Select Board, two three-year seats on the Planning Board, and two three-year seats on the Board of Library Trustees.

Applications for Vote By Mail (VBM) ballots must be received by the Town Clerk before 5 p.m. on May 13. To be valid, completed VBM ballots must be returned to the Town Clerk’s office by the close of polls (8 p.m.) on Election Day. Absentee voting in person at the Town Clerk’s office can take place through Monday, May 19 at noon.
