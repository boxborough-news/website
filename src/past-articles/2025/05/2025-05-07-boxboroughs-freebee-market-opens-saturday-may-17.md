Boxborough’s Free Bee Market, a volunteer-driven free food network and market formed in the wake of the pandemic and Swanson Road apartment fire in 2021, is launching its 2025 season on Saturday May 17 from 10:30 a.m. to noon on the lawn of the UCC Boxborough church at 30 Middle Road.

Volunteers and neighbors will join together for another season of fighting food insecurity by promoting home gardening and reducing other food waste, all while making and amplifying local connections between people and organizations. All items are free; the goal is to have nothing left at the end of the market.

During the season, there will be six full FreeBee “Community Markets” held one Saturday per month through October: May 17, June 28, July 19, August 16 (with school supplies), September 13 (with a harvest theme), and October 18 (with Halloween costumes and toys).

Full “Community Markets” include fresh bread and produce, locally prepared food gleaned from Roche Bros, craft supplies and books, along with a free bouncy house and community booths. May 17 will also be a chance to bring and share extra plants. As usual, the food is varied and arrives last minute; it is always a surprise dependent on what is leftover locally.

“Food Rescue” market days will be smaller, focusing on sharing extra food collected from local supermarkets and farms. They will start in July when the produce harvest season begins.

FreeBee volunteer Mary Pavlik shares the following message for those interested in participating: “Would you like to share info about your group or initiative in a booth or showcase an activity during the Community Market? Contact Mary Pavlik [mary.j.pavlik@gmail.com](mailto:mary.j.pavlik@gmail.com) or [freebeecommunity@gmail.com](mailto:freebeecommunity@gmail.com).

Volunteers are always welcome to help with food pickup and sorting, as well as market setup and cleanup. FreeBee only works because the community makes it happen - together! All welcome! All FREE!”
