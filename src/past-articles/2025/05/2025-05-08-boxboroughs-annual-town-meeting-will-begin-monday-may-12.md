On Monday, May 12, Boxborough residents will gather for this year’s Annual Town Meeting (ATM). The meeting will begin at 7 p.m. at Blanchard Memorial School, 493 Massachusetts Ave. The meeting will continue as needed on the subsequent nights of the same week: May 13, 14, and 15. Voter check-in for each session of the ATM begins at 6:30 p.m.

What is a Town Meeting? According to Boxborough’s guide to Town Meeting, “Town Meeting is the foundation of Town Government, the purest form of direct democracy and fiscal responsibility. While the elected officials and appointed department heads supervise the day-to-day running of the town and schools, their authority extends only to managing employees and administering the expenditures that have already been voted by Town Meeting.”

A copy of the 2025 ATM Warrant was mailed to the household of every registered voter in Boxborough in April, and is available on the Town’s website at [https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/5033/FY26-ATM-Warrant?bidId=](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/5033/FY26-ATM-Warrant?bidId=).

The meeting will begin with a Special Town Meeting session, with six articles, followed by the ATM with 49 articles.

The Town of Boxborough has posted a “2025 Boxborough Annual Town Meeting Preview,” featuring Town Administrator Michael Johns and Town Moderator Dennis Reip, to help residents prepare for ATM.

The video gives an overview of what to expect at Town Meeting, and walks through this year’s warrant articles. Johns and Reip also explain what “Special Town Meeting” is and what “consent agenda” means. The video is available at [https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/boxborough/video/68028eca119aac0008ba9453?page=HOME](https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/boxborough/video/68028eca119aac0008ba9453?page=HOME).

Can’t attend ATM this year? Check [www.BoxboroughNews.org](http://www.boxboroughnews.org/) for daily summaries of ATM proceedings.
