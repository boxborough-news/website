The second night of Boxborough 2025 Town Meeting was called to order by Town Moderator Dennis Reip at 7 p.m. on Tuesday May 13 at the Blanchard Memorial School gymnasium with roughly 150 voters in attendance.

The first item of business was to take up the remaining four articles of the special town meeting. All four articles passed by show of hands votes.

After the Special Town Meeting was dissolved, the Annual Town Meeting reconvened with brief reports from the Acton Boxborough Regional School Committee Chair Adam Klein, the Planning Board Clerk Rebecca Verner, and Town Planner Alec Wade. Wade focused his report on the MBTA Communities law, also known as Section 3A of the Zoning Act, which will be presented and voted at Fall Town Meeting.

Voters then approved salaries for the town clerk and constable and changes to the Personnel Administration Plan and the Plan’s Classification and Compensation Schedule, which included a 2.7% wage adjustment for non-union and non-contract employees.

After hearing the Finance Committee report by Chair Tony Newton and 45 minutes of debate and questions, the Meeting voted a town operating budget of $29,299,232, a 4.6% increase from fiscal year 2025. The only change from the budget printed in the warrant was the addition of $4,100 to the consulting budget for the Planning Board.

The meeting then proceeded through the next 20 articles. Nineteen were approved, eight as part of a consent agenda. All articles passed by show of hands votes.

Article 15, funds for the assessor cyclical revaluation, was tabled until Wednesday night, when more information could be available.

Among others, items approved included funding of the first year of union contracts for employees of the Public Works, Fire, and Police departments; increased hours for the Community Services Coordinator and Treasurer/Collector; and a part-time staff addition for the transfer station. Also approved was $20,000 for the continuation of the Boxborough Emergency Assistance Program, which previously was funded with American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) funds. The meeting adjourned at 10:53 p.m.

Twenty-five articles (Article 15 and Articles 26 through 49) remain for deliberation on Wednesday May 14 at 7 p.m.
