The Blanchard Memorial School BrassWind Ensemble, under the direction of Chris Baird, earned a gold medal on Saturday May 10 at the Massachusetts Instrumental and Choral Conductors Association (MICCA) Solo and Ensemble Festival, which took place at Salem High School.

This is the 26th Gold Medal for Blanchard's Chamber Music Ensembles. They have also been awarded six Silver Medals.

Members of the Blanchard group that performed were all sixth graders, and included Jane Mao, Sebnem Ozkan, and Yesim Kocer playing flute; Clara Gao on clarinet; Eowyn Dowdy on trombone; and Sadie Harris playing tuba. Catherine Wang and Isha Kaduskar, both flute players, are also part of the ensemble but were not able to attend.

The group performed G.F. Handel's March from Scipione, Johann Pezel's Sarabande, and John Adson's Ayre.

Groups participating in the festival performed three pieces and were evaluated against established guidelines, not against other groups. Awards were given out, with the gold medal being the highest award available.

Through the years, Blanchard has been fortunate to have Brass and BrassWind Ensembles directed by Chris Baird, Vocal Ensembles directed by Margie Callaghan, Percussion Ensembles directed by Cesar Garde, Woodwind Ensembles directed by Henry Tervo and Amy McGlothlin, and Flute Ensembles directed by Chris Dentino.
