In the course of its meeting on April 22nd the Boxborough Housing Board (BHB) voted to continue funding the remediation of a contaminated well that serves one of the affordable housing units in Boxborough Meadows that is monitored by the Housing Board.

The Affordable Housing Trust, which funds the BHB’s expenses, has allocated $30,000 as discretionary spending that the BHB can use with no further approvals from the AHT. BHB members voted unanimously to commit to future funding of the water treatment maintenance costs up to a total of $20,000. To date, $8,925 has been spent.

In 2022, funds from the Affordable Housing Trust were used to install a filtration system to remediate the contamination, and in May 2022, the Housing Board voted to allocate up to $20,000 to cover the cost of yearly maintenance for this specific well for a three-year period.

In a unanimous vote at their April 22, 2025 meeting, the Housing Board voted to “earmark” the remaining $11,000 of the $20,000 allocation for the maintenance of this well. The BHB plans to review the expenses in 2026 to ensure that expenses have not exceeded the budget.
