At its meeting on Wednesday, June 11, the Public Celebrations and Ceremonies Committee (PCCC) met to review the many nominations that were received for the Golden Fife and Parade Marshal awards.

The Golden Fife Award is presented annually to a Boxborough resident who has demonstrated long-term volunteer service to the town. The parade marshal is awarded to a resident or non-resident who has done something exceptional for the town.

After reviewing the numerous nominations and engaging in discussion, the Committee selected the winners. Town tradition holds that the recipients’ names are kept secret until the Fifer’s Day Parade, which steps off from Blanchard School at 11 a.m. on Saturday, June 21.

Both recipients will ride in the parade and will be formally recognized at Flerra Field. The ceremony also recognizes two 6th grade Blanchard students who play the part of Luther Blanchard, the Minuteman fifer, and Lucie Hager, the historian who shared Luther’s story.

The students are selected by the Blanchard teachers for exhibiting “STARR Values.” STARR values reflect **support, teamwork, attitude, respect, and responsibility**. This year’s Luther and Lucie will be played by Zach O’Connor and Ellie Seifaee, respectively.

PCCC Chair Jennette Kollman shared her sentiments on the Fifer’s Day parade. “The Fifer’s Day Parade is more than just a walk through Boxborough; it’s a celebration of our community spirit and local pride.

I encourage everyone to join the parade whether it is cheering from the sidelines and catching candy or marching along or riding bikes. Come join us and be a part of our annual tradition.”

The PCCC welcomes additional groups—clubs, committees, organizations, businesses, and individuals—to march in the parade. For more information, contact the Boxborough PCCC at [pccc@boxborough-ma.gov.](mailto:pccc@boxborough-ma.gov)
