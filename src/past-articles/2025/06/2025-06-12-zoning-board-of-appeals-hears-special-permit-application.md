At its virtual meeting on Tuesday June 3, the Boxborough Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA) held a public hearing to review the Special Permit application from Brett Gutheil of BHG109 LLC for the construction of an indoor, 8-unit contractor storage facility at 975 Massachusetts Ave.

Gutheil developed and owns the adjacent property at 881 Mass Ave, which serves as building trade rental units. The proposed building will have the same aesthetics of the building at 881 Mass Ave, which is a corrugated steel exterior with a single garage bay for each unit.

During the hearing, the applicant’s engineer, Limhuot Tiv of Goldsmith, Prest & Ringwall, Inc., presented the application and answered questions from the board that focused on the slope of the property, the proximity of wetlands, and the access to the site from Mass Ave.

Board members briefly commented on the appearance of the proposed building. Although it will not be visible from the road, ZBA Chair Mark Barbadoro opined that steel facade buildings are an unattractive aesthetic for Mass Ave, which is Boxborough’s “main street.”

The Boxborough Zoning Bylaw does not regulate the exterior materials and aesthetics in the Business (B) zoning district in which this building is proposed, so board members may only make suggestions on the building’s appearance.

The ZBA voted to continue the public hearing on July 15 at 7:35 p.m.
