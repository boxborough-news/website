At its October 21 meeting, the Water Resources Committee (WRC) finalized the Comprehensive Water Resources Report from Weston & Sampson (W&S), the consulting firm hired by the town for “water resources planning services.” W&S has been working with the town for over a year to assess and report on Boxborough’s water resources, future water needs, and wastewater demands.

WRC members were joined by Emily DiFranco of W&S, who previewed the presentation that she will give to the Boxborough Select Board on Monday, October 27. DiFranco’s presentation focuses on the general conclusions of the (water resources) study and outlines W&S’s findings on Boxborough’s present and future capability to provide drinking water and to dispose of wastewater (septic-system management). WRC Chair Les Fox noted that the report should be of interest to the general public and will be “the beginning of an educational process.”

In other business, WRC members continued their discussion related to Boxborough’s firefighting infrastructure and observed that a current evaluation is needed. Member Bryon Clemence reported that he had located old(er) maps of the fire ponds, hydrants, and cisterns in Boxborough and had conveyed them to Fire Chief Kivlan for review. On the west side of Interstate 495, where the Littleton waterline project is progressing, fire hydrants have been installed, and the system is charged for firefighting. The Littleton waterline project is being conducted by the Littleton Electric Light and Water Departments (LELWD) to bring clean, treated drinking water to properties located in Boxborough on Beaver Brook Road, Swanson Road, and Codman Hill Road. For project updates, visit [www.lelwd.com/boxborough-water-main](http://www.lelwd.com/boxborough-water-main).

The next WRC meeting will be on November 18, 2025, at 7:30 p.m., via Zoom.
