At its meeting on Thursday, October 30, the Boxborough Planning Board reopened the public hearing for the renewal of a Special Permit Application received from Campanelli-Trigate LLC for the buildout of The Park at Beaver Brook (former Cisco campus). Prior to hearing public comments, Planning Board Chair Rebecca Verner noted that the special permit decision requires a super majority vote in favor (four out of five Planning Board members).

Campanelli is seeking to reinstate a lapsed Open Space Commercial Development (OSCD) Special Permit with “minor modifications to parking.” Their proposal offers to donate Lot 100 to the Town of Boxborough for open space and passive recreation purposes and put an additional 94 acres into conservation/open space restriction in return for 667,500 square feet of new development, which translates to the construction of five new buildings.

Boxborough Sustainability Committee Chair Kate Davies was the only speaker at the hearing. In her comments, Davies urged the Planning Board to require Campanelli to build in compliance with the Massachusetts specialized stretch energy code as a condition of the special permit.

“This code is designed to accelerate decarbonization and is aligned with Boxborough’s sustainability and climate goals…the proposed development should reflect the adopted sustainability policies and relevant planning documents of both the Town and the Commonwealth.” In closing, Davies said, “We (the Sustainability Committee) believe that by investing in the stretch code at this stage, Campanelli is going to benefit…in the long run.”

The Board spent two hours going through the special permit “decision document” and discussing and inserting the various comments. The Board asked Town Land Use Attorney Adam Costa for feedback regarding the OSCD and Special Permit criteria and directed questions on wastewater use, traffic, parking, and vegetative buffer screening to Campanelli attorney Johanna Schneider and Campanelli partner Russ Dion.

The Board did not complete their review of the decision document and voted to continue the public hearing on Monday, December 1 at 8 p.m.
