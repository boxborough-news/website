On Monday, November 10, Thinking Flower owner Divya Suryaprasad officially opened the doors to Boxborough’s first bakery and coffee shop. State Senator Jamie Eldridge and State Representative Dan Sena joined members of the Boxborough Economic Development Committee (EDC), Boxborough Select Board, and town administration to congratulate Suryaprasad on her accomplishment and thanked her for “investing in Boxborough.”

Flanked by her supporters, Suryaprasad cut the ceremonial ribbon and officially opened the doors at 629 Mass Ave (next to Middlesex Bank). A line of eager customers filed in from the rain to taste Suryprasad’s artisan pastries and hot and cold coffee drinks.

“Thinking Flower is a forward thinking small-batch artisan bakery specializing in handcrafted viennoiserie (croissants) and cakes baked fresh daily, paired with specialty coffees sourced from a local roastery,” Suryaprasad [previously told Boxborough News](https://www.boxboroughnews.org/features#h.g63es3dulz36). The shop has a seating capacity of 25, with several small tables, one larger communal table, and a counter along the front window.

Currently, Thinking Flower’s hours are Wednesday through Friday from 7:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. and Saturday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

The Boxborough Select Board approved an On-Premises Restaurant All-Alcoholic Beverages License for Thinking Flower on October 27. Suryaprasad is awaiting approval from the state's Alcoholic Beverages Control Commission (ABCC). If approved, Suryaprasad may extend her hours into early evening.

In 2024, Suryaprasad was awarded $15,000 from the EDC’s Boxborough Business Improvement Grant Program. EDC member Rich Guzzardi attended the ribbon-cutting ceremony. When reached for comment afterward, Guzzardi responded, “Thinking Flower Bakery is a great addition to Boxborough’s local business community, providing a warm and comfortable place for residents and visitors to enjoy excellent baked goods and coffees. Divya has done a tremendous job with the space, has hired experienced staff, and is serving amazing baked products.”
