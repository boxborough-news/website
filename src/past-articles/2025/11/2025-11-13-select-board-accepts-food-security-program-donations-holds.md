At their meeting on November 10, the Select Board voted unanimously to accept $4,750 in donations to the town’s Community Services Gift Account, all contributed in the week since the town launched its Food Security Program.

Boxborough Well-Being Committee Chair Mary Pavlik spoke to the Select Board about the town’s efforts to create and launch a Food Security Program in response to the uncertainty surrounding access to Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) benefits.

Pavlik shared how Boxborough’s Community Services Coordinator Wendy Trinks, with the support of other town staff and the Well-Being Committee, had mobilized over the past week to collect and distribute food and gift cards. “It’s really remarkable and very heartening; I want to thank the entire community…it’s a time to be really proud to be [part of] this town,” said Pavlik. Trinks is working to distribute the collected resources to those in need of assistance.

The Select Board also hosted Boxborough’s 2025 Civic Academy graduation, led by Assistant Town Administrator Rajon Hudson. Civic Academy is a community education program that teaches participants about town government with the goal of inspiring further civic participation. This is the second time Boxborough has held this program; the first was in 2023. Hudson recognized the 11 graduates of the program and thanked all the town staff and volunteers who presented to the Civic Academy participants over the course of the program. Hudson presented certificates and pins to the graduates.

Select Board Chair Kristin Hilberg announced that Town Administrator Mike Johns will increase the depth and breadth of his “reports” to the Select Board going forward, in an effort to keep all members of the Board more aware of the work happening at Town Hall.

Johns’ expanded report included an update on efforts to get quotes for temporary quarters and swing space for the fire department; the town’s ongoing pursuit of grants; the town’s work on an emergency management plan; and an update on the fiscal year 2027 budget efforts, including the development of two budgets – “Budget A” (if override passes) and “Budget B” (if override fails).

Johns noted that he will come back to the Select Board in two weeks with a budget policy recommendation so that the Board can discuss and set the town’s budget policy for the coming year. School Committee Vice Chair Adam Klein commented that the School Committee will need guidance about what Boxborough’s “B budget” could look like, as the School Committee will need to prepare for the possibility of the “B budget” and associated cuts to the school budget.

The Select Board’s next meeting is Monday, November 24 at 7 p.m.
