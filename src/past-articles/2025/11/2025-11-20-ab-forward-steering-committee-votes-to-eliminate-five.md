On November 18, the AB Forward Steering Committee, with an audience of roughly 70, began the difficult task of whittling down the list of possible district reorganization options developed by the Acton-Boxborough Regional School District’s consultant DM Group in connection with the “AB Forward” strategic planning and reorganization initiative.

By the end of the meeting, the Steering Committee voted unanimously to eliminate five options from consideration: Option 1 (status quo), Option 9 (expand the district), and Options 7v1, 7v2, and 8v1 (all involving moving sixth graders to the Junior High School). All other reorganization options remain on the table for further consideration.

Notably, all of the remaining options include closing the building that currently houses the Conant elementary school.

In the Steering Committee’s second and final straw poll of the evening to “keep” or “eliminate” each option, the options with the most “keep” votes were Option 5v2 (five grade-banded schools, with Blanchard remaining K-6) with 16 out of 18 members voting to “keep” and Option 6 (three K-6 schools, one of which would be Blanchard), with 14 out of 18 members voting to “keep.”

In the same straw poll, only 3 of the 18 members voted to “keep” Option 5v1, the “full grade-band” model. This option would make Blanchard Memorial School, the district’s only elementary school located in Boxborough, into a school for only Pre-K and Kindergarten students – with students moving to other schools for grades 1-3 and 4-6.

“AB Forward” is a strategic planning and reorganization initiative to “create an updated strategic plan as well as a set of 3-5 actionable options for district reorganization that could guide district operations for the next 5-10 years.” The Steering Committee includes administrators, school committee members, educators, and parents and is charged with guiding the district’s work with the consultant.

The Steering Committee next meets on December 2 and is expected to discuss the remaining options with the goal of narrowing to a short list of options that will be presented to the School Committee at their meeting on December 4.

The School Committee’s January 8 meeting will be reserved for public comment on AB Forward, and the School Committee is expected to vote on a final reorganization plan at their meeting on January 22.

Across the two-and-a-half hour Steering Committee meeting, members engaged in an initial discussion about the reorganization options, an initial poll about the options, a group exercise to discuss pros and cons of each option, and then a second poll about the options.

During the Steering Committee’s discussion, School Committee Vice Chair (from Boxborough) Adam Klein asked the consultant about “equitable resource distribution” in the potential scenario where Blanchard becomes the smallest of three elementary school buildings.

DM Group consultant Joe Costello responded that having buildings of different sizes within a district can “create problems” with respect to staffing ratios. However, he noted that some districts solve this problem by using a set of “minimum guarantees” of staff per building. Costello indicated that it would be important to “lean into those conversations” whenever budget and staffing decisions are being made.

Towards the end of the meeting, Superintendent Peter Light thanked the Steering Committee members for their work thus far and the work ahead, noting that “there isn’t a right answer.”

The following day, Superintendent Light released the following statement: “This stage of the process involves incredibly difficult decision-making. A decision that may feel like relief to some may represent a significant loss for others. I would strongly encourage us all to act with both empathy and compassion for all of the members of our community as we move through the next month or so together. This is not easy and is deeply impacting many of us in different ways.”
