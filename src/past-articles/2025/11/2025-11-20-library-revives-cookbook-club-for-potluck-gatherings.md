The “A Recipe for…” cookbook club is back, and it’s a hit (again!) with Boxborough residents. In September, the Sargeant Memorial Library re-introduced the cookbook club, reviving a very popular club that ran from 2019 until the COVID-19 shutdown forced it to end.

Jen Bauder, Senior Library Assistant, plans and organizes the bi-monthly club. With the help of community members, a cookbook is selected and residents can sign-up to make dishes from the recipes in the chosen cookbook to contribute to a potluck dinner at the library.

In November, the club used “Indian(-ish),” a cookbook by Priya Krishna. Some of the dishes cooked and sampled were: Chhole (chickpea and tomato stew), Priya’s favorite Dal, Kaddu (sweet and sour butternut squash), Caramelized Ginger Mushrooms, Roasted Aloo Gobhi (potatoes and cauliflower), Matar Paneer, and several different raitas. The feast was topped off with an Eggless Pineapple Cake.

Bauder said she has a background in anthropology and loves to explore cultures through food.

Boxborough residents and November cookbook club participants Marcia Nelson and Dianne Hoff said they love sharing a meal and getting a chance to meet and connect with other members of the community. Participant Mamata Nanda said she often visits the library with her daughter, attending different events, and thought the cookbook club sounded like fun.

Anyone in the community can sign up, but the group is limited to 15 people. The next cookbook club potluck is scheduled for Thursday, January 29 at 6:15 pm and will feature “Healthyish” by Lindsay Hunt.

Registration starts on Wednesday December 17 at 10 a.m. Visit the Sargent Memorial Library website at [https://boxlib.org/](https://boxlib.org/) for more info and to register.
