At their meeting on Thursday, November 20, the Boxborough Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) received reports from consultant Context Architecture about 1300 Mass Ave and 984 Mass Ave, two of the three potential fire station locations that remain under consideration. The reports did not flag any significant concerns with either site.

With respect to 984 Mass Ave, the committee discussed whether and how fire station traffic might enter and exit via Sara’s Way, which is currently a private road, and/or whether the town would need to work with the Massachusetts Department of Transportation to make a site entrance along Mass Ave.

With respect to 1300 Mass Ave, the committee talked through possible areas of further study, but decided that it was not necessary to authorize any additional work beyond the studies already in progress.

At their last meeting on November 13, the FSBC voted to move forward on only three potential fire station sites: 984 Mass Avenue, 1300 Mass Avenue, and 72 Stow Road. The committee also voted to authorize a series of additional studies at 984 Mass Ave and 1300 Mass Ave so that those sites can be compared to the already-studied 72 Stow Road.

Once the committee has collected and evaluated the results of the requested studies, it will turn back to the site selection matrix tool to compare the three locations and choose a site, or sites, to bring to Special Town Meeting in March 2026.

At Special Town Meeting, the FSBC is expected to ask for land purchase approval(s), as well as funding for the design and bid phase of the project.

The Committee must decide on final language for the Special Town Meeting warrant by January 8. They have meetings scheduled for December 4, December 11, January 5, and January 8.
