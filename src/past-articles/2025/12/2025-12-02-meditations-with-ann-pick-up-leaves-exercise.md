Last month, we learned about the anatomy of your foot and ankle and how to decrease foot pain and increase balance. Now, let me talk about another important exercise to add to your preventative and curative foot movements.

If you go to a podiatrist for foot pain, they tell you about this particular exercise under the name of “ Pick Up Tissues” exercise. I hate anything virus-related so I have changed the name in my Yoga classes to “ Pick Up Leaves” exercise. If you Google it, you will probably find it under the name of “Short Foot Exercise.” All exercises are describing the same actions, so choose a label, stand up, and try it.

First, stand on both feet evenly with your legs slightly wider than hip distance apart. Remember that your “hip distance’ has nothing to do with the flesh of your body and only refers to the distance of your pelvic girdle. Next, lean a bit forward so you can see your right foot easily, soften the knees and plant the toes securely on the floor. Next, curl your toes under as tightly as possible while keeping them on the floor. Keep the toes curled as you take the entire ball of the foot off the ground keeping your heel planted. Spread the toes to “release the leaf” you have grabbed with your toes then place the ball of the foot and the toes back on the floor. Each move is a distinct action so don’t rush through any of the movements. Repeat 5-10 times, daily if you can.

Once you have tried this, you will feel how important this movement is to build strength and flexibility at the foot level. Your foot may feel tired and achy, maybe a movement was particularly challenging so not much happened when you tried it, or maybe a cramp was borderline the entire time. But if you persevere, slowly and consistently, the arches will become more supported which affects the alignment of the toes. Bunion discomfort can be minimized as better foot dynamics are created. Some people can reverse or slow down this progression. Ankles become more supple and stronger so balance improves, and our brain is stimulated by the body mapping activity you have given it to focus on. This is a simple and accessible exercise that you can do anytime day or night. Try it for a few weeks and feel the difference.
