At their meeting on December 4, the Boxborough Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) began to draft two warrant articles for the Special Town Meeting scheduled for March 9, 2026. At Special Town Meeting, the FSBC is expected to ask for land purchase approval(s) as well as funding for the design and bid phase of the fire station project.

The FSBC has not yet chosen a site for the fire station. In mid-November, the FSBC voted to move forward on only three potential sites: 984 Mass Avenue, 1300 Mass Avenue, and 72 Stow Road. The committee also voted to authorize a series of additional studies at 984 Mass Ave and 1300 Mass Ave so that those sites can be compared to the already-studied 72 Stow Road.

The committee is still waiting to receive the requested site information but expects to have at least preliminary findings before January. FSBC Chair Mary Brolin reminded members that the committee will have “homework” to review site information materials in advance of the FSBC’s two meetings scheduled for the first week of January.

During their two meetings on January 5 and 8, the committee expects to use their site selection matrix tool to compare the three locations and choose a site, or sites, to bring to Special Town Meeting. The committee must decide on final language for the Special Town Meeting warrant by January 8.

The committee also briefly discussed “green” construction strategy and whether to pursue actual LEED certification or simply design to LEED “certifiable” standards. The town’s consultants from Vertex and Context Architecture noted that designing towards “certifiable” standards without pursuing actual certification would save money on design fees and allow for more flexibility. Chair Brolin stated that she will put this issue on the agenda for the FSBC’s next meeting on December 11 in case members of the public wish to weigh in.
