**Planning Board Continues Public Hearing for The Park at Beaver Brook**

At its meeting on Monday, December 1, the Planning Board reopened the public hearing for the renewal of an Open Space Commercial Development (OSCD) Special Permit Application received from Campanelli-Trigate LLC for the buildout of The Park at Beaver Brook (former Cisco campus). The hearing first opened on June 2, 2025 and has been continued eight times.

Planning Board members worked through a decision document, discussing the proposed $25,000 gift to the town that is tied to the donation of Lot 100, as well as proposed conditions, such as limiting building heights and requiring sustainable building and landscaping practices.

The primary “sticking point” is the length of time of the permit. Applicant Campanelli is requesting that the Commercial Development Period be extended twenty years, until January 1, 2045, to allow for the full build-out of the project as shown on their 2025 Master Plan. The original permit was for fifteen years.

The Board did not resolve this issue and will return to it at the continued hearing on January 12, 2026.

**Housing Board Discusses Resale of Affordable Unit and Educating Residents on Cohousing**

At its meeting on Tuesday, December 2, the Boxborough Housing Board (BHB) discussed challenges related to the resale of an affordable unit that the BHB monitors. The homeowner is deceased, and there are legal complications with the estate. BHB members are focused on reselling the unit and ensuring that it remains in Boxborough’s affordable housing inventory.

The Board briefly discussed how and where a cohousing project could fit into Boxborough’s housing inventory. Board members agreed that educating residents about cohousing would be the next step and proposed hosting a book club to discuss a book titled, *Cohousing: A Contemporary Approach to Housing Ourselves* (Charles Durrett and Kathryn McCamant).

The next BHB meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, January 6, 2026 at 7 p.m. in hybrid format.
