In 2025, Boxborough News published 350 stories over 47 editions. Our all-volunteer team reported on roughly 175 Boxborough board and committee meetings and wrote more than 100 feature stories about people, places, and events in our community – including three regular columns on gardening, sustainability, and wellness.

Over the course of 2025, we grew our subscriber list by almost 280 readers, bringing the total weekly newsletter subscribers to almost 800.

We followed the town elections and annual and special town meetings; the development of long-term projects like the fire station, MBTA Communities, and AB Forward; ICE activity in Boxborough; the launch of new town initiatives like the swap shed at the transfer station and “Village for a Day;” and local business news.
