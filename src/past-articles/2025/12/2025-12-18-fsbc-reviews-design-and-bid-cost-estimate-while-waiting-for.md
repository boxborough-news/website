At their meeting on December 11, the Boxborough Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) reviewed an estimate of “Design and Bid” costs for the next phase of the fire station building project. The FSBC is expected to ask for “Design and Bid” funding, as well as land purchase approval(s), at a Special Town Meeting (STM) scheduled for March 2026.

According to the estimate presented by Steve Kirby of Vertex, the town’s Owner’s Project Manager, the design and bid costs for the fire station project are expected to be approximately $2.5 million, plus $272,000 for additional “green” design.

The committee reviewed the estimate line by line and asked questions about traffic control lights, green building standards, and pre-bid commissioning services. The FSBC held no votes on the estimate, but the committee is expected to incorporate the estimate into their STM warrant article(s), which will be finalized in January.

The FSBC is still considering three potential sites for the fire station: 984 Mass Avenue, 1300 Mass Avenue, and 72 Stow Road. The estimates differ slightly depending on the site, ranging from $2,480,310 (984 Mass Ave) to $2,496,060 (72 Stow Road).

In November, the committee voted to authorize a series of additional studies at 984 Mass Ave and 1300 Mass Ave so that those sites can be compared to the already-studied 72 Stow Road. The committee is still waiting to receive the results of these studies but expects to have at least preliminary findings before January.

During their two meetings on Monday January 5 and Thursday January 8, the FSBC expects to use their site selection matrix tool to compare the three locations and choose a site, or sites, to bring to the Special Town Meeting. The committee must decide on final language for the Special Town Meeting warrant by January 8.
