At their meeting on March 26, the Boxborough Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) walked through the process of selecting a firm to design the town’s new fire station.

The town’s Request for Qualifications (RFQ) for a designer is currently posted, with proposals due April 1. Steve Kirby of Vertex, the town’s owner’s project manager (OPM), reported to the FSBC that the town held a “designer briefing session” on March 25 with nine firms in attendance. He noted that a total of 39 firms have requested the RFQ, 16 of them architectural firms.

Proposals received by the April 1 deadline will be reviewed and scored by each FSBC member in advance of the FSBC’s next meeting on April 9. At their April 9 meeting, the FSBC is expected to choose three firms to interview. The FSBC is expected to conduct interviews and select a finalist on April 15. After a firm is selected, a contract will be negotiated and signed. A project kickoff meeting is expected by late May.

FSBC members discussed the logistics and structure of the April 15 interviews, which will begin at 6 p.m. The committee agreed that there will be no public input or other public participation allowed at the interviews, but the interviews will be open for the public to attend in person. The interviews will not be broadcast live but will be recorded.

For the past several years, the town has worked with design firm Context Architecture (Context). Context was originally hired after the town put out an RFQ in August 2022, and Context was the only applicant. In February 2026, a complaint was filed with the Massachusetts Office of the Attorney General regarding that selection process. After voters approved funding for the “design and bid” phase of the fire station project at the March 9 Special Town Meeting, the town confirmed that it would conduct another designer selection process.

The FSBC’s April meetings are scheduled for Thursday April 9, Wednesday April 15, and Thursday April 23.
