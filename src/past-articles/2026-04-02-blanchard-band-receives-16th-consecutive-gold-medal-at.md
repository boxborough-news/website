On Saturday March 28, the Blanchard Memorial School Advanced Band earned a Gold Medal at the Massachusetts Instrumental and Choral Conductors Association (MICCA) Concert Festival. They performed three pieces and were evaluated against a performance standard, not against other groups. Awards were given out, with the Gold Medal being the highest award, signifying a superior performance.

The band is led by Chris Baird with Cesar Garde as the percussion director. This is their 16th Gold Medal in their last 16 appearances, their 17th gold overall. Said Baird, "I was so proud of how well our students performed in earning our 16th consecutive gold medal. They rose to the occasion, as they always do and played a very exciting concert."

This year the Blanchard Advanced Band is made up of 37 musicians from the 4th, 5th and 6th grades. The pieces they performed were Activity March, Two English Dances, and Imperium.

Dr. Walter Pavasaris, Professor Emeritus at the Berklee College of Music, was one of the adjudicators. He also led the band in a clinic after their performance, where they received valuable feedback. About their performance, he stated, “nice attention to detail, there's a nice quality of sound.”

The festival was held at multiple sites in Massachusetts; the Blanchard Band performed at King Philip Regional High School in Wrentham, MA. The band was judged by three adjudicators on eight categories, such as dynamics, balance and blend, tone quality, and tempo. In addition, it was judged on the overall choice of music performed.
