Note from the Editors: “Being Well in Boxborough” is a column written by the Boxborough Well-Being Committee, led by Chair Mary Pavlik.*

**Message for the Community from the Well-Being Committee:** After a long cold winter and a demanding flu season, spring has finally arrived. The Well-Being Committee is dedicated to fostering community connection as we emerge into the light of a new season.

**Updates from the Community Services Coordinator (CSC):** Wendy Trinks, CSC, recently visited Household Goods in Acton and noticed the urgent need for smaller appliances, kitchenware and linens. If you have further questions, please email [wtrinks@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:wtrinks@boxborough-ma.gov).

How to Help: Drop off the essential items, listed below, at Household Goods, 530 Main Street Acton on Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays from 9:00 AM to Noon.

- Small Appliances: coffee makers (drip/K-cup), toasters, and countertop microwaves, air fryers and more.
- Kitchen Essentials: Silverware, plates, pots, pans, and glassware (no stemware).
- Linens: Clean sheets (they don’t have to match), towels, and blankets in all sizes (no weighted blankets).
- **Link to All Accepted Items:** [https://www.householdgoods.org/donate/donate-goods/donation-guidelines/](https://www.householdgoods.org/donate/donate-goods/donation-guidelines/)

**May Movement Series:** Thanks to a grant from the AB Cultural Council obtained by Ms. Trinks, the Well-Being Committee is excited to announce the spring Movement Series beginning this May. These popular sessions are free to all residents and will be held in the fresh air on the Sargent Memorial Library lawn, weather permitting. Registration for each class is on the library events calendar, available at [https://boxlib.org/news-events/events](https://boxlib.org/news-events/events).

**Resources and Volunteer Opportunities: Bee-Long Boxborough Community Event:** Another way to stay mentally healthy and combat helplessness in these challenging times is by taking small, positive actions together. The Bee-Long Boxborough Community Event, is an open house to support neighboring immigrant families with simple, impactful gestures. Come as you are, bring a friend, and help us ensure that every neighbor "bee-longs." This event, hosted by the Boxborough UCC Church and supported by the Well-Being Committee, takes place this Saturday, April 11th from 9:30-11am at the Community Center, 30 Middle Road. Join others for a welcoming, low-pressure gathering where everyone is invited to connect with neighbors and learn more about how to help.

- Education: Learn foundational information about local immigration.
- Advocacy: Use provided postcards and letters to reach out to elected officials.
- Direct Support: We are collecting diaper donations and holding a coin drive for LUCE to support local immigrant families.
- Family Fun: Kids can enjoy a banner-making activity while everyone enjoys coffee, cookies, and music.

**Staying Healthy with Nashoba Health:** While the peak of the flu season may be behind us, early detection remains critical. Combined Flu A, Flu B, and COVID-19 tests are now widely available at local pharmacies for quick respiratory screening. For more information on avoiding sickness and preventing the spread of germs in Massachusetts, visit [https://www.mass.gov/info-details/protect-yourself-and-others-from-getting-sick](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/protect-yourself-and-others-from-getting-sick).
