At its much anticipated four-hour meeting on Thursday, April 9, the Acton-Boxborough Regional School Committee voted on the district’s new system for elementary school enrollment. The new system includes three geographic zones corresponding to the three elementary campuses; “flex zones” for balancing enrollment in future years; and “transition policies” for current students.

First, the School Committee voted 7-1 for geographic zone “Scenario 7.” Scenario 7 assigns all of Boxborough, plus a contiguous “Blanchard Expansion Zone” along the western edge of Acton, to the Blanchard Memorial School in Boxborough. The remainder of Acton is split in two, with the students in the western half assigned to the Boardwalk Campus and students in the eastern half assigned to the Parker-Damon Building.

Second, the School Committee voted unanimously on the “flex zone” locations and associated policies. The “flex zones” are four designated areas (one in Boxborough and three in Acton) that have a second alternate school assignment in addition to their primary geographic assignment. If the district needs to balance enrollment in future years, they can assign incoming students (and siblings) living in flex zones to the alternate school instead of the primary geographic assignment.

In their motion to approve the flex zones, the School Committee asked that the district bring back for approval before the end of the school year the administrative procedure that will be written to implement the flex zone policies.

Third, the School Committee voted on transitional enrollment policies. These policies are a series of school placement adjustments for current students through a system of “grandfathering” and district discretion. They are also temporary; they will only be used to place current K-5 students and will no longer be used after the 2026-27 school year.

The approved package of transitional policies include allowing Acton residents currently at Blanchard (but not their siblings) to stay at Blanchard; allowing Acton residents in the “Blanchard Expansion Zone” – the portion of Acton assigned to Blanchard School – (but not their siblings) to stay at their current campus to the extent possible; allowing families to opt-out of grandfathering in order to keep siblings together; and allowing the district the discretion to manage class sizes with further adjustments to be utilized in a particular priority order.

The vote on the transitional enrollment policies was split 4 to 4, but the motion passed 8.5 to 7 due to Acton members’ votes carrying more weight than Boxborough members’ votes. Boxborough member Adam Klein voted in favor of the transitional enrollment policies, noting that they will serve as a “relief valve to balance class sizes” that is mutually beneficial to the district and to families. Two Boxborough members, Liz Fowlks and Lakshmi Kaja, voted against the transition enrollment policies, noting that the “grandfathering” systems can be perceived as “favoritism” and “cherry-picking.” Boxborough member Vikram Parikh was not present at the meeting.

After the discussion and voting on geographic enrollment concluded, ABRSD Superintendent Peter Light presented about the AB Forward implementation timeline, student and staff placement process, and the short- and long-term expected budgetary impacts of the AB Forward reorganization. Supporting materials and further details can be found at [https://abschools.community.diligentoneplatform.com/Portal/MeetingInformation.aspx?Org=Cal&Id=224](https://abschools.community.diligentoneplatform.com/Portal/MeetingInformation.aspx?Org=Cal&Id=224).

In other business, the Committee voted to approve FY27 athletics, activities, and parking fees; voted to establish the “Common Thread Taskforce;” and voted to revise the school calendar to reflect new half-days for elementary schools on May 22, June 22, and June 23.
