At their meeting on Thursday, April 9, the Boxborough Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) chose three design firms to interview at their next meeting on April 15: Context Architecture, Kaestle Boos Associates Inc., and Tecton Architects.

In late March, after Boxborough voters approved funding for the “design and bid” phase of the fire station construction project, the town published a Request for Qualifications (RFQ) for a design firm. In early April, the town received a total of eight responses to the RFQ.

To decide which firms to interview, committee members reviewed, scored, and ranked each designer’s proposal, providing Steve Kirby of Vertex, the town’s owner’s project manager (OPM), with their top three choices.

Kirby gave a brief summary of the proposals and explained that two of the designers were disqualified because they did not meet the minimum requirement of experience designing at least three fire station projects with a cost greater than $20 million. He also provided an overview of the references that were obtained for each of the firms.

The committee discussed qualifications and experience of each firm’s proposed project manager and staff, experience with relevant projects, and the design styles presented in the proposals.

FSBC Chair Mary Brolin noted the high quality of the submission packages and said that based on the choices, “we can make a good decision.”

The FSBC will conduct interviews and select a finalist on April 15. They have scheduled interviews at 6 p.m., 7 p.m., and 8 p.m. The interviews will be open to the public to attend in person, but there will be no public input or other public participation allowed. The interviews will not be broadcast live but will be recorded for later viewing.

Noting the strong qualifications of the three shortlisted firms, the committee directed Kirby to inform the three firms to focus their presentations on the proposed staff/team that will be working on the project, as this is a key consideration for the FSBC.

After a firm is selected, a contract will be negotiated and signed in mid-May. A project kickoff meeting is expected by late May.

The FSBC’s next meeting after the interviews is scheduled for Thursday, April 23.
