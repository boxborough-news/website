On April 15, the Boxborough Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) met for four and a half hours to interview and rank three design firms: Context Architecture, Kaestle Boos Associates, Inc., and Tecton Architects. The FSBC voted unanimously to proceed with contract negotiations with Context Architecture.

The town has worked with Context on the fire station project for the past several years. Context was originally hired after the town put out an RFQ in August 2022 and Context was the only applicant. In February 2026, there was a complaint filed with the Massachusetts Office of the Attorney General regarding the 2022 selection process. Town Administrator Mike Johns decided that it was in the best interest of the town to put out another RFQ and conduct another designer selection process.

The committee met with representatives of each firm for roughly 45 minutes each, listening to the firm’s pitch and then asking questions. The committee held brief discussions after each interview. After all three interviews concluded, the committee compared the three firms and ranked them. All but one member ranked Context first; all but one member ranked Tecton second; and all members agreed that Kaestle Boos Associates should be ranked third.

In comparing the firms, many members agreed that it was hard to choose between Context and Tecton, as both had strong presentations and written materials. In ultimately choosing Context, members noted that Context had provided a detailed timeline of the project, while Tecton had not provided any timeline; and that Context had brought with them to the interview a larger team and Tecton had brought a smaller team. While some members noted the potential advantage of having Tecton’s fresh eyes on the project, others thought that Context’s familiarity with the project would be a benefit that could potentially translate to time savings.

The FSBC asked Fire Chief John Kivlan to share his opinion as well. Chief Kivlan remarked that all three firms were excellent and he was willing to work with any of them, but that Tecton and Context were his top two picks. He asked the FSBC to think about which firm is the best fit for the community and said he was willing to defer to the judgment of the committee.

FSBC Chair Mary Brolin noted that choosing which firm to rank first was very difficult because the committee had “really good choices.”

After voting 6-1 to rank Context first, the FSBC voted unanimously to ask Mike Johns to proceed with contract negotiations with Context, and if those negotiations do not work out, to proceed with contract negotiations with Tecton, followed by Kaestle Boos Associates. The town is aiming to have an executed contract by the last week in May.

The FSBC’s next meeting is scheduled for May 14.
