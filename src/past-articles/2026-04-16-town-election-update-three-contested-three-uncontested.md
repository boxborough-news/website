With the nomination papers filing deadline past, Boxborough’s June 2, 2026 town election currently has three contested and three uncontested races.

There are currently three candidates running for two Select Board seats; three candidates running for two Planning Board seats; and three candidates running for two School Committee seats.

Select Board candidates are Kristin Hilberg (incumbent), Priya Sundaram (incumbent), and Patrick Kyle. Planning Board candidates are Rich Guzzardi (incumbent), Cindy Markowitz (incumbent), and Gary Kushner. School Committee candidates are Andrew Boncoddo, Hilary Greven, and Felicia Knihtila.

The three uncontested races are for Moderator, Board of Health, and Board of Library Trustees. Incumbent Dennis Reip is running for Moderator, incumbent Marie Cannon is running for Board of Health, and Nancy Settle-Murphy (incumbent) and Cynthia Macmillan are running for the two Library Trustees seats.

These individuals have until April 30 to withdraw their names from the ballot.

Voters who wish to vote in the town election on June 2 must register to vote by Saturday, May 9.
