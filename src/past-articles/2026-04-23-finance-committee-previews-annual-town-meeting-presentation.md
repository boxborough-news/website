At its meeting on April 14, the Boxborough Finance Committee (FinCom) previewed their presentation for Annual Town Meeting (ATM), scheduled for May 19 at Blanchard Memorial School.

According to FinCom Chair Tony Newton, the presentation is meant to educate taxpayers about the issues addressed in preparing the town’s budget.

The presentation starts by explaining the overall structure of the budget, including the operating budget and warrant articles. The operating budget is funded through taxation. Warrant articles are funded through taxation, “free cash”, bonds, or the Community Preservation Act (CPA), which funds projects for open space, historic preservation, affordable housing and outdoor recreation. CPA projects are funded through a 1% surcharge on real estate taxes. “Free cash” is a term that refers to money that is left over at the end of the fiscal year. The use of “free cash” has no impact on the levy (property taxes).

When the budget process started in November 2025, there was a strong possibility that the town would need an operational override of Proposition 2 ½. An “override” increases the amount of the property tax revenue a community may raise and becomes part of the base for calculating future years’ levy limits. The result is a permanent increase in taxing authority.

With the possibility of an operational override, FinCom issued guidance to town department heads, through town management, to develop two budgets. Budget “A” was a “level services” budget, which would freeze positions, and budget “B” was a “level funded” budget with staffing reductions.

In the course of reviewing revenues from last year (FY2025) and revenues from the current year (FY2026), FinCom found that state aid and local receipts (motor vehicle excise taxes, investment income, hotel taxes, fees, licenses/permits, rentals and charges), came in higher than estimated. Under the circumstances, the Town Administrator projected a $600K increase in local receipts for FY2027. At the same time, the Assessor’s office concluded that there were sufficient funds in the “overlay reserve” and would not require additional funding for FY2027. The “overlay reserve” is used to fund potential property tax abatements, exemptions and uncollected taxes.

The proposed FY2027 operating budget to be presented at ATM is 5.5% higher than the current fiscal year. There are no new positions, no new programs, and no cuts to services.

According to School Committee Vice Chair Adam Klein, an overall increase of 4.75% (5.4% for Boxborough) in the Acton Boxborough Regional School District (ABRSD) budget necessitated staff reductions of 10 positions (4.48 FTE), resulting in a $1.1M in savings.

While the proposed operating budget, including ABRSD, increases by 5.5%, the increase in the levy (property taxes) is 3.2%, in large part because of the use of free cash for warrant articles and the projected increase in revenues.

FinCom wants taxpayers to know that the proposed FY2027 budget brings the town within $900,000 of the allowable levy limit. Under the circumstances there may be an operational override in FY2028 due to the cost of education, debt service, pensions, benefits and insurance, and regional dispatch.

Members of the FinCom will be available to answer questions at the Annual Town Meeting Information Session, scheduled for May 14 at the Boxborough Library.
