The Sargent Memorial Library has now completed phase two of its interior rearrangement project. On April 29 and 30, the library was closed to the public to allow for the installation of an indoor “cabana” structure that frames the new Young Adult lounge area in the middle of the main hall. The library will be back open Saturday May 2 for regular hours (10 a.m. - 3 p.m.).

Back in January, the library added two study “pods” to the former Young Adult Room, now a Study Room. The pods are small, enclosed study areas that provide quiet zones for individual or small group work. The Study Room also features a copier corner and reading corner.

The rearrangement project was funded by The Sargent Memorial Library Foundation, the Friends of the Boxborough Library, and the Town of Boxborough. The project was initiated in response to repeated community feedback about the need for private meeting spaces within the library.

The library has also welcomed a new staff member, Jessica Archer. Archer is the new Information Services Librarian, responsible for outreach and programming. Archer came to librarianship later in life, despite having a librarian mother, and will be instilling a love of libraries in her young son. She is a musician, and her favorite genre to read (for now) is biography. The library is excited to have her join their team.

Finally, the library now offers Biblio+ for Boxborough cardholders. Biblio+ is different from Hoopla, Kanopy, and other databases because it offers a curated collection of films and television shows, including BBC series. Library users can create an account by visiting [biblioplus.com](https://wowbrary.org/i.aspx?Njg0NnxtYWlsY3xodHRwczovL2JpYmxpb3BsdXMuY29tfHM0aTEwOTAxNg..), searching for Sargent Memorial Library, and entering their library card number.
