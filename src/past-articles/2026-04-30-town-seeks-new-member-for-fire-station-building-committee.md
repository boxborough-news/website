The Town of Boxborough has announced that it is seeking one new member for the town’s Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) after the recent resignation of member Larry Grossman. The town’s posting states that “experience in architecture or civil engineering is preferred but not required.”

At the Select Board’s April 27 meeting, FSBC Chair Mary Brolin told the Board, “I think it would be great to get someone who had architectural experience given the phase that we’re in… that is a skill that no other current member has [and] Larry had… we’ll need a fair amount of civil engineering work as well.. so somebody with either one of those skills would be fantastic.”

Brolin also thanked Grossman for contributing “his time and professional experience” to the FSBC, noting that he “asked some of the hard questions… and it made our work better….” Town Administrator (TA) Mike Johns also expressed his appreciation for Grossman’s time and contributions to the committee. The Select Board accepted Grossman’s resignation “with regret and gratitude for his service.”

Residents interested in serving on the FSBC can submit a resume and letter of interest to [mjohns@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:mjohns@boxborough-ma.gov), copying [fsbc@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:fsbc@boxborough-ma.gov) and [selectboard@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:selectboard@boxborough-ma.gov). The Select Board has requested that the FSBC conduct interviews and choose one applicant to recommend to the Select Board. In addition to new applicants, the Select Board also asked the FSBC to contact those who previously applied to be an FSBC member but were not selected.

In his email to the Select Board dated April 17, Grossman stated “I’ve truly enjoyed my five years with the Fire Station Building Committee. With the project moving into a new chapter, I feel it’s a good time for me to move on and resign from my position.”

When asked for comment, Grossman told Boxborough News, “I would like to keep my comments regarding my resignation brief. I also encouraged the Select Board and Fire Station Committee to identify a new member with architectural design and construction experience. The upcoming design phase will require seasoned, thoughtful input to effectively balance functional and aesthetic requirements with economic considerations.”

At its most recent meeting on April 15, the FSBC interviewed three firms for the “design” phase of the fire station building project, selected Context Architecture, and asked TA Mike Johns to negotiate a contract with Context. The town is aiming to have an executed contract by the last week in May.
