The Boxborough gardening community will, once again, come together for the Boxborough Garden Club’s Annual Plant Sale on Saturday, May 9. Perennially scheduled the day before Mother’s Day, this annual event offers more than just an opportunity to purchase plants; it represents a meaningful gathering involving community spirit, environmental appreciation, and local support.

Held on the lawn of the UCC Congregational Church at 723 Massachusetts Avenue, the plant sale runs from 9 a.m. to noon and welcomes gardeners of all experience levels. Whether attendees are seasoned horticulturists or beginners looking to start their first garden, the event provides a wide variety of options.

Plants suited for both sun and shade will be available, including annuals, perennials, seedlings, and house plants. This diversity ensures that visitors can find something for their specific needs, whether they are cultivating a backyard garden, enhancing indoor spaces, or experimenting with new varieties.

Boxborough News reached out to Garden Club President Flo Hanover to ask if there was anything new this year. “We have a whole bunch of peonies,” Hanover said, describing some of the colors that will be available for sale.

Proceeds from the sale are directed toward funding a scholarship for an Acton-Boxborough graduate, reinforcing the Garden Club’s commitment to education and community development. In addition, funds support the Colonial Herb Garden on Hill Road, a local historical and botanical site that reflects Boxborough’s heritage and dedication to preserving traditional plant knowledge.

At the sale, gardeners share advice, exchange ideas, and learn from one another in a relaxed, welcoming environment. The informal setting encourages conversation and collaboration, making it just as much a social gathering as it is a marketplace. Additionally, the plant sale promotes sustainable practices.

By purchasing locally grown plants, attendees support environmentally responsible gardening and reduce the environmental impact associated with large-scale plant distribution. Many of the plants are often cultivated by members of the Garden Club themselves, ensuring quality and care.

As spring reaches its peak in early May, the timing of the event is ideal. It allows gardeners to begin or expand their planting at a moment when conditions are favorable, setting the stage for a vibrant growing season. The colorful selection of flowers and greenery enhances landscapes and contributes to local biodiversity, supporting pollinators and other wildlife.

The Boxborough Garden Club Plant Sale reflects the intersection of nature, community, and purpose. It highlights how a simple activity, buying and planting flowers, can enhance spaces, support education, and preserve history. The event offers a chance to engage with the community while embracing the joys of gardening.

With its combination of practical benefits and meaningful impact, the plant sale stands as a cherished annual tradition in Boxborough. To learn more about the Boxborough Garden Club, visit the plant sale tomorrow or email Flo Hanover at [fhanover10@gmail.com](mailto:fhanover10@gmail.com).
