The Boxborough Public Celebrations and Ceremonies Committee (PCCC) is now accepting nominations for the 2026 Golden Fife and Parade Marshal awards. This year, the PCCC has launched an online nominations form, available at [https://forms.gle/ibS1KbGCbvrcJFBC7](https://forms.gle/ibS1KbGCbvrcJFBC7).

Paper nomination forms are also available at Boxborough Town Hall and Sargent Memorial Library. Nomination forms will be at Boxborough’s Annual Town Meeting on May 19, as well. Nomination forms must be submitted by Friday, May 22.

The Golden Fife is awarded to a Boxborough resident who has demonstrated long-term volunteer service to the town. The Parade Marshal is someone who has done something exceptional for the town, but not necessarily long-term or as a volunteer, and does not have to be a resident.

This year marks fifty years since the PCCC presented the first Golden Fife award in 1976. The first recipient of the Golden Fife was Ann Kirby (Steele) Atwood.

The PCCC’s entry in the 1976 Boxborough Town Report, submitted by PCCC Chair Dorris Mae Hilberg, states, “The ‘Golden Fife Award’ was initiated this year. This award will be presented annually at the Fifer’s Festival, to an outstanding citizen of Boxborough.”

Since then, the Golden Fife has been awarded each year except for 2020 and 2021 (due to the COVID-19 pandemic). Recipients in chronological order are Anne Kirby Steele Atwood, Grace Priest, Norm Gundersen, Al Cobleigh, Reggie Brown, Judge Tom Morse, Ruth Burroughs, George Robinson/Don Morse, Arthur Furbush, Officer James Staples, Virginia Richardson, Berda Treyz, Pat and Fred Hugel, Doris Custance, Charlene Golden, Lois Greene, Boxborough Minutemen, Warren Morse, Gary Morse, Harry Hilberg, Boxborough Grange, Fred Joyce, Eleanor Smith, Glen Schricker, John Fallon, Shirley Warren, Dorris Hilberg, Pat Fallon, Dick Golden, Elaine Garabedian, Don Wheeler, Cesar Garde, Alan Rohwer, Mary Larson, Ken and Laraine King, Simon Bunyard, Vic “Buzz” Tremblay, Anne Canfield, Rob and Anne McNeece, George Krusen, Jeanne Kangas, Francie Nolde & Hugh Fortmiller, Owen Neville, Bo Sokolowski and Larry Roche, Elizabeth Markiewicz, Les Fox, Rita Gibes Grossman, and John Markiewicz.

The PCCC will present this year’s awards at Fifer’s Day on Saturday, June 20.
