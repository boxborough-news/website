The Boxborough Strengthening Police Community Partnerships (SPCP) Council met on April 1 to review community feedback and consider its mandate going forward.

The SPCP currently collects feedback using a suggestion box at the library and the SPCP [website](https://sites.google.com/view/spcpboxborough) that was set up for this purpose. As of the April 1 meeting, there was just one suggestion card submitted and no inquiries through the website. The suggestion card inquiries have been answered on the website.

The Council reviewed the mandate of the community-wide gathering in March 2024, when 60 residents gathered and voiced the need for a Boxborough SPCP Council. The Council considered whether lack of input from the community indicates that the mandate no longer exists or that the Council has still not identified an acceptable means to gather input.

To test whether the mandate still exists, the Council devised a simple survey and sent it to the attendees of the original 2024 meeting. The survey asks the people who called for an SPCP Council whether the Council has completed its mission or whether there is still a need to improve the relationship between the Boxborough Police Department and the community it serves.

The next meeting is scheduled for June 3. At this meeting, the Council intends to review feedback from the survey and determine if there is further need to meet on a regular basis. The Council is considering a process of only meeting ad hoc when there is a specific task to be accomplished, rather than monthly or bi-monthly.

In the meantime, the SPCP will continue to accept community feedback through the suggestion box and the website.

The wooden suggestion box, along with a stack of SPCP flyers and a small sign explaining the purpose of the suggestion box, are currently situated at the Sargent Memorial Library to the right of the reception desk (on a middle bookshelf, as shown in the photo).

The SPCP website, [https://sites.google.com/view/spcpboxborough](https://sites.google.com/view/spcpboxborough), is a portal for members of the Boxborough community to submit questions and to look for answers to questions that have been anonymously submitted.
