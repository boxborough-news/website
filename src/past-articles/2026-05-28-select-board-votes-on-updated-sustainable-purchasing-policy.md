At their meeting on Tuesday, May 26, the Boxborough Select Board unanimously approved an updated “Sustainable Purchasing and Environmentally Preferred Procurement (EPP) Policy” for the town. The policy “establishes the framework for a sustainability-based purchasing program” for the town and aligns with the current Mass Department of Environmental Protection standards.

The updated policy broadens the town’s 2019 Recycled Products Procurement Policy, which committed the town to purchasing recycled and “environmentally-preferable” products whenever products met quality requirements and were available at reasonable cost.

Associate Town Planner Ian Gilson, who presented the policy to the Select Board, explained that the new policy “encourages the town to think more broadly about how we purchase goods and services in the first place” and goes beyond recycling to a more “holistic…contemporary understanding of what sustainability is.” This includes “conserve[ing] natural resources” and “minimize[ing] environmental impacts from pollution” in addition to minimizing waste and purchasing recycled and recyclable products.

There are “no hard and fast restrictions,” Gilson noted, but the goal is to “establish these sustainable purchasing standards as a town practice and expectation.” He recognized that factors like price, performance, availability, and other department needs “still matter.”

After asking several questions about how the new policy is different from the 2019 policy and how the policy will be implemented and enforced, the Board unanimously approved.

The Board also debriefed with Town Moderator Dennis Reip about this year’s Annual Town Meeting, which was held on May 19 at Blanchard Memorial School.

Reip stated that the meeting went “very well… despite the heat” and shared his thoughts on the “relatively low” turnout of roughly 160 voters; the efficiency of a lengthy consent agenda; and the technical issues with the microphones.

Board members discussed possible changes that could be made in the future regarding the presentation and explanation of the town operating budget; the order of the articles in the warrant; and childcare logistics.

The Board also voted to set next year’s Annual Town Meeting for May 10-12, 2027, at the Boxboro Regency.
