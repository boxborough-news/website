Boxborough’s Office of Land Use and Permitting has initiated the process of updating the town’s Open Space and Recreation Plan (OSRP), which is set to expire in 2027. The previous OSRP was completed in 2022 and [published in May 2023](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/3325/Boxborough-OSRP-PDF). According to the Massachusetts Executive Office of Energy and Environmental Affairs (EEA) Division of Conservation Services (DCS), “Open Space and Recreation Plans (OSRPs) are a tool through which a community plans for the future of its conservation and recreation resources.

They are ‘informed by a thorough public participation process and reflect the needs of its community members,’ and once approved by the state, the community becomes eligible for DCS grant programs for up to ten years.” “We have an exciting opportunity ahead of us to recalibrate our goals and expand the scope of our 2027 OSRP, which will be valid for 10 years instead of just 5. This process began on April 29 with the OSRP Advisory Group's kickoff meeting,” Associate Town Planner Ian Gilson told Boxborough News.

The OSRP Advisory Group has representatives from several town boards and committees with vested interests in open space, conservation, agriculture, and recreation. According to the Town’s website, “the group will help identify key issues, support summer public outreach, and lay the groundwork for an informed and inclusive OSRP process.” Gilson told Boxborough News that during their kick-off meeting, the Advisory Group discussed their preliminary engagement strategies for Summer 2026, which include pop-up outreach at town events, a Fifer’s Day booth, and an Ice Cream Social on August 22 to engage with residents about their open space priorities.

The Advisory Group “will meet throughout the fall to analyze the takeaways from their summer outreach. These takeaways will set the stage for a robust and informed planning process in 2027. By early next year, the Advisory Group will transition into a formally established OSRP Committee and will select a planning consultant to work with in developing the 2027-2037 OSRP draft.

Throughout next year, the OSRP Committee and the planning consultant will continue engaging residents as the OSRP is drafted and finalized,” Gilson wrote.

All OSRP Advisory Group meetings are open to the public and will become more regular later this year and throughout 2027.
