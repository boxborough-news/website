This year’s Fifer’s Day celebration will take place on Saturday, June 20. The festivities start at 9:30 a.m. with the Fifer’s Four Mile Road Race, followed by a parade kicking off at 11 a.m. from the Blanchard School to Flerra Meadows, where the Golden Fife and Parade Marshal awards are presented to kick off an afternoon of food, music, craft and vendor booths, and a volleyball tournament. The event is jointly sponsored by the Boxborough District Minutemen Company and the Town of Boxborough's Public Celebrations and Ceremonies Committee (PCCC).

The Fifer’s Day tradition goes back to 1967, when the newly-formed Boxborough District Minutemen Company celebrated the first “Fifer’s Festival” to commemorate local contributions to the American Revolution. While the town of Boxborough did not exist during the American Revolution, namesake fifer Luther Blanchard lived in the portion of Littleton that later became Boxborough. Fifer’s Day has grown considerably since the 1960s, but it remains true to its roots: a parade, a cookout, activities for the kids … a quintessential small-town party. Boxborough News spoke with Minuteman Lieutenant Tim Blankenship about how the Minutemen prepare for Fifer’s Day.

**Q: How big is the team that organizes Fifer's Day (including race, volleyball, festival)?**

A: Most all of the Minutemen pitch in at some point but there are easily 20+ who play key roles in organizing some part of it.

**Q: What is the most difficult part of planning?**

A: Praying for a sunny day (just kidding, sort of). Coordinating with all the groups who are involved (Select Board, Rec Com, PCCC, Board of Health, Building Dept., DPW, insurance, and the various vendors and suppliers) is easily the most complicated part.

**Q: What is the easiest or most fun part of planning?**

A: After all the permits are obtained and tents, portapotties, and inflatables are delivered, the fun part is putting the finishing touches on the tents and food service areas then relaxing with pizza with our fellow minutemen on the day before Fifer’s Day. Then, of course, comes Saturday the 20th and the REAL fun begins!

**Q: How many vendors are you expecting this year?**

A: We are expecting around 70 vendors, which is on par with last year.

**Q: How many visitors are you expecting? Does attendance continue to grow each year?**

A: It does. If it's a nice day we are expecting somewhere around 800 to 1,000 visitors this year.

**Q: Will there be anything new or different at Fifer's Day this year?**

A: We will be featuring Cloud Hopper IPA from Dirigible as our craft beer choice at our beer tent as well as Carlson's Cider. In addition to our traditional road race and volleyball tournament, we will be having a fun treasure hunt for the kids with prizes and two bands, Rocky Woods Express and Final Mile.

**Q: What are the big tasks left for the Minutemen to do in the final week before the big day?**

A: We will be getting the field marked and supervising the setup of the tents next week and setting up the pavilion, band, merchandise, food service and remote beer tents. The day before Fifer’s Day is when we all come together to set up tables and chairs, cooking stations and booth spaces, and the volleyball nets. It's a lot of work but a lot of fun too.

**Q: Anything else you want to share about how the Minutemen prepare for Fifer's Day?**

A: Preparation starts around March and involves many different aspects. Over the years, many of our members have specialized in one or more aspects and can be counted on to handle things without much oversight which helps a great deal.
