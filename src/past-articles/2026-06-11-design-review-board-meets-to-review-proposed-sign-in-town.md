On Monday, June 8, the Boxborough Design Review Board (DRB) met for the first time in over four years. The meeting was convened to review the design of a proposed sign at 25A Stow Road, the antique brick building at the corner of Stow Road and Route 111 (Mass Ave). The building is owned by Debra Jeanson and is in the Town Center zoning district. Barbara Shapokas Taglier, Jeanson’s tenant and owner of the The Art Studio, submitted an application for a Special Permit to the Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA) to install a new sign at the property.

Per the Boxborough Zoning Bylaw, the DRB provides a detailed review of “certain structures and new or modified signs in the Town Center District,” using the Town’s Design Review Board Design Guidelines, and makes advisory recommendations to the ZBA. The intent is “to protect and preserve the historic and cultural aspects and heritage of the town.” DRB members referenced the Design Guidelines when discussing the attributes of the proposed sign and weighing in on its size, content, colors, materials, and supports.

Most of the discussion focused on the sign’s colors. Several members expressed concern about the “bright” colors depicted in the plan. According to the Design Review Guidelines, “No more than 2-3 colors should be used. Colors used for the sign should match either the background or the trim color of the structure that it serves. This will visually link the sign to the business.” All of the members expressed that they want to see a sign that is “more historic looking,” and pointed to the UCC Church sign directly across Mass Ave as an example of a sign that complements its building.

The DRB met again on Thursday, June 11 to further discuss their recommendations. Their report emphasizes “the building’s value as an 1832 historic property” that is in a “highly prominent location within the heart of the Town Center district.” As such, their recommendations include “a wooden sign, either painted or carved” and a “simplification of the proposed signage colors” as being “appropriate to the character of the Town Center District…”

The DRB voted unanimously to send their recommendation report to the ZBA for consideration at the public hearing scheduled for Tuesday, June 16.
