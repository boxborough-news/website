At the Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) meeting on June 11, Chris Long of Context Architecture presented a new set of conceptual fire station design options to the committee for their feedback. The committee undertook a similar process at its last meeting and will continue to do so as Context collects more feedback and refines the design options.

The new design options reflected different building layouts, building orientations, and parking and driveway arrangements. Review of the options led to discussions about whether the building should be one-story or two-story; whether to have fire apparatus exit directly onto Massachusetts Avenue and/or onto Sara’s Way (a private road); how to make the visitor parking and visitor entrance clear and accessible to the public; what the building should look like from Massachusetts Avenue; and how to design around the topography of the site.

The committee agreed that input from the Boxborough Fire Chief and design-specific cost estimates will be important to consider before any design decisions are made. In May, the town hired Context for the “design and bid” phase of the project. Context is currently in the “schematic design work” portion of the project, which will continue through August 2026. Context will collect feedback from the public at the FSBC’s Fifer’s Day booth on June 20 and will conduct another public engagement session on July 23.

In response to a question from a resident during the public comment period at the beginning of the meeting, Town Administrator Mike Johns noted that the town has not yet completed the purchase of the fire station site and will probably not close on the property until late July. He explained that there are “no red flags or roadblocks” but that there is a “complicated title process.” The FSBC’s next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, June 25 at 7 p.m.
