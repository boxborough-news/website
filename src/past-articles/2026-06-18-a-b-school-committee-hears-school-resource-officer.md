At their meeting on June 11, the Acton-Boxborough Regional School Committee heard an annual presentation about the district’s Student Resource Officer (SRO) program from representatives of the Boxborough and Acton police departments.

The officers spoke about the role of SROs, the training they undertake for this program, and statistics about what they do in the schools. The SRO program is governed by Memorandums of Understanding between the district and each police department.

The Boxborough police department serves only Blanchard Memorial School, as it is the district’s only school located in Boxborough. The high school, junior high school, and the district’s other elementary schools, all located in Acton, are served by the Acton police department.

The two SROs from the Boxborough police department, Sgt. Phillip Gath and Officer Diosmar Reynoso, explained that they visit Blanchard during their patrols, help students cross at the crosswalk, and conduct drills and training with teachers and students. They presented this year’s annual Blanchard SRO statistics, which included five drills/trainings, two discussions with faculty, and zero arrests/citations/court referrals.

Moving on to the Superintendent search process, the Committee voted to establish a Superintendent Search Subcommittee of three members: Adam Klein (Chair), Lisa Adil, and Jacob Fay.

At their last meeting on June 4, the Committee voted to choose the Collins Center for Public Management as the district’s Superintendent search firm. In February, Superintendent Peter Light announced that he had accepted a position as Superintendent at the American Community School in Abu Dhabi starting in July 2027.

School Committee Chair Tori Campbell also delivered Superintendent Peter Light’s performance evaluation in the form of an “Annual Summative Review,” and the committee voted unanimously to set Light’s salary for fiscal year 2027 at $263,017.11, an increase of 2.5%.
