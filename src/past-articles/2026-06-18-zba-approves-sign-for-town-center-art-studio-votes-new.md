At its meeting on Tuesday, June 16 the Boxborough Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA) conducted a public hearing to review the application from Barbara Shapokas Taglier for a Special Permit to install new signage at 25A Stow Road, the antique brick building at the corner of Stow Road and Route 111 (Mass Ave). The building is owned by Debra Jeanson and is in the Town Center zoning district. Shapokas is Jeanson’s tenant and owner of The Art Studio, which is on the second floor of the building.

Chair Mark Barbadoro opened the public hearing and invited Shapokas to present her application. Shapokas told the board that for the past 11 years, she placed a sandwich board outside when she was in the building and that the “sign” had a black background with her logo and phone number. Jeanson recently told Shapokas that she could use the sign structure that is currently in front of the building for her own (permanent) sign.

Since the building is in the Town Center zoning district, a detailed review of “certain structures and new or modified signs” must be reviewed by the Design Review Board (DRB), which uses the Town’s DRB Design Guidelines to make advisory recommendations to the ZBA.

Last week the DRB met twice to discuss Shapokas’ sign and sent their recommendations to the ZBA and Shapokas. (See related article: [https://www.boxboroughnews.org/archives/jan-jun-2026-news#h.bix6aksulb8q](https://www.boxboroughnews.org/archives/jan-jun-2026-news#h.bix6aksulb8q))

Prior to the ZBA hearing, with the DRB’s recommendations in hand, Shapokas made some revisions to her sign plan, most notably muting the colors on her three-color logo and using a white background instead of a black one. ZBA members looked favorably upon the changes.

However, there were two recommended changes that Shapokas had not made: she had not removed the phone number, and she did not change the proposed material from PVC to wood.

ZBA members spent considerable time discussing these two elements. The DRB’s Design Guidelines recommend that signs do not show phone numbers. Shapokas argued in favor of including the phone number, and ZBA Chair Mark Barbadoro empathized. But Barbadoro and all members weighed in and explained that although the DRB does not have approval authority, ZBA members respect the DRB’s recommendations per the Design Guidelines.

The use of PVC for the sign elicited a robust conversation among board members because PVC is plastic. The Boxborough Zoning Bylaw (Section 6.5.12, Signs in the Town Center District) states, “Materials such as wood or stone shall be used. Plastic signs and internally lit signs are not appropriate in the Town Center and are expressly prohibited.”

In explaining the board’s decision to prohibit the use of PVC for the sign, ZBA Chair-Elect Shawn McCormack stated, “The prohibition on plastic is not a recommendation. That’s Town Meeting, and I agree that PVC can look great and that it’s probably a dated provision…It should be considered for an amendment, but I’m hard pressed to say that we have authority to authorize” its use.

The board voted unanimously to grant the Special Permit to install the sign with a set of written conditions that include the Design Review Board's recommendations and adherence to the Boxborough Zoning Bylaw.

The Board also conducted their annual reorganization. Shawn McCormack will replace Mark Barbadoro as Chair, and Nathaniel (Than) Stuntz will replace McCormack as ZBA Clerk.
