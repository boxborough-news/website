Hello Boxborough News Readers,

Tomorrow is Fifer’s Day! We are excited to march in the parade tomorrow morning and meet you, our readers, at our festival booth in the afternoon.

We’re celebrating three years since we launched Boxborough News and our goals remain the same: to strengthen our community and promote civic engagement by delivering unbiased, factual reporting on local events, issues, and people.

We love Fifer’s Day not just because it brings our community together, but also because it’s our annual opportunity to get your feedback and ideas about Boxborough News.

What do you want to read in Boxborough News? More board and committee news? More human interest stories and community features? Is there a topic that we aren’t covering that we should add? We can’t wait to hear from you!

And of course, we’re always looking for more people to join our amazing volunteer team. If you want to help us grow this hyperlocal news project, or you’re just curious about what it means to be on the Boxborough News team, come talk to us.

See you tomorrow!

Cassie LaRussa & Abby Reip

Boxborough News Co-Editors
