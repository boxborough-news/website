At their meeting on June 25, the Boxborough Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) continued to review fire station concept designs from Context Architecture (“Context”).

The Committee discussed whether to have fire apparatus exit directly onto Mass Ave or onto Sara’s Way (a private road that leads to Mass Ave). Chris Logan from Context explained that significant earthwork, retaining walls, and tree removal would be needed for a Mass Ave entrance and estimated this work at a cost of at least $3 million. When asked for his opinion on where the apparatus should exit the site, Fire Chief John Kivlan responded that while having both access points would be ideal, he “wouldn’t be opposed to” exiting only onto Sara’s Way.

After comparing designs with one-story and two-story buildings, the Committee came to a consensus that they would prefer a one-story building but would not foreclose a two-story design. Chief Kivlan noted that a one-story building would be preferable from an operations perspective. The Committee also discussed other design options, including the placement of various interior rooms, as well as roof styles. FSBC members also made plans to visit the site in person to help them visualize the design options.

Towards the end of the Committee’s discussion on various design options, FSBC Chair Mary Brolin asked Logan, “When do you want [our feedback] to be more definitive?”, to which Logan answered, “next week.” Logan explained that Context would “like to finish up concept design” and move on to creating a more refined site design.

Town Administrator Mike Johns gave the committee an update on the purchase of the property at 984 Mass Ave. Johns explained that an “American Land Title Association (ALTA) survey,” required by the title insurance company, is in progress.

Johns commented via email that “This survey mitigates risks associated with title defects (a few of which the legal title search uncovered, which was not unexpected). The title insurance company requires the ALTA survey to properly cover and cure any title defects.”

Johns told the Committee that the town is on track to sign a purchase and sale agreement by the end of June and close on July 17.

The Committee also discussed their next public outreach event. On Thursday, July 23, at 7 p.m., Context will host a public forum at Town Hall to present a “design update.”

The FSBC’s next meeting is scheduled for Thursday, July 9, at 7 p.m.
