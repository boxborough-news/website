by Nate Kim

A rainy Tuesday paused but did not stop the summer fun for those at Flerra Summer Playground this week. Despite having to cancel the program on Tuesday, the Flerra program welcomed new faces and many returning campers for the first week of its four-week summer season.

Flerra Summer Playground is a half-day summer program held outdoors at Flerra Meadows in Boxborough during the month of July. It is open to children who are entering kindergarten through seventh grade.

The first week brought back many familiar Flerra traditions including dodgeball on the field, gaga ball, a modified soccer game celebrating 2026 FIFA World Cup, "Water Wednesday," featuring the return of the familiar waterslide, and Tie-Dye “Tuesday” which became Tie Dye Thursday this week due to the cancelled Tuesday session.

When the summer sun got too warm, there were still plenty of options under the shade of the tent, including board games, bracelet making, coloring, and ice cream making, a favorite camper tradition. The canopies also provide a place for campers to rest, rehydrate, and socialize between activities.

Many former campers returned as Counselors-in-Training to provide the guidance and fun that they enjoyed when they were campers.

Veteran Flerra counselor Eric Zhou, a rising sophomore at Syracuse University and Blanchard and Acton-Boxborough Regional High School alumnus, described the program as, “something I look forward to every year.” He added that he is “glad the program has offered [him] a position again this year.”

Looking ahead to the rest of the summer, counselor Kareem Elsallal, a rising senior at Acton-Boxborough said, “I'm just excited for what is to come. The best part has always been the kids for me.”

Flerra Summer Playground runs weekdays from 8:30 a.m. to noon. Residents are encouraged to watch for counselors and campers walking and biking to and from the program throughout the camp season. Keep up with the program with "Flerra Summer Playground" on Facebook and @flerra.summerplayground on Instagram.
