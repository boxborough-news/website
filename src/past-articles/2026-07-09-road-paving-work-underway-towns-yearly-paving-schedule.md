Roadworkers endured steamy temperatures last week as they began the work of paving Boxborough roads.

In addition to Middle, Cobleigh, and Depot Roads, [which the town announced last week](https://www.boxboroughnews.org/archives/jan-jun-2026-news#h.cvvflyefe80n), the town’s contractor has also begun paving Guggins Lane, Boxmill Road, Prescott Road, Benjamin Drive, and Tokatawan Spring Lane.

Boxborough News reached out to Department of Public Works Director Ed Kukkula for an update and learned that the Fiscal Year 2027 (July 1, 2026 - June 30, 2027) paving schedule has been revised to include all eight roads listed above. Kukkula indicated that the updated schedule will be posted on the Town’s website in the near future.

While the work is underway, roads are closed to through traffic. Local residents will still be able to access their homes and police details are stationed to assist with traffic.

For further information contact [ekukkula@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:ekukkula@boxborough-ma.gov).
