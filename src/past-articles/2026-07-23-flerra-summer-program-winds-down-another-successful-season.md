The sounds of kids arriving by foot, by bike, and by car signaled a new week at Flerra Summer Playground, a half-day summer program held outdoors at Flerra Meadows in Boxborough during the month of July.

In week three of four, with arguably the best weather yet, campers started their days with favorites like German Dodgeball, and then cooled off with arts and crafts under the tent or enjoyed a wide variety of board games. “Tie-Dye Tuesday” brought excitement as campers transformed their blank white shirts into colorful creations, while “Water Wednesday” helped campers beat the heat with the water slide on a sunny, picture-perfect summer day.

Behind the scenes, however, the program’s smooth, clockwork-like operation has come with new challenges this year. Flerra Summer Playground has faced budget constraints and changing enrollment numbers, which have impacted staffing.

Flerra Summer Playground director Brian Picca explained, “Two years ago, we had record-breaking numbers of registrants. We anticipated our number last year to reflect that same number, but [last year] we were down about 150 campers.” Due to the enrollment changes, the program had to reduce the number of paid staffers as well.

“Overall, registration-wise we still aren’t at the number we’d like to be,” continued Picca, “so we may have to reduce staff again next year. But we’d prefer to increase the costs a little bit more just to help cover the costs of paying counselors, vendors and craft supplies.”

In advance of this summer’s season, the town increased the cost of the Flerra program “to help offset increasing vendor and supplies costs.”

Picca emphasized, “We don’t want to have to reduce the number of counselors[;] we value having the high schoolers working for us and giving back to them since they were campers themselves.”

The increased scarcity of jobs has also led to a more selective evaluation of current counselors. Picca said, “We’re really looking at who’s doing a total job of keeping the kids safe, watching campers interact with other campers, performance overall to determine if we can hire back those same counselors and which 8th-graders get paying jobs next year.”

Even with one of the tightest budgets in recent memory, Flerra Summer Playground remains a hit with Acton and Boxborough residents.

“I think it’s going really great,” said Laura Spurling, another one of Flerra Summer Playground’s directors. “Kids seem really happy and parents have been giving lots of positive feedback about the program. Our last week is our big fun week with the water balloon fight and talent show. We still have openings, so if people still [want to] sign up, we’d definitely take more campers.”

Flerra’s fourth and final week will run from July 27 to July 31. Registration is still open online at [https://boxboroughma.myrec.com](https://boxboroughma.myrec.com/info/activities/program_details.aspx?ProgramID=29851).
