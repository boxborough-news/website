On Wednesday, July 22, the Boxborough Sustainability Committee (“BSC”) was joined by representatives of Context Architecture (“Context”), the firm designing the town’s new fire station. The BSC heard a presentation from Context about their sustainability goals for the fire station, including “specialized stretch energy code compliance.”

Chris Logan and Jeff Shaw of Context presented sketches of the one-story, roughly 21,000 square foot building “as it presently exists in the design process”, emphasizing that this is the “concept” for the building and it has not been formally designed. Logan walked through the sustainability goals for the fire station, including energy-efficient design, use of renewable energy, and sustainable site design.

Shaw explained that since they are still early in the design process, they are exploring many different ways to incorporate sustainability. He noted that energy modeling and cost estimations scheduled to be completed over the next few months will help the town understand the benefits and costs of various systems.

Context also shared that an upcoming Fire Station Building Committee meeting on August 13 will include a “more robust” discussion of sustainability; they encouraged members of the BSC and the public to join that meeting.

BSC members asked questions about solar and geothermal systems, energy usage modeling, and timing for various decisions around building sustainability. Both Context representatives and BSC members expressed appreciation to connect with each other early in the design process.

Moving on to other business, the BSC discussed the timing for bringing a potential warrant article to town meeting for the adoption of the Specialized Stretch Code. BSC members agreed to work toward Annual Town Meeting in May 2027 instead of bringing it to this year’s Fall Town Meeting. They agreed that delaying it until next year would give them more time to gather data about potential impact to the town and conduct more public outreach.

The BSC also voted unanimously to pursue Green Communities funding for a series of projects across town, including a heat pump at Town Hall, weatherization at the Police Department, and LED lighting at Blanchard Memorial School.
