At the Boxborough Finance Committee (FinCom) meeting in July, FinCom discovered that some departments in town were “deficit spending;” when there were insufficient funds to pay invoices under a specific line item, the town paid the invoices under an account where funding was available.

When contacted by Boxborough News, Town Administrator Michael Johns said that after consulting with the Department of Local Services (DLS), he now has “clarity” that because Boxborough votes the “salary” line and the all “other” expense lines separately at Annual Town Meeting, funds cannot be moved between those two lines.

At their August meeting, FinCom Chair, Tony Newton proposed a “budget transfer” policy as it pertains to deficit spending and reserve fund transfers (RFT). The policy “requires that the town plan ahead for budget transfers by reviewing all budget lines at the beginning of the 4th quarter and then making transfers proactively, rather than allow the line to go into deficit.”

If there are insufficient funds, the town/department has several options. They could request an RFT to “fund extraordinary or unforeseen expenses.” (M.G.L. c 40, section 5A and M.G.L. c 40, section 6). Alternatively, they could request a warrant article at Fall Town meeting or at a Special Town Meeting to transfer funds from either the “salary” line to the “other” line, or vice versa. The other option is an inter-departmental transfer during the last two months of the fiscal year (May 1 to June 30), as provided by M.G.L. c. 44, section 33B. An interdepartmental transfer must be approved by the Select Board, with the agreement of the FinCom.

According to FinCom Chair Tony Newton, the proposed budget transfer policy “makes clear that deficit spending is not permitted.” The proposed policy will be sent to the Town Administrator for review by the Select Board followed by a final vote by FinCom.

In related matters, Finance Director Honghoa Le reported that she is still working on closing out fiscal year 2026 (ending on June 30, 2026), and therefore could not provide any 4th quarter or year-end financial data.

When asked by FinCom members, Le said that the financials should be completed and sent to the Department of Revenue (DOR) by the end of September. Likewise, the year-end submission for “free cash” will be completed by the end of September. “Free Cash” is a term that refers to the money that is left over at the end of a Fiscal Year plus unspent budget surpluses from previous years.

In preparation for the next budget year, FinCom asked the town to provide standardized and simplified organization charts for each department. Chair Newton said that FinCom also plans to develop a calendar which clarifies deadlines for filing the recapitulation sheet (tax recap) and other financial statements with the Department of Revenue.

The next FinCom meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, August 18 at 7 p.m. at Town Hall. The meeting is hybrid.
