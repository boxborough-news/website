Lavanya (“Lala”) Selvaraj opened Two Friends Chocolates at 629 Massachusetts Ave in Boxborough in 2020 after starting the business in her Boxborough home in 2016. The business has evolved from making chocolates and hot cocoa to serving up hot and cold “drinking chocolate” as well as “sparkler” cold fruit drinks. Over the past few years, Selvaraj has hired several local teens. She loves teaching them and helping them understand what it takes to run a business, she explained, from making the product to packaging to customer service.

Izzy and Melinda are both Boxborough residents and rising high school juniors who are now also chocolate shop employees. Boxborough News asked them some questions about what it’s like to work at Two Friends Chocolates. Responses have been edited for length and clarity.

**Boxborough News: How did you hear about the job opportunity?**

**IZZY:** I used to come here as a kid with my mom and my mom introduced me to Lala. So when the opportunity came up to work here, I was happy to spend my time in a place surrounded by the smell of chocolates.

**MELINDA:** I moved to town in 2023 and I used to work at McDonalds. Then I found out that the Two Friends Chocolate shop was looking for employees. I applied and got accepted. That’s how I started working here.

**Boxborough News: Why did you decide to apply?**

**IZZY:** I always wanted to work here because of my earlier visits with my mom. The chocolate shop is a delight.

**MELINDA:** For me, it was travel time. I live five minutes away from the shop and so thought of applying here instead of driving to McDonalds, where I used to work before. I can literally walk to the shop in 5 minutes from where I live. It’s much easier than driving.

**Boxborough News: How long have you been working here?**

**IZZY:** I have been working here for more than a year now.

**MELINDA:** I joined Two Friends Chocolates in February this year.

**Boxborough News: What are your typical job responsibilities?**

**IZZY:** My responsibilities are wrapping bars, keeping the shop up to date and talking to customers.

**MELINDA:** I’m responsible for making drinks like the hot and cold chocolate and I also prepare chocolate boxes.

**Boxborough News: What have you learned about chocolate? About running a small business?**

**IZZY:** Working at the chocolate shop has given me an opportunity to learn how to grow a small business. Watching it grow has given me insight into the workings of a small business.

**MELINDA:** I have learned the process of making handmade chocolates.

**Boxborough News: What did you expect the job to be like, and how has it been the same or different from what you expected?**

**IZZY:** I had no idea on what to expect from the job. It was all new to me. However, I started learning and there are so many different things to do. I try to work on one thing at a time. That gives me clarity on how things work.

**MELINDA:** This job is very different from what I did at McDonalds. At McDonalds, we were expected to jump in wherever it was required. It was crazy. Things are better here and I get to work with one thing at a time. This work atmosphere is much better than McDonalds.

**Boxborough News: What is your favorite part of working there?**

**IZZY:** One of my favorite parts of working at the chocolate shop is when I open the box of chocolate powder and the waft/smell of the chocolate makes my day. Apart from that, I also enjoy making drinks and talking to people.

**MELINDA:** I love the smell of chocolates in the store and the smell of the chocolate powder from the box.
