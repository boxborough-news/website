On Thursday, August 20, the Acton-Boxborough Regional School Committee held its first meeting of the 2026-2027 school year. Chairperson Tori Campbell welcomed committee members back, noting that the Committee has “significant work ahead of us this year.”

In his Superintendent’s update, Superintendent Peter Light thanked district teachers and staff for their work over the summer to implement the changes to the district’s elementary schools per the AB Forward reorganization voted earlier this calendar year.

Light said, “The biggest thing I would ask of all our community and families is to give the educators in our building[s] a lot of grace as we start the school year and know that this is a tremendous change for them and to really make sure that we give our educators enough leeway to get in, get settled, and really start to establish the communities…”

The Committee voted to approve a revision to the district’s “Physical Restraint of Students,” based on changes to state law. Superintendent Light explained that the revision is intended to comply with state requirements and that the district is required to implement the policy.

Some Committee members expressed concern that the Committee was being asked to approve a policy on the same night it was first presented. Light acknowledged that this is not best practice, but said “[I] would ask that we try to pass it so we’re not putting our educators in the position where we have a school committee policy that says one thing and a state law that says you have to do something different.”

Committee member Adam Klein (Boxborough) gave an update on the Superintendent search process. He stated that the job posting will be announced in early September and that there will be several opportunities for public involvement and feedback throughout the month of September. Klein added that information about the formation of a “screening committee” will be released soon, and the screening committee is expected to be established by the end of September.

In February, Superintendent Light announced that he had accepted a position as Superintendent at the American Community School in Abu Dhabi starting in July 2027. In June, the Committee voted to choose the Collins Center for Public Management as the district’s Superintendent search firm and voted to establish a Superintendent Search Subcommittee of three members: Klein (Chair), Lisa Adil, and Jacob Fay.

The Committee also heard a presentation from the Assistant Superintendent for Teaching and Learning, Gabby Abrams, about the District Goals for the 2026-2027 school year and a Fiscal Year 2026 Finance Update from Director of Finance and Operations Sheri Matthews.
