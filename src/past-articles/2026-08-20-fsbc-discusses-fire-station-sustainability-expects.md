At its meeting on August 13, the Boxborough Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC) heard a “Sustainability Visioning” presentation by consultant Thornton Tomasetti. The presentation walked through five categories of “sustainability opportunities” for the new fire station building -- health and wellbeing; circularity and embodied carbon; resilience and energy efficiency; regenerative site design; and operations and maintenance.

No decisions were made, but committee members offered feedback about the presentation and about town priorities regarding sustainability. A member of the Boxborough Sustainability Committee was also present and provided comments.

The committee expects to hear a “life cycle cost analysis,” a framework for selecting HVAC and other energy systems, later this month. In addition, Thornton Tomasetti is expected to provide “whole building energy modeling” after more of the building design work has been completed.

The Board then heard a design update from consultant Context Architecture (“Context”). Chris Logan of Context presented several design options with different types of roofs, including flat roofs and gabled roofs. Committee members offered questions and comments on roof options. Logan explained that he was looking for “general thoughts and preferences” of the committee, not final decisions.

Two days before their meeting, FSBC members and consultants gathered for a site visit at 984 Mass Ave, their first time on the future fire station site since [the town closed on the property](https://www.boxboroughnews.org/news#h.l8833oiodk0p) on July 30.

At their next meeting on August 27, the FSBC expects to hear a “preliminary cost estimate.” Steve Kirby of Owners Project Manager Vertex initially explained that the estimate will include “construction costs only.” FSBC Chair Mary Brolin asked him to include an estimate of “soft costs” as well. The FSBC also discussed public outreach efforts and announced that they will be holding another “design update” for the public on Wednesday, September 30.
