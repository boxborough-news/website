by Nate Kim

At its hybrid meeting on Thursday, August 13, the Boxborough Recreation Commission (Rec Com) discussed staffing, facility operations, and long-term recreation planning.

In their first order of business, the commission discussed the recently vacated Recreation Coordinator/Human Services Administrative Assistant position. The position is posted to the Town’s website, and Rec Com members will work with Assistant Town Administrator Rajon Hudson during the interview process. In the meantime, staff and commission members will share responsibility for recreation operations, including field permits, administrative work, and other recreation coordinator duties.

Rec Com members discussed the new Recreation Fields and Courts calendar, which was created to organize field, court, and pavilion use. Members acknowledged that additional work is needed to identify facility information and reservations, but once ready, the calendar will be a valuable tool for scheduling for ABYS soccer, birthday parties, tournaments, fall running club activities, flag football, and winter basketball programming.

The commission approved purchasing two portable pickleball nets for Liberty Field and continued work on new field-rental fees, which are expected to be reviewed in September. Members also discussed improving access and training for the MyRec reservation system.
