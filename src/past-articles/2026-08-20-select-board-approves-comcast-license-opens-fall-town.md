The Boxborough Select Board opened their August 10 meeting with a public hearing regarding the renewal of a cable television license for Comcast. After a presentation from town counsel, KP Law attorney Nicole Costanzo, the Board unanimously approved the license.

Assistant Town Administrator Rajon Hudson told the Board that the town has been working with Costanzo to “push back against Comcast” in negotiating the license and that the town was successful in getting “a lot of the terms we were looking for.”

The Board also opened the warrant for this year’s Fall Town Meeting. Fall Town Meeting is scheduled for Monday, November 16. Town Administrator Michael Johns explained that he is expecting the warrant to include “zoning, planning board, and some clean-up articles.” The warrant is expected to close on Monday, September 14.

The Board also heard a “Resident Request” regarding water accumulation on Blanchard Road. After a discussion with Department of Public Works (DPW) Director Ed Kukkula, the town agreed to add paving on Blanchard Road to address the issue. The conversation continued with a discussion of water drainage issues where Patch Hill Road meets Depot Road and other “clean-up work” that DPW is handling after the recent paving work.

Finally, the Board unanimously approved a new charge for the town’s Diversity Equity and Inclusion Committee. Select Board member Kristin Hilberg, who took the lead in revising the charge, noted that the revisions “broaden the scope” of the committee.

In his reports to the Board, Assistant Town Administrator Rajon noted that the town has two new employees. Kevin Jordan has joined as Administrative Assistant in the Assessors Department, and Brennan McPherson has joined as Administrative Assistant in the Police Department. The Select Board’s next meeting is Monday, August 24.
