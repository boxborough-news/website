At their meeting on March 24, the Boxborough Recreation Commission (Rec Com) unanimously approved a proposal for a competitive six-wicket American croquet league at Fifer's Field. The league will include eight teams of three players each, playing a ten-game season. The Rec Com agreed to waive the field fee in exchange for 20% of the league proceeds.

The Commission also had a lengthy discussion with pickleball players about the reservation system for the Liberty Field pickleball courts, which are now open through November. Three courts can be reserved and one will remain available for walk-on play. The courts can be reserved within a 10-day rolling window at [https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/359/Recreation-Commission.](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/359/Recreation-Commission) or [https://www.yourcourts.com/](https://www.yourcourts.com/).

The Rec Com continues to prepare for RunBXB on Sunday, April 12, at Craft Food Halls (registration at [https://runsignup.com/Race/MA/Boxborough/RunBXB](https://runsignup.com/Race/MA/Boxborough/RunBXB)) and Flerra Summer Playground (registration at [https://boxboroughma.myrec.com/info/activities/program_details.aspx?ProgramID=29851#activities](https://boxboroughma.myrec.com/info/activities/program_details.aspx?ProgramID=29851#activities)).

At their next meeting, scheduled for Tuesday April 28, the Rec Com is expected to discuss a proposal to restructure field fees by replacing the resident/non-resident distinction with a flat sports league fee and a separate out-of-town fee for casual bookings like birthday parties.
