At its meeting on March 30, the Boxborough Select Board voted on the final two warrant articles for the town’s 2026 Annual Town Meeting and closed the warrant.

The Board voted unanimously to recommend both articles. The first is the town’s FY2027 operating budget, which came in at $31,144,981. The second article, proposed by the Housing Board, asks for $15,000 in “grant matching funds” to bolster the town’s application for grant funding to develop a Housing Production Plan.

The Board then turned to a document provided by Town Administrator (TA) Mike Johns entitled “Select Board and Town Administrator Goals.”

Select Board Chair Kristin Hilberg explained the discussion was intended to be an opportunity for the TA to share updates on his goals and for the Board to offer input about priority areas.

Board members first raised questions about the purpose of the exercise, given that the Board voted on Town Administrator goals last year. Board members asked whether the TA would be evaluated based on the goals document that was voted last year or this newer version.

Chair Hilberg clarified that the TA is still accountable to the goals that the Board voted on in Fall 2025. However, she noted, “we said [when voting] that we weren’t going to set it and forget it…we said we were going to try to keep up with it and offer support.”

Town Administrator Johns indicated that he welcomed the opportunity to discuss the goals, saying that the criteria for his evaluation is “less important” and that “it’s more important to set town goals between the Board and administration about the future of the town…I want us to come up with an instrument that is strategic and moves the town forward.”

The Board then walked through the document, which was organized by category and by timing, i.e., “short-term goals (0-12 months)” and “long-term goals (3-5 years).” Board members asked questions about the status of particular projects and gave feedback about areas of concern or priority.

The wide-ranging discussion included updates about town website improvements; succession planning and cross-training; cybersecurity efforts; regional collaboration; and development of a survey about town services for residents and town employees.
