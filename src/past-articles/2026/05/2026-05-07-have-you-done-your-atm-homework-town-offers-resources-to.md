Have you read the warrant for Boxborough's 2026 Annual Town Meeting (ATM)? Are you prepared to vote on this year's fifty articles? Whether you're a veteran of Annual Town Meeting or this will be your first time and you're not sure what to expect, the Town has put together several resources to help you prepare.

For a basic, eight-minute overview of ATM, the town has released the “2026 Annual Town Meeting Preview Show,” featuring Town Administrator Michael Johns and Town Moderator Dennis Reip. The video gives an overview of what to expect at Town Meeting and briefly walks through this year’s warrant articles. The video is available at [https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/boxborough/video/69e12098d0222d0002bbb6a7?page=HOME](https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/boxborough/video/69e12098d0222d0002bbb6a7?page=HOME).

The Boxborough Finance Committee has also created and shared a video specifically about the budget. The town’s FY27 operating budget is Article 5 on the ATM warrant.

The “FY 2027 Boxborough Budget Preview” video, featuring Finance Committee Chair Tony Newton and Select Board member Bob Stemple, walks through the town’s operating expenses and other elements of the budget. In the video, Newton also explains the town’s overall budget development approach and explains municipal finance vocabulary like “levy,” “levy limit,” and the different types of “overrides.” The video is available at [https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/boxborough/video/69f36bd4a34a090002c7ab79?page=HOME](https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/boxborough/video/69f36bd4a34a090002c7ab79?page=HOME).

On Thursday, May 14, the Town of Boxborough will again hold a Town Meeting “information session” at the Sargent Memorial Library from 2 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. Residents are invited to drop in and learn about topics and issues related to the ATM warrant articles. Tables will be set up around the library meeting room, and town staff and volunteers will be present to speak with residents and answer questions. The event will also include activities for kids – an effort to make it more accessible to caregivers.

“No registration, no commitment, and no more than 20 minutes of your time,” says Town Planner and event organizer Alec Wade. “Come and go as you please, and children are welcome!”

This year’s warrant articles include a citizen’s petition; two general bylaws proposed by the Planning Board regarding plastic reduction and rodenticides; four zoning bylaw amendments; and a multi-part consent agenda (non-financial consent, financial consent, capital consent, and Community Preservation Committee consent).

Town Meeting warrants were mailed to every residence last week. If you did not receive one, head over to Town Hall or the library to pick one up. Or, you can read it electronically at [https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/6343/2026-Annual-Town-Meeting-Warrant-FY27?bidId=](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/6343/2026-Annual-Town-Meeting-Warrant-FY27?bidId=).

The town is also offering ATM shuttle service from designated parking areas (and door-to-door services for seniors), as well as free childcare in the Blanchard cafeteria during ATM. Information about shuttles and childcare (and childcare registration) is available at [https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/507/Town-Meeting](https://www.boxborough-ma.gov/507/Town-Meeting).

Boxborough’s Annual Town Meeting begins Tuesday, May 19 at the Blanchard Memorial School, 493 Massachusetts Ave. Voter check-in begins at 6:30 p.m., and the meeting starts at 7 p.m. If necessary, ATM will continue on Wednesday, May 20 and Thursday, May 21.
