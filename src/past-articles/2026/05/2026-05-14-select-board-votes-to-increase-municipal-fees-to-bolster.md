At their meeting on May 11, the Boxborough Select Board continued to examine municipal fees and consider ways to increase town revenue.

The Board voted unanimously for two fee increases proposed by the Town Treasurer/Collector: increasing the demand fee for the nonpayment of taxes from $10 to $25 and increasing the Municipal Lien Certificate Fee from $25 to $50.

Boxborough Treasurer/Collector Emily Roux presented to the Board on both proposed fee increases. She noted that Massachusetts allows towns to charge a demand fee of up to $30 for the nonpayment of taxes and suggested that Boxborough increase the fee from $10 to $25.

With respect to the Municipal Lien Certificate Fee, Roux explained that these certificates are requested from the town when a property is being transferred or refinanced. She estimated that she receives several requests per week. She noted that most towns charge $50 and suggested that Boxborough increase the fee from $25 to $50.

With acknowledgement from Chair Kristin Hilberg that fee increases can be challenging for residents in these “tough economic times,” the Board voted unanimously for both fee increases.

The Board also approved several adjustments to a list of electrical, plumbing, and gas inspection fees in accordance with the Board’s vote at their last meeting to change the inspection fee structure.

In other business, the Board voted to accept two grants awarded to the Boxborough Fire Department: a Student Awareness of Fire Education (SAFE) grant and a Senior SAFE grant. The grants will allow the Fire Department to conduct fire safety education at Blanchard Memorial School and conduct home safety visits for senior citizens. Fire Chief John Kivlan noted that the grant applications are increasingly time-consuming and commended Captain Jason Malinowski for his work to obtain these grants for the town.

The Board also voted to set the 2026 Fall Town Meeting for Monday, November 16 at Blanchard Memorial School and voted to appoint Channing Wagg to the Boxborough Housing Board for a one-year term (term length at Wagg’s request). At the end of the meeting, after an executive session, the Board returned to open session to approve a contract with the town’s new Principal Assessor, Lynda Cajka.
