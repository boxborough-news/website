It’s Spring in Boxborough, and that means the Public Celebrations and Ceremonies Committee (PCCC) has been busy planning two town parades: the annual Memorial Day parade on Monday, May 25 and the annual Fifer’s Day parade on Saturday, June 20.

PCCC members coordinate with town staff, volunteers, and community groups to organize these back-to-back parades in May and June, which feature both Boxborough Police and Fire Departments, Select Board members, state representatives, Boxborough Minutemen, Boxborough scouting groups, and the Blanchard band under the direction of Chris Baird.

The Memorial Day parade, honoring soldiers who died in service, as well as all veterans buried in Boxborough cemeteries, begins at 8:30 a.m. on Monday, May 25 at the corner of Hill Road and Wetherbee Lane. The parade proceeds along Hill Road to Middle Road and across Route 111 to Stow Road. Along the way, the parade will pause for ceremonies at North Cemetery, Boxborough Town Hall, and South Cemetery.

The Fifer’s Day parade on June 20 is just one component of the town’s annual celebration of Fifer’s Day. The Fifer’s Day tradition goes back to 1967, when the newly-formed Boxborough District Minutemen Company celebrated the first “Fifer’s Festival” to commemorate local contributions to the American Revolution. While the town of Boxborough did not exist during the American Revolution, namesake fifer Luther Blanchard lived in the portion of Littleton that later became Boxborough.

Fifer’s Day is a collaboration between the Boxborough Minutemen, who organize the annual road race, festival, and volleyball tournament, and the PCCC, which coordinates the parade and the presentation of the Golden Fife and Parade Marshal awards.

The Golden Fife is awarded to a Boxborough resident who has demonstrated long-term volunteer service to the town. The Parade Marshal is someone who has done something exceptional for the town, but not necessarily long-term or as a volunteer, and does not have to be a resident. Nominations for both awards can be submitted online at [https://forms.gle/ibS1KbGCbvrcJFBC7](https://forms.gle/ibS1KbGCbvrcJFBC7). Nomination forms must be submitted by Friday, May 22.

The Fifer’s Day parade steps off from Blanchard Memorial School at 11 a.m. and proceeds down Route 111 and Stow Road to Flerra Meadows, arriving in time for the start of the Fifer’s Day festival at noon.

Each year as the parade steps off, the winners of the Golden Fife and Parade Marshal awards are finally revealed, Blanchard Memorial School sixth graders selected to represent Luther Blanchard and Lucie Hager are celebrated, and community groups throw candy to spectators.

This year, the PCCC is inviting even more Boxborough community groups and businesses to join the parade, aiming to increase parade participation and community spirit.

“We would like to extend an invitation to all Boxborough businesses and organizations to join us in celebrating Fifer’s Day by marching in the Fifer’s Day Parade!” said PCCC Chair Jennette Kollmann. “Showcase your community spirit by creating a float, decorating a vehicle, or walking with your team to highlight all the wonderful businesses and organizations that make Boxborough such a special place.”

For more information about the Memorial Day parade, the Fifer’s Day parade, or the Golden Fife and Parade Marshal Nominations, contact [pccc@boxborough-ma.gov.](mailto:pccc@boxborough-ma.gov)
