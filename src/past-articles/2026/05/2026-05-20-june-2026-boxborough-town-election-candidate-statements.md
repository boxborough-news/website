On Tuesday, June 2, Boxborough voters will go to the polls to fill ten town positions. Offices on this year’s ballot are Select Board (two seats), School Committee (two seats), Planning Board (two seats), Library Trustees (two seats), Board of Health (one seat), and Town Moderator (one seat). There are contested races for Select Board, School Committee, and Planning Board, with three candidates running for two seats in each race.

Boxborough News reached out to all thirteen candidates and asked them to answer the question “Why are you running?” in 150 words or less.

The responses that we received from the candidates are below, with candidates listed in the order they appear on the ballot. The candidate’s responses are printed as received; they have not been edited by Boxborough News. Candidate Marie Cannon, running in an uncontested race for Board of Health, did not respond to requests for a statement.

For more information about the candidates running in the three contested races, watch a BXB-TV recording of the Boxborough Candidate Forum hosted by the Acton Area League of Women Voters on May 6 at [https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/boxborough/video/69fc13d973f3290002c8d2ac?page=HOME](https://cloud.castus.tv/vod/boxborough/video/69fc13d973f3290002c8d2ac?page=HOME).

### Kristin Hilberg (Select Board, incumbent)

I’m running for re-election to the Select Board because I love our town. I’ve volunteered here since I was right out of college, serving in both town government and the schools, and that experience has shown me the value of collaboration, listening and thoughtful problem-solving. I believe the Select Board should be approachable, and I want residents to feel comfortable sharing their concerns, questions and ideas. Today, three generations of my family live in town, giving me firsthand perspective on the needs of residents at every stage of life. As we face growing budget pressures, I remain committed to balancing services and fiscal responsibility while finding practical solutions that benefit the community as a whole. I will also continue advocating for state and federal funding to support our schools and town services. I would truly appreciate your vote and would be honored to continue serving and working every day to keep Boxborough the community we all value.

![Priya Sundaram, candidate for Select Board](/media/archive/2026/05-june-2026-boxborough-town-election-candidate-statements-3.jpg)

### Priya Sundaram (Select Board, incumbent)

As your Select Board representative for the last 3 years, I have strived to provide steady, hands-on leadership to address issues of concern to Boxborough residents. Recognizing the need for a new fire station, I worked to build consensus in the community to advance the project through feasibility study, site selection, design funding and zoning changes. In collaboration with my Select Board colleagues and town administrator, I worked to address staffing shortages in Town Hall, Police and Fire departments. To facilitate resident participation, I brought to fruition volunteer run child care at Town meetings. If re-elected, I will provide steady leadership to take the fire station project to its completion, strengthen town fiscal health through thoughtful commercial redevelopment, advocate for additional state funding, and promote proactive measures on water resources, and climate resilience. I will continue to incorporate transparency, civility, communication and cooperation in all of my endeavors.

### Patrick Kyle (Select Board)

I'm running because I want to give back to the town and keep Boxboro as a great place to raise my kids and to keep it that way for future generations to come.

![Rich Guzzardi, candidate for Planning Board](/media/archive/2026/05-june-2026-boxborough-town-election-candidate-statements-5.jpg)

### Rich Guzzardi (Planning Board, incumbent)

My wife Karen and I have lived in Boxborough for 34 years, where we raised our family and remain deeply involved in the community. Over the years, I have served on the Planning Board, chaired the Economic Development Committee, helped lead the town’s small business grant program, and volunteered in youth sports and other community efforts. I am running for re-election because I believe Boxborough needs thoughtful planning, strong collaboration, and disciplined leadership as we address the opportunities and challenges ahead. I have consistently worked to balance growth pressures with preserving Boxborough’s character, open space, natural resources, and quality of life. That includes thoughtful work on the village center, Mass Ave corridor, MBTA Communities implementation, and redevelopment opportunities for aging office parks and other commercial properties that strengthen our tax base. I also believe the Planning Board should operate with clear expectations, better coordination, and timely, well-grounded decisions that serve the entire community.

![Cindy Markowitz, candidate for Planning Board](/media/archive/2026/05-june-2026-boxborough-town-election-candidate-statements-6.jpg)

### Cindy Markowitz (Planning Board, incumbent)

I believe that we can bring together the talent and resources in our community to build a better future through smart growth with a community focus. We can achieve thoughtful, low-impact development while protecting the town’s natural resources and growing the commercial tax base. As a long time Boxborough resident, I bring over 30 years of professional experience in environmental permitting for development projects. I understand careful planning needed to grow responsibly and protect our health, especially in our town, where the drinking water flows from local aquifers. As a Planning Board member, I have approved over 700,000 square feet of commercial space while applying the rigorous review necessary to ensure these projects are in the best interest for Boxborough residents and businesses. I am continuing to explore ways to streamline our review processes. I ask for your vote as I look forward to continuing to serve the Town.

![Gary Kushner, candidate for Planning Board](/media/archive/2026/05-june-2026-boxborough-town-election-candidate-statements-7.jpg)

### Gary Kushner (Planning Board)

Hello, I am Gary Kushner, 31-year resident of Boxborough. We raised our two children through Blanchard and the AB school system. I spent 18 years on the FinCom and 3 years on the Blanchard school committee prior to regionalization. I also served on the library building committee and Boardwalk campus building committee. I created the Boxborough Capital plan and currently reside on the capital committee. I am a program manager at Dell Technologies. I was disappointed that a minority of the planning board members voted against correcting a major mistake made by a previous planning board in leaving "municipal" off our zoning bylaw tables at last year’s STM. This in turn has led to a big delay in the fire station process and an increased cost to the town. I can also see no reason why it should take 8 months to a year to review and either approve or deny a project.

![Andrew Boncoddo, candidate for School Committee](/media/archive/2026/05-june-2026-boxborough-town-election-candidate-statements-8.jpg)

### Andrew Boncoddo (School Committee)

I’m running for School Committee because many issues need to be addressed that require thoughtful leadership. We’re implementing an elementary reset, leading the search for a new superintendent, and managing a budget that is not entirely under our control. These decisions will shape the experience of families and residents for decades, regardless of their participation level in our school district. My background is in managing complex budgets, weighing trade-offs, building consensus, and showing my work. These are skills this committee will need as we monitor progress across these priorities. Effective representation means listening and working closely and respectfully with educators, families, residents, and committee members. It means asking hard questions and being transparent about our answers. We must ensure that every student can take full advantage of the high-quality education that defines our district, and that we are thoughtful about costs and the tax impact for residents.

![Hilary Greven, candidate for School Committee](/media/archive/2026/05-june-2026-boxborough-town-election-candidate-statements-9.jpg)

### Hilary Greven (School Committee)

I am running for School Committee because I want to make a difference within our school district and I am ready to tackle the very important tasks ahead of us. I am committed to a smooth, stable reorganization transition for everyone affected. I believe our next superintendent must fit well with our community, understand our values, and be able to build and maintain trust. I will balance the needs of the district with the financial constraints of the towns and taxpayers, and advocate for strong school funding at both the town and state levels. Given my 14 years of experience in local government and 9 years of volunteering and engagement within the school district — along with a commitment to listening, collaborating, and being transparent — I believe I can contribute meaningfully as a School Committee member and help move our district in a positive direction.

![Felicia Knihtila, candidate for School Committee](/media/archive/2026/05-june-2026-boxborough-town-election-candidate-statements-10.jpg)

### Felicia Knihtila (School Committee)

I’m running for the AB School Committee because I care about the future of our schools and community. As a parent with children in the district, a Licensed Professional Engineer, project manager, and youth hockey coach, I bring personal investment and professional experience to this role. My career has centered on balancing budgets, planning for long term needs, solving complex problems, and bringing the right people together to make thoughtful, data driven decisions. Our schools are at an important turning point. We need to support students, families, and educators through the AB Forward reorganization while addressing long term financial sustainability and the search for our next superintendent. We cannot continue reducing services and expect the same outcomes for students. I believe in transparency, collaboration, and respectful communication. My goal is to ensure Acton Boxborough remains a strong, supportive, and academically excellent district for all students, now and in the future.

![Nancy Settle-Murphy, candidate for Library Trustee](/media/archive/2026/05-june-2026-boxborough-town-election-candidate-statements-11.jpg)

### Nancy Settle-Murphy (Library Trustees, incumbent)

As a Sargent Memorial Library trustee, I want to continue to build on the great work Director Peishan Bartley and her staff have accomplished to create a welcoming, safe space that supports the changing needs of all community members. Over the last six years, we’ve seen a remarkable transformation in the kind of content, programs and services patrons want from our library. We’ve also had more requests for spaces to convene. The library staff redesigned the patio and indoor space in response to requests by community members. (If you haven’t dropped by lately, come see our new indoor gazebo and study pods!) A rich and varied series of programs for children, adults and families continues to draw in people looking to learn new skills, share ideas, or simply to have fun. I see our library as the heartbeat of our town, and I want to continue to be part of it.

![Cynthia MacMillan, candidate for Library Trustee](/media/archive/2026/05-june-2026-boxborough-town-election-candidate-statements-12.jpg)

### Cynthia MacMillan (Library Trustees)

Libraries are more than just places to borrow books — they are welcoming spaces where people of all ages can learn, connect, and grow. My family and I visit the library often, taking advantage of the incredible programs, events, and resources it provides. From children’s and adult activities to community events, I have seen firsthand how important the library is in bringing people together. Libraries inspire curiosity, creativity, learning, and a lifelong love of reading, and I want to help ensure those opportunities continue for everyone in our community. As a frequent library visitor and supporter, I would be proud to serve as a trustee and help the library continue to thrive as a vital community resource for years to come.

![Dennis Reip, candidate for Moderator](/media/archive/2026/05-june-2026-boxborough-town-election-candidate-statements-13.jpg)

### Dennis Reip (Moderator, incumbent)

I am running for reelection to the one-year term for Town Moderator so that I can continue to serve the Town in this essential role. I offer the voters the right combination of experience, ability, and interest to perform as their Moderator. I have presided over 7 Town Meetings in my 4 terms of service and have demonstrated my commitment to providing a fair and productive meeting experience for all. I understand how Town Meeting is supposed to work and will continue to seek ways to improve the meeting process and voter experience by working in partnership with the Select Board, Town Clerk, and Town Administrator. As the appointing authority over the Finance Committee, I will continue to seek qualified volunteers to serve on the Finance Committee. I welcome the opportunity to continue my service to the Town as its Moderator and respectfully ask for your vote on June 2nd.
