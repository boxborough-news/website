The 244th Boxborough Annual Town Meeting (ATM) was called to order by Town Moderator Dennis Reip at 7:05 p.m. on Tuesday May 19 with roughly 160 intrepid voters plus town staff in attendance in a very steamy Blanchard Memorial School gymnasium.

When the meeting ended three hours later, the budget and 47 other articles had been passed, most as part of consent agendas. No articles failed; out of the fifty articles in the warrant, 48 were passed and one was “passed over” with no action taken. Article 1 is the election of town officers, which will take place on June 2.

To the best of anyone’s knowledge, this was the first time in 40 years, and maybe ever, that a Boxborough Annual Town Meeting had finished in one night.

After opening remarks by the moderator, the meeting listened to brief reports from the Acton-Boxborough Regional School Committee (by Boxborough Vice Chair Adam Klein) and the Planning Board (by Chair Rebecca Verner).

Voters then approved salaries for the town clerk and constable and changes to the Personnel Administration Plan and the Plan’s Classification and Compensation Schedule, which included a 3% wage adjustment for non-union and non-contract employees.

After hearing the Finance Committee report (by Chair Tony Newton), and about 30 minutes of questions, the meeting voted a town operating budget of $31,144,981, a 5.5% increase from fiscal year 2026. Approximately 57% of the total was the Acton-Boxborough Regional School assessment, which was up 5.4%.

Traditionally, changes to general and zoning bylaws are the last items on the warrant. In a change from past practice, the Select Board placed three general bylaw changes and four zoning bylaw changes immediately after the budget. Article 6 was a citizen’s petition article to codify the usual “reconsideration motion” as a “bylaw.” This passed with no debate.

Article 7 was originally (as printed in the warrant) a general bylaw proposed by the Planning Board to ban plastic bags and certain types of disposable food containers and other items. However, due to feedback from the public and local businesses, the actual motion brought forward by the Planning Board at ATM banned only plastic bags “as a first step.” This motion passed after about 30 minutes of debate with support being expressed to possibly expand the scope in future years.

Article 8, a Home Rule petition to the State Legislature authorizing the Town of Boxborough to prohibit the use or sale of second generation anticoagulant rodenticides was approved after questions about alternative means of rodent control.

A majority of the Planning Board, as well as the Finance Committee and the Economic Development Committee, asked that Town Meeting take no action on Article 9, a zoning bylaw which would have expanded the scope of the Design Review Board to include the Business and Business-1 Districts. A minority of the Planning Board was opposed to the motion to “pass over” Article 9 and, instead, urged passage of the article. The meeting voted to take no action by passing over the article.

The next three zoning articles to expand the size of accessory dwelling units from 900 to 1,000 square feet, to reduce the required parking for the majority of office and business uses, and to change the definition of specialty food shop to include sale of beverages, including alcohol, all passed by the required two-thirds vote as called by the moderator.

The meeting then proceeded through two articles of the non-financial consent agenda and the ten articles of the financial (non-capital) consent agenda, approving all twelve with debate on only one article. Among others, these included a compensation, classification, and benefits study for town employees, removal of the underground fuel tanks at the Department of Public Works (the only article that was “held” for debate), and a contingency fund to cover costs associated with officers attending the police academy.

Article 25, which asked for $116,000 for cost of implementation and three years of maintenance for a new online permitting system in the Building Department, passed after 20 minutes of vigorous debate.

The meeting then proceeded through 16 articles of the free cash capital consent agenda and two articles of the debt (two-thirds vote required) capital consent agenda, with all 18 of these articles voted with no debate. Among others, these included a police command vehicle; purchase of Automatic External Defibrillators (AEDs) and other life saving medical equipment for fire apparatus, police cars, and public locations in town; purchase of Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus (SCBA) for the Fire Department; purchase of mobile and portable radios for fire department vehicles and firefighters; and two 2-yard compactors for the transfer station.

The meeting ended with the passage of seven Community Preservation Fund articles without debate as part of the Community Preservation Committee consent agenda, including replacement of the Town Hall roof and renovation and improvement of the playground at Flerra Field.

The meeting dissolved at 10:06 p.m.

Following the meeting, Boxborough News reached out to Moderator Reip who stated, “I appreciate the time and attention of all the voters participating in the Annual Town Meeting and the informative debate on a number of articles. We continue to see improvement with process and time savings from the effective use of consent agendas for the articles deemed likely to pass without debate. I thank the Select Board and Finance Committee for their concerted effort to reach unanimous recommendations for so many consent agenda articles.”
