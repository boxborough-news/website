Summer is almost here and that means that the Sargent Memorial Library will, once again, host the Summer Reading Program.

This year's theme is “Dig Up A Story,” celebrating paleontology and archaeology all summer long! That means lots of digging and lots of discoveries. There will be ancient artifacts, dinosaurs, and fossils, and they’ll dig up the best stories about all of it.

Boxborough News reached out to Youth Services Librarian Heather Waddell who told us, “After last year's amazing Level Up At The Library theme, we knew we needed to find something just as cool this year. Luckily, just about everyone loves dinosaurs, so we know this year will be RAWR-some. I'm excited to learn new dinosaur facts, dig into great books, make too many dinosaur puns, and have lots of fun at our events this summer. There are also some surprises planned that are going to be hard to keep secret, but I'm trying!”

The Summer Reading Program starts on June 29. Registration for the events, activities, and the Reading Challenge opens on June 10 on the library’s website ([boxlib.org](http://boxlib.org/)).

The Reading Challenge is open to all ages, from babies through high school. Not-yet-readers are encouraged to participate because listening to books also counts toward the Challenge.

Some of the “bigger events” this summer include a Dinosaur Egg Hunt Kickoff on June 29 and a Fossil Dig (with real fossils) on July 22. “Readers of the Lost Book”, a music, comedy, magic, and storytelling performance, will take place on July 29, and “Mindfulness Story Time with Magical Ms. Kimberly” is scheduled for August 3.

The Summer Reading Program culminates on August 13 with an end-of-summer party, featuring games, ice cream, and a bouncy house.
