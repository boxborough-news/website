This spring, people of all ages flocked to the Sargent Memorial Library to participate in the Spring Movement Series, hosted by the Boxborough Well-Being Committee and the town’s Community & Social Services department.

The series was held on four Saturdays in May; the free classes included Jazzercise/stretch combo, line dancing, Tai Chi/Qi Gong combo, and Bollywood dancing. According to Community Services Coordinator Wendy Trinks, the May 2026 Movement Series was attended by 72 participants.

The program continues to grow in popularity, with a significant increase in participation compared with the prior Movement Series, which had 44 participants.

“The series has seen a huge increase in participation over the last three years, as people are looking to move more and enjoy multicultural programs. A big difference in the 2026 series was increased family participation with parents attending alongside children," Trinks told Boxborough News.

Mary Halvey Dove, Movement Series organizer and Well-Being Committee member, also commented on the success of the Series. “...All sessions were well attended, and when the weather cooperated, they were held outside. Feedback from the attendees was very positive. We hope to continue this series next year."

The Movement Series was made possible by a grant from the Acton-Boxborough Cultural Council.
