With just about a week left until Fifer’s Day on June 20, the Boxborough Public Celebrations and Ceremonies Committee (PCCC) has been busy with preparations for the annual Fifer’s Day parade and ceremony. Fifer’s Day is a collaboration between the Boxborough Minutemen, who organize the annual road race, festival, and volleyball tournament, and the PCCC, which coordinates the parade and the presentation of the Golden Fife and Parade Marshal awards.

This year, the PCCC has focused on growing the parade – inviting a long list of Boxborough businesses and community groups to participate. Several new groups are expected to join the parade for the first time this year, including the Blanchard PTF and the Boxborough Gymnastics Center. The parade will also include perennial favorites like the Boxborough Minutemen, the Blanchard Band, local scouting groups, the Flerra Summer Playground team, and the Boxborough Police and Fire Departments.

The parade will be led by the 2026 Parade Marshal and 2026 Golden Fife winner. The parade will also feature two Blanchard sixth-graders recognized by their teachers for exhibiting Blanchard’s “STARR values”: support, teamwork, attitude, respect, and responsibility. The two students will play the parts of Luther Blanchard, the namesake Revolutionary War fifer, and Lucie Hager, the Boxborough historian who shared Luther’s story.

The Fifer’s Day parade steps off from Blanchard Memorial School at 11 a.m. and arrives at Flerra Meadows by noon. Spectators are encouraged to watch along Massachusetts Avenue and Stow Road. Many of the parade groups plan to toss candy to the crowd. After the parade arrives at Flerra, the Minutemen and the PCCC will hold their traditional ceremony to present the Golden Fife and Parade Marshal awards and recognize this year’s “Luther and Lucie” sixth-graders.

Boxborough businesses and community groups interested in joining the parade can contact the PCCC at [pccc@boxborough-ma.gov.](mailto:pccc@boxborough-ma.gov) “Showcase your community spirit by creating a float, decorating a vehicle, or walking with your team to highlight all the wonderful businesses and organizations that make Boxborough such a special place,” said PCCC Chair Jennette Kollmann.
