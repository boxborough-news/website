In an effort to warn residents about upcoming roadwork, the Boxborough Department of Public Works (DPW) has placed a flashing message sign in front of Town Hall and posted a notice on the Town website.

According to the notice, paving work is set to begin on Monday, June 29, when contractors will be milling (grinding off the top surface of) Middle, Cobleigh, and Depot Roads.

Boxborough News reached out to DPW Director Ed Kukkula to get additional information.

According to Kukkula, the entirety of Middle and Cobleigh Roads will be paved. Depot Road paving work will be limited to the segment between Middle and Liberty Square Roads. Kukkula could not provide an exact time frame for the work to be completed, but he estimated one month as long as the weather cooperates.

The notice indicates that the roads will be closed to through traffic while work is being completed. However, local residents will still be able to access their homes. Police details will be stationed at either end of the roads to assist with traffic.
