The Town of Boxborough welcomed Lynda Cajka (pronounced Ch-eye-ka) as the new Principal Assessor in July 2026. The Principal Assessor “is responsible for determining the value of every real and personal property within the Town for the purpose of taxation.”

Cajka comes to Boxborough with considerable experience in both the private and public sectors. She started her career in residential real estate sales, transitioning to appraising, eventually starting her own business. Cajka said she didn’t always aspire to be an assessor; she just “sort of stumbled into the field” when she was recruited by the town of Westford. After Westford, she joined Boxborough, but left during a turbulent time for the town. In retrospect, Cajka said she “wished she had never left.” Cajka is happy to be back in Boxborough working in a “stable, professional environment with supportive management.”

As the Principal Assessor, Cajka envisions doing more resident outreach. She plans to send out postcards to residents notifying them of upcoming inspections. She said “a property owner’s tax is based on the assessment, which is the full and fair cash value of the property.” Cajka recommends that residents view their property record cards, found on the Assessors page on the town website, every year and reach out to her office with any questions. She can be reached at 978-264-1700, extension 720, or by email at [lcajka@boxborough-ma.gov](mailto:lcajka@boxborough-ma.gov).

In addition to sending out postcards for inspections, Cajka recently developed a flyer that explains exemptions for seniors, veterans, and the blind. She plans to work with the Human Services Director and Community Services Coordinator to do a presentation on this topic in the near future.

When Cajka is not analyzing home sales, conducting inspections, administering abatements and exemptions, or preparing the annual recapitulation for the Department of Revenue, she and her husband can be found on a beach on the Cape; biking; traveling to Europe, the Caribbean, and South America; or gardening.

As an avid gardener, Cajka installed a waterfall feature and a pond where three very large Koi produced 60 baby Koi, which attracted a number of bears. To protect the Koi, she installed an ultrasonic device as a deterrent. The system seems to be working without harming either the bears or the Koi.
