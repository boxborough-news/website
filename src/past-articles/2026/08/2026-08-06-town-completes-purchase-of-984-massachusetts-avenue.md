On Thursday, July 30, the town of Boxborough took ownership of 984 Massachusetts Avenue, the property that the town has selected for its new fire station.

In October 2025, after many years of discussion about whether and where to build a new fire station, the town identified 984 Massachusetts Avenue as a possible location. At the March 2026 Special Town Meeting, residents voted to fund the purchase of the property at 984 Massachusetts Ave.

Since then, the Boxborough Fire Station Building Committee (FSBC), along with town staff and consultants, has been working towards the design and construction of a fire station at that location.

In June and July, Town Administrator Michael Johns reported delays with the property purchase, including the identification of title defects, the completion of an American Land Title Association (ALTA) survey, and a delay of the closing from July 17 to July 30. On July 23, Johns told the FSBC “it’s not unusual for a complex acquisition like this to sometimes be delayed… I want to note that there are no red flags… it’s just a delay in the acquisition.”

The Boxborough Select Board voted for acceptance of the deed on July 27 and the closing took place on July 30.

“We have posted no trespassing signs and put up road barriers,” Johns told Boxborough News in response to a request for comment. “In the coming weeks our consultants will conduct additional borings and soil analysis to better understand the underground rock/soil structure and determine the best placement for the building, well, septic and storm water management system.”

“I think the community will be very pleased with this site for the new firehouse, considering its central location on Massachusetts Avenue, great sightlines for apparatus egress and ingress, relatively flat topography, and very limited need for tree removal,” added Johns. “This was a wise choice made by Boxborough residents, based on the recommendation of the Fire Station Building Committee and the Select Board. This will provide a safe home and effective base of operations for our dedicated team of Boxborough Firefighter/EMTs.”
