So long lemonade stands; hello school buses. The students of Boxborough will soon be returning to school as the Acton-Boxborough Regional School District begins the 2026-2027 academic year on Monday, August 31 (grades K-7 and 9) and Tuesday, September 1 (grades 8 and 10-12).

This school year is the first to implement the “AB Forward” elementary school reorganization and re-enrollment plan that the Acton-Boxborough Regional School Committee voted on earlier this year.

The AB Forward strategic planning process began in Spring 2025 in response to the district’s ongoing budget challenges. In January 2026, the School Committee voted to close the Conant School, one of the six elementary schools in the district, and establish five reorganized elementary schools: Blanchard Memorial School (K-6), Boardwalk Campus Lower (K-3) and Upper (4-6) and Parker Damon Building Lower (K-3) and Upper (4-6). At that meeting, the School Committee also voted to end the district’s longstanding “open enrollment” policy effective June 30, 2026 and implement geography-based enrollment. On April 9, the School Committee voted on a new geography-based enrollment system to replace the district’s prior “open enrollment” system.

In April 2026, the School Committee voted on a geographic zoning system that assigns all of Boxborough, plus a contiguous “Blanchard Expansion Zone” along the western edge of Acton, to the Blanchard Memorial School.

The School Committee also approved a package of transitional policies that included allowing some Acton residents already enrolled at Blanchard to stay at Blanchard, and also voted to implement a “flex zone” in the eastern part of Boxborough. This area, which includes the Reed Farm Road, Sargent Road, and Littlefield Road neighborhoods, as well as parts of the Flagg Hill Road neighborhood, has a primary assignment to Blanchard but a secondary assignment, as needed, to the Boardwalk Campus.

As a result, the vast majority of Boxborough children are expected to attend Blanchard Memorial School for kindergarten through sixth grade, after which they will move on to the regional junior high school in Acton.

On Thursday, August 20, Blanchard held a “new student orientation” for all new students in Grades 1-6 and will hold a kindergarten orientation next week. Throughout the summer, the Blanchard PTF also hosted meet-ups for each grade so that current and new students at each grade-level could get acquainted.

AB Forward has resulted in new educators at Blanchard as well as new students. A number of teachers and staff who were at Blanchard last year were moved to the district’s other elementary schools, while teachers and staff from other schools were reassigned to Blanchard. Blanchard Principal Michael Votto and Vice Principal Julie Babson remain in their respective roles. According to Blanchard PTF President Michelle Norris, approximately 15 of 85 staff members are new to the building this year.

“To help welcome our new staff and support those moving classrooms and getting acquainted with new grade level teacher cohorts, the PTF also organized a ‘Stock the Lounge’ drive, which was hugely successful and will allow us to provide extra snacks and drinks to our staff when they come back to set up their classrooms next week,” said Blanchard PTF President Michelle Norris. “We are also providing breakfast [to staff] on August 27 and are super grateful to Blanchard mom and Thinking Flower owner, Divya Suryaprasad, for supporting us with her donation of pastries.”

“Mr. Votto and Ms. Babson are both energized by the new staff and families joining us this year, and their excitement is contagious,” Norris continued. “I look forward to the opportunity to support our families and caregivers transition and work with them to ensure an awesome year for our students and staff. As always, we're looking for community support as well as new ideas, so please reach out to president@blanchardptf.org.”

The 2026-2027 district calendar can be found at [https://www.abschools.org/calendar](https://www.abschools.org/calendar). More information about the Blanchard PTF is available at [https://www.blanchardptf.org/](https://www.blanchardptf.org/).
